非结构网格格心格式的二维标量方程离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of a two-dimensional scalar equation, on an unstructured cell-centred scheme

算例是一张环形（O 型）混合网格：内圈是物面，外圈是远场特征边界，贴壁一层用四边形、外层用三角形。物面的两种处理全文并行讲解：作无粘壁面时是弱边界条件（写在通量内部，h=0），作无滑移粘性壁面时同样是弱边界条件（通量里取常数右状态 uR=uw）——格心格式的未知量是单元平均值，物面上没有可以强加的自由度。未知量放在单元里。本页先把离散方法拆到最小——每个面上的一维通量；再把同一个面循环依次改写成 matrix-free 前向（计算 Av）和 matrix-free 伴随（计算 ATw）。每个循环都可以逐句播放。The test case is an annular (O-type) hybrid mesh: the inner ring is the body surface, the outer ring a far-field characteristic boundary, with quadrilaterals in the layer against the wall and triangles outside it. The two wall treatments are developed side by side throughout: as an inviscid wall it is a weak boundary condition (imposed inside the flux, h=0); as a no-slip viscous wall it is again a weak condition (a constant right state uR=uw inside the flux) — the unknowns of a cell-centred scheme are cell averages, so there is no degree of freedom on the body to constrain strongly. The unknowns live in the cells. This page first reduces the discretisation to its smallest piece — the one-dimensional flux on each face — and then rewrites that same face loop into matrix-free forward (computing Av) and matrix-free adjoint (computing ATw). Every loop can be played statement by statement.

网格：混合网格、单元与面

Mesh: hybrid grid, cells and faces

采用格心格式有限体积法：每个单元上有一个未知量（单元平均值），画在单元中心处。算例是一张绕物体的环形 O 型网格，3 圈节点、每圈 8 个，共 24 个节点。贴着物面的一层用四边形（8 个），外面一层用三角形（16 个）——这正是实际 CFD 常用的混合网格：近壁面用规则的四边形层，外场用三角形。所以未知量个数 N=24（）。物体本身是非对称的：它有弯度，而且相对远场圆心有偏置，所以流场、阻力和形状梯度都不带任何对称性——换成规则的圆形物体，在连续问题里垂直于来流的那个分量会因对称而为零（网格本身不对称，离散结果只近似为零）。

A cell-centred finite-volume scheme: every cell carries one unknown (its average value), drawn at the cell centre. The test case is an annular O-type mesh around a body — 3 rings of nodes, 8 per ring, 24 nodes in all. The layer against the body uses quadrilaterals (8 of them) and the layer outside uses triangles (16) — exactly the hybrid mesh that real CFD uses: regular quadrilateral layers near the wall, triangles in the far field. So the number of unknowns is N=24 (). The body itself is asymmetric: it has camber and sits off-centre relative to the far field, so the flow, the drag and the shape gradient carry no symmetry at all — with a plain circular body the component normal to the free stream would vanish by symmetry in the continuous problem (only approximately on a mesh that is not itself symmetric).

网格共有 48 条边，每条边就是一个面：其中 32 个内部面各连接两个单元，16 个边界面各属于一个单元。两圈边界的意义完全不同：

The mesh has 48 edges, and every edge is one face: 32 interior faces each join two cells, and 16 boundary faces each belong to a single cell. The two boundary rings mean entirely different things:

  • 内圈（8 个面）：无粘壁面，也就是物面（本页默认；若为无滑移粘性壁面，则在通量里弱加常数右状态 uR=uw，见第 3 节）；
  • 外圈（8 个面）：远场特征边界。
  • Inner ring (8 faces): inviscid wall — the body surface (the default on this page; a no-slip viscous wall enters the flux weakly, as the constant right state uR=uw, see Section 3);
  • Outer ring (8 faces): far-field characteristic boundary.

每个面 f 只需要记住三件事（）：

Each face f only has to remember three things ():

  • 端点 a→b：存储顺序使左单元 L 位于 a→b 的左侧；
  • 左、右单元 L=left(f)、R=right(f)：边界面没有右单元，记 R=0，右状态由边界条件给出；
  • 法向：把边向量 t=xb−xa 顺时针转 90°，n=(ty,−tx)，它从 L 指向 R（边界面指向域外），长度 |n| 等于面长度。
  • Endpoints a→b: stored so that the left cell L lies on the left of a→b;
  • Left and right cells L=left(f), R=right(f): a boundary face has no right cell, written R=0, and its right state is supplied by the boundary condition;
  • Normal: rotate the edge vector t=xb−xa clockwise by 90° to get n=(ty,−tx), pointing from L towards R (out of the domain on a boundary face), with length |n| equal to the face length.

混合网格不影响面循环。四边形单元有 4 个面、三角形有 3 个，但残差、前向、伴随都是按面循环的，循环本身完全不知道单元有几条边——这正是面循环写法的好处。

A hybrid mesh does not disturb the face loop. A quadrilateral has 4 faces and a triangle 3, but the residual, the forward and the adjoint all loop over faces, and the loop itself never learns how many edges a cell has — which is precisely what makes the face-loop formulation convenient.

要离散的方程与面上的一维通量

The equation to discretise, and the one-dimensional flux on a face

要离散的方程

The equation to discretise

为什么用标量方程，而不是 Euler / N–S。本页要讲的是离散伴随的结构：面循环怎么写、怎么对它逐项求导、怎么把它转置。真实的 Euler 方程在每个面上的通量是 4 维（二维）或 5 维（三维）的向量，它的局部导数是 4×4 或 5×5 的块，再加上 Roe 的特征分解、限制器、N–S 的粘性项与湍流模型，∂h/∂uL 就从一个数变成一个矩阵——这些细节会把“收集—计算—分发”这条主线淹没。换成标量守恒律之后，两个局部导数 ∂h/∂uL、∂h/∂uR 各自只是一个数，整条链路才能逐句播放、逐项和差分对照。但骨架完全一样：面循环的写法、边界条件收在一维通量内部、前向与伴随的转置关系，在真实的格心格式求解器里逐字成立——把标量换成状态向量、把局部导数换成块矩阵即可。

Why a scalar equation rather than Euler / N–S. What this page is about is the structure of a discrete adjoint: how the face loop is written, how it is differentiated term by term, how it is transposed. For the real Euler equations the flux on a face is a 4-vector (2-D) or 5-vector (3-D) whose local derivatives are 4×4 or 5×5 blocks, and with Roe's characteristic decomposition, limiters, the N–S viscous terms and a turbulence model on top, ∂h/∂uL turns from a number into a matrix — detail that would drown the gather–compute–scatter storyline. With a scalar conservation law the two local derivatives ∂h/∂uL and ∂h/∂uR are each just one number, so the whole chain can be played statement by statement and checked term by term against finite differences. But the skeleton is identical: the face-loop formulation, boundary conditions kept inside the one-dimensional flux, and the transpose relation between forward and adjoint all hold verbatim in a real cell-centred solver — replace the scalar by a state vector and the local derivatives by block matrices.

本页要离散的是二维标量守恒律：

The page discretises a two-dimensional scalar conservation law:

∂u∂t+∇·F(u)=0,F(u)=12u2β,β=(1,0.5).

通量 F 对 u 是非线性的（∂F/∂u=uβ），所以离散之后得到的 R(u) 同样非线性——这正是后面要用 Newton 迭代求解、并在收敛状态 u* 处线性化的原因。

The flux F is nonlinear in u (with ∂F/∂u=uβ), so the discrete R(u) is nonlinear too — which is exactly why it is solved below by Newton iteration and linearised at the converged state u*.

有限体积离散

Finite-volume discretisation

在单元 c 上积分并用高斯公式，体积分变成控制体边界上的通量之和：

Integrating over cell c and applying the divergence theorem turns the volume integral into a sum of fluxes over the boundary of the control volume:

Vcducdt+Rc(u)=0,Rc=Σf∈∂c±Hf.

到这里，空间离散剩下的唯一问题就是：一个面上的 Hf 怎么算。

At this point the only question left in the spatial discretisation is how to evaluate Hf on a single face.

为了方便说明，本页采用一阶空间离散。面上的左右状态直接取两侧单元当前的值，不做任何重构——也就是把解看成分片常数，因此空间精度是一阶的。真实求解器会在这一步加上二阶重构（MUSCL 或最小二乘梯度）再配一个限制器，那会带来两件事：一是模板从“两侧单元”扩大到邻居的邻居，面通量的局部导数不再只涉及两个单元；二是限制器通常不可微，实用做法是把它在 u* 处冻结——与第 3 节远场入流/出流开关的处理完全一样。但面循环、前向、伴随的结构一点不变：仍然是收集 → 算一维通量 → 分发，仍然靠转置得到伴随。一阶的代价只体现在精度上，不体现在本页要讲的那套代数上。

For clarity this page uses a first-order spatial discretisation. The left and right states on a face are taken directly as the current values of the two adjacent cells, with no reconstruction at all — the solution is treated as piecewise constant, so the spatial accuracy is first order. A real solver would add second-order reconstruction here (MUSCL or a least-squares gradient) together with a limiter, which brings two things: the stencil grows from "the two adjacent cells" to the neighbours' neighbours, so the local derivatives of a face flux involve more than two cells; and the limiter is usually not differentiable, the practical remedy being to freeze it at u* — exactly as the far-field inflow/outflow switch is treated in Section 3. But the face loop, the forward mode and the adjoint keep their structure unchanged: still gather → one-dimensional flux → scatter, still a transpose for the adjoint. First order costs accuracy, not any of the algebra this page is about.

每个面都是一个一维问题

Every face is a one-dimensional problem

通过一个面的通量只和法向分量 F·n 有关。沿法向看，面两侧就是一维问题：左边常数状态 uL，右边常数状态 uR（）。数值通量取左右平均 − 常数耗散系数 ×（右状态 − 左状态）：

The flux through a face depends only on the normal component F·n. Along the normal, the two sides of the face form a one-dimensional problem: a constant state uL on the left and a constant state uR on the right (). The numerical flux is taken as the average of the two sides − a constant dissipation coefficient × (right state − left state):

H(uL,uR)=12[F(uL)+F(uR)]·n左右平均−ε|n|(uR−uL)常数耗散,ε=0.8.
H(uL,uR)=12[F(uL)+F(uR)]·naverage−ε|n|(uR−uL)dissipation,ε=0.8.

记 vn=β·n，由 F(u)·n=12u2vn，中心项就是 14vn(uL2+uR2)。在里拖动滑块，观察两项如何组成通量。以下把面 f 上的这个数记作 h=H(uL,uR)。

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈和外圈的物理意义完全不同，处理方式也不同。两者都只在上一节那个一维通量内部起作用：边界面没有“右单元”，远场由边界条件给出右状态 uR，无粘壁面则直接令 h=0。把各个面的通量拼成残差的面循环在第 4 节展开——它本身一行都不用改。

The inner and outer rings mean physically different things and are treated differently. Both of them act only inside the one-dimensional flux of the previous section: a boundary face has no "right cell", so at the far field the boundary condition supplies the right state uR, while the inviscid wall sets h=0 outright. The face loop that assembles these fluxes into the residual is unfolded in Section 4 — and not one line of it has to change.

内圈：无粘壁面

Inner ring: the inviscid wall

无粘壁面的物理条件是无穿透：流体不能穿过物面。对本页的标量模型，这意味着通过壁面的对流通量直接为零：

The physical condition at an inviscid wall is no penetration: fluid cannot cross the body surface. For the scalar model on this page that means the convective flux through the wall is simply zero:

hwall=0.

于是壁面不改变残差。这一点在后面的演示里可以直接看到：播放到壁面时，h=0（）。

So a wall face does not change the residual. The demos show this directly: step onto a wall face and h=0 ().

外圈：远场特征边界

Outer ring: the far-field characteristic boundary

远场要按特征方向决定用哪一侧的信息。标量守恒律 F(u)=12u2β 的波速是 uvn（vn=β·n，n 指向域外）。算例中 u>0，所以 sign(uvn)=sign(vn)，判据是纯几何的：

At the far field the characteristic direction decides which side supplies the information. The wave speed of the scalar law F(u)=12u2β is uvn (with vn=β·n and n pointing out of the domain). In the test case u>0, so sign(uvn)=sign(vn) and the test is purely geometric:

  • 入流（vn<0，特征从域外进来）：右状态取自由来流 uR=u∞=1.0；
  • 出流（vn≥0，特征从域内出去）：右状态由内部外推，uR=uL。
  • Inflow (vn<0, the characteristic enters from outside): the right state is the free stream, uR=u∞=1.0;
  • Outflow (vn≥0, the characteristic leaves the domain): the right state is extrapolated from inside, uR=uL.

算例的 8 个远场面里 4 个入流、4 个出流（）。开关在冻结状态 u* 处固定（frozen switch），前向与伴随都在这个固定的分支上线性化。

Of the 8 far-field faces in the test case, 4 are inflow and 4 outflow (). The switch is frozen at the converged state u*, and both the forward and the adjoint are linearised on that fixed branch.

这是把边界条件收在右状态与一维通量这一层的好处：面循环的骨架——收集、算通量、分发——与边界条件是哪几种、有几种都无关（代码里这些分支都在 flux 一侧，上面的清单把它们逐行摊开，只为能逐句播放）。

This is the benefit of keeping the boundary conditions at the level of the right state and the one-dimensional flux: the skeleton of the face loop — gather, evaluate, scatter — depends on neither which kinds of boundary there are nor how many (in the code these branches all sit on the flux side; the listing above spells them out line by line only so they can be played statement by statement).

物面无滑移：格心格式的真实做法

No-slip on the body: what a cell-centred solver really does

上面两种边界都是弱的：条件写在一维通量内部，未知量本身照样参加求解。无滑移是加在状态上的 Dirichlet 条件（真实 N–S 里是速度矢量在物面为零，本页的标量模型写成 u=uw）。它不替代上面的无粘壁面，而是“物面改成粘性壁”时的另一套处理。

Both boundaries above are weak: the condition lives inside the one-dimensional flux and the unknown itself still takes part in the solve. No-slip is a Dirichlet condition on the state (in real N–S the velocity vector vanishes on the body; in the scalar model here it is written u=uw). It does not replace the inviscid wall above; it is the alternative treatment when the body is made a viscous wall.

格心格式没有“住在物面上”的自由度——未知量是单元平均值，物面上不存在任何未知量。所以真实的格心格式求解器把无滑移弱加：在通量里给一个常数右状态 uR=uw，结构上和上面的入流分支完全一样（右状态是常数，不含未知量）。面循环、边界分支、残差的写法全都不变。本页的演示与实测都采用这种弱加（）。

A cell-centred scheme has no unknown living on the body — the unknowns are cell averages and nothing sits on the surface. So a real cell-centred solver imposes no-slip weakly: a constant right state uR=uw in the flux, structurally identical to the inflow branch above (the right state is a constant, carrying no unknown). The face loop, the boundary branch and the residual are all written exactly as before. The demos and measurements on this page all use this weak form ().

残差 = 面循环：收集、计算、分发

Residual = the face loop: gather, compute, scatter

残差计算对每个面做三件事：从两侧单元收集状态，调用一维通量计算 h，再把 h 分发回两个单元——左单元加、右单元减。同一个 h 以相反符号进入两个单元，这就是守恒。

Computing the residual does three things on every face: gather the states from the two adjacent cells, compute h by calling the one-dimensional flux, and scatter h back into the two cells — added to the left cell, subtracted from the right. The same h enters the two cells with opposite signs, and that is conservation.

反过来看一个单元：它的残差就是自己所有面的通量之和，符号由它在每个面上是 L 还是 R 决定（）。四边形单元有 4 项，三角形有 3 项。

Seen from a cell instead: its residual is the sum of the fluxes of all its own faces, each signed by whether the cell is L or R on that face (). A quadrilateral contributes 4 terms, a triangle 3.

逐句执行这个循环。网格上：紫色是当前面，箭头从单元中心指向面（收集）、再从面指向中心（分发）。面板底部是两条数组：上面是输入的单元状态，下面是正在累加的残差。可以按 ▶ 连续播放，也可以用“下一步”或键盘左右键逐句走，或用右上角的下拉框直接跳到某个面（例如跳到一个壁面，看无粘壁面的 h=0；切到无滑移后，同一个面改走 uR=uw 的通量分支）。

无滑移（弱加）在 primal 里的位置。只在壁面分支里：右状态从“不需要”（无粘，直接 h=0）换成常数 uw，然后照常调用 flux。循环之外什么都不做——没有后处理，也没有需要特殊对待的行；tangent 与 adjoint 同样如此，壁面面就像一个右状态为 uw 的入流面。

Where weak no-slip sits in the primal. Only in the wall branch: the right state goes from "not needed" (inviscid, straight to h=0) to the constant uw, and flux is called as usual. Nothing happens outside the loop — no post-processing, and no row that needs special treatment; the tangent and the adjoint are the same, with the wall face behaving like an inflow face whose right state is uw.

弱加保住了守恒，代价是条件不再逐点精确。每一行仍然是“该单元上通量之和为零”：壁面通量 h 就是穿过物面的那一份，照常计入残差。另一面，uw 只是写进通量的右状态，贴壁单元的值并不等于 uw——算例的无滑移解里，8 个贴壁单元取值在 0.295 到 0.775 之间。强加正好相反：那几行不再守恒，但 ui=uw 精确成立，这是节点中心格式的做法。

Weak imposition keeps conservation; the price is that the condition no longer holds pointwise. Every row still says "the fluxes over this cell sum to zero": the wall flux h is the share crossing the body and enters the residual like any other. On the other hand, uw is only a right state written into the flux, so the wall-adjacent cells do not take the value uw — in the no-slip solution of the test case the 8 wall-adjacent cells lie between 0.295 and 0.775. Strong imposition is the opposite: those rows stop being conservative, but ui=uw holds exactly, which is what a node-centred scheme does.

演示里可以直接切换两种壁面处理。第 4、7、8 节的三个演示面板底部都有一对按钮：无粘（弱加 h=0） 与 有粘：无滑移（弱加 uR=uw）。两种处理共用同一份伪代码，行数和循环结构都不变：切换之后，壁面面不再走 h←0 那一行，而是取常数右状态 uw 再算通量；面板同时改用无滑移问题自己的收敛解 u* 与它的 Jacobian，矩阵视图照常显示。

从残差到 Jacobian

The residual(u) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u)∈ℝN. Its Jacobian A=∂R/∂u is N×N (24×24 in the test case). This section forms it explicitly — with only 24 unknowns in the test case, Newton can solve with it directly; Sections 7 and 8 then show how to multiply by A without ever forming it.

A 的每一份贡献都来自一个面：对第 2 节那个一维通量求局部导数，

Every contribution to A comes from one face: differentiate the one-dimensional flux of Section 2 locally,

再把这两个数按和残差完全相同的符号累加到 A 的四个位置：ALL+=cL、ALR+=cR、ARL−=cL、ARR−=cR。边界面只有 L 一侧，所以只落在 ALL 上；无粘壁面 cL=cR=0，整个面对 A 没有贡献。

and accumulate those two numbers into four entries of A with exactly the signs the residual used: ALL+=cL, ALR+=cR, ARL−=cL, ARR−=cR. A boundary face has only an L side, so it lands on ALL alone; on an inviscid wall cL=cR=0 and the face contributes nothing to A at all.

怎么把 R(u)=0 解出来

How R(u)=0 is actually solved

上面已经把 residual(u) 和它的 Jacobian 都造出来了，却还没有说这个非线性方程组是怎么解开的。这里补上——顺带说清一件容易误会的事：primal 求解这一步并不是 matrix-free 的，而且是故意如此。

This step does form A explicitly. The primal solve uses the dumbest and most robust method available: accumulate the whole 24×24 A face by face, then one dense LU with partial pivoting. With only N=24 that is both cheap and unobtrusive: its sole job is to produce the u* that is then frozen. At realistic sizes this would become Krylov plus a preconditioner, and the Av each of its steps needs is exactly the subject of Section 7.

固定轮次，但会检查。代码固定跑 60 轮，而不是一收敛就退出：页面上每一个实测常数都是在这些轮次下量出来的，提前退出会让 u* 在末位比特上改变，从而把所有有限差分对照的数字整体挪动。真正要紧的是有没有检查——现在它记录判据在第几轮满足，若 60 轮内从未满足就直接报错，而不会把一个未收敛的状态当成 u* 返回。

A fixed sweep count, but a checked one. The code runs a fixed 60 sweeps rather than exiting on convergence: every measured constant quoted on this page was produced under those sweeps, and exiting early would shift u* in its last bits and move every finite-difference comparison with it. What actually matters is whether it is checked — it now records the sweep at which the criterion was met, and raises an error if 60 sweeps never meet it, rather than returning an unconverged state as u*.

“伴随”就是转置

"Adjoint" means transpose

对任意矩阵 A 和向量 v,w，恒有

For any matrix A and vectors v,w, it always holds that

⟨w,Av⟩=wTAv=(ATw)Tv=⟨ATw,v⟩.

满足这一关系的算子称为伴随算子，对实矩阵就是转置。前向乘积把“状态上的扰动”映射成“残差的变化”；伴随乘积方向相反，把“残差上的权重”映射成“状态上的敏感度”。

An operator satisfying this relation is called the adjoint; for a real matrix it is the transpose. The forward product maps "a perturbation of the state" to "the change in the residual"; the adjoint product runs the other way, mapping "a weight on the residual" to "a sensitivity on the state".

What matrix-free means

v↦Av前向乘积w↦ATw伴随乘积

On a real mesh N can reach 105–108. Krylov solvers such as GMRES never read an individual matrix entry; all they need is a function that takes a vector and returns the product (Jacobi needs only the diagonal besides). matrix-free means exactly that: never form or store A, and instead write residual_d(u, v) returning Av and residual_b(u, w) returning ATw.

Forward is easy, adjoint is not. Av can be approximated with a single perturbation: Av≈[R(u+δv)−R(u)]/δ. There is no such formula for ATw — the residual computation has to be differentiated in reverse.

Reversing the one-dimensional flux

前向的一维通量是“两个输入、一个输出”：(vL,vR)↦hd=cLvL+cRvR。它的转置是“一个输入、两个输出”：给面上的权重 hb，返回

The forward one-dimensional flux is "two inputs, one output": (vL,vR)↦hd=cLvL+cRvR. Its transpose is "one input, two outputs": given a weight hb on the face, it returns

(uL,b,uR,b)=(cLhb,cRhb).

整个面循环也按同样的规则倒过来：原来写 r[L] 的地方现在读 w[L]，原来读 u[L] 的地方现在写 y[L]。注意符号：原来是 r[L]+=h、r[R]−=h，转置后就是 hb=w[L]−w[R]；两个分发则都是加。

在里对比第 7 节：底部数组的箭头方向反过来了——输入权重 w 在下面（残差一侧），结果 y 在上面（状态一侧）。这就是“转置”在数据流上的样子。无粘壁面上 cL=0，所以它在伴随里同样什么都不贡献；无滑移壁面则和入流面一样：y[L] 照常加上 cLhb，uR,b 因为 uw 是常数而丢弃。

Compare with Section 7: the arrows on the bottom arrays have reversed — the input weights w are below (the residual side) and the result y above (the state side). That is what "transpose" looks like as data flow. Weak no-slip adds no step to the adjoint. No zeroing and no adding back: the wall face just produces one more cLhb inside flux_b. Three steps — compute nb, transpose the rotation back onto the endpoints, scatter to the two nodes — are the whole of the geometric adjoint. 无滑移（弱加）时的几何项

The geometric term under weak no-slip

弱加的无滑移壁面照常算通量 h=H(uL,uw,n)，所以它和其他面一样，通过 ∂h/∂n 进入 ψT∂R/∂X——这是它与无粘壁面（h≡0，对这一项毫无贡献）唯一的区别。右状态 uw 是常数，与网格无关，所以公式和别的面一字不差；目标函数的显式项 ∂L/∂n=12uL2e 也不变。不需要清零任何种子，gradient 还是那一个面循环。

A weakly imposed no-slip wall computes its flux h=H(uL,uw,n) as usual, so like every other face it enters ψT∂R/∂X through ∂h/∂n — its one difference from an inviscid wall (h≡0, which contributes nothing to that term). The right state uw is a constant with nothing to do with the mesh, so the formula is word for word that of any other face, and the explicit term of the objective, ∂L/∂n=12uL2e, is unchanged. No seed has to be zeroed: gradient is still that one face loop.

对照强加。若像节点中心格式那样强加 ui=uw，物面上每个 u 都是常数，这个 L 会恒等于零（物体封闭，Σ壁面n=0）；primal、tangent、adjoint 与几何梯度也都要各做一次替换或清零。弱加没有这些：条件只住在通量里，四个面循环都不用改——代价是 uw 不再逐点精确成立（第 4 节）。

Against strong imposition. Imposing ui=uw strongly, as a node-centred scheme does, makes every u on the body a constant, so this L becomes identically zero (the body is closed, Σwalln=0), and primal, tangent, adjoint and the geometric gradient each need their own replacement or zeroing. Weak imposition needs none of this: the condition lives only in the flux, and none of the four face loops changes — the price being that uw no longer holds pointwise (Section 4).

验证：dL/dX 对有限差分

Verification: dL/dX against finite differences

上一节把 dL/dX 推了出来：伴随只解一次线性系统，就拿到全部分量。这一节只做一件事——验证它是对的。

The previous section derived dL/dX: the adjoint solves one linear system and gets every component. This section does one thing only — check that it is right.

判据是最朴素的那个，逐个坐标分量做中心差分：

The criterion is the plainest one there is, a central difference taken coordinate by coordinate:

dLdXk≈L(Xk+h)−L(Xk−h)2h.

关键在于 L(Xk±h) 是完整的目标函数：每次都要把网格改形、重新解一次非线性 primal、再算目标。所以这里验证的是全导数——状态对几何的响应也算在内——而不是冻结状态的偏导数。代价也正因如此：中心差分每个分量两次扰动，一共 2M 次非线性求解，而伴随只用了一次线性求解。

What matters is that L(Xk±h) is the complete objective: every evaluation deforms the mesh, solves the nonlinear primal again, and only then computes the objective. So what is verified here is the total derivative — the response of the state to the geometry included — and not a partial derivative at a frozen state. That is also what it costs: two perturbations per component, 2M nonlinear solves in all, against the single linear solve of the adjoint.

还有省一半的做法——前向差分，只往一侧扰动：

There is also a way to halve that — a forward difference, perturbing to one side only:

dLdXk≈L(Xk+h)−L(X)h.

基准值 L(X) 全局只算一次，于是总共约 M 次非线性求解而不是 2M 次。代价是精度低一个档次：截断误差是 O(h) 而不是 O(h2)。两者的误差随步长怎么走， 底部可以逐个变量扫描出来。

The baseline L(X) is computed once for all, so the total is about M nonlinear solves rather than 2M. The price is one order of accuracy: the truncation error is O(h) instead of O(h2). The sweep at the foot of plots how the two errors run with the step size, one variable at a time.

The horizontal axis is the step h and the vertical axis the relative error — the difference between the finite-difference and the adjoint result, divided by the magnitude of that component's own adjoint gradient — both logarithmic. The left half is dominated by truncation: for every tenfold reduction of h the forward scheme falls by 10 (O(h)) and the central scheme by 100 (O(h2)). The right half is dominated by round-off, identical for both (each rising about tenfold), so the two curves close together. The two rings mark the optimal step of each: typically the forward one lies near ε1/2 and the central one near ε1/3, and the best accuracy of the central scheme is usually around two orders of magnitude better; the exact figures vary from component to component. 步长为什么要能调。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ε/h)（ε 为机器精度），随 h 变小反而增大。两者相抵，最佳步长大致在 h∼ε1/3，也就是 10−5 到 10−6 量级。把 h 调到 10−3 会看到截断误差主导，调到 10−10 会看到舍入噪声主导——两头都会让“验证”看起来像是失败了。这正是差分校验最容易骗人的地方，也是点积测试（第 8 节）比它更可靠的原因：点积测试是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。

Why the step has to be adjustable. The error of a central difference has two parts: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks as h shrinks; and round-off, about O(ε/h) (with ε the machine precision), which grows as h shrinks. Between them the best step lies roughly at h∼ε1/3, of order 10−5 to 10−6. Set h to 10−3 and truncation dominates; set it to 10−10 and round-off noise does — at either end the "verification" looks like a failure. This is exactly where a difference check is most deceptive, and why the dot-product test (Section 8) is the more reliable of the two: it is an exact identity, with no step size in it at all.

那么哪一侧更糟？要看离最优点多远，因为两侧的斜率根本不同：h 每放大一个数量级，截断误差涨约 100 倍（二次）；h 每缩小一个数量级，舍入误差只涨约 10 倍（线性）。实测扫描里截断一侧的斜率很干净，舍入一侧则起伏不定（舍入误差本身近乎随机）；中心差分的最小值多数落在 h 约 10−4 到 10−6 之间，与 ε1/3 的量级一致。于是离最优点越远，偏大的那一侧通常越吃亏，但对个别分量两侧可能相差不到一个数量级。实用结论是——宁可把 h 取偏小，也不要取偏大。

So which side is worse? It depends how far from the optimum you are, because the two slopes are quite different: enlarging h by one order multiplies the truncation error by about 100 (quadratic), while shrinking h by one order multiplies the round-off error by only about 10 (linear). In the measured sweep the truncation slope is clean while the round-off side is ragged (round-off is close to random); the central minimum mostly lies between h≈10−4 and 10−6, consistent with the ε1/3 estimate. So the further from the optimum, the more the too-large side usually costs, though for a few components the two sides differ by less than an order of magnitude. The practical conclusion — better to take h a little too small than a little too large.

  • 混合网格不改变写法：四边形 4 个面、三角形 3 个面，但面循环按面走，完全不关心单元类型。
  • A hybrid mesh changes nothing in how this is written: a quadrilateral has 4 faces and a triangle 3, but the face loop walks faces and never asks what kind of cell it is.
  • 边界条件只住在 flux 里面：无粘壁面取 h=0，无滑移壁面取常数右状态 uw，远场按特征方向选入流/出流；面循环一行都不用改。
  • Boundary conditions live inside flux alone: an inviscid wall takes h=0, a no-slip wall the constant right state uw, and the far field picks inflow or outflow by the characteristic direction; not one line of the face loop changes.
  • 无滑移也不例外：本页弱加，壁面面就像右状态为 uw 的入流面，primal、tangent、adjoint 与几何梯度都只在壁面分支里多出一项，循环之外没有任何步骤。强加（整行替换）是节点中心格式的做法：那时 primal 与 tangent 在循环之后替换或覆盖行，adjoint 在循环之前清零、之后加回。
  • No-slip is no exception: this page imposes it weakly, so the wall face behaves like an inflow face with right state uw; primal, tangent, adjoint and the geometric gradient each gain one term in the wall branch, and nothing happens outside the loop. Strong imposition (replacing the row) is what a node-centred scheme does: there primal and the tangent replace or overwrite rows after the loop, and the adjoint zeroes weights before it and adds them back after.
  • 一维通量是核心：格心格式的残差就是“对每个面收集两侧状态、调用一次一维通量、再分发”。
  • The one-dimensional flux is the core: the residual of a cell-centred scheme is simply "on every face, gather the two states, call the one-dimensional flux once, then scatter".
  • matrix-free 前向：对一维通量求局部导数 cL,cR，面循环不变。
  • Matrix-free forward: take the local derivatives cL,cR of the one-dimensional flux; the face loop is unchanged.
  • matrix-free 伴随：每个面的贡献转置——一维通量从“两个输入、一个输出”变为“一个输入、两个输出”，面循环中收集与分发互换、数据方向反转。
  • Matrix-free adjoint: transpose the contribution of each face — the one-dimensional flux goes from "two inputs, one output" to "one input, two outputs", and in the face loop gather and scatter swap while the data direction reverses.
  • 验证：点积测试检查转置，差分检查梯度。
  • Verification: the dot-product test checks the transpose, finite differences check the gradient.
