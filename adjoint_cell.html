非结构网格格心格式的二维标量方程离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of a two-dimensional scalar equation, on an unstructured cell-centred scheme

算例是一张环形（O 型）混合网格：内圈是物面，外圈是远场特征边界，贴壁一层用四边形、外层用三角形。物面是无粘壁面，一个弱边界条件（写在通量内部，h=0）；粘性壁面是另一对页面——二维标量对流扩散方程——的题目。未知量放在单元里。本页先把离散方法拆到最小——每个面上的一维通量；再把同一个面循环依次改写成 matrix-free 前向（计算 Av）和 matrix-free 伴随（计算 ATw）。每个循环都可以逐句播放。The test case is an annular (O-type) hybrid mesh: the inner ring is the body surface, the outer ring a far-field characteristic boundary, with quadrilaterals in the layer against the wall and triangles outside it. The body is an inviscid wall, a weak boundary condition (imposed inside the flux, h=0); a viscous wall is the subject of another pair of pages, the two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation. The unknowns live in the cells. This page first reduces the discretisation to its smallest piece — the one-dimensional flux on each face — and then rewrites that same face loop into matrix-free forward (computing Av) and matrix-free adjoint (computing ATw). Every loop can be played statement by statement.

网格：混合网格、单元与面

Mesh: hybrid grid, cells and faces

采用格心格式有限体积法：每个单元上有一个未知量（单元平均值），画在单元中心处。算例是一张绕物体的环形 O 型网格，3 圈节点、每圈 8 个，共 24 个节点。贴着物面的一层用四边形（8 个），外面一层用三角形（16 个）——这正是实际 CFD 常用的混合网格：近壁面用规则的四边形层，外场用三角形。所以未知量个数 N=24（）。物体本身是非对称的：它有弯度，而且相对远场圆心有偏置，所以流场、阻力和形状梯度都不带任何对称性——换成规则的圆形物体，在连续问题里垂直于来流的那个分量会因对称而为零（网格本身不对称，离散结果只近似为零）。

A cell-centred finite-volume scheme: every cell carries one unknown (its average value), drawn at the cell centre. The test case is an annular O-type mesh around a body — 3 rings of nodes, 8 per ring, 24 nodes in all. The layer against the body uses quadrilaterals (8 of them) and the layer outside uses triangles (16) — exactly the hybrid mesh that real CFD uses: regular quadrilateral layers near the wall, triangles in the far field. So the number of unknowns is N=24 (). The body itself is asymmetric: it has camber and sits off-centre relative to the far field, so the flow, the drag and the shape gradient carry no symmetry at all — with a plain circular body the component normal to the free stream would vanish by symmetry in the continuous problem (only approximately on a mesh that is not itself symmetric).

网格共有 48 条边，每条边就是一个面：其中 32 个内部面各连接两个单元，16 个边界面各属于一个单元。两圈边界的意义完全不同：

The mesh has 48 edges, and every edge is one face: 32 interior faces each join two cells, and 16 boundary faces each belong to a single cell. The two boundary rings mean entirely different things:

  • 内圈（8 个面）：无粘壁面，也就是物面；
  • 外圈（8 个面）：远场特征边界。
  • Inner ring (8 faces): inviscid wall — the body surface;
  • Outer ring (8 faces): far-field characteristic boundary.

每个面 f 只需要记住三件事（）：

Each face f only has to remember three things ():

  • 端点 a→b：存储顺序使左单元 L 位于 a→b 的左侧；
  • 左、右单元 L=left(f)、R=right(f)：边界面没有右单元，记 R=0，右状态由边界条件给出；
  • 法向：把边向量 t=xb−xa 顺时针转 90°，n=(ty,−tx)，它从 L 指向 R（边界面指向域外），长度 |n| 等于面长度。
  • Endpoints a→b: stored so that the left cell L lies on the left of a→b;
  • Left and right cells L=left(f), R=right(f): a boundary face has no right cell, written R=0, and its right state is supplied by the boundary condition;
  • Normal: rotate the edge vector t=xb−xa clockwise by 90° to get n=(ty,−tx), pointing from L towards R (out of the domain on a boundary face), with length |n| equal to the face length.

混合网格不影响面循环。四边形单元有 4 个面、三角形有 3 个，但残差、前向、伴随都是按面循环的，循环本身完全不知道单元有几条边——这正是面循环写法的好处。

A hybrid mesh does not disturb the face loop. A quadrilateral has 4 faces and a triangle 3, but the residual, the forward and the adjoint all loop over faces, and the loop itself never learns how many edges a cell has — which is precisely what makes the face-loop formulation convenient.

要离散的方程与面上的一维通量

The equation to discretise, and the one-dimensional flux on a face

要离散的方程

The equation to discretise

为什么用标量方程，而不是 Euler / N–S。本页要讲的是离散伴随的结构：面循环怎么写、怎么对它逐项求导、怎么把它转置。真实的 Euler 方程在每个面上的通量是 4 维（二维）或 5 维（三维）的向量，它的局部导数是 4×4 或 5×5 的块，再加上 Roe 的特征分解、限制器、N–S 的粘性项与湍流模型，∂h/∂uL 就从一个数变成一个矩阵——这些细节会把“收集—计算—分发”这条主线淹没。换成标量守恒律之后，两个局部导数 ∂h/∂uL、∂h/∂uR 各自只是一个数，整条链路才能逐句播放、逐项和差分对照。但骨架完全一样：面循环的写法、边界条件收在一维通量内部、前向与伴随的转置关系，在真实的格心格式求解器里逐字成立——把标量换成状态向量、把局部导数换成块矩阵即可。

Why a scalar equation rather than Euler / N–S. What this page is about is the structure of a discrete adjoint: how the face loop is written, how it is differentiated term by term, how it is transposed. For the real Euler equations the flux on a face is a 4-vector (2-D) or 5-vector (3-D) whose local derivatives are 4×4 or 5×5 blocks, and with Roe's characteristic decomposition, limiters, the N–S viscous terms and a turbulence model on top, ∂h/∂uL turns from a number into a matrix — detail that would drown the gather–compute–scatter storyline. With a scalar conservation law the two local derivatives ∂h/∂uL and ∂h/∂uR are each just one number, so the whole chain can be played statement by statement and checked term by term against finite differences. But the skeleton is identical: the face-loop formulation, boundary conditions kept inside the one-dimensional flux, and the transpose relation between forward and adjoint all hold verbatim in a real cell-centred solver — replace the scalar by a state vector and the local derivatives by block matrices.

本页要离散的是二维标量守恒律：

The page discretises a two-dimensional scalar conservation law:

∂u∂t+∇·F(u)=0,F(u)=12u2β,β=(1,0.5).

通量 F 对 u 是非线性的（∂F/∂u=uβ），所以离散之后得到的 R(u) 同样非线性——这正是后面要迭代求解、并在收敛状态 u* 处线性化的原因。

The flux F is nonlinear in u (with ∂F/∂u=uβ), so the discrete R(u) is nonlinear too — which is exactly why it is solved below by iteration and linearised at the converged state u*.

有限体积离散

Finite-volume discretisation

在单元 c 上积分并用高斯公式，体积分变成控制体边界上的通量之和：

Integrating over cell c and applying the divergence theorem turns the volume integral into a sum of fluxes over the boundary of the control volume:

Vcducdt+Rc(u)=0,Rc=Σf∈∂c±Hf.

到这里，空间离散剩下的唯一问题就是：一个面上的 Hf 怎么算。

At this point the only question left in the spatial discretisation is how to evaluate Hf on a single face.

为了方便说明，本页采用一阶空间离散。面上的左右状态直接取两侧单元当前的值，不做任何重构——也就是把解看成分片常数，因此空间精度是一阶的。真实求解器会在这一步加上二阶重构（MUSCL 或最小二乘梯度）再配一个限制器，那会带来两件事：一是模板从“两侧单元”扩大到邻居的邻居，面通量的局部导数不再只涉及两个单元；二是限制器通常不可微，实用做法是把它在 u* 处冻结——与第 3 节远场入流/出流开关的处理完全一样。但面循环、前向、伴随的结构一点不变：仍然是收集 → 算一维通量 → 分发，仍然靠转置得到伴随。一阶的代价只体现在精度上，不体现在本页要讲的那套代数上。

For clarity this page uses a first-order spatial discretisation. The left and right states on a face are taken directly as the current values of the two adjacent cells, with no reconstruction at all — the solution is treated as piecewise constant, so the spatial accuracy is first order. A real solver would add second-order reconstruction here (MUSCL or a least-squares gradient) together with a limiter, which brings two things: the stencil grows from "the two adjacent cells" to the neighbours' neighbours, so the local derivatives of a face flux involve more than two cells; and the limiter is usually not differentiable, the practical remedy being to freeze it at u* — exactly as the far-field inflow/outflow switch is treated in Section 3. But the face loop, the forward mode and the adjoint keep their structure unchanged: still gather → one-dimensional flux → scatter, still a transpose for the adjoint. First order costs accuracy, not any of the algebra this page is about.

每个面都是一个一维问题

Every face is a one-dimensional problem

通过一个面的通量只和法向分量 F·n 有关。沿法向看，面两侧就是一维问题：左边常数状态 uL，右边常数状态 uR（）。数值通量取左右平均 − 常数耗散系数 ×（右状态 − 左状态）：

The flux through a face depends only on the normal component F·n. Along the normal, the two sides of the face form a one-dimensional problem: a constant state uL on the left and a constant state uR on the right (). The numerical flux is taken as the average of the two sides − a constant dissipation coefficient × (right state − left state):

H(uL,uR)=12[F(uL)+F(uR)]·n左右平均−ε|n|(uR−uL)常数耗散,ε=0.8.
H(uL,uR)=12[F(uL)+F(uR)]·naverage−ε|n|(uR−uL)dissipation,ε=0.8.

记 vn=β·n，由 F(u)·n=12u2vn，中心项就是 14vn(uL2+uR2)。在里拖动滑块，观察两项如何组成通量。以下把面 f 上的这个数记作 h=H(uL,uR)。

Writing vn=β·n and using F(u)·n=12u2vn, the central term is 14vn(uL2+uR2). Drag the sliders in to watch how the two terms make up the flux. From here on this number on face f is written h=H(uL,uR).

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈和外圈的物理意义完全不同，处理方式也不同。两者都只在上一节那个一维通量内部起作用：边界面没有“右单元”，远场由边界条件给出右状态 uR，无粘壁面则直接令 h=0。把各个面的通量拼成残差的面循环在第 4 节展开——它本身一行都不用改。

The inner and outer rings mean physically different things and are treated differently. Both of them act only inside the one-dimensional flux of the previous section: a boundary face has no "right cell", so at the far field the boundary condition supplies the right state uR, while the inviscid wall sets h=0 outright. The face loop that assembles these fluxes into the residual is unfolded in Section 4 — and not one line of it has to change.

内圈：无粘壁面

Inner ring: the inviscid wall

无粘壁面的物理条件是无穿透：流体不能穿过物面。对本页的标量模型，这意味着通过壁面的对流通量直接为零：

The physical condition at an inviscid wall is no penetration: fluid cannot cross the body surface. For the scalar model on this page that means the convective flux through the wall is simply zero:

hwall=0.

于是壁面不改变残差（）。这一点在第 4 节的演示里可以直接看到：播放到壁面时，h=0（）。

So a wall face does not change the residual (). The demo of Section 4 shows this directly: step onto a wall face and h=0 ().

外圈：远场特征边界

Outer ring: the far-field characteristic boundary

远场要按特征方向决定用哪一侧的信息。标量守恒律 F(u)=12u2β 的波速是 uvn（vn=β·n，n 指向域外）。算例中 u>0，所以 sign(uvn)=sign(vn)，判据是纯几何的：

At the far field the characteristic direction decides which side supplies the information. The wave speed of the scalar law F(u)=12u2β is uvn (with vn=β·n and n pointing out of the domain). In the test case u>0, so sign(uvn)=sign(vn) and the test is purely geometric:

  • 入流（vn<0，特征从域外进来）：右状态取自由来流 uR=u∞=1.0；
  • 出流（vn≥0，特征从域内出去）：右状态由内部外推，uR=uL。
  • Inflow (vn<0, the characteristic enters from outside): the right state is the free stream, uR=u∞=1.0;
  • Outflow (vn≥0, the characteristic leaves the domain): the right state is extrapolated from inside, uR=uL.

算例的 8 个远场面里 4 个入流、4 个出流（）。开关在冻结状态 u* 处固定（frozen switch），前向与伴随都在这个固定的分支上线性化。

Of the 8 far-field faces in the test case, 4 are inflow and 4 outflow (). The switch is frozen at the converged state u*, and both the forward and the adjoint are linearised on that fixed branch.

这是把边界条件收在右状态与一维通量这一层的好处：面循环的骨架——收集、算通量、分发——与边界条件是哪几种、有几种都无关（代码里这些分支都在 flux 一侧，下面第 4 节的清单把它们逐行摊开，只为能逐句播放）。

This is the benefit of keeping the boundary conditions at the level of the right state and the one-dimensional flux: the skeleton of the face loop — gather, evaluate, scatter — depends on neither which kinds of boundary there are nor how many (in the code these branches all sit on the flux side; the listing of Section 4 below spells them out line by line only so they can be played statement by statement).

为什么这里没有无滑移壁面。本页的方程是纯对流的（无粘）：边界上只能在特征进入区域的地方给数据。物面是一条闭合曲线，β 是常数，所以 β·n 沿物面必然有正有负（本算例 u>0，特征速度 uβ·n 也是如此）；在特征离开区域的那部分物面上再规定 u=uw，问题就多给了一个条件：没有扩散，那里没有任何机制能把壁面值带进流场。无滑移这类 Dirichlet 壁面条件属于带扩散项的方程——拉普拉斯算子是二阶的，它让 u=uw 在整条物面上都适定。所以壁面上的 Dirichlet 条件放在另一对页面里讲：二维标量对流扩散方程（格心）。

Why there is no no-slip wall here. The equation on this page is purely convective (inviscid): boundary data can be given only where characteristics enter the domain. The body is a closed curve and β is constant, so β·n must take both signs along it (with u>0 in this test case, so does the characteristic speed uβ·n); prescribing u=uw where characteristics leave the domain gives the problem one condition too many: without diffusion nothing can carry the wall value into the flow there. A Dirichlet wall such as no-slip belongs to an equation with diffusion — the Laplacian is second order, and it makes u=uw well posed along the whole wall. So the Dirichlet wall is the subject of another pair of pages: the two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation (cell-centred).

残差 = 面循环：收集、计算、分发

Residual = the face loop: gather, compute, scatter

残差计算对每个面做三件事：从两侧单元收集状态，调用一维通量计算 h，再把 h 分发回两个单元——左单元加、右单元减。同一个 h 以相反符号进入两个单元，这就是守恒。

Computing the residual does three things on every face: gather the states from the two adjacent cells, compute h by calling the one-dimensional flux, and scatter h back into the two cells — added to the left cell, subtracted from the right. The same h enters the two cells with opposite signs, and that is conservation.

反过来看一个单元：它的残差就是自己所有面的通量之和，符号由它在每个面上是 L 还是 R 决定（）。四边形单元有 4 项，三角形有 3 项。

Seen from a cell instead: its residual is the sum of the fluxes of all its own faces, each signed by whether the cell is L or R on that face (). A quadrilateral contributes 4 terms, a triangle 3.

求解：Jacobian 与 Jacobi 迭代

The solve: the Jacobian and Jacobi iteration

从残差到 Jacobian

From residual to Jacobian

第 4 节的 residual(u) 就是离散残差 R(u)∈ℝN。它的 Jacobian 矩阵 A=∂R/∂u 是 N×N 的（算例中 24×24）。本节先把它显式写出来，看清它的结构；下面求解 R(u)=0 只用到它的对角元，第 7、8 节再讲怎样不形成 A 也能乘它。

The residual(u) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u)∈ℝN. Its Jacobian A=∂R/∂u is N×N (24×24 in the test case). This section writes it out explicitly, to show its structure; solving R(u)=0 below needs only its diagonal, and Sections 7 and 8 show how to multiply by A without ever forming it.

A 的每一份贡献都来自一个面：对第 2 节那个一维通量求局部导数，

Every contribution to A comes from one face: differentiate the one-dimensional flux of Section 2 locally,

cL=∂h∂uL=12vnuL+ε|n|,cR=∂h∂uR=12vnuR−ε|n|.

再把这两个数按和残差完全相同的符号累加到 A 的四个位置：ALL+=cL、ALR+=cR、ARL−=cL、ARR−=cR。边界面只有 L 一侧，所以只落在 ALL 上；无粘壁面 cL=cR=0，整个面对 A 没有贡献。

and accumulate those two numbers into four entries of A with exactly the signs the residual used: ALL+=cL, ALR+=cR, ARL−=cL, ARR−=cR. A boundary face has only an L side, so it lands on ALL alone; on an inviscid wall cL=cR=0 and the face contributes nothing to A at all.

出流面的导数为什么会“合并”。出流时 uR=uL，同一个 uL 同时进入通量的两个槽位，链式法则要把两个局部导数相加，耗散项正好抵消：

Why the derivatives "merge" on an outflow face. At outflow uR=uL, so the same uL enters both slots of the flux; the chain rule adds the two local derivatives and the dissipation terms cancel exactly:

cL=(12vnuL+ε|n|)∂h/∂uL+(12vnuL−ε|n|)∂h/∂uR=vnuL.

于是边界面对 A 的贡献全都只落在 ALL 上，边界条件决定的只是这一项的大小：无粘壁面 cL=cR=0；入流 uR=u∞ 是常数，cR=0，只剩 cL；出流按上式合并成 vnuL。 在真实网格上对照一个贴壁单元与一个远场入流单元。

怎么把 R(u)=0 解出来

How R(u)=0 is actually solved

上面已经把 residual(u) 和它的 Jacobian 都造出来了，却还没有说这个非线性方程组是怎么解开的。本页的 primal 方程、切线方程和伴随方程（切线在第 9 节，伴随在第 10 节）用的是同一个 Jacobi 迭代：每一轮算一次残差，再逐单元除以 Jacobian 的对角元：

Both residual(u) and its Jacobian have now been built, but nothing has yet been said about how the nonlinear system is solved. On this page the primal equation, the tangent equation and the adjoint equation (Sections 9 and 10) are all solved by one and the same Jacobi iteration: every sweep evaluates the residual once and divides it, cell by cell, by the diagonal of the Jacobian:

uk+1=uk−D(uk)−1R(uk)
D=diag(A),A=∂R∂u

D 不需要把 A 造出来：diagonal(u) 就是残差的那个面循环，每个面只把 cL 加到左单元、把 −cR 加到右单元，也就是它在 A 里的四个元素中落在对角线上的那两个。所以一轮只有一次 residual 和一次 diagonal：不形成矩阵，也不解线性方程组，每个单元各自更新。初值取均匀来流 u0≡1，判据是残差的均方根 ‖R‖<10−13（）。

D does not need A to be formed: diagonal(u) is the residual's own face loop, in which each face adds only cL to its left cell and −cR to its right cell, the two of its four entries in A that land on the diagonal. So a sweep is one residual and one diagonal: no matrix is formed and no linear system is solved; every cell is updated on its own. The initial guess is the uniform free stream u0≡1, and the criterion is the rms of the residual, ‖R‖<10−13 ().

为什么用 Jacobi，而不用 LU。本页只有 24 个未知量，理论上最快的办法是直接分解：把 A 整个造出来，用 Newton 法，每一步做一次稠密 LU 分解，k=4 时残差就已低于 10−13。可那样就说明不了 matrix-free。Jacobi 是最简单的迭代法：对 A 它只要对角元，其余的一切都经由残差作用进来（切线方程里是 residual_d，伴随方程里是 residual_b），这正是 matrix-free 的意思。代价是线性收敛：在解附近，误差每一轮乘以迭代矩阵 I−D−1A，速率由它的谱半径决定，本算例是 0.791，判据在 k=121 时满足。切线与伴随的迭代矩阵与它同谱，所以进入渐近阶段以后，三条收敛曲线以同一个速率下降（第 9、10 节）。

Why Jacobi, and not LU. With only 24 unknowns the fastest method in principle is a direct factorisation: form the whole of A, run Newton, and factorise with one dense LU per step; the residual is below 10−13 by k=4. But that would show nothing about matrix-free. Jacobi is the simplest iterative method there is: of A it needs only the diagonal, and everything else comes in through the residual (through residual_d in the tangent equation and residual_b in the adjoint equation), which is exactly what matrix-free means. The price is linear convergence: near the solution the error is multiplied every sweep by the iteration matrix I−D−1A, at a rate set by its spectral radius, 0.791 here, and the criterion is met at k=121. The tangent and adjoint iteration matrices have the same spectrum, so once in the asymptotic phase all three convergence curves fall at the same rate (Sections 9 and 10).

固定轮次，但会检查。代码固定跑 300 轮，而不是一满足判据就退出：余下的轮次把 u* 一直带到舍入误差平台（从 k=153 起），页面上每一个实测常数都是在这些轮次下量出来的；得到的 u* 与 Newton 法的解最多差 4.4×10−16。真正要紧的是有没有检查：它记录判据在第几轮满足，若 300 轮内从未满足就直接报错，而不会把一个未收敛的状态当成 u* 返回。

A fixed sweep count, but a checked one. The code runs a fixed 300 sweeps rather than exiting at the criterion: the remaining sweeps take u* down to its round-off floor (from k=153 on), and every measured constant on this page was produced under those sweeps; the u* it returns is within 4.4×10−16 of Newton's. What actually matters is whether it is checked: it records the sweep at which the criterion was met, and raises an error if 300 sweeps never meet it, rather than returning an unconverged state as u*.

“伴随”就是转置

"Adjoint" means transpose

对任意矩阵 A 和向量 v,w，恒有

For any matrix A and vectors v,w, it always holds that

⟨w,Av⟩=wTAv=(ATw)Tv=⟨ATw,v⟩.

满足这一关系的算子称为伴随算子，对实矩阵就是转置。前向乘积把“状态上的扰动”映射成“残差的变化”；伴随乘积方向相反，把“残差上的权重”映射成“状态上的敏感度”。

An operator satisfying this relation is called the adjoint; for a real matrix it is the transpose. The forward product maps "a perturbation of the state" to "the change in the residual"; the adjoint product runs the other way, mapping "a weight on the residual" to "a sensitivity on the state".

几何量怎么依赖坐标

How the geometric quantities depend on the coordinates

残差对 X 的依赖全部藏在面法向里。第 1 节说过一个面只存三件事，其中只有法向与坐标有关：

The dependence of the residual on X hides entirely in the face normals. Section 1 said a face stores only three things, and of those only the normal has anything to do with the coordinates:

n=(yb−ya,−(xb−xa)),|n|2=nx2+ny2,vn=β·n.

再把 n 对两个端点求导。n 是边向量旋转 90° 的结果，旋转的转置还是旋转，所以回传只是把 nb 的两个分量交换并变号：

Then differentiate n with respect to the two endpoints. n is the edge vector rotated by 90°, and the transpose of a rotation is again a rotation, so propagating backwards merely swaps the two components of nb and flips a sign:

xa,b+=(nb,y,−nb,x),xb,b+=(−nb,y,nb,x),nb=∂h∂nhb.

三步——算 nb、把旋转转置回端点、分发到两个节点——就是几何伴随的全部内容。它与第 8 节的 residual_b 是同一个面循环，只是被求导的对象从 u 换成了 X，hb 的算法一字不变。

Three steps — compute nb, transpose the rotation back onto the endpoints, scatter to the two nodes — are the whole of the geometric adjoint. 验证：dL/dX 对有限差分

Verification: dL/dX against finite differences

上一节把 dL/dX 推了出来：伴随只解一次线性系统，就拿到全部分量。这一节只做一件事——验证它是对的。

The previous section derived dL/dX: the adjoint solves one linear system and gets every component. This section does one thing only — check that it is right.

判据是最朴素的那个，逐个坐标分量做中心差分：

The criterion is the plainest one there is, a central difference taken coordinate by coordinate:

dLdXk≈L(Xk+h)−L(Xk−h)2h.

关键在于 L(Xk±h) 是完整的目标函数：每次都要把网格改形、重新解一次非线性 primal、再算目标。所以这里验证的是全导数——状态对几何的响应也算在内——而不是冻结状态的偏导数。代价也正因如此：中心差分每个分量两次扰动，一共 2M 次非线性求解，而伴随只用了一次线性求解。

What matters is that L(Xk±h) is the complete objective: every evaluation deforms the mesh, solves the nonlinear primal again, and only then computes the objective. So what is verified here is the total derivative — the response of the state to the geometry included — and not a partial derivative at a frozen state. That is also what it costs: two perturbations per component, 2M nonlinear solves in all, against the single linear solve of the adjoint.

还有省一半的做法——前向差分，只往一侧扰动：

There is also a way to halve that — a forward difference, perturbing to one side only:

dLdXk≈L(Xk+h)−L(X)h.

步长为什么要能调。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ϵ/h)（ϵ 为机器精度），随 h 变小反而增大。两者相抵，最佳步长大致在 h∼ϵ1/3，也就是 10−5 到 10−6 量级。把 h 调到 10−3 会看到截断误差主导，调到 10−10 会看到舍入噪声主导——两头都会让“验证”看起来像是失败了。这正是差分校验最容易骗人的地方，也是点积测试（第 8 节）比它更可靠的原因：点积测试是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。

Why the step has to be adjustable. The error of a central difference has two parts: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks as h shrinks; and round-off, about O(ϵ/h) (with ϵ the machine precision), which grows as h shrinks. Between them the best step lies roughly at h∼ϵ1/3, of order 10−5 to 10−6. Set h to 10−3 and truncation dominates; set it to 10−10 and round-off noise does — at either end the "verification" looks like a failure. This is exactly where a difference check is most deceptive, and why the dot-product test (Section 8) is the more reliable of the two: it is an exact identity, with no step size in it at all.

那么哪一侧更糟？要看离最优点多远，因为两侧的斜率根本不同：h 每放大一个数量级，截断误差涨约 100 倍（二次）；h 每缩小一个数量级，舍入误差只涨约 10 倍（线性）。实测扫描里截断一侧的斜率很干净，舍入一侧则起伏不定（舍入误差本身近乎随机）；中心差分的最小值多数落在 h 约 10−4 到 10−6 之间，与 ϵ1/3 的量级一致。于是离最优点越远，偏大的那一侧通常越吃亏，但对个别分量两侧可能相差不到一个数量级。实用结论是——宁可把 h 取偏小，也不要取偏大。

So which side is worse? It depends how far from the optimum you are, because the two slopes are quite different: enlarging h by one order multiplies the truncation error by about 100 (quadratic), while shrinking h by one order multiplies the round-off error by only about 10 (linear). In the measured sweep the truncation slope is clean while the round-off side is ragged (round-off is close to random); the central minimum mostly lies between h≈10−4 and 10−6, consistent with the ϵ1/3 estimate. So the further from the optimum, the more the too-large side usually costs, though for a few components the two sides differ by less than an order of magnitude. The practical conclusion — better to take h a little too small than a little too large.

伴随给出的是离散格式的精确导数，与格式本身是几阶精度无关；格式的精度阶决定的是离散解，以及由它算出的 L 与 dL/dX，离连续问题有多远。这一节用构造解把它测出来：先选一个光滑函数当精确解，由它反推出源项与边界数据，再在一族逐次一致加密的网格上求解，看误差随网格尺寸按几次方减小。

The measured L2 orders climb towards 1 from below, one level at a time: 0.42, 0.48, 0.60, 0.72, 0.81, 0.87 on this page, 0.47, 0.54, 0.58, 0.65, 0.73, 0.82 on the node-centred page. The max-norm orders are less orderly: 0.46, 0.26, 0.45, 0.59, 0.69, 0.80 on this page, falling before they rise.

在规则网格上，一阶格式的截断误差是 O(h)。在这张不规则网格上并非如此：在本页，它的 L2 范数逐层是 9.8e−2、8.4e−2、7.8e−2、7.5e−2、7.3e−2、7.3e−2、7.2e−2，几乎不变；在格点页面上，它大约按 h1/2 减小。不规则的控制体各个面上的 Taylor 展开项在控制体内并不逐点抵消。可是解的误差照样在减小：相邻控制体的局部误差在解里互相抵消，误差仍然是一阶的（这叫超收敛）。这种抵消是逐层慢慢显现出来的，耗散越强显现得越慢：把 ε 从 0.8 换成 0.3，同一层上的 L2 阶就从 0.72 升到 0.86（格点页面从 0.65 升到 0.82）。 把截断误差与解的误差画在一起。

On a regular mesh the truncation error of a first-order scheme is O(h). On this irregular mesh it is not: here its L2 norm is 9.8e−2, 8.4e−2, 7.8e−2, 7.5e−2, 7.3e−2, 7.3e−2, 7.2e−2 level by level, hardly changing; on the node-centred page it falls about like h1/2. The Taylor terms of the faces of an irregular control volume do not cancel pointwise inside it. The solution error falls all the same: the local errors of neighbouring control volumes cancel in the solution, and the error is still first order (supraconvergence). That cancellation shows itself only gradually, level by level, and the more slowly the stronger the dissipation: with ε=0.3 instead of 0.8 the L2 order on the same level rises from 0.72 to 0.86 (the node-centred page: from 0.65 to 0.82). draws the truncation error beside the solution error.

为什么不能照搬本页的壁面

Why this page's wall cannot be carried over

本页算例的壁面在每个壁面面上规定通量（h=0）。照搬到构造解问题上，就是在每个壁面面上规定精确解的法向通量 12ue(xf)2vn。在流体撞向物面的迎风一侧（vn>0），特征离开计算域；这里通量 12u2vn 一旦给定，u 本身也就给定了，问题多了一个条件。这与第 3 节解释本页为什么没有 Dirichlet 壁面的，是同一个理由。

The wall of this page's test case prescribes the flux on every one of its wall faces (h=0). Carried over to the manufactured problem, that means prescribing the exact solution's normal flux 12ue(xf)2vn on every one of them. On the windward side, where the flow runs into the body (vn>0), characteristics leave the domain; there a given flux 12u2vn gives u itself, and the problem has one condition too many. It is the same reason Section 3 gives for this page having no Dirichlet wall.

实测：在每个壁面面上都规定通量时，迎风侧壁面单元上的最大误差逐层是 3.2e−2、2.4e−2、3.0e−2、3.6e−2、3.6e−2、4.0e−2，并不减小；其余地方的最大误差是 1.1e−1、6.8e−2、5.8e−2、4.3e−2、2.8e−2、2.3e−2。L2 误差仍在减小（阶 0.51、0.49、0.59、0.72、0.77），因为出问题的只是贴着迎风侧壁面的一层，这一层随网格加密越来越薄。 把两者画在一起。

Measured: with the flux prescribed on every one of the wall faces, the max error on the windward wall cells is 3.2e−2, 2.4e−2, 3.0e−2, 3.6e−2, 3.6e−2, 4.0e−2 level by level, and does not fall; everywhere else it is 1.1e−1, 6.8e−2, 5.8e−2, 4.3e−2, 2.8e−2, 2.3e−2. The L2 error still falls (orders 0.51, 0.49, 0.59, 0.72, 0.77), because the trouble is confined to one layer against the windward wall, and that layer gets thinner with every refinement. draws the two side by side.

这对伴随意味着什么

What this means for the adjoint

第 10–13 节验证的是：伴随与几何梯度对离散的 L 精确到舍入误差，这与格式几阶无关。离散的 L 离连续值有多远，本节没有直接测：构造解换掉了壁面条件，而 L 正是在壁面单元上取值，其中包括迎风侧的那些；上一小节表明，照本页壁面的做法，那里的误差并不随网格减小。优化器拿到的梯度与它所优化的离散目标函数完全一致，问题是自洽的；网格加密决定的是这个离散问题离连续问题有多近。要让它更近，得提高格式的阶（例如对流通量用重构），而不是改伴随。

What Sections 10 to 13 verified is that the adjoint and the geometric gradient are exact, to round-off, for the discrete L, whatever the scheme's order. How far the discrete L is from a continuous value is not measured here: the manufactured problem replaces the wall condition, and L is evaluated on the wall cells, the windward ones included, where, with this page's wall, the error does not fall (the previous subsection). The gradient the optimiser gets agrees exactly with the discrete objective it optimises, so the problem is self-consistent; refinement decides how close that discrete problem is to the continuous one. To bring it closer one has to raise the scheme's order (a reconstruction in the convective flux, say), not change the adjoint.

原零通量内壁：连续问题的兼容性限制

The original zero-flux body: a compatibility obstruction

本页的壁面数值通量直接为零，与内侧状态无关。但原 PDE 的物理通量是 12u2β·n；固定的 β=(1,0.5) 不可能沿闭合物面处处相切。β·n≠0 处零物理通量要求 u=0。对从远场 u=1 进入、最后撞到迎风面的特征线，这与沿特征保持 u=1 矛盾；u=0 还使特征速度退化。

The original numerical wall flux is zero independently of the interior state. The PDE physical flux is 12u2β·n, but the fixed β=(1,0.5) cannot be tangent everywhere to a closed body. Where β·n≠0, zero physical flux requires u=0. A characteristic entering from the far field at u=1 and ending on the upwind body cannot satisfy that: its state stays one. At u=0 the characteristic speed also degenerates.

因此不能宣称原零通量算例具有这里所需的光滑、正值连续伴随参考。这不否定已有离散梯度，也不是排除所有弱解理论；它说明必须先另行定义弱解、边界迹及其灵敏度，不能把二维标量的“壁面”直接当作 Euler 滑移壁面。

The original zero-flux example therefore does not supply the smooth positive continuous reference required here. Its discrete gradients remain valid. This does not exclude every weak-solution theory; such a theory would need its own boundary traces and sensitivities. A scalar “wall” cannot simply be treated as an Euler slip wall.

  • 混合网格不改变写法：四边形 4 个面、三角形 3 个面，但面循环按面走，完全不关心单元类型。
  • A hybrid mesh changes nothing in how this is written: a quadrilateral has 4 faces and a triangle 3, but the face loop walks faces and never asks what kind of cell it is.
  • 边界条件只住在 flux 里面：无粘壁面取 h=0，远场按特征方向选入流/出流；面循环一行都不用改。
  • Boundary conditions live inside flux alone: an inviscid wall takes h=0, and the far field picks inflow or outflow by the characteristic direction; not one line of the face loop changes.
  • 一维通量是核心：格心格式的残差就是“对每个面收集两侧状态、调用一次一维通量、再分发”。
  • The one-dimensional flux is the core: the residual of a cell-centred scheme is simply "on every face, gather the two states, call the one-dimensional flux once, then scatter".
  • matrix-free 前向：对一维通量求局部导数 cL,cR，面循环不变。
  • Matrix-free forward: take the local derivatives cL,cR of the one-dimensional flux; the face loop is unchanged.
  • matrix-free 伴随：每个面的贡献转置——一维通量从“两个输入、一个输出”变为“一个输入、两个输出”，面循环中收集与分发互换、数据方向反转。
  • Matrix-free adjoint: transpose the contribution of each face — the one-dimensional flux goes from "two inputs, one output" to "one input, two outputs", and in the face loop gather and scatter swap while the data direction reverses.
  • 求解：primal、切线、伴随三个方程都用同一个 Jacobi 迭代，只用到对角元 D 与 matrix-free 的乘积；进入渐近阶段以后，三者以同一个速率收敛（谱半径 0.791）。
  • Solving: the primal, tangent and adjoint equations are all solved by the same Jacobi iteration, which needs only the diagonal D and the matrix-free products; once asymptotic, all three converge at the same rate (spectral radius 0.791).
  • 验证：点积测试检查转置，差分与复数步长检查梯度。复数步长从收敛解出发，每一轮都与前向迭代一致，与伴随梯度相符到舍入误差。
  • Verification: the dot-product test checks the transpose, finite differences and the complex step check the gradient. Started from the converged solution, the complex step matches the forward iteration at every sweep, and the adjoint gradient to round-off.
  • 精度阶：构造解检验（第 14 节）表明格式是一阶的，耗散项相当于一个系数为 O(h) 的扩散。在这张不规则网格上一阶来得很慢：L2 阶逐层往 1 爬，最细一层是 0.87（格点页面 0.82）。伴随对离散的 L 始终是精确的；格式的阶决定的是离散问题离连续问题有多远。
  • Order of accuracy: a manufactured solution (Section 14) shows the scheme to be first order, the dissipation acting as a diffusion of coefficient O(h). On this irregular mesh first order comes slowly: the L2 orders climb towards 1 level by level, 0.87 on the finest level (0.82 on the node-centred page). The adjoint is exact for the discrete L either way; the order decides how far the discrete problem is from the continuous one.
