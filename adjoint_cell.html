网格：保留原来的混合 O 型网格

Mesh: retain the original hybrid O-grid

仍使用原来的 24 个节点、8 个四边形和 16 个三角形，内外边界的坐标与连接关系不变。改变的是边界的数学含义和空间重构，不是重建一个新的几何算例。数组在本页新增交互中从 0 开始编号。

The original 24 nodes, eight quadrilaterals and sixteen triangles remain unchanged, including boundary coordinates and connectivity. We change the boundary interpretation and spatial reconstruction, not the geometry. New interactive arrays use zero-based indexing.

未知量放在单元顶点平均中心，解释为控制体平均量。每条原始边就是一个计算面；内部面只计算一次，然后向两侧分发相反的通量。对一般非规则四边形，顶点平均中心与面积质心不完全相同，本页加密误差会包含这种几何近似。

Unknowns sit at vertex-average cell centres and represent control-volume averages. Each primal edge is a face; an internal flux is evaluated once and scattered with opposite signs. Vertex-average and area centroids need not coincide on irregular quadrilaterals; refinement errors include this geometric approximation.

第 2 节Section 2

方程、数值通量与一阶／二阶重构

Equation, numerical flux and first/second-order reconstruction

方程和内部数值通量形式保持不变：β=(1,0.5)、ε=0.8。u 是正的标量，不是二维速度。非线性来自 u²；特征方向与 β 一致，特征速度大小随 u 改变。此处只研究光滑正解，不加入激波或限制器。

Keep the equation and interior flux form: β=(1,0.5), ε=0.8. The positive scalar u is not a two-dimensional velocity. Nonlinearity comes from u²: characteristics follow β, with speed depending on u. This example studies smooth positive solutions only, without shocks or limiters.

∇⋅(u22β)=0,Hf=β⋅nf4[(uf−)2+(uf+)2]−εℓf(uf+−uf−).\nabla\cdot\left(\frac{u^2}{2}\boldsymbol\beta\right)=0,\qquad H_f=\frac{\boldsymbol\beta\cdot\boldsymbol n_f}{4}\big[(u_f^-)^2+(u_f^+)^2\big]-\varepsilon\ell_f(u_f^+-u_f^-).
ui(xf)=ui+∇hui⋅(xf−xi),Mi∇hui=bi,Mi=∑j∈NiwijdijdijT,bi=∑j∈Niwijdij(uj−ui),wij=1/∥dij∥2.u_i(x_f)=u_i+\nabla_hu_i\cdot(x_f-x_i),\quad M_i\nabla_hu_i=b_i,\quad M_i=\sum_{j\in N_i}w_{ij}d_{ij}d_{ij}^T,\quad b_i=\sum_{j\in N_i}w_{ij}d_{ij}(u_j-u_i),\quad w_{ij}=1/\|d_{ij}\|^2.

一阶把梯度设为零，直接使用控制体值。二阶由相邻控制体做距离加权最小二乘，求一个 2×2 系统；每条直线段采用两点 Gauss 积分。若 u 是线性函数，梯度应精确恢复到舍入误差；这是重构实现的第一道检查。节点坐标改变时，d、w、M、b 以及重构位置全部参与求导。

First order sets gradients to zero. Second order uses inverse-distance-squared weighted least squares over adjacent control volumes, solving a 2×2 system, followed by two-point Gauss quadrature on each straight segment. Linear fields should reproduce their gradients to roundoff. Geometry derivatives include d, w, M, b and reconstruction locations.

for unknown i:

Why a small linear system? Approximate each neighbour jump u_j−u_i by d_ij·g_i and minimise the weighted squared mismatch. Setting its derivative with respect to g_i to zero gives the normal equations above. Two dimensions require at least two non-collinear directions; a nearly zero determinant means that the stencil cannot reliably resolve one gradient component. $\min_{g_i}\frac12\sum_{j\in N_i}w_{ij}\left[d_{ij}\cdot g_i-(u_j-u_i)\right]^2,\quad x_{q\pm}=\frac{x_a+x_b}{2}\pm\frac{x_b-x_a}{2\sqrt3}.$

通量的第一项近似物理输运，第二项抑制相邻状态的非物理振荡。ε 固定为 0.8，不对谱半径取最大值，因此这里没有波速选择分支。二阶改的是两个面迹的近似及积分；不会把 ε 简单除以网格尺度。边界值和耗散同样逐积分点求值。

The first flux term approximates physical transport; the second damps nonphysical inter-state oscillation. The fixed ε=0.8 avoids a spectral-radius maximum branch. Second order changes traces and quadrature, not ε by an arbitrary mesh scaling. Boundary data and dissipation are evaluated at each quadrature point.

第 3 节Section 3

边界条件：内外边界统一按特征方向处理

Boundaries: characteristic conditions on both inner and outer boundaries

原来的内边界零通量分支已删除。保持固定 β 时，封闭边界不可能处处与 β 相切；强制 u²β·n/2=0 会在非切向位置要求 u=0，与光滑正值参考不相容。现在内边界允许标量通量通过，称为“内边界”，不再称为不可穿透固壁。法向始终指向计算域外，内边界法向因此指向孔洞。

The former zero-flux inner-boundary branch is removed. A fixed β cannot be tangent everywhere to a closed contour, so u²β·n/2=0 would require u=0 where β·n is nonzero, contradicting a smooth positive reference. The inner boundary now transmits scalar flux and is not an impermeable wall. Normals point out of the computational domain, hence into the hole at the inner boundary.

ue(x,y)=a+0.2sin⁡[π(y−0.5x)],a=1,β⋅∇ue=0,∇⋅(12ue2β)=0.u_e(x,y)=a+0.2\sin[\pi(y-0.5x)],\quad a=1,\quad \boldsymbol\beta\cdot\nabla u_e=0,\quad\nabla\cdot\left(\tfrac12u_e^2\boldsymbol\beta\right)=0.
uf+={g(xf,yf)=ue(xf,yf),β⋅nf<0,uf−,β⋅nf≥0.u_f^+=\begin{cases}g(x_f,y_f)=u_e(x_f,y_f),&\boldsymbol\beta\cdot\boldsymbol n_f<0,\\u_f^-,&\boldsymbol\beta\cdot\boldsymbol n_f\ge0.\end{cases}

入流给定函数 g，出流由内部解决定；不在出流再指定一个 Dirichlet 值。内外入流都取同一个解析场，因而特征线穿过孔洞后重新进入计算域时不会引入不匹配的数据。二阶在每个 Gauss 点计算 g，不把它冻结为面中心常数。几何扰动必须包含 δg=∇g·δx_f+δa。

Specify g on inflow and let the interior determine outflow; do not impose another Dirichlet value there. Both inner and outer inflow sample the same analytic field, avoiding incompatible data when a characteristic re-enters after crossing the hole. Second order evaluates g at each Gauss point. Geometry perturbations include δg=∇g·δx_f+δa.

第 4 节Section 4

残差：梯度收集、面计算与守恒分发

Residual: gradient gathering, face evaluation and conservative scatter

残差仍是流出通量的积分和，不除以体积。本例没有源项。内部面向左加 H、向右减 H，因此全域求和时内部贡献抵消，只剩边界通量。二阶先计算所有梯度，再做面循环；格点面分段的每一份贡献都向同一对控制体守恒分发。

The residual remains the sum of integrated outward fluxes, without volume normalisation. There is no source. Each internal face adds H to the left and subtracts H from the right, so global summation cancels interior contributions and leaves boundary flux. Second order computes gradients before the face loop; every dual-face segment conservatively scatters to the same adjacent volumes.

Ri(u,X,a)=∑fBifHf,BLf=+1,  BRf=−1,R(u∗,X,a)=0.R_i(u,X,a)=\sum_f B_{if}H_f,\quad B_{Lf}=+1,\;B_{Rf}=-1,\qquad R(u^*,X,a)=0.

R[:] = 0
for f in computational_faces:
    H = integrate_reconstructed_flux(f,grad)
    R[L] += H

第 5 节Section 5

Jacobian 与原始求解

Jacobian and primal solution

一阶保留原来的 Jacobi 形式 u←u−D⁻¹R，D 是一阶残差 Jacobian 的对角。边界数据变了，收敛流场和旧版常数当然也随之改变。二阶精确 Jacobian 包含重构邻点，不能只沿用原来的两个面邻点导数。

First order retains u←u−D⁻¹R with the first-order residual Jacobian diagonal. The changed boundary data naturally changes the converged field and old quoted numbers. The second-order exact Jacobian includes reconstruction neighbours; the original two face-state derivatives alone are insufficient.

二阶使用带回溯的 Newton 修正，线性系统由 GS 预处理 GMRES 求解。预处理仅保留一阶近似，执行三次 GS 扫描；外层乘积始终使用完整二阶残差的精确前向算子。回溯检查未知量和面重构状态为正，并要求真残差下降。精确算子没有组装成全局矩阵。

Second order uses backtracked Newton corrections solved by GS-preconditioned GMRES. Three GS sweeps of a first-order approximation form the preconditioner; outer products always use the exact derivative of the second-order residual. Backtracking requires positive unknowns and face traces and a decrease in the true residual. The exact global matrix is not assembled.

AΔu=−R,A=Ru,unew=u+αΔu,P≈A from first order.A\Delta u=-R,\quad A=R_u,\quad u^{new}=u+\alpha\Delta u,\quad P\approx A\text{ from first order}.

先看一阶的一次 Jacobi：所有分量都从同一轮旧状态计算 R 与 D，然后同时更新。它不同于立即覆盖分量的 GS。对内部面，两个状态导数为 c_L 和 c_R；左单元对角加 c_L，右单元对角减 c_R。入流外侧 g 与状态无关；出流 u⁺=u⁻，必须把 c_L+c_R 合并到同一个对角项。

A first-order Jacobi sweep evaluates R and D from the same old state, then updates all components simultaneously. GS instead immediately reuses updated components. For an interior face, add c_L to the left diagonal and −c_R to the right diagonal. Inflow g is independent of the state; outflow has u⁺=u⁻, so c_L+c_R belongs on a single diagonal entry.

$u_i^{k+1}=u_i^k-\frac{R_i(u^k)}{D_i(u^k)},\qquad z_i^{m+1}=\frac{b_i-\sum_{ji}P_{ij}z_j^m}{P_{ii}}.$

二阶中，P 是按一阶残差组装的稀疏近似。每次预处理从 z=0 开始做三次 GS，形成一个固定线性映射 B；GMRES 对 A B 构造 Krylov 子空间，得到修正 Δu=B y。A v 由完整二阶前向计算给出。即使 P 忽略重构邻点，收敛时检查的仍是完整系统 AΔu+R，而不是近似系统 PΔu+R。

For second order, P is the sparse first-order approximate Jacobian. Each preconditioner application starts at z=0 and performs three GS sweeps, defining a fixed linear map B. GMRES builds its Krylov space for A B and forms Δu=B y. Exact A v comes from the full second-order tangent. Although P omits reconstruction neighbours, convergence checks AΔu+R, not PΔu+R.

if order == 1:
    R, D = residual_and_diagonal(u_old)
    u_new[:] = u_old[:] - R[:] / D[:]
else:

第 6 节Section 6

目标函数：内边界上的数学泛函

Objective: a mathematical inner-boundary functional

保留原来的有方向内边界积分形式，但不再解释为不可穿透物面的真实受力。令 e=β/|β|，目标为内边界上 u²/2 的投影积分。一阶取原来的控制体值，二阶使用边界重构和同样的 Gauss 积分；目标的导数也必须穿过重构。

Keep the original direction-projected inner-boundary integral, but no longer interpret it as a physical force on an impermeable body. With e=β/|β|, integrate u²/2 against e·n. First order uses the original control-volume value; second order uses boundary reconstruction and the same Gauss quadrature. Objective derivatives must include reconstruction.

J=∫Γinner12u2e⋅n ds,Jh=∑f∈Γinner∑qwq2(uf,q−)2e⋅nf.J=\int_{\Gamma_{inner}}\frac12u^2\boldsymbol e\cdot\boldsymbol n\,ds,\qquad J_h=\sum_{f\in\Gamma_{inner}}\sum_q\frac{w_q}{2}(u_{f,q}^-)^2\boldsymbol e\cdot\boldsymbol n_f.

由于解析场在孔洞内部也满足散度为零，其整个封闭内边界积分恰好为零。粗网格的 J_h 一般不为零，因此本例也可观察离散积分及其设计梯度如何趋近零。这一退化的连续目标不是优化算例的有效性能指标；在这里用于检验实际离散求导链。

The analytic field is divergence-free even inside the hole, so the closed inner-boundary integral is exactly zero. A coarse-grid J_h generally is not zero; its value and design derivatives can be studied as discretisation errors. This degenerate continuous objective is not a useful optimisation performance metric; here it tests the actual discrete differentiation chain.

第 7 节Section 7

前向：通量之前还有重构导数

Tangent: differentiate reconstruction before flux

给状态方向 v 与几何／入流参数方向 δp，逐个标量操作应用链式法则。固定网格时，最小二乘梯度对 u 是线性的；几何变化时，M 本身也会改变。漏掉 δM 项会使状态点积检验仍然通过，却使形状梯度错误。

Given state direction v and geometry/inflow direction δp, apply the chain rule to every scalar operation. The least-squares gradient is linear in u for fixed geometry, but M changes under geometry perturbations. Omitting δM may still pass a state transpose test while corrupting shape gradients.

δ∇hui=Mi−1(δbi−δMi∇hui),δuf=δui+δ∇hui⋅(xf−xi)+∇hui⋅(δxf−δxi).\delta\nabla_hu_i=M_i^{-1}(\delta b_i-\delta M_i\nabla_hu_i),\qquad\delta u_f=\delta u_i+\delta\nabla_hu_i\cdot(x_f-x_i)+\nabla_hu_i\cdot(\delta x_f-\delta x_i).
cL=12(β⋅n)u−+εℓ,cR=12(β⋅n)u+−εℓ,δH=cLδu−+cRδu++Hn⋅δn.c_L=\tfrac12(\boldsymbol\beta\cdot\boldsymbol n)u^-+\varepsilon\ell,\quad c_R=\tfrac12(\boldsymbol\beta\cdot\boldsymbol n)u^+-\varepsilon\ell,\quad\delta H=c_L\delta u^-+c_R\delta u^++H_n\cdot\delta n.
differentiate coordinates, centres, normals, lengths
differentiate M and b; dgrad = solve(M, db-dM*grad)
differentiate reconstructed traces and inflow g(xq)
dH = chain_rule(flux)
dR[L] += dH; dR[R] -= dH
$\delta w_{ij}=-2\frac{d_{ij}\cdot\delta d_{ij}}{(d_{ij}\cdot d_{ij})^2},\quad\delta M_i=\sum_j\left[\delta w_{ij}d_{ij}d_{ij}^T+w_{ij}(\delta d_{ij}d_{ij}^T+d_{ij}\delta d_{ij}^T)\right].$
$\delta b_i=\sum_j\left[(\delta w_{ij}d_{ij}+w_{ij}\delta d_{ij})(u_j-u_i)+w_{ij}d_{ij}(\delta u_j-\delta u_i)\right].$

先解出原梯度 g=M⁻¹b，再对 M g=b 求导：M δg=δb−δM g。这样不用显式推导逆矩阵的每个元素，也清楚地看到几何项来自哪里。固定网格令 δd=δw=δM=0；几何方向则不能省略这些项。面板用固定示例方向 v_i=sin(i+1) 展示逐面累加，下一节求解面板再计算真正的参数敏感度。

First solve g=M⁻¹b, then differentiate M g=b to obtain M δg=δb−δM g. This exposes the geometry terms without separately differentiating every inverse entry. Fixed-grid directions set δd=δw=δM=0; shape directions cannot. The face player uses the sample direction v_i=sin(i+1); the tangent-solve panel later computes actual parameter sensitivities.

第 8 节Section 8

伴随：反向传播并累加到整个重构邻域

Adjoint: reverse and accumulate through the reconstruction stencil

内部面通量的反向种子仍是 w_L−w_R。新增的是面状态向梯度和中心值反传，再把梯度的伴随传给所有最小二乘邻点。共享节点、中心和几何量上的贡献都要累加。面板逐面分解完整反向贡献，包括该面的重构链；它不是仅把旧通量的两个局部导数调换位置。

An internal face still receives reverse seed w_L−w_R. Trace adjoints now pass to gradients and centre values, then to all least-squares neighbours. Accumulate every path to shared nodes, centres and geometry. The panel decomposes the complete reverse contribution of each face, including reconstruction; it does not merely swap two old flux derivatives.

H‾=wL−wR,u‾i+=u‾f,∇hui‾+=(xf−xi)u‾f,wTAv=vTATw.\overline H=w_L-w_R,\quad\overline u_i\mathrel{+}=\overline u_f,\quad\overline{\nabla_hu_i}\mathrel{+}=(x_f-x_i)\overline u_f,\quad w^TAv=v^TA^Tw.
for face in reverse order:
    Hbar = w[L] - w[R]
    reverse flux, boundary closure and reconstructed traces
    reverse the least-squares dependency for this contribution
    accumulate into all state and coordinate inputs
# Full operator uses the reverse of the same scalar graph.

反向经过线性重构时，一份面迹种子分别累加到中心状态、梯度和重构偏移。反向经过 g=M⁻¹b 时先解 Mᵀ b̄=ḡ，再得到 M̄=−b̄gᵀ。之后沿 M 和 b 的求和定义反传到每个邻点、权重和坐标。同一个邻点可能被多个面访问，所以必须使用 +=。

Reverse a reconstructed trace by accumulating its seed into the centre state, gradient and offset. Reversing g=M⁻¹b first solves Mᵀ b̄=ḡ and then gives M̄=−b̄gᵀ. Reverse the sums defining M and b into every neighbour, weight and coordinate. Multiple faces share neighbours, so contributions must use +=.

$\bar b_i=M_i^{-T}\bar g_i,\qquad\bar M_i=-\bar b_i g_i^T,\qquad\bar u_j\mathrel{+}=w_{ij}d_{ij}\cdot\bar b_i,\quad\bar u_i\mathrel{-}=w_{ij}d_{ij}\cdot\bar b_i.$

上式最后两项只展示固定几何时 b 的状态路径；完整形状反向还包括 M、权重、偏移和边界函数。实际代码用标量计算图记录加减乘除、开方与正弦，再按相反顺序执行其解析局部导数。它没有保存全局 A 的所有元素，也没有通过有限差分构造 Aᵀw。

The last two terms show only the state path through b at fixed geometry. Full shape reversal includes M, weights, offsets and boundary data. The implementation records scalar arithmetic, square roots and sine on a tape and reverses their analytic local derivatives. It neither stores every entry of global A nor constructs Aᵀw by finite differences.

第 9 节Section 9

前向方程：一个设计方向一次求解
