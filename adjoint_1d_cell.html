第 1 节Section 1

网格、未知量与空间精度

Mesh, unknowns and spatial order

未知量是 NN 个单元上的平均值 uiu_i，中心坐标 xi=(i+1/2)hx_i=(i+1/2)h。单元宽度和目标函数权重均为 h=1/Nh=1/N。二阶重构保持每个单元的平均值。

Unknowns are cell averages uiu_i in NN cells, with centres xi=(i+1/2)hx_i=(i+1/2)h. Cell widths and objective weights are h=1/Nh=1/N. Linear reconstruction preserves each cell average.

页首的一阶／二阶按钮控制整页计算。求解器选择独立于空间精度。所有实时示例使用当前网格、边界处理与设计变量；精度研究使用固定的网格序列，并同时画出两个阶数。

The first-/second-order buttons control calculations throughout this page. Solver choice is independent of spatial order. Live examples share the selected mesh, boundary treatment and design; the accuracy study uses a fixed mesh sequence and displays both orders.

第 2 节Section 2

方程、线性重构与面通量

Equation, linear reconstruction and face flux

研究光滑正解分支 u>0u>0 上带源项的定常 Burgers 方程。当前源项为 s=0.5s=0.5，入口为 uin=1u_{in}=1；精确解为 u=1+xu=\sqrt{1+x}。目标源项是 sin⁡(πx)\sin(\pi x)。本例不含激波，二阶格式使用无斜率限制器的线性重构。

We study steady Burgers with a source on the smooth positive branch u>0u>0. The current source is s=0.5s=0.5, with uin=1u_{in}=1, giving the exact solution u=1+xu=\sqrt{1+x}. The target source is sin⁡(πx)\sin(\pi x). This example has no shocks and uses unlimited linear reconstruction.

ddx(u22)=s(x),x∈[0,1],u(0)=uin.\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{u^2}{2}\right)=s(x),\quad x\in[0,1],\qquad u(0)=u_{in}.

一阶格式在控制体内取常值。二阶内部斜率用中心差分，端点斜率用单边差分。虽然端点斜率本身只有一阶精度，但乘以 O(h)O(h) 的外推距离后，面值仍有 O(h2)O(h^2) 精度；实际全域阶数由网格加密验证。

First order uses piecewise constant values. Second order uses centred interior slopes and one-sided endpoint slopes. The endpoint slope is only first-order accurate, but multiplication by an O(h)O(h) extrapolation distance gives an O(h2)O(h^2) face value; mesh refinement checks the resulting global order.

di=ui+1−ui−12h,d0=u1−u0h,dM−1=uM−1−uM−2h,ui(x)=ui+(x−xi)di.d_i=\frac{u_{i+1}-u_{i-1}}{2h},\quad d_0=\frac{u_1-u_0}{h},\quad d_{M-1}=\frac{u_{M-1}-u_{M-2}}{h},\qquad u_i(x)=u_i+(x-x_i)d_i.
ufL=ui+h2di,ufR=ui+1−h2di+1,H(a,b)=a2+b24−ε(b−a),ε=0.8.u_f^L=u_i+\frac h2d_i,\quad u_f^R=u_{i+1}-\frac h2d_{i+1},\qquad H(a,b)=\frac{a^2+b^2}{4}-\varepsilon(b-a),\quad\varepsilon=0.8.

其中 MM 是未知量个数。通量的两个偏导为 cL=a/2+εc_L=a/2+\varepsilon、cR=b/2−εc_R=b/2-\varepsilon。重构之后仍使用同一个通量；二阶精度来自面值重构。下图可选择一个面，查看两侧斜率与重构值。

Here MM is the number of unknowns. Flux derivatives are cL=a/2+εc_L=a/2+\varepsilon and cR=b/2−εc_R=b/2-\varepsilon. Both orders use the same numerical flux: reconstruction changes the face values. Select a face below to inspect the slopes and reconstructed states.

第 3 节Section 3

边界闭合也是离散格式的一部分

Boundary closure is part of the discretisation

入口通量使用外部给定值 uinu_{in} 与从第一个单元重构至 x=0x=0 的内部迹。出口从末单元重构到 x=1x=1，再计算物理通量。二阶出口若仍直接使用末单元平均值，就会引入一阶边界误差。

The inlet flux uses the prescribed exterior value uinu_{in} and the interior trace reconstructed to x=0x=0. At the outlet, reconstruct from the final cell to x=1x=1 before applying the physical flux. Using the final cell average directly at a second-order outlet would introduce a first-order boundary error.

uinR=u0−h2d0,Hin=H(uin,uinR),uout=uN−1+h2dN−1,Hout=12uout2.u_{in}^{R}=u_0-\frac h2d_0,\quad H_{in}=H(u_{in},u_{in}^{R}),\qquad u_{out}=u_{N-1}+\frac h2d_{N-1},\quad H_{out}=\frac12u_{out}^2.

“边界对照”允许改用迎风通量 H=a2/2H=a^2/2：格心改入口面，格点改第一内部对偶面。它是另一种明确标记的离散边界闭合，用于第 15 节比较。格点二阶迎风面依赖重构斜率，因此它仍依赖相邻未知量。

The boundary comparison replaces one flux by H=a2/2H=a^2/2: the inlet face for cells, or the first internal dual face for nodes. This is a distinct, explicitly labelled boundary closure studied in Section 15. At second order, the node-based upwind face depends on its slope and therefore still on neighbouring unknowns.

第 4 节Section 4

完整残差：重构、通量与分发

Full residual: reconstruct, evaluate and scatter

设计变量为每个原始区间上的常源项 σk\sigma_k 和入口值。源项先积分到控制体，面通量按左正右负分发。符号约定固定为“流出减流入减源项”。

Design variables are the constant source values σk\sigma_k on the primal intervals and the inlet value. Integrate sources over control volumes, then scatter each face flux with a positive sign to its left and a negative sign to its right. The residual convention is outflow minus inflow minus source.

Si=hσi,Ri=Hi+1/2−Hi−1/2−Si.S_i=h\sigma_i,\qquad R_i=H_{i+1/2}-H_{i-1/2}-S_i.

格点源项公式中不存在的区间项取零。将重构写为线性映射 TL,TRT_L,T_R，将面到控制体的有向分发写为 B\mathcal B，可以把两个空间阶数统一为下面的计算链。边界常数和入口行替换需要额外处理。

Missing interval terms in the node-based source formula are zero. Linear reconstruction maps TL,TRT_L,T_R and the oriented face-to-volume scatter B\mathcal B unify both spatial orders. Boundary constants and the inlet row replacement are handled separately.

u →TL,TR (qL,qR) →H Hf →B R=BH−S.u\ \xrightarrow{T_L,T_R}\ (q_L,q_R)\ \xrightarrow{H}\ H_f\ \xrightarrow{\mathcal B}\ R=\mathcal B H-S.
第 5 节Section 5

Jacobian 与 GS 预估矫正

Jacobian and GS predictor–corrector

精确 Jacobian 是当前空间格式的残差导数 Aq=∂Rq/∂uA_q=\partial R_q/\partial u。一阶内部通常是三对角，二阶重构扩大为五对角；边界单边模板也必须求导。求解时只用在当前状态上计算的一阶 Jacobian P=A1(u)P=A_1(u) 构造修正步骤。

The exact Jacobian is Aq=∂Rq/∂uA_q=\partial R_q/\partial u for the selected spatial order. Interior first-order rows are generally tridiagonal; reconstruction widens second-order rows to five diagonals. One-sided boundary stencils must also be differentiated. The correction uses the first-order Jacobian P=A1(u)P=A_1(u) evaluated at the current state.

Pkδu=−Rq(uk),uk+1=uk+ωδu,Pk=A1(uk),q∈{1,2}.P_k\delta u=-R_q(u^k),\qquad u^{k+1}=u^k+\omega\delta u,\qquad P_k=A_1(u^k),\quad q\in\{1,2\}.

把 P=D+L+UP=D+L+U 分为对角、严格下三角和严格上三角部分。内层从零修正开始做固定次数 GS 扫描；一轮扫描中，已经经过的行使用新值。默认一次扫描，属于不精确的线性求解；因此需要继续外层残差矫正。

Split P=D+L+UP=D+L+U into diagonal, strictly lower and strictly upper parts. The inner solve starts from zero correction and performs a fixed number of GS sweeps. Already visited rows use new values. The default single sweep is an inexact linear solve, so outer residual corrections continue.

(D+L)δu(ℓ+1)=−Rq−Uδu(ℓ),δui(ℓ+1)=−Rq,i−∑j<iPijδuj(ℓ+1)−∑j>iPijδuj(ℓ)Pii.(D+L)\delta u^{(\ell+1)}=-R_q-U\delta u^{(\ell)},\qquad \delta u_i^{(\ell+1)}=\frac{-R_{q,i}-\sum_{j<i}P_{ij}\delta u_j^{(\ell+1)}-\sum_{j>i}P_{ij}\delta u_j^{(\ell)}}{P_{ii}}.

这不是 MacCormack 时间推进，也不是把二阶残差替换成一阶残差。迭代最终检查完整 RqR_q。本例保持正解分支；若修正导致非正状态，会减小步长。下面可逐行观察第一次 GS 扫描，并比较 AqA_q 与 PP 的非零结构。

This is neither MacCormack time marching nor replacement of the second-order residual by a first-order one. Convergence is checked against the full RqR_q. The solve stays on the positive branch by damping updates that would produce nonpositive states. Inspect the first GS sweep row by row and compare the sparsity of AqA_q and PP.

第 6 节Section 6

目标函数与固定的目标场

Objective and a frozen target field

目标是让解接近目标源项 sin⁡(πx)\sin(\pi x) 产生的流场。每种离散格式先求出自己的目标场 uˉq\bar u_q，然后在设计求导时保持它不变。切换阶数或边界格式会重建目标场；它不会随当前设计变量的扰动重新求解。

The inverse objective matches the field generated by sin⁡(πx)\sin(\pi x). Each discretisation first computes its own target uˉq\bar u_q, which is then frozen during design differentiation. Changing spatial order or boundary closure rebuilds this target; perturbing the current design does not.

Jq(u)=12∑iWi(ui−uˉq,i)2,gi=∂Jq∂ui=Wi(ui−uˉq,i).J_q(u)=\frac12\sum_i W_i(u_i-\bar u_{q,i})^2,\qquad g_i=\frac{\partial J_q}{\partial u_i}=W_i(u_i-\bar u_{q,i}).

因此，在目标设计处，离散 JqJ_q 和梯度同时为零。第 14 节的精度比较另用统一连续参考，防止把各网格自己的目标当作误差基准。

Thus the discrete objective and gradient vanish at the target design. Section 14 uses a shared continuous reference for its accuracy comparisons, rather than treating each grid’s discrete target as its own error reference.

精确 tangent：对重构也求导

Exact tangent: differentiate the reconstruction

令 v=δuv=\delta u。无斜率限制器时，重构矩阵与状态无关，因此 δqL=TLv\delta q_L=T_Lv、δqR=TRv\delta q_R=T_Rv。链式法则必须经过重构、通量和分发三步。

Let v=δuv=\delta u. Without a limiter, reconstruction matrices are state independent, so δqL=TLv\delta q_L=T_Lv and δqR=TRv\delta q_R=T_Rv. The chain rule must pass through reconstruction, flux evaluation and residual scatter.

Aqv=B[diag⁡(cL)TLv+diag⁡(cR)TRv].A_qv=\mathcal B\left[\operatorname{diag}(c_L)T_Lv+\operatorname{diag}(c_R)T_Rv\right].

对一个内部二阶面，重构系数和该面通量的完整导数如下。这里 cL,cRc_L,c_R 在重构面值上计算，不能在未经重构的中心值上计算。

For an interior second-order face, the reconstruction and complete flux derivative are as follows. Evaluate cL,cRc_L,c_R at the reconstructed face states, not at the unreconstructed centre values.

qL=−14ui−1+ui+14ui+1,qR=14ui+ui+1−14ui+2.q_L=-\frac14u_{i-1}+u_i+\frac14u_{i+1},\qquad q_R=\frac14u_i+u_{i+1}-\frac14u_{i+2}.
δHf=−cL4vi−1+(cL+cR4)vi+(cL4+cR)vi+1−cR4vi+2.\delta H_f=-\frac{c_L}{4}v_{i-1}+\left(c_L+\frac{c_R}{4}\right)v_i+\left(\frac{c_L}{4}+c_R\right)v_{i+1}-\frac{c_R}{4}v_{i+2}.

出口的物理通量导数为 uoutδuoutu_{out}\delta u_{out}；格心出口的 δuout\delta u_{out} 仍需通过重构传播。格点最后覆盖入口行为 (Aqv)0=v0(A_qv)_0=v_0。下面的播放器使用实际边界模板；上述四点式只用于内部面。

The outlet physical-flux derivative is uoutδuoutu_{out}\delta u_{out}; a cell-based outlet must still differentiate its reconstructed trace. For nodes, overwrite the inlet row with (Aqv)0=v0(A_qv)_0=v_0. The player below uses the actual boundary stencils; the four-point formula above applies only to interior faces.

第 8 节Section 8

精确 adjoint：逆向累加重构权重

Exact adjoint: accumulate reconstruction weights in reverse

伴随是同一条计算链的转置。先收集残差权重，形成面权重 ηf=wL−wR\eta_f=w_L-w_R；再通过通量偏导得到左右迹的权重；最后通过 TLT,TRTT_L^T,T_R^T 累加到所有受影响未知量。不能只把结果放回面两侧的中心。

The adjoint transposes the same chain. Gather residual weights into a face weight ηf=wL−wR\eta_f=w_L-w_R, multiply by the two flux derivatives, then accumulate through TLT,TRTT_L^T,T_R^T into every affected unknown. Returning contributions only to the two adjacent centres would omit reconstruction derivatives.

AqTw=TLT[cL⊙(BTw)]+TRT[cR⊙(BTw)].A_q^Tw=T_L^T\left[c_L\odot(\mathcal B^Tw)\right]+T_R^T\left[c_R\odot(\mathcal B^Tw)\right].

格点强边界需要先清零被替换行的输入权重，完成所有面及重构的反向累加后，再把约束行贡献加到第 0 个输出。不得覆盖已经从其他残差行传回 u0u_0 的贡献。

For a strong node-based boundary, zero the weight of the replaced row before reverse accumulation. After all face and reconstruction contributions, add the constraint-row contribution to output 0. Do not overwrite contributions already propagated to u0u_0 from other residual rows.

Q=I−e0e0T,Aq=QAraw+e0e0T,AqTw=ArawTQw+e0w0.Q=I-e_0e_0^T,\quad A_q=QA_{raw}+e_0e_0^T,\qquad A_q^Tw=A_{raw}^TQw+e_0w_0.

点积检验 wT(Aqv)=vT(AqTw)w^T(A_qv)=v^T(A_q^Tw) 检查转置关系，但两个方向若同时漏掉重构，仍可能通过。第 13 节会用独立复数残差和总导数再做检验。

The dot test wT(Aqv)=vT(AqTw)w^T(A_qv)=v^T(A_q^Tw) checks transposition, but can pass if both operators omit reconstruction. Section 13 additionally checks an independent complex residual and total derivatives.

第 9 节Section 9

tangent 的 GS 固定点求解

GS fixed-point solution of the tangent equation

对收敛残差 Rq(u(p),p)=0R_q(u(p),p)=0 求导，令 f=Rq,pδpf=R_{q,p}\delta p，得到精确 tangent 方程。在该线性求解期间固定原始解、AqA_q 和一阶预处理矩阵 PP。

Differentiate the converged residual Rq(u(p),p)=0R_q(u(p),p)=0 and set f=Rq,pδpf=R_{q,p}\delta p. The primal state, exact AqA_q and first-order preconditioning matrix PP remain fixed during this linear solve.

Aqv=−f,rt=Aqv+f,Pδv=−rt,vm+1=vm+ωδv.A_qv=-f,\qquad r_t=A_qv+f,\qquad P\delta v=-r_t,\qquad v^{m+1}=v^m+\omega\delta v.

每次修正方程从零开始，用所选次数的 GS 扫描，得到线性算子 Bs≈P−1B_s\approx P^{-1}。一次扫描时 B1=(D+L)−1B_1=(D+L)^{-1}。精确求出修正方程时，可以将预估矫正写成 Pvm+1=−f−(Aq−P)vmPv^{m+1}=-f-(A_q-P)v^m（ω=1\omega=1）；有限次 GS 时应使用下面的实际迭代式。

Starting each correction from zero and taking the selected fixed number of GS sweeps defines a linear operator Bs≈P−1B_s\approx P^{-1}. One sweep gives B1=(D+L)−1B_1=(D+L)^{-1}. If the correction is solved exactly, predictor–corrector becomes Pvm+1=−f−(Aq−P)vmPv^{m+1}=-f-(A_q-P)v^m for ω=1\omega=1; with finite GS sweeps, the actual iteration is the following.

vm+1=vm−ωBs(Aqvm+f),em+1=(I−ωBsAq)em.v^{m+1}=v^m-\omega B_s(A_qv^m+f),\qquad e^{m+1}=(I-\omega B_sA_q)e^m.

收敛需要误差传播矩阵的谱半径小于 1；GS 或低阶预处理并不对任意二阶问题保证收敛。下面可更换设计变量，查看解及真实线性残差。

Convergence requires a spectral radius below 1 for the error propagator; GS or low-order preconditioning does not guarantee convergence for arbitrary second-order problems. Select a design variable below and inspect the solution and true linear residual.

第 10 节Section 10

adjoint 的反向 GS 固定点求解

Reverse-GS fixed-point solution of the adjoint equation

采用拉格朗日函数 L=J+ψTRq\mathcal L=J+\psi^TR_q，消去状态扰动得到 AqTψ=−gA_q^T\psi=-g。修正方程使用 PTP^T，并按相反方向扫描。

With L=J+ψTRq\mathcal L=J+\psi^TR_q, cancellation of state variations gives AqTψ=−gA_q^T\psi=-g. The correction uses PTP^T and scans in the opposite direction.

PTδψ=−(AqTψ+g),(D+LT)δψ(ℓ+1)=−(AqTψ+g)−UTδψ(ℓ).P^T\delta\psi=-(A_q^T\psi+g),\qquad (D+L^T)\delta\psi^{(\ell+1)}=-(A_q^T\psi+g)-U^T\delta\psi^{(\ell)}.

相同固定次数、零初值的反向扫描恰好构成 BsTB_s^T，不是把矩阵转置后仍沿原方向扫描。于是实际更新和误差传播如下。

With the same fixed sweep count and zero initial correction, reverse sweeps implement BsTB_s^T. Transposing the matrix while retaining the original sweep direction would give a different preconditioner. The actual update and error propagation are therefore:

ψm+1=ψm−ωBsT(AqTψm+g),eam+1=(I−ωBsTAqT)eam.\psi^{m+1}=\psi^m-\omega B_s^T(A_q^T\psi^m+g),\qquad e_a^{m+1}=(I-\omega B_s^TA_q^T)e_a^m.

注意 I−ωBsTAqTI-\omega B_s^TA_q^T 并不直接等于 tangent 迭代矩阵的转置，因为乘法顺序不同。这里解的是残差伴随变量 ψ\psi；若逆向微分整个迭代程序，需要额外说明其迭代伴随变量与 ψ\psi 的转换。

Note that I−ωBsTAqTI-\omega B_s^TA_q^T is not directly the transpose of the tangent iteration matrix: the multiplication order differs. We solve for the residual adjoint ψ\psi. Reverse differentiation of the entire iteration program would require an additional relation between its iterative adjoint variable and ψ\psi.

第 11 节Section 11

设计导数与反问题

Design derivatives and inverse design

本例目标函数对设计变量没有显式依赖，因而一次伴随求解即可计算所有源项和入口导数。tangent 对每个方向单独求解，结果应与伴随内积一致。

The objective has no explicit design dependence, so one adjoint solve yields derivatives for every source and for the inlet. A tangent solve handles one direction at a time and must agree with the adjoint contraction.

δJ=gTv=ψTRq,pδp,dJdpk=ψT∂Rq∂pk.\delta J=g^Tv=\psi^TR_{q,p}\delta p,\qquad \frac{dJ}{dp_k}=\psi^T\frac{\partial R_q}{\partial p_k}.

每个源项导数为 dJ/dσi=−hψidJ/d\sigma_i=-h\psi_i。入口导数来自边界通量的外部状态偏导：dJ/duin=−ψ0 ∂Hin/∂uindJ/du_{in}=-\psi_0\,\partial H_{in}/\partial u_{in}。外部入口状态与内部重构迹是不同的求导路径。

Source derivatives are dJ/dσi=−hψidJ/d\sigma_i=-h\psi_i. The inlet derivative comes from the boundary flux’s exterior-state derivative: dJ/duin=−ψ0 ∂Hin/∂uindJ/du_{in}=-\psi_0\,\partial H_{in}/\partial u_{in}. The exterior inlet state and the reconstructed interior trace have distinct differentiation paths.

反问题演示只优化源项，固定入口，避免入口与首源项近似互相补偿。每次 BFGS 步都重新收敛原始方程并计算伴随，使用 Armijo 回溯接受下降步；重置或切换格式会恢复基准设计。

The inverse-design demo optimises only sources and fixes the inlet, avoiding near compensation between the inlet and first source. Each BFGS step reconverges the primal and adjoint equations and uses Armijo backtracking to accept a descent step. Resetting or changing discretisation restores the base design.

第 12 节Section 12

有限差分：检查总导数

Finite differences: check total derivatives

固定目标场，对当前设计做正负扰动，每次重新收敛完整原始残差。中心差分误差先按 O(δ2)O(\delta^2) 减小，随后受舍入误差与求解容差影响。不能仅根据最小步长的结果判断伴随是否正确。

Freeze the target field, perturb the current design in both directions, and reconverge the full primal residual each time. Central-difference error initially decreases as O(δ2)O(\delta^2), then is affected by roundoff and solve tolerance. The smallest step alone is not a reliable adjoint check.

第 13 节Section 13

复数步长与相互独立的检验

Complex step and independent checks

重构、固定耗散通量和目标函数在本例中均为解析运算。将设计扰动为 p+iηekp+i\eta e_k，保持目标场为实数常量，用独立复数残差收敛复数状态。虚部残差按 η\eta 缩放后也必须收敛；只检查实部会错误地提前停止。

Reconstruction, the fixed-dissipation flux and the objective are analytic here. Perturb the design to p+iηekp+i\eta e_k, keep the target real and fixed, and converge a complex state using an independent complex residual. The imaginary residual divided by η\eta must also converge; checking only the real part would stop too early.

dJdpk=Im⁡J(p+iηek)η+O(η2),η=10−30.\frac{dJ}{dp_k}=\frac{\operatorname{Im}J(p+i\eta e_k)}{\eta}+O(\eta^2),\qquad\eta=10^{-30}.

复数步长验证使用冻结在收敛实解处的实数 GS 预处理，不经过 GMRES 的范数和 Arnoldi 分支。它验证收敛离散方程的导数；不声称 GMRES 与复数迭代逐步相同。本例没有 minmod、绝对值通量开关或限幅分支；加入它们后必须重新分析可微性。

The complex-step check uses a real GS preconditioner frozen at the converged real state, bypassing GMRES norms and Arnoldi branches. It validates derivatives of the converged discrete equations; it does not claim iteration-by-iteration equality with GMRES. There are no minmod, absolute-value flux switches or clipping branches here; adding them requires a new differentiability analysis.

故障对照分别检查转置、残差微分与总导数。二阶时把两个方向都替换为一阶 Jacobian，点积检验仍然成立，但复数残差导数检验会失败；这是“自洽但求错导数”的例子。

The fault comparison checks transposition, residual differentiation and total derivatives separately. At second order, replacing both operators by a first-order Jacobian still passes a dot test, but fails the complex-residual derivative check: the pair is internally consistent yet differentiates the wrong residual.

第 16 节Section 16

GS 固定点与 GS 预处理 GMRES

GS fixed point and GS-preconditioned GMRES

固定点方法反复沿 GS 给出的修正方向更新。GMRES 则在 Krylov 子空间中组合方向，求使残差二范数最小的近似解。GMRES 仍是迭代法，不是直接分解求解器，也不保证每个小问题都比 GS 更省工作。

Fixed point repeatedly updates in the correction direction supplied by GS. GMRES combines directions in a Krylov subspace to minimise the residual 2-norm. It is still an iterative method, not a direct factorisation solver, and need not use less work than GS on every small problem.

AqBsz=−f,v=Bsz;AqTBsTy=−g,ψ=BsTy.A_qB_sz=-f,\quad v=B_sz;\qquad A_q^TB_s^Ty=-g,\quad\psi=B_s^Ty.

采用右预处理，BsB_s 是固定次数、零初值的 GS 扫描；精确算子始终是 AqA_q 或 AqTA_q^T，一阶 Jacobian 仅用于 GS 预处理。伴随使用对应反向扫描 BsTB_s^T。

Right preconditioning uses BsB_s, a fixed number of zero-initialised GS sweeps. The exact operator remains AqA_q or AqTA_q^T; the first-order Jacobian is used only in the GS preconditioner. The adjoint uses the corresponding reverse sweeps BsTB_s^T.

每一步先计算 zj=Bsvjz_j=B_sv_j，再计算 w=Aqzjw=A_qz_j，用两遍修正 Gram–Schmidt 正交化，Givens 旋转更新小型最小二乘问题。达到重启长度后，用当前真实残差重新建立子空间。页面记录的是 ∥b−Aqx∥2\|b-A_qx\|_2，不是预处理残差。

Each step applies zj=Bsvjz_j=B_sv_j, then w=Aqzjw=A_qz_j, orthogonalises with two-pass modified Gram–Schmidt, and updates the small least-squares problem with Givens rotations. At the restart length, rebuild the subspace from the current true residual. The page records ∥b−Aqx∥2\|b-A_qx\|_2, not the preconditioned residual.

每次真实残差检查也计入矩阵向量乘次数。比较同时列出 GS 扫描次数与 Krylov 存储量。本实现固定预处理；若在一次线性求解内改变 GS 扫描次数或用变化的内层容差，应使用适合可变预处理的 FGMRES。

True-residual checks are included in matrix-vector-product counts. Comparisons also report GS sweep counts and Krylov storage. This implementation has fixed preconditioning; changing GS sweep counts or using a variable inner stopping tolerance during one linear solve calls for a method such as FGMRES.

第 17 节Section 17

从离散方程到可信的导数

From discretisation to trustworthy derivatives

完整链条是：选择空间格式与边界闭合，收敛完整原始残差，对同一残差建立精确 tangent 和 adjoint，再选择 GS 固定点或 GS 预处理 GMRES 求解。近似 Jacobian 只改变求解过程，不改变应当满足的导数方程。

The complete chain is: choose spatial discretisation and boundary closure, converge the full primal residual, differentiate that same residual exactly, then solve with GS fixed point or GS-preconditioned GMRES. An approximate Jacobian changes the solver, not the derivative equations that must be satisfied.

验证分为四层：重构线性再现与网格阶数；残差微分与转置检验；tangent／adjoint／有限差分／复数步长总导数一致；连续伴随、边界闭合和目标误差的独立比较。求解器收敛、离散导数正确、连续伴随一致性分别回答不同问题。

Verification has four layers: linear reconstruction reproduction and spatial order; residual differentiation and transpose checks; tangent/adjoint/finite-difference/complex-step total-derivative agreement; and independent comparisons with continuous adjoints, boundary closure and goal-error estimates. Solver convergence, correct discrete derivatives and continuous adjoint consistency answer different questions.
