一维 Burgers 方程的离散伴随：格心格式

The discrete adjoint of the one-dimensional Burgers equation, on a cell-centred scheme

这是整个系列的入门页。方程是带源项的一维定常 Burgers 方程，网格只有 8 个单元，每一个数组、整个 Jacobian 都能完整地摆在页面上。离散用的就是二维页面上的那个面通量，只是法向取 n=1；所以这一页讲清楚的每一件事——残差是一个面循环、前向把它换成导数、伴随把它倒过来——到了二维对流页都原样成立。未知量放在单元上，入口条件通过通量弱加。This is the introductory page of the series. The equation is the steady one-dimensional Burgers equation with a source, the grid has only 8 cells, and every array and the whole Jacobian fit on the page. The discretisation is the face flux of the two-dimensional pages with the normal taken as n=1; so everything this page makes clear — the residual is a loop over faces, forward mode replaces it by its derivative, adjoint mode runs it backwards — carries over unchanged to the two-dimensional advection page. The unknowns live in the cells, and the inflow condition is imposed weakly, through the flux.

第 1 节
Section 1

网格：8 个单元与设计变量

The grid: 8 cells and the design variables

区间 [0,1] 等分成 8 段，Δx=1/8。采用格心格式：每个单元 ci=[xi−1/2,xi+1/2] 上有一个未知量 ui（单元平均值），画在单元中心 xi=(i−12)Δx；单元之间是面 f0,…,f8，面 f0 在入口 x=0，面 f8 在出口 x=1。

The interval [0,1] is split into 8 equal parts, Δx=1/8. The scheme is cell-centred: each cell ci=[xi−1/2,xi+1/2] carries one unknown ui (the cell average), drawn at the cell centre xi=(i−12)Δx; between cells are the faces f0,…,f8, face f0 at the inflow x=0 and face f8 at the outflow x=1.

源项 s(x) 取成 8 段分片常数，第 k 段上的值是 σk。这 8 个值，加上入口值 uin，就是本页的 9 个设计变量：伴随要算的，是目标函数对它们的导数（）。

The source s(x) is piecewise constant on the 8 intervals, with value σk on the k-th. These 8 values, together with the inflow value uin, are the 9 design variables of this page: what the adjoint computes is the derivative of the objective with respect to them ().

第 2 节
Section 2

方程与面上的一维通量

The equation, and the one-dimensional flux on a face

本页离散的是带源项的一维定常 Burgers 方程：

The page discretises the steady one-dimensional Burgers equation with a source:

ddx(12u2)=s(x),0<x<1,u(0)=uin

通量是 F(u)=12u2：二维页面上的 F(u)=12u2β 取 β=1。在一个控制体上积分，流出减流入等于源项的积分：

The flux is F(u)=12u2: the two-dimensional pages' F(u)=12u2β with β=1. Integrate over one control volume; what flows out minus what flows in equals the integral of the source:

F(u(xi+1/2))−F(u(xi−1/2))=∫xi−1/2xi+1/2sdx

离散格式把面上的 F 换成数值通量 h，它只用面两侧的状态 uL、uR：

The discrete scheme replaces F on a face by a numerical flux h, which uses only the two states either side of it, uL and uR:

h(uL,uR)=14(uL2+uR2)−ε(uR−uL)

前一项是两侧物理通量的平均（中心项），后一项是耗散项（本页 ε=0.8）：它像一点人工扩散，让格式稳定。两侧状态相同时 h(u,u)=12u2=F(u)，这就是相容。这一页里后面的一切——Jacobian、前向、伴随——都只用到 h 的两个局部导数：

The first part averages the physical fluxes of the two sides (the central part); the second is the dissipation (ε=0.8 here): a little artificial diffusion that keeps the scheme stable. With equal states h(u,u)=12u2=F(u), which is consistency. Everything that follows on this page — the Jacobian, forward mode, adjoint mode — uses nothing of h but its two local derivatives:

cL=∂h∂uL=12uL+ε
cR=∂h∂uR=12uR−ε

本页的 u 在 1.06 到 1.40 之间，所以 cL 在 1.33 到 1.50 之间，cR 在 −0.27 到 −0.10 之间：左侧（上游）的状态对通量的影响大得多。 让你拖动两侧状态，看两项怎样组成 h。

On this page u lies between 1.06 and 1.40, so cL lies between 1.33 and 1.50 and cR between −0.27 and −0.10: the left (upstream) state weighs far more in the flux. lets you drag the two states and watch the two parts make up h.

第 3 节
Section 3

边界条件

Boundary conditions

方程可以写成 uu'=s：信息沿特征 dx/dt=u 传播。本页处处 u>0，信息从左往右走，所以只在入口 x=0 给数据，出口 x=1 什么也不给——那里的解由上游决定，再给一个条件就多了。

The equation can be written as uu'=s: information travels along the characteristics dx/dt=u. Here u>0 everywhere, so information runs left to right, and data are given only at the inflow x=0; the outflow x=1 gets nothing — the solution there is decided upstream, and one more condition would be one too many.

格心格式里入口面 f0 的左边没有单元，就放一个 ghost 状态 uL=uin，通量照常用 h(uin,u1) 算：条件通过通量弱加，u1 本身并不等于 uin。出口面 f8 的右边也没有单元，就外推 uR=uL，于是 h8=12u82，正是物理通量。（）

On the cell-centred scheme the inflow face f0 has no cell on its left, so it gets a ghost state uL=uin and the flux is computed as usual, h(uin,u1): the condition is imposed weakly, through the flux, and u1 itself does not equal uin. The outflow face f8 has no cell on its right either, so it extrapolates, uR=uL, and h8=12u82, the physical flux itself. ()

这就是整个系列里两种格式的分歧所在，一维里它只涉及一个面。到了二维与拟一维页面，同样的区别会以更复杂的样子出现。

That is where the two schemes of the whole series part company, and in 1-D it involves a single face. On the two-dimensional and quasi-one-dimensional pages the same difference returns in more elaborate forms.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 面循环：收集、计算、分发

Residual = the face loop: gather, compute, scatter

把第 2 节的积分形式写成离散方程：控制体的残差是流出减流入，再减去它收到的源项。

Write the integral form of Section 2 as discrete equations: the residual of a control volume is what flows out minus what flows in, less the source it receives.

Ri(u)=hi+1/2−hi−1/2−σiΔx

这些方程不是一个一个写出来的，而是一个面循环：对每个面，收集两侧状态，算一次通量，加到左侧、从右侧减去。每个面只算一次通量，却同时服务两个控制体。 逐句播放这个循环。

These equations are not written out one by one: they are one loop over faces. For each face, gather the two states, compute one flux, add it on the left and subtract it on the right. Every flux is computed once and serves two control volumes. plays the loop statement by statement.

在收敛解 u* 上，循环算出的残差处处是 0（到舍入误差）：每个控制体的通量与源项正好平衡。

At the converged solution u* the residual the loop computes is zero everywhere, to round-off: in every control volume the fluxes and the source balance.

第 5 节
Section 5

求解：Jacobian 与 Jacobi 迭代

The solve: the Jacobian and Jacobi iteration

Jacobian：每个面贡献四个数

The Jacobian: four entries per face

残差对未知量的导数 A=∂R/∂u 也是一个面一个面拼起来的：面的通量只依赖两侧状态，它的两个局部导数按残差里的符号落进 A 的四个位置：

The derivative of the residual with respect to the unknowns, A=∂R/∂u, is assembled face by face as well: a face's flux depends only on the two states either side, and its two local derivatives land in four entries of A with the signs of the residual:

ALL+=cL,ALR+=cR
ARL−=cL,ARR−=cR

所以 A 是三对角的。对角元是同一个状态的 cL−cR=2ε=1.6；最后一个单元的出口面把 cL+cR 合并成 u8，它的对角元是 12u8+ε=1.501。对角线下面那一带是 −cL（−1.48 … −1.33），是上游对下游的影响；上面那一带是 cR（−0.24 … −0.10），是耗散项带来的一点反向耦合（）。

So A is tridiagonal. A diagonal entry is cL−cR=2ε=1.6 of one and the same state; in the last cell the outflow face merges cL+cR into u8, and its diagonal is 12u8+ε=1.501. The band below the diagonal is −cL (−1.48 … −1.33), upstream acting on downstream; the band above is cR (−0.24 … −0.10), the small backward coupling of the dissipation ().

Jacobi 迭代

Jacobi iteration

解 R(u)=0 用最简单的迭代法：每一轮算一次残差，除以 A 的对角元：

R(u)=0 is solved by the simplest iteration there is: every sweep computes the residual once and divides it by the diagonal of A:

u←u−D(u)−1R(u),D=diag(A)

对角元 D 由同一个面循环收集（只取落在对角线上的那两个数），整个过程既不形成矩阵，也不解线性方程组。只有 8 个未知量，直接做 LU 分解最快，但那样看不出 matrix-free；Jacobi 天然是 matrix-free 的，而且第 9、10 节的前向与伴随方程用的也是它。从 u=uin 出发，判据（残差均方根小于 10−13）在第 58 轮满足，之后每轮大约乘以 0.59（）。

The diagonal D is collected by the same face loop (only the two numbers that land on the diagonal), and at no point is a matrix formed or a linear system solved. With only 8 unknowns a direct LU factorisation would be fastest, but it would show nothing of matrix-free; Jacobi is matrix-free by construction, and it is also what solves the forward and adjoint equations of Sections 9 and 10. From u=uin the criterion (rms residual below 10−13) is met at sweep 58, each sweep multiplying the residual by about 0.59 from then on ().

收敛解是 u*：u1=1.0584，…，u8=1.4024。它逐渐爬升，与精确解 1+x 接近但不相等：出口处精确值是 1.3919。差别是离散误差，第 13 节会测它。

The converged solution is u*: u1=1.0584, …, u8=1.4024. It climbs steadily, close to but not equal to the exact solution 1+x, whose outflow value here is 1.3919. The difference is discretisation error, which Section 13 measures.

目标函数：反设计

The objective function: inverse design

目标是让解 u 贴近一个给定的目标分布 ū：

The goal is to bring the solution u close to a given target ū:

J(u)=12Σiwi(ui−ūi)2

wi 是控制体长度。目标 ū 不是凭空写的：它是把源项换成 sinπx 的区间平均 σ̄ 以后同一个格式的解。当前设计是均匀源项 σk=0.5，于是 J=3.56e−3（）。由此还白送一个检验：在 σ=σ̄ 处 J=0，梯度也必须精确为零，第 12 节用它。

wi are the control-volume lengths. The target ū is not made up: it is the solution of the same scheme with the source replaced by the interval averages σ̄ of sinπx. The current design is the uniform source σk=0.5, which gives J=3.56e−3 (). This also provides a check for free: at σ=σ̄, J=0 and the gradient must be exactly zero; Section 12 uses it.

伴随方程的右端就是 J 对 u 的偏导数：

The right-hand side of the adjoint equation is the partial derivative of J with respect to u:

gi=∂J∂ui=wi(ui−ūi)
第 7 节
Section 7

matrix-free 前向：计算 Av

Matrix-free forward mode: computing Av

给未知量一个扰动 v，残差变化多少？把循环的每一句换成它的导数就行：收集两侧的扰动 vL、vR，面上算 hd=cLvL+cRvR，照原来的符号分发。入口的 ghost 状态是常数，扰动为 0；出口面上 uR=uL，两个导数合并成 cL+cR=u8。

Perturb the unknowns by v: how much does the residual change? Replace every statement of the loop by its derivative: gather the two perturbations vL and vR, compute hd=cLvL+cRvR on the face, and scatter with the original signs. The inflow ghost state is a constant, with zero perturbation; on the outflow face uR=uL, and the two derivatives merge into cL+cR=u8.

rd=∂R∂uv=Av

循环的结构、每个面的顺序都不变，代价也是一次残差，而矩阵 A 从来没有形成过。 逐句播放，最后与显式的 A×v 对照。

The structure of the loop and the order of the faces are unchanged, the cost is that of one residual, and the matrix A is never formed. plays it statement by statement and compares the result with the explicit A×v at the end.

第 8 节
Section 8

matrix-free 伴随：计算 ATw

Matrix-free adjoint mode: computing ATw

伴随要的是转置 ATw。不用形成矩阵，只要把前向循环里每个面的贡献“转置”：前向里面 f 把 hd 加到左侧、从右侧减去，所以它对 ⟨w,Av⟩ 的贡献是（记 hb=wL−wR）

The adjoint needs the transpose, ATw. There is no need to form the matrix: transpose the contribution of each face of the forward loop. In forward mode face f adds hd on its left and subtracts it on its right, so its contribution to ⟨w,Av⟩ is, writing hb=wL−wR,

hd(wL−wR)=(cLvL+cRvR)hb

再按 vL、vR 重新组合：

and regrouped by vL and vR:

(cLvL+cRvR)hb=vL(cLhb)+vR(cRhb)

把所有面加起来，右边正是 ⟨ATw,v⟩。于是伴随循环是：收集 hb=w[L]−w[R]（原来的分发），面上算 (cLhb,cRhb)（原来是两个输入、一个输出，现在是一个输入、两个输出），再分发到 y[L]、y[R]（原来的收集）。数据流的方向整个倒过来了。入口面上 cLhb 属于常数 ghost 状态，丢掉。 逐句播放。

Summed over all faces, the right-hand side is exactly ⟨ATw,v⟩. So the adjoint loop gathers hb=w[L]−w[R] (what was the scatter), computes (cLhb,cRhb) on the face (two inputs and one output have become one input and two outputs), and scatters into y[L] and y[R] (what was the gather). The data flow is reversed end to end. On the inflow face cLhb belongs to the constant ghost state and is dropped. plays it statement by statement.

点积测试

The dot-product test

怎样确认 residual_b 写对了？转置满足一个与步长无关的恒等式，对任意 v、w：

How do we know residual_b is right? A transpose satisfies an identity with no step size in it, for any v and w:

⟨w,Av⟩=⟨ATw,v⟩

两边分别用 residual_d 与 residual_b 算，应当只差舍入误差：页面打开时第一对随机向量的相对误差是 0（）。

Compute the two sides with residual_d and residual_b and they should differ by round-off only: the first random pair the page draws gives a relative error of 0 ().

前向求解：切线方程

The forward solve: the tangent equation

设计变量 pm 变一点，解 u 变多少？对 R(u(p),p)=0 求导：

If a design variable pm moves a little, how much does the solution u move? Differentiate R(u(p),p)=0:

Adudpm=−∂R∂pm

右端是残差对设计变量的偏导数。源项 σk 只出现在单元 k 的方程里：∂R/∂σk=−Δxek。入口值 uin 通过面 f0 的 ghost 状态进入单元 1 的方程：∂R/∂uin=−(12uin+ε)e1。解出 v=du/dpm，目标函数的导数就是 gTv。

The right-hand side is the partial derivative of the residual with respect to the design variable. The source σk appears only in cell k's equation: ∂R/∂σk=−Δxek. The inflow value uin enters cell 1's equation through the ghost state of face f0: ∂R/∂uin=−(12uin+ε)e1. Solve for v=du/dpm, and the derivative of the objective is gTv.

这个线性方程组用的还是第 5 节的 Jacobi 迭代，对角元 D 也还是同一个，只是把残差换成 residual_d：

This linear system is solved by the Jacobi iteration of Section 5 again, with the same diagonal D, the residual replaced by residual_d:

v←v−D−1(Av+∂R∂pm)

对 σ4，判据在第 54 轮满足。问题在于：一次前向求解只给出一个设计变量的导数。本页有 9 个设计变量，就要解 9 次；设计变量有一千个，就要解一千次（）。

For σ4 the criterion is met at sweep 54. The trouble is that one forward solve gives the derivative with respect to one design variable only. This page has 9 design variables and needs 9 solves; with a thousand design variables it would need a thousand ().

其中 ψ 满足伴随方程：

伴随倒着流

The adjoint runs backwards

第 5 节看到，AT 把大的那一带搬到了对角线上面：伴随方程从右边（下游）取信息。所以伴随在出口附近最小，往上游一路累积：本页 ψ 在 9.65e−3 到 4.95e−2 之间，一维里转置就是把流动方向倒过来。−ψi 的意思很直接：在控制体 i 里加一个单位源项，J 变化多少。

Section 5 showed that AT moves the large band above the diagonal: the adjoint equation takes its information from the right (downstream). So the adjoint is smallest near the outflow and accumulates upstream: here ψ lies between 9.65e−3 and 4.95e−2, in 1-D the transpose is simply the flow direction reversed. The meaning of −ψi is direct: how much J changes when a unit source is added to control volume i.

Design derivatives: dJ/dp

有了 ψ，每个设计变量的导数就是 ψ 与 ∂R/∂pm（第 9 节）的点积：

梯度用来做什么

What the gradient is for

优化器每走一步都要梯度。 用 BFGS 从均匀源项出发去找目标源项 σ̄：每一步一次 primal 求解、一次伴随求解。入口值不参与优化——u 只通过 12uin2+∫s 依赖它，它与 σ1 几乎可以互相抵换。本页第 53 步 J 降到 10−20 以下，源项与 σ̄ 的最大差是 2.7e−9。

An optimiser needs the gradient at every step. runs BFGS from the uniform source towards the target source σ̄, one primal solve and one adjoint solve per step. The inflow value is left out of the optimisation: u depends on it only through 12uin2+∫s, so it and σ1 can almost stand in for each other. On this page J falls below 10−20 at step 53, with the source within 2.7e−9 of σ̄.

第 12 节
Section 12

验证：对有限差分

Verification against finite differences

最直接的检验是有限差分：把一个设计变量挪一个小步长 h，重新求解，看 J 变多少。

The most direct check is a finite difference: move one design variable by a small step h, solve again, and see how much J changes.

dJdpm≈J(p+hem)−J(p−hem)2h

中心差分每个分量要两次非线性求解，前向差分 (J(p+hem)−J(p))/h 只要一次。取 h=10−6，9 个分量与伴随梯度的最大相对差：中心差分 3.5e−9，前向差分 7.1e−6。步长太大，截断误差占上风；太小，舍入误差占上风——两头都会让一个正确的梯度看起来像错的（）。

A central difference costs two nonlinear solves per component, a forward difference (J(p+hem)−J(p))/h one. At h=10−6 the largest relative difference from the adjoint gradient over the 9 components is 3.5e−9 for central differences and 7.1e−6 for forward ones. Too large a step and truncation dominates; too small and round-off does — either end makes a correct gradient look wrong ().

复步长

The complex step

差分的两头都受限：截断误差要 h 小，舍入误差要 h 大。复步长把这个两难拿掉：把设计变量挪一个虚数步长 ih，整个 primal 求解都用复数算，J 的虚部就是导数：

Both ends of a difference are limited: truncation wants a small h, round-off a large one. The complex step removes the dilemma: move the design variable by an imaginary step ih, run the whole primal solve in complex arithmetic, and the imaginary part of J is the derivative:

dJdpm=ImJ(p+ihem)h+O(h2)

这里没有两个相近的数相减，也就没有相消，h=10−30 也可以。Jacobi 迭代的虚部走的正是第 9 节的前向迭代：复步长就是用复数算出来的前向模式。每个分量一次复数求解，9 个分量与伴随梯度之差的最大值（除以梯度的最大分量）是 2.3e−15。在里把格式选成复步长就能看到；步长扫描里它是绿线，σ1 的误差从步长 10⁻⁶ 起就一直停在舍入误差上。

No two nearly equal numbers are subtracted, so nothing cancels and h=10−30 is fine. The imaginary part of the Jacobi iterate follows exactly the forward iteration of Section 9: the complex step is forward mode computed in complex arithmetic. One complex solve per component; over the 9 components its largest difference from the adjoint gradient (over the gradient's largest component) is 2.3e−15. Choose the complex step among the schemes of to see it; in the step sweep it is the green line, and for σ1 it stays on the round-off level from a step of 10⁻⁶ down.

两个检验不含步长。一个是第 8 节的点积测试；另一个是第 6 节的目标：在 σ=σ̄ 处，J 与 dJ/dp 都逐比特为零（J = 0, max|dJ/dp| = 0）。梯度在一个独立构造出来的最优点上归零，比任何差分对照都更有说服力。

Two checks have no step size in them. One is the dot-product test of Section 8; the other is the target of Section 6: at σ=σ̄ both J and dJ/dp are zero to the last bit (J = 0, max|dJ/dp| = 0). A gradient vanishing at an independently constructed optimum is better evidence than any finite-difference comparison.

哪一种检验抓哪一种错误

Which check catches which bug

点积测试只查一件事：伴随循环是不是前向循环的转置。梯度检验（这里对复步长）查的是最后那个数对不对。两者各管一段， 把几种常见的错误放进同一张表：

The dot test checks one thing: that the adjoint loop is the transpose of the forward loop. The gradient check (here against the complex step) checks whether the final number is right. Each covers its own stretch, and puts some common bugs in one table:

分发的符号写反、漏掉一个面、cL 与 cR 对调，都是转置错误，点积测试一眼就能看出来：误差在 0.16 到 1.0 之间，而正确的循环是 1.1e−16。另外三种错误它看不出来。第一种是 flux() 里导数写错：前向与伴随调用的是同一个 flux()，伴随仍是那个（错的）前向的精确转置，点积测试照样通过（6.1e−17），梯度却差了 1.4e−2。第二种是伴随求解停得太早，梯度差 6.7e−3。第三种是组装梯度时符号写反，差 2.0。这三种只有梯度检验抓得到，可它又说不出错在哪里。所以两个都要做：先用点积测试把循环查干净，再用梯度检验查其余的一切。

A flipped scatter sign, a missed face, cL and cR swapped: these are transposition errors, and the dot test sees them at once, with errors between 0.16 and 1.0 against 1.1e−16 for the right loop. Three other bugs it cannot see. The first is a wrong derivative inside flux(): forward and adjoint mode call the same flux(), so the adjoint is still the exact transpose of a (wrong) forward mode and the dot test passes (6.1e−17) while the gradient is off by 1.4e−2. The second is an adjoint solve stopped too early, off by 6.7e−3. The third is a gradient assembled with the wrong sign, off by 2.0. Only the gradient check catches these three, and it cannot say where the bug is. So do both: the dot test first, to get the loops right, then the gradient check for everything else.

这并不说明伴随算错了：第 12 节验证过，ψ 给出的是离散 J 的精确梯度，优化器要的正是它。它说明的是另一件事：离散伴随不一定是连续伴随的好近似。离散格式在边界上怎么处理，会以转置的形式出现在伴随里。

This does not mean the adjoint is wrong: Section 12 verified that ψ gives the exact gradient of the discrete J, which is what an optimiser needs. It shows something else: a discrete adjoint need not be a good approximation of the continuous one. Whatever the scheme does at a boundary reappears, transposed, in its adjoint.

Adjoint consistency, and estimating the error in J

一个伴随一致的入口

A dual-consistent inflow

连续伴随给的梯度

The gradient a continuous adjoint gives

另一条路是先对连续问题推导伴随（第 13 节的 λ），再把它离散：用网格上的 u、ū 从出口往回积分 λ'=−(u−ū)/u，然后

The other road derives the adjoint of the continuous problem first (the λ of Section 13) and discretises it afterwards: integrate λ'=−(u−ū)/u back from the outflow with the grid's u and ū, then

dJdσk≈∫Ikλdx,dJduin≈uinλ(0)

（Ik 是第 k 个区间，积分用中点公式。）它近似的是连续 J 的梯度，而优化器算的是离散 J，两者之差就是离散误差。内部各分量的差是 O(Δx)：除以梯度的最大分量，N=8 时 1.7e−2，N=128 时 4.2e−4。σ1 却不一样（ 右）。本页的入口下，连续伴随给的 dJ/dσ1 是离散伴随的 1.30 倍，加密也不变。这正是 1/(1−r)：第 13 节那层边界层直接写进了梯度。换成迎风入口，这个比值是 1.06（N=8），到 N=1024 是 1.0001。

(Ik is the k-th interval, integrated by the midpoint rule.) This approximates the gradient of the continuous J, while the optimiser evaluates the discrete J; the two differ by the discretisation error. In the interior the components differ by O(Δx): over the gradient's largest component, 1.7e−2 at N=8 and 4.2e−4 at N=128. σ1 is another matter (, right). With this page's inflow, the continuous adjoint's dJ/dσ1 is 1.30 times the discrete adjoint's, and refinement does not change that. It is exactly 1/(1−r): the boundary layer of Section 13 is written straight into the gradient. With the upwind inflow the ratio is 1.06 at N=8 and 1.0001 at N=1024.

所以优化器要的是它实际计算的那个 J 的梯度，也就是离散伴随给的梯度。连续伴随的梯度与它差一个离散误差；在格式不一致的边界上，差的是一个不随网格消失的量。

So an optimiser needs the gradient of the J it actually evaluates, which is what the discrete adjoint gives. A continuous adjoint's gradient differs from it by a discretisation error, and at a boundary where the scheme is inconsistent by an amount that does not vanish with the grid.

Dual consistency is not what the gradient needs: Section 12 verified that whatever the inflow does, the discrete adjoint gives the exact gradient of the discrete J. It decides whether the discrete adjoint approximates the continuous one, and with that whether a continuous adjoint's gradient and an adjoint-based error estimate can be trusted.

  • 前向与伴随是同一个循环：前向把每一句换成它的导数；伴随把每个面的贡献转置，收集与分发对调，数据倒着流。谁都不形成矩阵。
  • 边界条件：格心页的入口在通量里弱加；伴随里 ghost 状态的那一份丢掉。
  • 一次伴随求解给出全部梯度：前向每个设计变量解一次，伴随只解一次，代价与设计变量个数无关。
  • 检验：点积测试检验转置；已知最优点上的零梯度、有限差分与复步长检验梯度。转置错误点积测试抓得到，导数、求解与组装的错误只有梯度检验抓得到，两个都要做。精确解检验格式的阶。
  • 伴随一致性：入口面的耗散让离散伴随在第一个控制体里偏离连续伴随；换成迎风通量，偏差随网格消失。它不影响梯度对离散 J 的正确性，但决定连续伴随的梯度与伴随的误差估计是否可信。
  • The one-dimensional flux is the core: the residual is "on every face, gather the two states, call the one-dimensional flux once, add on the left and subtract on the right", less the source.
  • Forward and adjoint are the same loop: forward mode replaces each statement by its derivative; adjoint mode transposes each face's contribution, swapping gather and scatter and running the data backwards. Neither forms a matrix.
  • Boundary conditions: on the cell-centred page the inflow is imposed weakly, in the flux; in the adjoint the ghost state's share is dropped.
  • One adjoint solve gives the whole gradient: forward mode needs one solve per design variable, adjoint mode one in all, whatever the number of design variables.
  • Checks: the dot-product test checks the transpose; the zero gradient at a known optimum, finite differences and the complex step check the gradient. The dot test catches transposition errors, and only the gradient check catches wrong derivatives, loose solves and assembly errors, so both are needed. The exact solution checks the order of the scheme.
  • Adjoint consistency: the dissipation on the inflow face pulls the discrete adjoint away from the continuous one in the first control volume; with the upwind flux the gap vanishes with the grid. The gradient of the discrete J is exact either way, but whether a continuous adjoint's gradient and an adjoint-based error estimate can be trusted depends on it.
