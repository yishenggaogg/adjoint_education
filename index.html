离散伴随教学页

教学主线与目标：面向学过微积分、线性代数和数值方法的本科生，从小模型的残差、前向和伴随逐句实现，走向2D非结构网格 RANS 的完整离散伴随。

Learning path: for undergraduates familiar with calculus, linear algebra and numerical methods, develop residuals, tangents and adjoints statement by statement, toward a complete discrete adjoint for 2D unstructured-grid RANS.

Discrete adjoint, taught page by page · 中文 / English

十二个单文件、可交互的网页，把有限体积法的全离散伴随从头到尾讲一遍： 从一个面上的一维通量开始，把同一个循环依次改写成 primal、matrix-free 前向与 matrix-free 伴随， 再做前向与伴随求解、几何导数，最后用有限差分与复数步长逐项校验。 六组算例（一维 Burgers、二维标量对流、二维标量对流扩散、拟一维 Euler、一维层流 N–S与二维 Euler）各有格心与格点两版， 十二页教学主题相互对应，可以任意两两并排对照。第一次接触离散伴随，请从一维 Burgers 那一对读起。

一维 Burgers 方程/ The one-dimensional Burgers equation

带源项的定常 Burgers 方程，支持一阶／二阶空间精度切换。二阶线性重构的完整 tangent 与 adjoint 包含斜率和边界导数。 原始方程用 GS 预估矫正，线性方程可选 GS 固定点或 GS 预处理 GMRES，并比较网格收敛、求解成本与连续伴随一致性。
Steady Burgers with a source, selectable first-/second-order space, exact reconstruction derivatives, GS predictor–corrector and GS-preconditioned GMRES. Interactive studies compare mesh convergence, solver work and continuous-adjoint consistency.

二维标量对流方程 / A two-dimensional scalar advection equation

保留原有正文、网格图和逐句播放器，各节新增光滑特征边界模型的一阶／二阶扩展；同页附录 B 提供对应连续伴随。精度切换仅作用于新增模型。
Original prose, mesh diagrams and players are preserved. Each section adds a first/second-order smooth characteristic-boundary model; Appendix B supplies its continuous adjoint. The order selector controls only the added model.

二维标量对流扩散方程 / A two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation

同一张网格、同一个对流通量，加上常数扩散。扩散通量要用最小二乘梯度，残差变成两遍循环； 精确 Jacobian 伸到邻居的邻居，Jacobi 迭代只用它的对角元，伴随用精确算子。物面是 Dirichlet 壁面 u = uw。
The same mesh and convective flux, with a constant diffusion added. The diffusive flux needs least-squares gradients, so the residual becomes two loops; the exact Jacobian reaches the neighbours of neighbours, the Jacobi iteration uses only its diagonal, and the adjoint the exact operator. The body is a Dirichlet wall, u = uw.

拟一维 Euler 方程 / The quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations

变截面流道，入口给总压总温、出口给背压，全场亚声速。方程组版本：局部导数是 3×3 块， 一阶／二阶重构可切换，提供块 GS 与 GS 预处理 GMRES、完整差分扫描，以及同页连续伴随推导、独立交互计算和附录 C 的 LU 直接伴随求解。
A variable-area duct with total conditions in, a back pressure out and subsonic flow throughout. Switch first/second-order reconstruction, solve with block GS or GS-preconditioned GMRES, and explore full finite-difference sweeps, an independent continuous adjoint and Appendix C’s direct LU adjoint solve.

一维层流 Navier–Stokes 方程 / One-dimensional laminar Navier–Stokes

等截面的光滑可压缩流：在 Euler 基础上加入轴向黏性应力、黏性功与热传导，不含湍流模型。 常物性／Sutherland 物性、一阶／二阶重构、局部加热和制造解，完整求导梯度、物性与边界。
Smooth constant-area compressible flow with molecular stress, viscous work and conduction, without turbulence. Explore constant/Sutherland transport, first/second-order convection, heating and manufactured solutions, with exact derivatives of gradients, transport and boundaries.
