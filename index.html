离散伴随教学页Discrete adjoint: interactive lessons

教学主线与目标：面向学过微积分、线性代数和数值方法的本科生，从模型问题的求解、前向和伴随逐句实现，走向2D非结构网格 RANS 的完整离散伴随。

Learning path: for undergraduates familiar with calculus, linear algebra and numerical methods, implement solvers, tangents and adjoints for model problems statement by statement, toward a complete discrete adjoint for 2D unstructured-grid RANS.

十九个单文件、可交互的网页，把有限体积法的全离散伴随从头到尾讲一遍：从一个面上的一维通量开始，把同一个循环依次改写成 primal、matrix-free 前向与 matrix-free 伴随，再做前向与伴随求解、几何导数，最后用有限差分与复数步长逐项校验。八组算例（一维 Burgers、二维标量对流、二维标量对流扩散、拟一维 Euler、一维层流 N–S、二维 Euler、二维层流 N–S与二维 RANS–SA）各有格心与格点两版，二维 RANS–SA 另有伴随一致的一对，十八页成对教学主题相互对应，另有独立黏性 Burgers 页，可以任意两两并排对照。第一次接触离散伴随，请从一维 Burgers 那一对读起。

Nineteen single-file, interactive web pages that teach the fully discrete adjoint of a finite-volume scheme from start to finish. Starting from a one-dimensional flux on a single face, the same loop is rewritten in turn as the primal, a matrix-free forward mode and a matrix-free adjoint; then come the forward and adjoint solves and the geometric derivatives, and finally everything is checked term by term with finite differences and the complex step. Eight model problems (one-dimensional Burgers, two-dimensional scalar advection, two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion, quasi-one-dimensional Euler, one-dimensional laminar N–S, two-dimensional Euler, two-dimensional laminar N–S and two-dimensional RANS–SA) each come in a cell-centred and a node-centred version, and the RANS–SA problem has an extra adjoint-consistent pair. The eighteen pages have corresponding teaching topics in pairs, and one independent viscous Burgers page stands apart, so any two can be compared side by side. If the discrete adjoint is new to you, start with the one-dimensional Burgers pair.

二维标量对流扩散方程A two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation

同一张网格、同一个对流通量，加上常数扩散。扩散通量要用最小二乘梯度，残差变成两遍循环；精确 Jacobian 伸到邻居的邻居，Jacobi 迭代只用它的对角元，伴随用精确算子。物面是 Dirichlet 壁面 u = uw。

The same mesh and convective flux, with a constant diffusion added. The diffusive flux needs least-squares gradients, so the residual becomes two loops; the exact Jacobian reaches the neighbours of neighbours, the Jacobi iteration uses only its diagonal, and the adjoint the exact operator. The body is a Dirichlet wall, u = uw.

拟一维 Euler 方程The quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations

变截面流道，入口给总压总温、出口给背压，全场亚声速。方程组版本：局部导数是 3×3 块，一阶／二阶重构可切换，提供块 GS 与 GS 预处理 GMRES、完整差分扫描，以及同页连续伴随推导、独立交互计算和附录 C 的 LU 直接伴随求解。

A variable-area duct with total conditions in, a back pressure out and subsonic flow throughout. For the system, local derivatives are 3×3 blocks; switch first/second-order reconstruction, solve with block GS or GS-preconditioned GMRES, and explore full finite-difference sweeps, an independent continuous adjoint and Appendix C’s direct LU adjoint solve.

格心格式Cell-centred finite volume

二维 RANS–SA 方程（无激波）Two-dimensional RANS with Spalart–Allmaras (shock-free)

沿用二维标量对流页的同一张混合网格与页面结构：可压缩 RANS 与标准 SA 模型，Roe 通量、无权最小二乘重构（不加限制器）与 SU2 式边上粘性通量，页首切换一阶／二阶。伪时间加对称块 Gauss–Seidel（近似 Jacobian 为一阶 Roe 与薄剪切层）；matrix-free tangent／adjoint 逐遍播放，前向、复数步长与重算差分三种办法核对全部 51 个设计导数。来流边界有两种处理：原来的一对伴随不一致（附录 B 说明原因）；另一对伴随一致（格点页来流节点保留质量行、能量行加上动量反作用的功，格心页来流面取边界状态的物理通量），加密时离散伴随在来流附近也趋于连续伴随。四页都有附录 A：同一问题的连续伴随推导、P1 有限元求解与逐级对照。

The scalar-advection pages’ hybrid mesh and lesson structure for compressible RANS with the standard SA model: Roe flux, unweighted least squares without a limiter, SU2-style edge viscous flux, and a first/second-order switch. Pseudo-time with symmetric block Gauss–Seidel on a first-order Roe plus thin-shear-layer Jacobian; matrix-free tangent and adjoint played pass by pass; all 51 design derivatives checked by the tangent, the complex step and re-solved finite differences. The inflow boundary comes in two treatments: the original pair is adjoint-inconsistent (Appendix B explains why), the other pair is adjoint-consistent (inflow nodes keep their mass row and the energy row takes the work of the momentum reaction; cell inflow faces take the boundary state’s physical flux), and under refinement its discrete adjoint approaches the continuous one next to the inflow as well. All four pages carry Appendix A: the continuous adjoint of the same problem, derived, solved by P1 finite elements and compared level by level.
