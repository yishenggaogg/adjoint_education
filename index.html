离散伴随教学页

教学主线与目标：面向学过微积分、线性代数和数值方法的本科生，从小模型的残差、前向和伴随逐句实现，走向工业级非结构网格 RANS 的完整离散伴随。RANS 是后续目标，当前页面是通向这一目标的基础算例。

Learning path: for undergraduates familiar with calculus, linear algebra and numerical methods, develop residuals, tangents and adjoints statement by statement, toward a complete industrial unstructured-grid RANS discrete adjoint. RANS is a future destination; the current lessons are foundational cases.

一维 Burgers 方程/ The one-dimensional Burgers equation

带源项的定常 Burgers 方程，支持一阶／二阶空间精度切换。二阶线性重构的完整 tangent 与 adjoint 包含斜率和边界导数。 原始方程用 GS 预估矫正，线性方程可选 GS 固定点或 GS 预处理 GMRES，并比较网格收敛、求解成本与连续伴随一致性。
Steady Burgers with a source, selectable first-/second-order space, exact reconstruction derivatives, GS predictor–corrector and GS-preconditioned GMRES. Interactive studies compare mesh convergence, solver work and continuous-adjoint consistency.

二维标量对流扩散方程 / A two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation

同一张网格、同一个对流通量，加上常数扩散。扩散通量要用最小二乘梯度，残差变成两遍循环； 精确 Jacobian 伸到邻居的邻居，Jacobi 迭代只用它的对角元，伴随用精确算子。物面是 Dirichlet 壁面 u = uw。
The same mesh and convective flux, with a constant diffusion added. The diffusive flux needs least-squares gradients, so the residual becomes two loops; the exact Jacobian reaches the neighbours of neighbours, the Jacobi iteration uses only its diagonal, and the adjoint the exact operator. The body is a Dirichlet wall, u = uw.

拟一维 Euler 方程 / The quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations

变截面流道，入口给总压总温、出口给背压，全场亚声速。方程组版本：局部导数是 3×3 块， 一阶／二阶重构可切换，提供块 GS 与 GS 预处理 GMRES、完整差分扫描，以及同页连续伴随推导、独立交互计算和附录 C 的 LU 直接伴随求解。
A variable-area duct with total conditions in, a back pressure out and subsonic flow throughout. Switch first/second-order reconstruction, solve with block GS or GS-preconditioned GMRES, and explore full finite-difference sweeps, an independent continuous adjoint and Appendix C’s direct LU adjoint solve.

每页讲什么 / What each page covers

  • 网格、一维通量与边界条件 — mesh, the one-dimensional flux, boundary conditions
  • 残差 = 一个循环：收集、计算、分发 — the residual as one gather–compute–scatter loop
  • 同一个循环改写成 matrix-free 前向 Av 与伴随 A⁠Tw，每一行都能逐句播放
  • 前向与伴随求解（Burgers 使用 GS／GS 预处理 GMRES，二维 Euler 使用 GS 预处理 GMRES，其余六页保留 Jacobi）、几何导数 dR/dX 与 dL/dX（一维页是对源项与入口值的设计导数）— the forward and the adjoint solve (GS / GS-preconditioned GMRES for Burgers, GS-preconditioned GMRES for 2-D Euler; Jacobi on the other six pages), and the geometric derivatives (on the 1-D pages, the design derivatives with respect to the sources and the inflow value)
  • 点积测试与有限差分校验，以及差分校验本身的陷阱 — the dot-product test, and where finite differences mislead
  • 复数步长：公式与实现；从收敛流场出发，复数迭代每一轮都等于前向迭代；用它验证梯度 — the complex step: the formula and how to implement it; started from the converged flow, the complex iteration is the forward iteration at every sweep; the gradient checked by it
  • 精度阶与网格加密：构造解、网格族、观测阶；二维 Euler 首版仅报告加密结果，尚不宣称精度阶 — accuracy and refinement: manufactured solutions and observed orders; the initial 2-D Euler study reports refinement data without claiming an observed order
  • （一维）源项作设计变量、伴随梯度驱动的 BFGS 反设计、离散伴随与连续伴随的对照 — (1-D) sources as design variables, a BFGS inverse design driven by the adjoint gradient, the discrete adjoint against the continuous one
  • （一维）哪一种检验抓哪一种错误、入口一致性对照与用伴随估计 J 的误差 — (1-D) which check catches which bug, an inflow-consistency comparison and an adjoint-based estimate of the error in J
  • （全部页面，第 15 节）伴随一致性；连续方程、边界条件与网格加密对照。二维纯对流另设特征边界对照，并说明原零通量内壁的限制。原八页附录 A 提供完整推导、实现与交互；二维 Euler 首版仅给出形式连续分析及验证范围 / The original eight pages include Appendix A; the new 2-D Euler pair provides formal continuous analysis and explicit validation limits — continuous dual equations, boundary conditions and refinement; a separate characteristic-boundary contrast for 2-D advection.
  • （对流扩散）最小二乘梯度、两遍循环、伸到邻居的邻居的精确 Jacobian、强加的 Dirichlet 壁面 — (advection–diffusion) least-squares gradients, two loops, an exact Jacobian reaching the neighbours of neighbours, a strongly imposed Dirichlet wall
  • （二维 Euler）四分量面循环、压力壁面、特征旋转、计算图正反传播与固定边界分支 / Four-component face loops, pressure walls, characteristic rotations and forward/reverse computational graphs.
  • （拟一维）源项、特征边界条件、3×3 块 — (quasi-1-D) the source term, characteristic boundary conditions, 3×3 blocks
