二维 RANS–SA 方程的离散伴随：格点格式，伴随不一致的远场

The discrete adjoint of 2-D RANS with Spalart–Allmaras: node-centred, adjoint-inconsistent far field

算例是二维对流页面的同一张环形混合网格：内圈是物面，外圈是远场，贴壁一层四边形、外层三角形。方程换成可压缩 RANS 与标准 Spalart–Allmaras 湍流模型：每个节点有 5 个未知量 (ρ, ρu, ρv, ρE, ρν̃)。物面是无滑移、绝热壁面（强条件，替换壁面节点的三行残差），远场分成来流与出口两部分：来流给定 ρ、u、v、ν̃（T 自由、热流为零），出口是牵引力出口（总牵引力 p n − τ·n 等于 p∞n）。空间离散：Roe 通量、无权最小二乘重构（不加限制器）、SU2 式的边上修正平均梯度粘性通量；求解：伪时间 + 对称块 Gauss–Seidel，近似 Jacobian 是一阶 Roe 加薄剪切层。本页的主线是离散伴随：先把残差拆成几遍循环，再把同样的循环改写成 matrix-free 前向（A v）和 matrix-free 伴随（ATw），最后用前向、复数步长与差分三种独立办法核对伴随给出的导数。附录 A 从同一个连续问题出发推导并求解连续伴随，在逐级加密的网格上与离散伴随比较。本页的来流边界沿用原来的处理，它是伴随不一致的：离散伴随在来流边界附近不趋于连续伴随。附录 B 说明原因，并与伴随一致的版本逐级比较。页首可以切换一阶／二阶空间离散，下面所有演示都随之重算。

The test case is the same annular hybrid mesh as the two-dimensional advection pages: the body on the inner ring, the far field on the outer, quadrilaterals in the layer against the wall and triangles outside. The equations become compressible RANS with the standard Spalart–Allmaras model: every node carries 5 unknowns (ρ, ρu, ρv, ρE, ρν̃). The body is a no-slip, adiabatic wall (a strong condition, replacing three rows of each wall node's residual), and the far field has two parts: the inflow fixes ρ, u, v and ν̃ (T is free, with zero heat flux), and the outlet is a traction outlet (the total traction p n − τ·n equals p∞n). Spatial discretisation: the Roe flux, unweighted least-squares reconstruction (no limiter), and SU2's corrected average gradient on the edges for the viscous flux; the solver: pseudo-time with symmetric block Gauss–Seidel, on an approximate Jacobian made of first-order Roe and the thin shear layer. The main line of this page is the discrete adjoint: it splits the residual into passes, rewrites the same passes as the matrix-free tangent (A v) and the matrix-free adjoint (ATw), and checks the adjoint's derivatives three independent ways: the tangent, the complex step, and finite differences. Appendix A derives and solves the continuous adjoint of the same continuous problem and compares it with the discrete adjoint on a sequence of refined meshes. This page keeps the original treatment of the inflow boundary, which is adjoint-inconsistent: near the inflow the discrete adjoint does not approach the continuous one. Appendix B explains why and compares level by level with the adjoint-consistent version. The header switches between first- and second-order spatial discretisation, and every demo below is recomputed.

网格：对偶控制体、对偶面与壁面距离

Mesh: dual control volumes, dual faces and wall distance

网格与二维对流页面完全相同，编号也相同：节点逐圈从壁面向外，节点逐圈编号，面按“内部、壁面、远场”排序。未知量放在节点上：每个节点的控制体是中位对偶，由相邻单元的边中点与单元中心围成。区别在于每个控制体现在有 5 个未知量，而不是 1 个。

The mesh is exactly that of the two-dimensional advection page, with the same numbering: nodes ring by ring from the wall outwards, nodes ring by ring, faces sorted interior, wall, far field. The unknowns live at the nodes: each node's control volume is its median dual, bounded by the edge midpoints and the centres of the cells around it. The difference is that every control volume now carries 5 unknowns instead of 1.

The geometric quantities a face loop reads are all in the figure below. Compared with the scalar pages there are two more: the reconstruction direction r (from a control-volume centre to the face point; the second-order scheme extrapolates the centre value along it) and the line d between the two centres (the viscous flux takes its two-point difference along it).

壁面距离

The wall distance

SA 模型的耗散项与 (ν̃/d)² 成正比，d 是到壁面的距离。本页的 di 是控制体中心到离散壁面多边形的精确欧氏距离：对每条壁面边求点到线段的距离，取最小。它是坐标的函数，所以在第 11 节的几何导数里也要求导。

The SA model's destruction term goes as (ν̃/d)², with d the distance to the wall. On this page di is the exact Euclidean distance from the control-volume centre to the discrete wall polygon: the distance from the point to each wall edge, and the smallest. It is a function of the coordinates, so the geometric derivatives of Section 11 differentiate it too.

方程与面通量

The equations and the face flux

要离散的方程

The equations to discretise

守恒形式的 RANS–SA 方程，带时间项：

RANS–SA in conservation form, with the time term:

∂U∂t+∇⋅(Fc(U)−Fv(U,∇U))=S(U,∇U,d),U=(ρρuρvρEρν~)

本页要的是定常解：∂U/∂t = 0，于是 ∇·(Fc − Fv) = S。时间项仍然要写出来，因为定常解正是靠伪时间推进得到的：从初始状态出发沿 t 推进，直到时间导数消失（第 5 节）。这里的 t 是伪时间，每个控制体取自己的步长，所以推进的中间状态不是真实的时间历程，只有推进到底得到的定常解才有物理意义。

This page wants the steady solution: ∂U/∂t = 0, so that ∇·(Fc − Fv) = S. The time term is still written, because the steady solution is reached by pseudo-time marching: from an initial state, U is advanced in t until the time derivative vanishes (Section 5). Here t is a pseudo time and every control volume takes its own step, so the intermediate states are not a physical time history; only the steady solution the marching ends at has a physical meaning.

The whole domain is adiabatic (no heat flux through the wall, the inflow or the outlet), so the viscous heating can only leave with the outflow, and the temperature lies above the free stream's T∞ = 1 throughout: on level 4, T is between 1.021 and 1.099 (T is not prescribed on the inflow faces, and heat reaches them upstream too). A mesh like this cannot resolve a real high-Reynolds boundary layer; the aim is a discrete problem that exercises every branch of the model and still converges to round-off: the modified branch of S̃, the cap on r, and μt/μ up to 0.09.

The finite-volume form

对每个控制体积分（Ui 取控制体上的平均值），散度变成面上的通量和，每个控制体得到一个关于 t 的常微分方程（半离散形式）：

Integrated over each control volume (Ui is the average over it), the divergence becomes a sum of face fluxes, and each control volume gets an ordinary differential equation in t (the semi-discrete form):

面通量 hf 从 L 流向 R：对 L 加、对 R 减，保证守恒。W = (ρ, u, v, T, ν̃) 是原始变量，G 是它们的最小二乘梯度，W+ 是重构到面上的状态。

The face flux hf runs from L to R: added to L and subtracted from R, which makes the scheme conservative. W = (ρ, u, v, T, ν̃) are the primitive variables, G their least-squares gradients, and W+ the state reconstructed to the face.

定常解满足 R(U) = 0：这就是本页要解、并在之后各节求导的离散方程；时间项只在求解时出现（第 5 节）。格点页壁面节点的 ρu、ρv、ρν̃ 三行与来流节点的 ρ、ρu、ρv、ρν̃ 四行不是上面的常微分方程：它们被替换成边界条件本身（第 3 节），推进中始终精确满足 u = v = ν̃ = 0 与 ρ、u、v、ν̃ 的来流值。

The steady solution satisfies R(U) = 0: that is the discrete equation this page solves and, in the later sections, differentiates; the time term appears in the solve only (Section 5). The ρu, ρv and ρν̃ rows of a wall node and the ρ, ρu, ρv and ρν̃ rows of an inflow node are not ODEs like the one above: they are replaced by the boundary conditions themselves (Section 3), and u = v = ν̃ = 0 and the inflow values of ρ, u, v, ν̃ hold exactly throughout the marching.

每个面仍是一个一维问题：Roe 通量

Every face is still a one-dimensional problem: the Roe flux

面的两侧各有一个状态，Roe 通量把它们的差分解成五道波：两道声波（速度 q̃ ∓ c̃）、熵波、剪切波与 ν̃ 波（速度都是 q̃），每道波按它速度的绝对值加耗散：

Each side of a face carries a state, and the Roe flux splits their difference into five waves: two acoustic waves (speeds q̃ ∓ c̃), and the entropy, shear and ν̃ waves (all at speed q̃); each wave is dissipated in proportion to the size of its speed:

roe(WL,WR,n)=∣n∣[12(F(WL)+F(WR))⋅n^−12∑k∣λk∣ αk rk]

~ 表示 Roe 平均（以 √ρ 加权）。ρν̃ 作为被动标量跟着质量通量走：它的耗散是 ν̃~ 乘质量方程的耗散，再加 |q̃|ρ̃Δν̃。按 NSM3D 的粘性流默认设置，残差里的特征值不做熵修正，所以 |q̃| = 0 处有一个不可导点（第 3 节列出它离当前解多远）。

The ~ marks a Roe average (weighted by √ρ). ρν̃ rides on the mass flux as a passive scalar: its dissipation is ν̃~ times the mass equation's, plus |q̃|ρ̃Δν̃. Following NSM3D's default for viscous flow, the residual's eigenvalues carry no entropy fix, so |q̃| = 0 is a point where the flux is not differentiable (Section 3 lists how far the solution is from it).

最小二乘重构：一阶与二阶

Least-squares reconstruction: first and second order

每个控制体的梯度来自无权最小二乘：

Each control volume's gradient comes from unweighted least squares:

Gi=Mi−1∑jdij (Wj−Wi),Mi=∑jdijdijT,dij=xj−xi

模板是边相邻的节点。壁面节点本身就是未知量（强条件下 u = v = ν̃ = 0），所以不需要额外的壁面点。二阶格式把中心值沿 r 外推：W+ = Wi + Gi·r，不加限制器（流动无激波）；一阶格式直接取 W+ = Wi。粘性通量与 SA 源项在两种格式里都用这些梯度，所以一阶／二阶开关只改变对流通量。

The stencil is the edge neighbours. A wall node is itself an unknown (u = v = ν̃ = 0 under the strong condition), so no extra wall point is needed. The second-order scheme extrapolates the centre value along r: W+ = Wi + Gi·r, without a limiter (the flow has no shocks); the first-order scheme takes W+ = Wi. The viscous flux and the SA source use these gradients in both schemes, so the first/second-order switch changes the convective flux only.

粘性通量：边上的修正平均梯度

The viscous flux: the corrected average gradient on the edge

SA 源项

The SA source

源项是每个控制体一个点的积分：Vi 乘以中心处的 S5。它读三样东西：中心的 W、最小二乘梯度 G（涡量、∇ν̃、∇ρ）、壁面距离 d。

The source is a one-point integral per control volume: Vi times S5 at the centre. It reads three things: the centre's W, the least-squares gradients G (the vorticity, ∇ν̃, ∇ρ), and the wall distance d.

第 3 节
Section 3

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈：强条件的无滑移绝热壁

Inner ring: the no-slip adiabatic wall, strongly

壁面节点的 ρu、ρv、ρν̃ 三行在所有循环之后被替换成 U 本身，于是解满足 u = v = ν̃ = 0。连续方程与能量方程不能替换，它们仍是通量平衡。壁面半面对它们没有贡献：质量通量 ρu·n = 0，能量通量为零（绝热，且应力不做功），所以面循环跳过壁面半面。

The ρu, ρv and ρν̃ rows of a wall node are replaced by U itself after all the loops, so the solution satisfies u = v = ν̃ = 0. The continuity and energy equations cannot be replaced; they remain flux balances. The wall half-faces add nothing to them: the mass flux ρu·n = 0 and the energy flux is zero (adiabatic, and the stress does no work), so the face loop skips the wall half-faces.

外圈：远场

Outer ring: the far field

来流节点的 ρ、ρu、ρv、ρν̃ 四行在循环之后替换成 U − U∞（强条件）；能量行保留，来流半面上的通量是本节点状态的对流通量减去本节点梯度给出的粘性通量，没有热流。出口半面：本节点的状态，压力换成 p∞ = 1，没有粘性通量。一个节点只要有一个来流半面就是来流节点，所以两个交界节点也是来流节点，它们的出口半面只进入能量行。

The ρ, ρu, ρv and ρν̃ rows of an inflow node are replaced by U − U∞ after the loops (a strong condition); the energy row stays, and the flux through an inflow half-face is the node state's convective flux minus the viscous flux of the node's gradients, with no heat flux. An outlet half-face: the node's own state with its pressure replaced by p∞ = 1, and nothing viscous. A node with one inflow half-face is an inflow node, so the two junction nodes are inflow nodes too, and their outlet half-faces enter their energy rows only.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 几遍循环：原始变量、梯度、面、源项、壁面行

The residual = passes: primitives, gradients, faces, source, wall rows

标量页面的残差是一个面循环。这里一个面要读两侧的梯度，而梯度要读整个模板的值，所以残差分成几遍：先把所有控制体的原始变量算出来，再算所有梯度，然后才是面通量和源项。

On the scalar pages the residual is one face loop. One symmetric sweep is a forward pass over i = 1…N followed by a backward pass over N…1; each control volume solves a 5×5 block equation with its neighbours' latest values. The pseudo-time step enters P (and so M), not R: it decides how the iteration travels and how fast, not where it ends. At convergence R(U*) = 0 whatever Δt, Â and the number of sweeps; the A = ∂R/∂U at the start of this section carries no time term either, so neither do the tangent and adjoint equations of Sections 7 and 8, A v = −f and ATψ = −g. The iterations that solve them in Sections 9 and 10 use this P as well, and there too it only sets how fast they converge. The marching also keeps the state positive: if a full step would change a control volume's ρ or T by more than 20%, or make its ν̃ negative, that control volume's step is shortened in proportion; this too changes only the path.

Appendix B: adjoint consistency

本页的来流处理是伴随不一致的；伴随一致的版本只在来流边界上与本页不同。本附录说明两者差在哪里、为什么这一点差别决定离散伴随在来流附近趋于什么，并在同一组网格上比较两者。

This page's inflow treatment is adjoint-inconsistent; the adjoint-consistent version differs from it on the inflow boundary only. This appendix explains the difference, why it decides what the discrete adjoint approaches near the inflow, and compares the two on the same meshes.

B.1 什么是伴随一致

B.1 What adjoint consistency means

两种版本的离散伴随 ψ 都满足 ATψ = −g，都是各自离散问题的精确导数（第 7–13 节的检验对两者都成立）。伴随一致性（也叫对偶一致性）问的是另一件事：把 ATψ = −g 本身看作一个离散格式，它是不是连续伴随方程连同边界条件的相容离散。若是，加密时 ψ 逐点趋于连续伴随 λ（换算到守恒方程的乘子，附录 A.2），边界附近也一样；若不是，ψ 在区域内部仍可以趋于 λ，在边界附近却趋于另一个问题的解。两种版本的区域内部格式、壁面与出口完全相同，连续问题也相同（附录 A.1），所以差别只可能来自来流边界。

The discrete adjoints ψ of both versions satisfy ATψ = −g and are the exact derivatives of their discrete problems (the checks of Sections 7–13 hold for both). Adjoint consistency (also called dual consistency) asks something else: read ATψ = −g itself as a discrete scheme; is it a consistent discretisation of the continuous adjoint equations together with their boundary conditions? If it is, ψ approaches the continuous adjoint λ (mapped to the multipliers of the conservative equations, Appendix A.2) point by point under refinement, next to the boundary as well; if it is not, ψ can still approach λ inside the domain, but next to the boundary it approaches the solution of a different problem. The two versions share the interior scheme, the wall and the outlet, and the continuous problem (Appendix A.1), so any difference comes from the inflow boundary.

B.2 离散伴随的边界条件从哪里来

B.2 Where the discrete adjoint's boundary conditions come from

离散伴随的边界条件不是另外写的：AT 是原始格式的转置，边界上的做法经转置自动变成伴随的边界条件。有两条规则：

The discrete adjoint's boundary conditions are not written separately: AT is the transpose of the primal scheme, and what the scheme does on the boundary turns into the adjoint's boundary conditions by that transpose. Two rules:

  • 被替换的行。替换后的一行 Ri,k = Ui,k − (给定值) 只依赖节点自己的 Ui,k（一致版本的来流行还依赖 Ui,ρ）。它的乘子 ψi,k 只出现在自己那一列的伴随方程里，是一个反作用；其余伴随方程看它，就像 ψi,k = 0。所以替换一行，等于给其余的离散伴随加上“这个分量在这个节点为零”。Replaced rows. A replaced row Ri,k = Ui,k − (given value) depends on the node's own Ui,k only (the consistent version's inflow rows on Ui,ρ as well). Its multiplier ψi,k appears in its own column's adjoint equation only and is a reaction; every other adjoint equation sees it as if ψi,k = 0. So replacing a row imposes on the rest of the discrete adjoint “this component is zero at this node”.

B.3 原来的来流处理为什么伴随不一致

B.3 Why the original inflow treatment is adjoint-inconsistent

格点格式原来把来流节点的 ρ、ρu、ρv、ρν̃ 四行替换成 U − U∞，能量行保留。按规则 1，其余伴随方程在来流节点上看到 ψρ = ψρu = ψρv = ψρν̃ = 0，也就是说离散伴随在来流上满足

The node scheme originally replaced an inflow node's ρ, ρu, ρv and ρν̃ rows by U − U∞ and kept its energy row. By rule 1 every other adjoint equation sees ψρ = ψρu = ψρv = ψρν̃ = 0 at an inflow node: on the inflow the discrete adjoint satisfies

α=0,β=0,ϕ=0
  • φ = 0 与连续条件相同。φ = 0 agrees with the continuous condition.
  • β = 0 与 β = −ζu 不同，而 ζ 在来流上并不为零（第 4 级网格上 180° 顶点处 λ 的 ζ = −35.2）。能量行虽然保留，它在来流半面上的功 u·τn 用的是节点自己梯度给出的牵引力，不是动量行的反作用，所以 A.3 里组成 β + ζu 的两项在离散里凑不到一起。β = 0 differs from β = −ζu, and ζ is not zero on the inflow (λ's ζ = −35.2 at the 180° vertex on level 4). The energy row is kept, but the work u·τn on its inflow half-faces uses the traction of the node's own gradients, not the momentum rows' reaction, so the two terms that form β + ζu in A.3 never meet in the discrete equations.
  • α = 0 是连续伴随没有的条件：α 在伴随的出口被钉住。这正是原始问题在出口多给一个条件的伴随版。结果是来流附近根本没有 A.3 的那一层：第 4 级 180° 网格线上离来流 0.055 的节点，ψρ = 12.3，λ 的对应值是 85.0（B.5 的剖面图）。α = 0 is a condition the continuous adjoint does not have: α is pinned at the adjoint's outlet. This is the adjoint version of giving the primal problem one condition too many at an outlet. The result is that the layer of A.3 is missing next to the inflow altogether: on level 4, at the node 0.055 from the inflow on the 180° mesh line, ψρ = 12.3 where λ gives 85.0 (the profiles of B.5).

B.6 两个侧面：来流几乎与边界平行

B.6 The two side faces: inflow almost along the boundary

远场八边形上的四个来流面并不一样：正面（135°–225°）|u∞·n| = 0.210–0.226，两个侧面（90°–135°、225°–270°）只有 0.071–0.109。侧面上两种版本的原始解不同。原处理把来流节点的 ρ 直接取为 ρ∞ = 1；一致的版本只给质量通量，侧面上边界节点的 ρ 最低 0.988、0.974、0.968、0.967（第 1、2、3、4 级），正面上都在 1 的 0.3% 以内。在 96° 处沿法向进入区域 0.36：原处理 ρ 从 1 降到 0.970、T 从 1.027 升到 1.056；一致的版本 ρ 在 0.962–0.969 之间，T 几乎不变。两者从第 3 级到第 4 级都不再变化。

The four inflow faces of the far octagon are not alike: on the front (135°–225°) |u∞·n| = 0.210–0.226, on the two side faces (90°–135° and 225°–270°) only 0.071–0.109. On the side faces the two versions' primal solutions differ. The original sets the inflow nodes' ρ to ρ∞ = 1 directly; the consistent version gives the mass flux only, and on the side faces its boundary nodes' ρ drops to 0.988, 0.974, 0.968, 0.967 (levels 1, 2, 3, 4), while on the front all stay within 0.3% of 1. Going 0.36 into the domain along the normal at 96°: the original's ρ falls from 1 to 0.970 while T rises from 1.027 to 1.056; the consistent version's ρ stays between 0.962 and 0.969 with T nearly constant. Neither changes any more from level 3 to level 4.

原因在连续问题里：把定常 Navier–Stokes 方程沿来流边界的法向线性化，亚声速来流上恰有一个离开边界衰减的粘性模态，厚度约 4μe|un|/(3p)：本页的数值是侧面 0.0022–0.0034、正面 0.0066–0.0071。在这一层里 ρ 变化而 ρun 与 T 几乎不变；本页任何一级网格都分辨不了它（最细的网格第 4 级，贴来流边界的第一排节点离边界 0.045）。于是层外的解看到的边界条件取决于离散怎样跨过这一层：原处理把 ρ = ρ∞ 放在边界节点上，一致的版本只给跨层不变的质量通量。同一个线性化的另一个模态朝区域内增长，厚度 0.15–0.47：它就是连续伴随在来流边界上的那一层（A.3）。附录 A 的连续伴随按光滑解推导，没有这一薄层；两种版本都在同一组网格上与它比较，结论只针对这些网格上的解与伴随。

The cause is in the continuous problem: linearise the steady Navier–Stokes equations along the normal of the inflow boundary, and a subsonic inflow has exactly one viscous mode that decays away from the boundary, of thickness about 4μe|un|/(3p): here 0.0022–0.0034 on the side faces and 0.0066–0.0071 on the front. Across this layer ρ changes while ρun and T stay nearly constant, and no mesh of this page resolves it (on the finest mesh, level 4, the first row of nodes lies 0.045 from the inflow boundary). So what boundary condition the solution outside the layer sees depends on how the discretisation steps across it: the original puts ρ = ρ∞ on the boundary node, the consistent version gives only the mass flux, which does not change across the layer. The same linearisation's other mode grows into the domain, with thickness 0.15–0.47: it is the continuous adjoint's layer at the inflow boundary (A.3). The continuous adjoint of Appendix A is derived for a smooth solution and has no thin layer; both versions are compared with it on the same meshes, and the conclusions are about the solutions and adjoints on these meshes.
