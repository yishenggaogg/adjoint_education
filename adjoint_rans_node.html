二维 RANS–SA 方程的离散伴随：格点格式

The discrete adjoint of 2-D RANS with Spalart–Allmaras: node-centred

算例是二维对流页面的同一张环形混合网格：内圈是物面，外圈是远场，贴壁一层四边形、外层三角形。方程换成可压缩 RANS 与标准 Spalart–Allmaras 湍流模型：每个节点有 5 个未知量 (ρ, ρu, ρv, ρE, ρν̃)。物面是无滑移、绝热壁面（强条件，替换壁面节点的三行残差），远场用 Roe 通量对来流状态。空间离散：Roe 通量、无权最小二乘重构（不加限制器）、SU2 式的边上修正平均梯度粘性通量；求解：伪时间 + 对称块 Gauss–Seidel，近似 Jacobian 是一阶 Roe 加薄剪切层。本页只做离散伴随：先把残差拆成几遍循环，再把同样的循环改写成 matrix-free 前向（A v）和 matrix-free 伴随（ATw），最后用前向、复数步长与差分三种独立办法核对伴随给出的导数。页首可以切换一阶／二阶空间离散，下面所有演示都随之重算。

The test case is the same annular hybrid mesh as the two-dimensional advection pages: the body on the inner ring, the far field on the outer, quadrilaterals in the layer against the wall and triangles outside. The equations become compressible RANS with the standard Spalart–Allmaras model: every node carries 5 unknowns (ρ, ρu, ρv, ρE, ρν̃). The body is a no-slip, adiabatic wall (a strong condition, replacing three rows of each wall node's residual), and the far field takes the Roe flux against the free stream. Spatial discretisation: the Roe flux, unweighted least-squares reconstruction (no limiter), and SU2's corrected average gradient on the edges for the viscous flux; the solver: pseudo-time with symmetric block Gauss–Seidel, on an approximate Jacobian made of first-order Roe and the thin shear layer. This page does the discrete adjoint only: it splits the residual into passes, rewrites the same passes as the matrix-free tangent (A v) and the matrix-free adjoint (ATw), and checks the adjoint's derivatives three independent ways: the tangent, the complex step, and finite differences. The header switches between first- and second-order spatial discretisation, and every demo below is recomputed.

第 1 节
Section 1

网格：对偶控制体、对偶面与壁面距离

Mesh: dual control volumes, dual faces and wall distance

网格与二维对流页面完全相同，编号也相同：节点逐圈从壁面向外，节点逐圈编号，面按“内部、壁面、远场”排序。未知量放在节点上：每个节点的控制体是中位对偶，由相邻单元的边中点与单元中心围成。区别在于每个控制体现在有 5 个未知量，而不是 1 个。

The mesh is exactly that of the two-dimensional advection page, with the same numbering: nodes ring by ring from the wall outwards, nodes ring by ring, faces sorted interior, wall, far field. The unknowns live at the nodes: each node's control volume is its median dual, bounded by the edge midpoints and the centres of the cells around it. The difference is that every control volume now carries 5 unknowns instead of 1.

一个面循环要读的几何量都画在下图里。和标量页面相比多了两样：重构方向 r（从控制体中心到面点，二阶格式用它把中心值外推到面上）与两侧中心的连线 d（粘性通量沿它取两点差分）。

The geometric quantities a face loop reads are all in the figure below. Compared with the scalar pages there are two more: the reconstruction direction r (from a control-volume centre to the face point; the second-order scheme extrapolates the centre value along it) and the line d between the two centres (the viscous flux takes its two-point difference along it).

壁面距离

The wall distance

SA 模型的耗散项与 (ν̃/d)² 成正比，d 是到壁面的距离。本页的 di 是控制体中心到离散壁面多边形的精确欧氏距离：对每条壁面边求点到线段的距离，取最小。它是坐标的函数，所以在第 11 节的几何导数里也要求导。

The SA model's destruction term goes as (ν̃/d)², with d the distance to the wall. On this page di is the exact Euclidean distance from the control-volume centre to the discrete wall polygon: the distance from the point to each wall edge, and the smallest. It is a function of the coordinates, so the geometric derivatives of Section 11 differentiate it too.

The equations and the face flux

The flow on this page: M = 0.2, 5° angle of attack, μref = 0.002 (Re ≈ 130 on the body's chord of 1.1), and ν̃∞ = 3ν∞ in the far field. A mesh like this cannot resolve a real high-Reynolds boundary layer; the aim is a discrete problem that exercises every branch of the model and still converges to round-off: the modified branch of S̃, the cap on r, and μt/μ up to 0.34.

有限体积形式

The finite-volume form

对每个控制体积分，散度变成面上的通量和：

Integrated over each control volume, the divergence becomes a sum of face fluxes:

Ri(U)=∑f∈∂Ωi± hf−ViSi=0,hf=roe(WL+,WR+,nf)⏟convective−visc(W,G,f)⏟viscous

面通量 hf 从 L 流向 R：对 L 加、对 R 减，保证守恒。W = (ρ, u, v, T, ν̃) 是原始变量，G 是它们的最小二乘梯度，W+ 是重构到面上的状态。

The face flux hf runs from L to R: added to L and subtracted from R, which makes the scheme conservative. W = (ρ, u, v, T, ν̃) are the primitive variables, G their least-squares gradients, and W+ the state reconstructed to the face.

每个面仍是一个一维问题：Roe 通量

Every face is still a one-dimensional problem: the Roe flux

面的两侧各有一个状态，Roe 通量把它们的差分解成五道波：两道声波（速度 q̃ ∓ c̃）、熵波、剪切波与 ν̃ 波（速度都是 q̃），每道波按它速度的绝对值加耗散：

Each side of a face carries a state, and the Roe flux splits their difference into five waves: two acoustic waves (speeds q̃ ∓ c̃), and the entropy, shear and ν̃ waves (all at speed q̃); each wave is dissipated in proportion to the size of its speed:

roe(WL,WR,n)=∣n∣[12(F(WL)+F(WR))⋅n^−12∑k∣λk∣ αk rk]

~ 表示 Roe 平均（以 √ρ 加权）。ρν̃ 作为被动标量跟着质量通量走：它的耗散是 ν̃~ 乘质量方程的耗散，再加 |q̃|ρ̃Δν̃。按 NSM3D 的粘性流默认设置，残差里的特征值不做熵修正，所以 |q̃| = 0 处有一个不可导点（第 3 节列出它离当前解多远）。

The ~ marks a Roe average (weighted by √ρ). ρν̃ rides on the mass flux as a passive scalar: its dissipation is ν̃~ times the mass equation's, plus |q̃|ρ̃Δν̃. Following NSM3D's default for viscous flow, the residual's eigenvalues carry no entropy fix, so |q̃| = 0 is a point where the flux is not differentiable (Section 3 lists how far the solution is from it).

最小二乘重构：一阶与二阶

Least-squares reconstruction: first and second order

每个控制体的梯度来自无权最小二乘：

Each control volume's gradient comes from unweighted least squares:

Gi=Mi−1∑jdij (Wj−Wi),Mi=∑jdijdijT,dij=xj−xi

模板是边相邻的节点。壁面节点本身就是未知量（强条件下 u = v = ν̃ = 0），所以不需要额外的壁面点。二阶格式把中心值沿 r 外推：W+ = Wi + Gi·r，不加限制器（流动无激波）；一阶格式直接取 W+ = Wi。粘性通量与 SA 源项在两种格式里都用这些梯度，所以一阶／二阶开关只改变对流通量。

The stencil is the edge neighbours. A wall node is itself an unknown (u = v = ν̃ = 0 under the strong condition), so no extra wall point is needed. The second-order scheme extrapolates the centre value along r: W+ = Wi + Gi·r, without a limiter (the flow has no shocks); the first-order scheme takes W+ = Wi. The viscous flux and the SA source use these gradients in both schemes, so the first/second-order switch changes the convective flux only.

粘性通量：边上的修正平均梯度

The viscous flux: the corrected average gradient on the edge

SA 源项

The SA source

源项是每个控制体一个点的积分：Vi 乘以中心处的 S5。它读三样东西：中心的 W、最小二乘梯度 G（涡量、∇ν̃、∇ρ）、壁面距离 d。

The source is a one-point integral per control volume: Vi times S5 at the centre. It reads three things: the centre's W, the least-squares gradients G (the vorticity, ∇ν̃, ∇ρ), and the wall distance d.

第 3 节
Section 3

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈：强条件的无滑移绝热壁

Inner ring: the no-slip adiabatic wall, strongly

壁面节点的 ρu、ρv、ρν̃ 三行在所有循环之后被替换成 U 本身，于是解满足 u = v = ν̃ = 0。连续方程与能量方程不能替换，它们仍是通量平衡。壁面半面对它们没有贡献：质量通量 ρu·n = 0，能量通量为零（绝热，且应力不做功），所以面循环跳过壁面半面。

The ρu, ρv and ρν̃ rows of a wall node are replaced by U itself after all the loops, so the solution satisfies u = v = ν̃ = 0. The continuity and energy equations cannot be replaced; they remain flux balances. The wall half-faces add nothing to them: the mass flux ρu·n = 0 and the energy flux is zero (adiabatic, and the stress does no work), so the face loop skips the wall half-faces.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 几遍循环：原始变量、梯度、面、源项、壁面行

The residual = passes: primitives, gradients, faces, source, wall rows

标量页面的残差是一个面循环。这里一个面要读两侧的梯度，而梯度要读整个模板的值，所以残差分成几遍：先把所有控制体的原始变量算出来，再算所有梯度，然后才是面通量和源项。

On the scalar pages the residual is one face loop. Here a face reads the gradients on both sides, and a gradient reads the values over its whole stencil, so the residual comes in passes: first the primitive variables of every control volume, then every gradient, and only then the face fluxes and the source.

W=π(U),G=ΓW,R(U)=F(W,G)−S(W,G,d)(then the wall rows)

π 在每个控制体上独立地把守恒变量换成原始变量；Γ 是线性的最小二乘算子（权重只依赖几何）；F 是面通量的装配，S 是源项。下面的演示逐句执行这几遍：538 步，可以用上方的按钮直接跳到某一遍。

π turns each control volume's conserved variables into primitives, independently; Γ is the linear least-squares operator (its weights depend on the geometry only); F assembles the face fluxes and S the source. 最后一步 max|R| 在舍入误差量级：U* 是收敛解。注意第 4 遍：壁面节点的三行在所有循环之后才被替换，面循环写进这些行的值全部被覆盖。第 8 节的伴随要先处理这件事。

At the last step max|R| is at round-off: U* is the converged solution. Note pass 4: the three rows of each wall node are replaced after all the loops, overwriting whatever the face loop wrote there. The adjoint of Section 8 has to deal with this first.

第 5 节
Section 5

求解：Jacobian、近似 Jacobian 与 Gauss–Seidel

The solve: the Jacobian, the approximate Jacobian and Gauss–Seidel

从残差到 Jacobian

From the residual to the Jacobian

A = ∂R/∂U 是 120×120 的矩阵，由 5×5 的块组成。一个面的通量读两侧的值与梯度，梯度又读整个模板，所以 A 的一行不只连到邻居，还连到邻居的邻居：264 个非零块。

A = ∂R/∂U is a 120×120 matrix of 5×5 blocks. A face's flux reads the values and gradients on both sides, and a gradient reads its whole stencil, so a row of A reaches past the neighbours to the neighbours' neighbours: 264 non-zero blocks.

近似 Jacobian：一阶 Roe 加薄剪切层

The approximate Jacobian: first-order Roe plus the thin shear layer

Gauss–Seidel 用的不是 A，而是一个只连接面两侧的近似 P：对流部分是一阶 Roe（|Ã| 冻结在两侧中心状态的 Roe 平均处），粘性部分是薄剪切层（只保留修正梯度沿 d 的两点差分，系数冻结）：

Gauss–Seidel does not use A but an approximation P that couples only the two sides of each face: first-order Roe for the convective part (|Ã| frozen at the Roe average of the two centre states), and the thin shear layer for the viscous part (only the two-point difference along d of the corrected gradient, with frozen coefficients):

∂hf∂UL≈12(A(UL)+∣A~∣)∣n∣+θfMv∂W∂U∣L∂hf∂UR≈12(A(UR)−∣A~∣)∣n∣−θfMv∂W∂U∣R,θf=nf⋅d∣d∣2

Mv 是薄剪切层矩阵：动量行 μe(I + ⅓n̂n̂T)，能量行加上功与导热 ke，ν̃ 行 ρa。对角块再加上 SA 源项对 ρν̃ 的导数中使对角增强的那一部分（耗散），以及伪时间项 V/Δt。壁面节点的三行是单位行。P 有 120 个非零块。

Mv is the thin-shear-layer matrix: the momentum rows μe(I + ⅓n̂n̂T), the energy row adds the work and the conduction ke, and the ν̃ row ρa. The diagonal blocks also receive the part of the SA source's derivative with respect to ρν̃ that strengthens the diagonal (the destruction), and the pseudo-time term V/Δt. The three rows of a wall node are unit rows. P has 120 non-zero blocks.

R(U) = 0 怎样解

How R(U) = 0 is solved

Uk+1=Uk−M R(Uk),M=two symmetric block Gauss–Seidel sweeps on P=VΔt+A~

一次对称扫描是 i = 1…N 的前扫接 N…1 的回扫；每个控制体解一个 5×5 的块方程，用到的是邻居最新的值。CFL 从 5 起按残差下降放大，上限 200。一个 5×5 块的求逆就是全部的“线性代数”，不形成、不分解大矩阵。

One symmetric sweep is a forward pass over i = 1…N followed by a backward pass over N…1; each control volume solves a 5×5 block equation with its neighbours' latest values. The CFL number starts at 5 and grows as the residual falls, capped at 200. Inverting a 5×5 block is all the “linear algebra” there is: no large matrix is formed or factorised.

The state of the test case

目标函数：阻力系数

The objective: the drag coefficient

CD=F⋅eDq∞,eD=(cos⁡α,sin⁡α),q∞=12ρ∞U∞2=0.7M2

F 是流体作用在物体上的力。格点页上壁面节点的动量行被替换了，力要从被替换之前的动量残差里取出来：一个壁面节点经内部对偶面流出的动量，正是物体经壁面半面对它的作用力的反作用。所以 F = −Σ壁面节点 Ri动量（替换前）。这是与离散动量守恒完全一致的“反作用力”。

F is the force the fluid exerts on the body. On the node page the wall nodes' momentum rows are replaced, so the force has to come from the momentum residual before the replacement: the momentum a wall node sends out through its interior dual faces is exactly the reaction to the force the body exerts on it through its wall half-faces. So F = −Σwall nodes Rimomentum (before replacement). This is the consistent “reaction force”: it agrees exactly with the discrete momentum balance.

对局部核求导：局部磁带

Differentiating the local kernels: local tapes

残差由三种核拼成：原始变量 π（每个控制体 5 进 5 出）、面核（内部对偶面读两侧的 W 与 G，共 30 个输入、5 个输出）、源项核（15 进 1 出）。每个核只写一次，同一份代码可以用实数、复数或“局部磁带”运行。磁带记下核里每个运算的值与它对一两个操作数的偏导数；向前扫一遍得到方向导数，向后扫一遍得到转置。本页在 U* 处对每个核录一次磁带（一个内部面最多 1224 个节点），反向扫 5 次得到它的 5×30 局部 Jacobian Jf。

The residual is built from three kinds of kernel: the primitives π (5 in, 5 out per control volume), the face kernel (an interior dual face reads W and G on both sides: 30 inputs, 5 outputs), and the source kernel (15 in, 1 out). Each kernel is written once, and the same code runs on real numbers, on complex numbers, or on a “local tape”. The tape records every operation's value and its partial derivatives with respect to its one or two operands; a forward sweep gives a directional derivative, a reverse sweep the transpose. This page tapes each kernel once at U* (at most 1224 nodes for an interior face) and sweeps it backwards 5 times for its 5×30 local Jacobian Jf.

Matrix-free adjoint mode: computing Aᵀw

转置一个乘积要把因子倒过来、每个因子转置：

Transposing a product reverses the factors and transposes each:

Structural checks that cannot fail

三个恒等式只依赖离散格式的不变性，与流动无关：整体平移不改变任何东西（Σ∂CD/∂xi = 0）；整体旋转等价于反向转动来流（Σ(x∂/∂y − y∂/∂x)CD = −∂CD/∂α）；长度与 μref 同时放大 λ 倍，CD（以固定参考长度 1 归一）也放大 λ 倍（ΣX·∂CD/∂X + μref∂CD/∂μref = CD）。实测残差：平移 8.9×10−15、旋转 2.2×10−14、伸缩 2.1×10−14。

The complex step

怎样实现

How to implement it

同一份核代码换上复数算术：加减乘直接算，除法用 Smith 算法（不会溢出，虚部很小时实部与实数除法逐位相同），sqrt、exp、实数次幂取主值；|x|、min、max 与所有分支（S̃ 的两支、r 的截断、壁面距离的最近边与垂足位置、Roe 的 |λ|）都只看实部。h = 10−30 时虚部比实部小约 30 个数量级，两个虚部的乘积小约 60 个数量级，永远碰不到实部的最后一位：每个实部都与实数代码逐位相同。

The same kernel code on complex arithmetic: addition, subtraction and multiplication as they are; division by Smith's algorithm (it never overflows, and with tiny imaginary parts its real part is bit for bit the real division); sqrt, exp and real powers on their principal branches; |x|, min, max and every branch (the two branches of S̃, the cap on r, the nearest wall edge and the foot position, Roe's |λ|) read the real part only. With h = 10−30 the imaginary parts stay about 30 orders of magnitude below the real ones and a product of two imaginary parts about 60, which never reaches the last bit of a real part: every real part is bit for bit the real code's.

  • 最小二乘让 A 的一行伸到邻居的邻居；伴随里对应的是梯度的权重回到整个模板。近似 Jacobian 只要一阶连接，Gauss–Seidel 与它的转置都只用 P。The least squares makes a row of A reach the neighbours' neighbours; in the adjoint this is a gradient's weight returning to its whole stencil. The approximate Jacobian needs first-order coupling only, and Gauss–Seidel and its transpose use P alone.
  • 对称 Gauss–Seidel 的转置就是在 PT 上的同一个算法：前向与伴随在每一步给出同一个导数估计，三个迭代共用一个收敛速率。The transpose of symmetric Gauss–Seidel is the same algorithm on PT: the tangent and the adjoint give the same derivative estimate at every iteration, and the three iterations share one rate.
  • 导数在当前解所在的分支上取；本页的解离所有分支点都足够远（第 3 节）。复数步长与伴随的相对差：流动参数 1.4×10−14，48 个坐标（按范数）3.8×10−15。Derivatives are taken on the branch the solution sits on; this page's solution is far enough from every switch (Section 3).
