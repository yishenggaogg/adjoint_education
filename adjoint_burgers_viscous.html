01 · PDE

明确方程、两端边界和目标Specify the equation, both boundaries and the objective

(12u2−νux)x=s(x,a,ϕ,ν),x∈(0,1),ν>0.\bigl(\tfrac12 u^2-\nu u_x\bigr)_x=s(x,a,\phi,\nu),\qquad x\in(0,1),\quad \nu>0.
u∗(x)=1+a sin⁡ ⁣(2π(x−ϕ)),u(0)=g0=u∗(0),u(1)=g1=u∗(1).u_*(x)=1+a\,\sin\!\bigl(2\pi(x-\phi)\bigr),\qquad u(0)=g_0=u_*(0),\quad u(1)=g_1=u_*(1).
q∗(x)=12u∗2−νu∗,x,s=q∗,x=u∗u∗,x−νu∗,xx.q_*(x)=\tfrac12 u_*^2-\nu u_{*,x},\qquad s=q_{*,x}=u_*u_{*,x}-\nu u_{*,xx}.

默认 a=0.2、φ=0.07、ν=0.01。参数改变时，制造源和解析 Dirichlet 数据同步改变；数值未知量仍由残差求解。固定正 ν 表示每次参数导数和网格加密都使用同一个正值，而不是令 ν 随网格趋于零。Defaults are a=0.2, φ=0.07 and ν=0.01. Changing a parameter changes both the manufactured source and analytic Dirichlet data; numerical unknowns are still solved from residuals. Fixed positive ν means using one positive value throughout a parameter differentiation or refinement study, without sending ν to zero with the mesh.

J(u)=∫01(1+x)u(x) dx,Jh(U)=h∑i=0N−1(1+xi)Ui.J(u)=\int_0^1(1+x)u(x)\,dx,\qquad J_h(U)=h\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}(1+x_i)U_i.
J∗=32−a cos⁡(2πϕ)2π,∂aJ∗=−cos⁡(2πϕ)2π,∂ϕJ∗=a sin⁡(2πϕ),∂νJ∗=0.J_*=\tfrac32-\frac{a\,\cos(2\pi\phi)}{2\pi},\quad \partial_aJ_*=-\frac{\cos(2\pi\phi)}{2\pi},\quad \partial_\phi J_*=a\,\sin(2\pi\phi),\quad \partial_\nu J_*=0.

连续目标使用真实积分；离散目标使用单元中心权重与未知的单元平均。因此有限网格目标和梯度允许有空间离散误差。ν 改变制造源中的黏性项，但 u* 本身不依赖 ν，故该制造族的连续 ν 梯度为零。The continuous objective is a true integral; the discrete objective uses centre weights and unknown cell averages. Finite-grid objectives and gradients therefore have spatial errors. Varying ν changes the viscous part of the manufactured source, while u* itself is independent of ν, so this family's continuous ν gradient is zero.

02 · CONTINUOUS

连续切向、伴随和不能丢的边界项Continuous tangent, adjoint and the required boundary term

对 p=a 或 φ，在固定正 ν 下令 v=∂p u。边界也有参数导数。For p=a or φ at fixed positive ν, let v=∂p u. Boundary data also have parameter derivatives.

(uv−νvx)x=sp,v(0)=g0,p,v(1)=g1,p.(uv-\nu v_x)_x=s_p,\qquad v(0)=g_{0,p},\quad v(1)=g_{1,p}.
ℒ(u,p,λ)=J(u)+∫01λ[(12u2−νux)x−s]dx.\mathcal L(u,p,\lambda)=J(u)+\int_0^1\lambda\bigl[(\tfrac12u^2-\nu u_x)_x-s\bigr]dx.
Ju[v]+∫01λ(uv−νvx)xdx=∫01[(1+x)−uλx−νλxx]v dx+ν[λxv]01,λ(0)=λ(1)=0.J_u[v]+\int_0^1\lambda(uv-\nu v_x)_x dx=\int_0^1\bigl[(1+x)-u\lambda_x-\nu\lambda_{xx}\bigr]v\,dx+\nu[\lambda_xv]_0^1,\quad \lambda(0)=\lambda(1)=0.

这里 J_u[v] 表示目标对状态的方向导数。两次分部积分后，λ=0 消去 v_x 的边界项，λ_x 与已知边界扰动相乘的项仍保留。Here J_u[v] denotes the objective's state directional derivative. After two integrations by parts, λ=0 removes the v_x boundary term, while the λ_x term multiplying the prescribed boundary perturbation remains.

−uλx−νλxx=−(1+x),λ(0)=λ(1)=0.-u\lambda_x-\nu\lambda_{xx}=-(1+x),\qquad \lambda(0)=\lambda(1)=0.
dJdp=−∫01λsp dx+ν[λxgp]01,p∈{a,ϕ}.\frac{dJ}{dp}=-\int_0^1\lambda s_p\,dx+\nu[\lambda_x g_p]_0^1,\qquad p\in\{a,\phi\}.

源项和边界必须一起求导。只计算 −∫λs_p 会漏掉 ν[λ_xg_p]。Python 连续参考通过边值求解计算 λ，再将源积分与边界贡献相加。Differentiate source and boundaries together. Using only −∫λs_p omits ν[λ_xg_p]. The Python continuous reference solves the adjoint boundary-value problem and adds source-integral and boundary contributions.

若 p=ν，还必须对物理黏性通量本身求导，不能直接套用上一行。该制造族 g_ν=0、s_ν=−u*xx。For p=ν, also differentiate the physical viscous flux itself. The preceding formula alone is insufficient. Here g_ν=0 and s_ν=−u*xx.

(uvν−νvν,x)x=sν+uxx,vν(0)=vν(1)=0.(uv_\nu-\nu v_{\nu,x})_x=s_\nu+u_{xx},\qquad v_\nu(0)=v_\nu(1)=0.
dJdν=−∫01λ(sν+uxx) dx=∫01λxux dx−∫01λsν dx=0at u=u∗.\frac{dJ}{d\nu}=-\int_0^1\lambda(s_\nu+u_{xx})\,dx=\int_0^1\lambda_xu_x\,dx-\int_0^1\lambda s_\nu\,dx=0\quad\text{at }u=u_*.

连续伴随的数值边值解属于独立参考计算，不能用它的数值残差替代连续 PDE 的严格证明。A numerical continuous-adjoint boundary-value solution is an independent reference computation; its numerical residual does not replace a rigorous PDE proof.

03 · FINITE VOLUME

与 D.3 和 Python 完全相同的离散残差The same discrete residual as D.3 and Python

h=N−1,xi=(i+12)h,Ui≈h−1∫ih(i+1)hu(x) dx,i=0,…,N−1.h=N^{-1},\qquad x_i=(i+\tfrac12)h,\qquad U_i\approx h^{-1}\int_{ih}^{(i+1)h}u(x)\,dx,\quad i=0,\ldots,N-1.
u‾∗,i=1+asin⁡(πh)πhsin⁡ ⁣(2π(xi−ϕ)).\overline u_{*,i}=1+a\frac{\sin(\pi h)}{\pi h}\sin\!\bigl(2\pi(x_i-\phi)\bigr).

误差始终比较单元平均与精确单元平均，不把单元中心点值当作精确平均。内部单元的左右差为下式；首末单元斜率恒为零。Errors compare cell averages with exact cell averages, without treating point samples as exact averages. Internal left/right differences are defined below; slopes in the first and last cells are always zero.

di−=Ui−Ui−1,di+=Ui+1−Ui,σ0=σN−1=0.d_i^-=U_i-U_{i-1},\quad d_i^+=U_{i+1}-U_i,\qquad \sigma_0=\sigma_{N-1}=0.
σi={0first,(di−+di+)/2none,di−di+(di−+di+)(di−)2+(di+)2+ϵ2smooth,ϵ=0.01.\sigma_i=\begin{cases}0&\text{first},\\(d_i^-+d_i^+)/2&\text{none},\\\dfrac{d_i^-d_i^+(d_i^-+d_i^+)}{(d_i^-)^2+(d_i^+)^2+\epsilon^2}&\text{smooth},\end{cases}\qquad \epsilon=0.01.

none 是无限幅线性重构，smooth 是固定 ε>0 的光滑重构。这里选择这三种口径以避免 minmod 切换点导数歧义；没有把 minmod 的固定选支导数解释为跨切换点的真导数。none is unlimited linear reconstruction; smooth uses a fixed ε>0 smooth reconstruction. These three choices avoid derivative ambiguity at minmod switches; a fixed minmod branch derivative is not being claimed as a true derivative across switches.

Lf={g0f=0,Uf−1+σf−1/21≤f≤N,Rf={Uf−σf/20≤f<N,g1f=N.L_f=\begin{cases}g_0&f=0,\\U_{f-1}+\sigma_{f-1}/2&1\le f\le N,\end{cases}\qquad R_f=\begin{cases}U_f-\sigma_f/2&0\le f<N,\\g_1&f=N.\end{cases}
ℓf={g0f=0,Uf−11≤f≤N,rf={Uf0≤f<N,g1f=N,df={h/2f∈{0,N},h0<f<N.\ell_f=\begin{cases}g_0&f=0,\\U_{f-1}&1\le f\le N,\end{cases}\qquad r_f=\begin{cases}U_f&0\le f<N,\\g_1&f=N,\end{cases}\qquad d_f=\begin{cases}h/2&f\in\{0,N\},\\h&0<f<N.\end{cases}
Hf=14(Lf2+Rf2)−α2(Rf−Lf)−νdf(rf−ℓf),α=2.H_f=\tfrac14(L_f^2+R_f^2)-\frac\alpha2(R_f-L_f)-\frac\nu{d_f}(r_f-\ell_f),\qquad \alpha=2.

对流使用重构值；黏性梯度使用原始单元平均之差。固定 α=2 不随状态或参数更新，所以没有隐藏的波速求导。两端扩散距离为 h/2。Convection uses reconstructed states; viscous gradients use differences of raw cell averages. Fixed α=2 does not depend on states or parameters, so no wave-speed derivative is hidden. Boundary diffusion distances are h/2.

Si=∫ih(i+1)hs dx=q∗((i+1)h)−q∗(ih),ℛi=Hi+1−Hi−Si=0.S_i=\int_{ih}^{(i+1)h}s\,dx=q_*((i+1)h)-q_*(ih),\qquad \mathcal R_i=H_{i+1}-H_i-S_i=0.

这是单元积分残差，没有除以 h。残差密度是 R/h，两种数值大小不能混用。Newton 的初值来自 u* 的中心点样本，但每一个最终 U 都来自非线性残差求解。This is an integrated cell residual, without division by h. The residual density is R/h; their numerical magnitudes differ. Newton starts from centre samples of u*, while every final U is obtained by solving the nonlinear residual.

04 · DIFFERENTIATE THE ROOT

同一个残差的切向与转置伴随Tangent and transpose adjoint of one residual

A=ℛU,Av=−ℛp δp,ATψ=−JUT,δJ=Jpδp+ψTℛpδp.A=\mathcal R_U,\qquad Av=-\mathcal R_p\,\delta p,\qquad A^T\psi=-J_U^T,\qquad \delta J=J_p\delta p+\psi^T\mathcal R_p\delta p.
(JU)i=h(1+xi),Jp=0 at fixed U,dJhdp=ψTℛp=JUv.(J_U)_i=h(1+x_i),\qquad J_p=0\ \text{at fixed }U,\qquad \frac{dJ_h}{dp}=\psi^T\mathcal R_p=J_Uv.

R_p 包含源积分、两个边界值和相关面通量的全部参数导数。Python 的 ν 方向同时对离散黏性通量和制造源求导。浏览器仅在选定 ν 下对 a、φ 求导。R_p includes derivatives of source integrals, both boundary values and all affected face fluxes. Python's ν direction differentiates both the discrete viscous flux and the manufactured source. The browser differentiates a and φ at the selected ν.

wT(Av)=(ATw)Tv,gFD(δ)=Jh(p+δep)−Jh(p−δep)2δ.w^T(Av)=(A^Tw)^Tv,\qquad g_{\mathrm{FD}}(\delta)=\frac{J_h(p+\delta e_p)-J_h(p-\delta e_p)}{2\delta}.

转置点积、切向／伴随一致和完整重算 FD 分别检查不同层面。它们不能单独证明空间收敛。网格表额外比较解析连续梯度；大网格也报告 Newton 修正，避免只以残差绝对值判断求解精度。Transpose dot tests, tangent/adjoint agreement and full-rerun FD check different implementation layers. They do not individually prove spatial convergence. Refinement tables additionally compare analytic continuous gradients; large grids report Newton corrections to supplement raw residual magnitudes.

浏览器复用 D.3 的计算图 JVP/VJP，显式小矩阵 LU 只用于 N≤128 的交互。Python 显式组装带状稀疏 Jacobian：一阶最多三条对角线，二阶／光滑重构最多五条；存储量随 N 线性增长，大网格没有稠密 N×N 矩阵。The browser reuses D.3 graph JVP/VJP; dense small-matrix LU serves only N≤128 interactions. Python assembles a banded sparse Jacobian: at most three diagonals for first order and five for unlimited/smooth reconstruction. Storage grows linearly with N, without a dense N×N matrix on large grids.
