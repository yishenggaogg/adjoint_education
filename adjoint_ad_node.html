非结构网格格点格式的二维标量对流扩散方程离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of a two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation, on an unstructured node-centred scheme

算例与对流方程的格点页面是同一张环形（O 型）混合网格，方程多了一项常系数扩散 −ν∇2u（ν=0.5）。有了扩散，内圈物面才能施加 Dirichlet 壁面条件 u=uw=0；本页把它强施加：壁面节点的方程在边循环之后被整行换成 ui−uw。扩散通量要用节点梯度，这里用最小二乘来算，所以残差成了两遍循环：先算梯度，再算通量。目标函数是流进物面的总通量。对照阅读：同一方程的格心页面。The test case is the same annular (O-type) hybrid mesh as the node-centred advection page, but the equation gains a constant-coefficient diffusion term −ν∇2u (ν=0.5). With diffusion the inner body surface can carry a Dirichlet wall condition, u=uw=0, and this page imposes it strongly: after the edge loop, each wall node's equation is replaced as a whole by ui−uw. The diffusive flux needs nodal gradients, taken here by least squares, so the residual becomes two loops: gradients first, then fluxes. The objective is the total flux into the body. Read alongside: the cell-centred page for the same equation.

网格：同一张混合网格，多了梯度模板

Mesh: the same hybrid grid, now with gradient stencils

算例网格

The test mesh

网格与对流页面第 1 节完全相同：3 圈节点、每圈 8 个，共 24 个节点；贴壁一层是 8 个四边形，外层是 16 个三角形。未知量放在节点上，控制体是中位对偶体。它的构造方法（在每个单元内把边中点与单元中心相连）在对流页面里有逐步演示，这里不再重复。网格有 48 条边，对应 48 个内部对偶面；另有 32 个边界半面（16 个在壁面上，16 个在远场上），所以边循环一共要走 80 个面。

The mesh is exactly the one of Section 1 of the advection page: 3 rings of 8 nodes, 24 nodes in all, with a layer of 8 quadrilaterals against the wall and 16 triangles outside it. The unknowns sit on the nodes and the control volumes are the median duals. How those are built (inside every cell, join the edge midpoints to the cell centre) is shown step by step on the advection page and is not repeated here. The 48 edges give 48 interior dual faces, and there are 32 boundary half-faces besides (16 on the wall, 16 on the far field), so the edge loop visits 80 faces.

变的是节点的角色（）。内圈 8 个节点落在物面上，而物面现在是 Dirichlet 壁面，这些节点的值是给定的 uw。它们仍然算未知量，在 24 个里占 8 个位置，只是它们的方程要被换掉（第 3 节）。外圈 8 个节点在远场上，其余 8 个是内部节点。

What changes is the role of the nodes (). The 8 inner-ring nodes lie on the body, which is now a Dirichlet wall, so their values are prescribed: uw. They still count as unknowns and take 8 of the 24 slots; only their equations will be swapped (Section 3). The 8 outer-ring nodes lie on the far field, and the remaining 8 are interior nodes.

新的几何量：边向量与梯度模板

New geometry: the edge vector and the gradient stencil

对流页面的边循环在每个面上只需要两样几何量：法向 n 与它的长度 |n|。扩散项还要两样。第一样是边向量 d=xj−xi，它连接对偶面两侧的两个未知量，两点差 uj−ui 就是沿它量出来的。第二样是每个节点的梯度模板，即最小二乘梯度要用哪些邻居的值。本页取最简单的选择：边邻居，也就是和该节点共用一条边的节点。壁面节点有 3 个边邻居，中圈节点有 5 个，外圈节点有 4 个。

The edge loop of the advection page needed two geometric quantities per face: the normal n and its length |n|. Diffusion needs two more. The first is the edge vector d=xj−xi, which joins the two unknowns on either side of the dual face; the two-point difference uj−ui is measured along it. The second is each node's gradient stencil, the neighbours whose values the least-squares gradient uses. This page takes the simplest choice, the edge neighbours: the nodes that share an edge with the node. A wall node has 3 of them, a middle-ring node 5, an outer-ring node 4.

要离散的方程与对偶面上的通量

The equation to discretise, and the flux on a dual face

要离散的方程

The equation to discretise

求解域 Ω 是物体与远场圆之间的环形区域。未知量 u(x,y) 满足定常的对流扩散方程：

The domain Ω is the annulus between the body and the far-field circle. The unknown u(x,y) obeys the steady advection–diffusion equation

∇·(12u2β)−∇·(ν∇u)=0,β=(1,0.5),ν=0.5

第一项就是对流页面的非线性守恒律，对流通量为 12u2β；第二项是常系数扩散，扩散通量为 −ν∇u。扩散把方程从一阶变成了二阶，于是边界上每一处都需要一个条件，物面上可以直接给定 u 的值（第 3 节）。

The first term is the nonlinear conservation law of the advection page, with convective flux 12u2β; the second is constant-coefficient diffusion, with diffusive flux −ν∇u. Diffusion turns the equation from first order into second order, so every part of the boundary now takes one condition, and on the body the value of u itself can be prescribed (Section 3).

有限体积离散

Finite-volume discretisation

在节点 i 的控制体 Vi 上积分，再用散度定理把体积分化成边界积分：

Integrate over the control volume Vi of node i and turn the volume integral into a boundary integral with the divergence theorem:

∮∂Vi(12u2β·n−ν∇u·n)ds=0⇒Ri(u)=Σe∋i±(ha,e+hv,e)=0

求和取遍节点 i 的所有对偶面 e。每个面上的通量只算一次，然后以相反符号分给两侧：i 一侧取 +，j 一侧取 −，所以离散格式是守恒的。ha 是对流通量（下标 a 取自 advection），hv 是扩散通量。

The sum runs over the dual faces e of node i. Each face's flux is computed once and handed to its two sides with opposite signs, + for side i and − for side j, so the scheme is conservative. ha is the convective flux (the subscript is from advection) and hv the diffusive one.

对流通量：与对流页面相同

The convective flux: as on the advection page

ha=14vn(uL2+uR2)−ε|n|(uR−uL),vn=β·n,ε=0.8

中心平均加常数耗散，外加远场上由 vn 的符号冻结的入流、出流开关。它的推导在对流页面第 2、3 节，这里原样沿用。

A central average plus constant dissipation, with the inflow/outflow switch on the far field frozen by the sign of vn. It is derived in Sections 2 and 3 of the advection page and is used here unchanged.

扩散通量：面上的法向导数

The diffusive flux: the normal derivative on the face

扩散通量 hv=−ν∇u·n 要的是面上的法向导数。面两侧只有两个节点值 ui、uj，而二维梯度有两个分量，只靠这两个值不够；除非对偶面恰好垂直于边（n 与 d 平行），那时两点差就是法向导数。一般网格上 n 与 d 并不平行（），做法是把 n 分成沿 d 的部分和垂直于 d 的部分：

The diffusive flux hv=−ν∇u·n needs the normal derivative on the face. Only two nodal values sit either side of it, ui and uj, while a two-dimensional gradient has two components, so those two values are not enough, unless the dual face happens to be perpendicular to the edge (n parallel to d), in which case the two-point difference is the normal derivative. On a general mesh n and d are not parallel (), and the way out is to split n into a part along d and a part perpendicular to it:

n=pd+k,p=d·n|d|2,k=n−pd(k·d=0)

沿 d 的部分用两点差 ∇u·d≈uj−ui；垂直的部分 k 用两端节点梯度的平均 ḡ=12(Gi+Gj)：

The part along d uses the two-point difference ∇u·d≈uj−ui; the perpendicular part k uses the average of the two nodal gradients, ḡ=12(Gi+Gj):

∇u·n≈p(uj−ui)+ḡ·k,hv=−ν[p(uj−ui)+ḡ·k]

它也常写成先修正平均梯度、再投影到 n 上：ḡ·n−(ḡ·d−Δu)p，展开就是上式。两点部分让 i 与 j 直接耦合，是格式稳定的来源；梯度部分补上 n 偏离 d 的那一块。两部分对线性场都精确，所以整个 hv 对线性场精确。

It is often written as a correction of the averaged gradient before projecting it on n, ḡ·n−(ḡ·d−Δu)p; expanded, that is the line above. The two-point part couples i and j directly and is where the scheme's stability comes from; the gradient part makes up for n leaning away from d. Both parts are exact on linear fields, so the whole hv is.

The gradient Gi of node i is the G for which the linear model ui+G·ds comes closest to the neighbour values us of the stencil (ds=xs−xi):

The weights wis depend on the geometry only and are computed once per mesh. Because ΣswisdsT=Mi−1Mi=I, the gradient of a linear field comes out exactly. This page uses no distance weighting (unweighted least squares). The gradient is linear in u, which Section 7 will use. ν 为什么取 0.5。耗散项 −ε|n|(uR−uL) 与扩散的两点部分 −νp(uR−uL) 形式相同，它就像一个数值扩散系数 ε|n|/p。在这张只有 24 个节点的粗网格上，这个系数在各内部面上介于 0.24 与 1.58 之间，每加密一次减半。在收敛解上，ν=0.5 时各内部面耗散通量的绝对值之和是扩散通量的 1.16 倍，两者相当；若取 ν=0.1，这个比值是 5.6，物理扩散只是数值耗散上的一点修正。第 14 节的精度阶研究会再遇到它。

Why ν=0.5. The dissipation −ε|n|(uR−uL) has the same form as the two-point part of diffusion, −νp(uR−uL), so it acts like a numerical diffusivity ε|n|/p. On this 24-node coarse mesh that diffusivity lies between 0.24 and 1.58 over the interior faces, and it halves with every refinement. At the converged solution with ν=0.5, the absolute dissipative fluxes over the interior faces sum to 1.16 times the diffusive ones: the two are comparable. With ν=0.1 the ratio would be 5.6, and the physical diffusion only a small correction to the numerical one. The order study of Section 14 meets it again.

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈：强施加的 Dirichlet 壁面

Inner ring: a strongly imposed Dirichlet wall

物面上 u=uw=0。先看壁面节点 n1 的控制体（）：它有 3 个内部对偶面，分别通往边邻居 n2、n8、n9，另外还有两个壁面半面。如果照常写守恒方程，就是“所有这些面的通量之和为零”。壁面半面上的通量是物面吸收（或放出）的量，事先并不知道：它就是让 u1 保持为 uw 所需要的那个量。由于 uw=0，对流通量 12u2β·n 在物面上为零，穿过物面的只有扩散通量。

On the body u=uw=0. Look first at the control volume of wall node n1 (): it has 3 interior dual faces, towards its edge neighbours n2, n8 and n9, and two wall half-faces. Written as usual, its conservation equation says that the fluxes through all these faces sum to zero. The flux through the wall half-faces is what the body absorbs (or gives off), and it is not known in advance: it is whatever it takes to hold u1 at uw. Because uw=0, the convective flux 12u2β·n vanishes on the body, and only diffusion crosses it.

Dirichlet 条件有两种施加方式。弱施加：把 uw 写进壁面半面上的通量公式，方程照常组装；格心页面只能这样做，因为那里物面上没有未知量。强施加：不写壁面节点的守恒方程，而是把这一行整个换成

A Dirichlet condition can be imposed in two ways. Weakly: write uw into the flux formula of the wall half-faces and assemble the equations as usual; the cell-centred page can only do this, since it has no unknown on the body. Strongly: do not write the wall nodes' conservation equations at all, and replace each of those rows as a whole by

Rbc,i(u)=ui−uw=0,i∈W={1,…,8}

本页用强施加。它的好处是 ui=uw 在收敛解上严格成立，而不只是在网格加密的极限下成立，而且那个未知的壁面通量根本用不到。代价有两个：壁面通量要事后找回来（第 6 节）；前向和伴随都要为这几行做专门的处理（第 7、8 节）。

This page imposes the wall strongly. The gain is that ui=uw holds exactly at the converged solution, not merely in the limit of refinement, and the unknown wall flux is never needed. There are two prices: the wall flux has to be recovered afterwards (Section 6), and the forward and the adjoint code both need special treatment of these rows (Sections 7 and 8).

在代码里：循环之后替换

In the code: replace after the loop

实现很简单。边循环照常把所有内部对偶面与远场半面的通量累加进 r，壁面半面直接跳过：它们的通量只会进入壁面节点的那一行，而那一行马上就要被丢掉。循环结束后，对每个壁面节点令 ri←ui−uw。在 Jacobian 里，这一行变成单位行 êiT：对角元为 1，其余为 0。替换后的残差与 Jacobian 记为 Rbc 和 Abc；替换之前的记为 R 和 A，第 6 节还要用到它们。

The implementation is short. The edge loop accumulates the fluxes of all interior dual faces and far-field half-faces into r as usual and simply skips the wall half-faces: their fluxes would only go into the wall nodes' rows, which are about to be thrown away. After the loop, set ri←ui−uw for every wall node. In the Jacobian this row becomes the unit row êiT: 1 on the diagonal, 0 elsewhere. The replaced residual and Jacobian are written Rbc and Abc; the ones before the replacement are R and A, and Section 6 still needs them.

外圈：远场

Outer ring: the far field

对流部分与对流页面相同：远场半面按 vn=β·n 的符号分成入流和出流（在初始几何上冻结），入流取 uR=u∞=1，出流取 uR=uL。扩散部分取零通量 ν∇u·n=0，也就是齐次 Neumann 条件，所以远场半面只带对流通量。16 个远场半面中，8 个入流，8 个出流。

The convective part is as on the advection page: the far-field half-faces are split into inflow and outflow by the sign of vn=β·n (frozen at the initial geometry), with uR=u∞=1 on inflow and uR=uL on outflow. The diffusive part takes zero flux, ν∇u·n=0, a homogeneous Neumann condition, so a far-field half-face carries the convective flux only. Of the 16 far-field half-faces, 8 are inflow and 8 outflow.

为什么对流页面没有这种壁面。纯对流方程是一阶的，边界上只能在特征进入区域的地方给数据；物面是闭合曲线，特征速度沿物面必然有正有负，在特征离开区域的那部分物面上再规定 u，就多给了一个条件（见对流页面第 3 节）。扩散项是二阶的，它让整条物面上都可以、也必须给一个条件，Dirichlet 壁面由此成为适定的。

Why the advection page has no such wall. A purely convective equation is first order: boundary data can be given only where characteristics enter the domain. The body is a closed curve, the characteristic speed takes both signs along it, and prescribing u where characteristics leave the domain is one condition too many (see Section 3 of the advection page). The diffusion term is second order: it lets, and requires, the whole body take one condition, and that is what makes the Dirichlet wall well posed.

残差 = 两遍循环：先梯度，后通量

The residual = two loops: gradients first, then fluxes

先固定离散问题与符号

Fix the discrete problem and its notation

本页每个节点存储 1 个未知量。以下用粗体 U_h 表示把所有位置、所有分量按代码顺序排成的列向量；标量问题也采用这个统一记号。它不是连续函数 U(x)。下标 h 表示固定网格及固定空间格式，而不是差分验证中的参数步长。物理方程中的空间导数只用于说明模型；求离散伴随时，求导对象是本页实际装配的有限维函数。

Each node stores 1 unknown. Below, bold U_h stacks all locations and components in code order, including for scalar problems. It is not a continuous function U(x). The subscript h labels the fixed mesh and spatial scheme, not the parameter step in finite-difference checks. Spatial derivatives in the physical equations explain the model; discrete differentiation acts on the actual finite-dimensional functions assembled on this page.

Uh∈Rnu,a∈Rna,Rh:Rnu×Rna→Rnu,Jh:Rnu×Rna→R.\mathbf U_h\in\mathbb R^{n_u},\quad a\in\mathbb R^{n_a},\quad R_h:\mathbb R^{n_u}\times\mathbb R^{n_a}\to\mathbb R^{n_u},\quad J_h:\mathbb R^{n_u}\times\mathbb R^{n_a}\to\mathbb R.

a 统一代表本页的设计变量（原文中的 p、b 或坐标 X），不是声速。原文中省略 h 的 R、J、A 在离散推导中分别指 R_h、J_h、A_h。系统方程中的局部 U_i 是一个小列向量；全局 U_h 才是 Jacobian 的输入。截面积、通量 Jacobian、全局残差 Jacobian 必须按定义区分，不能因为都曾简写为 A 而混用。

Here a denotes the page’s design variables (p, b or coordinates X in the original notation), not sound speed. In discrete derivations, unadorned R, J and A mean R_h, J_h and A_h. The residual of the advection page is one edge loop: each face gathers the two states, computes the one-dimensional flux and scatters it to both sides. The diffusive flux needs the nodal gradients at both ends of the face, and a nodal gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the gradients cannot be computed and used in the same sweep: the gradients of all nodes must be finished before the first flux is computed. The residual therefore becomes two loops, plus the replacement at the end:

When the loop ends, every Gi=Σswis(us−ui) is complete. It is again a gather–compute–scatter loop, over the 48 edges; only its target is an array of two-dimensional vectors.

用 Jacobi 迭代求解

Solving with the Jacobi iteration

本页的 primal、切线、伴随三个方程（后两个在第 9、10 节）用的是同一个 Jacobi 迭代：每一轮算一次残差，再逐节点除以精确 Jacobian 的对角元：

On this page the primal, the tangent and the adjoint equation (the last two in Sections 9 and 10) are all solved by one and the same Jacobi iteration: every sweep evaluates the residual once and divides it, node by node, by the diagonal of the exact Jacobian:

u←u−D−1Rbc(u)
D=diagAbc(u)

D 不需要把 Abc 造出来：diagonal(u) 就是残差的那个边循环，每个对偶面只留下落在对角线上的两项，即对流导数、两点部分，以及经由最小二乘梯度的 −12νk·∂G/∂ui。壁面行是替换行，D=1，残差是 ui−uw，一轮就把 uw 放上去；代码直接写入 uw，使 ui=uw 逐位成立。所以一轮只有一次 residual 和一次 diagonal：不形成矩阵，也不解线性方程组。初值取 u≡1（壁面节点取 uw），判据是残差的均方根 ‖Rbc‖<10−13（）。

D does not need Abc to be formed: diagonal(u) is the residual's own edge loop, keeping of each dual face only the two entries that land on the diagonal: the convective derivative, the two-point part, and the term through the least-squares gradients, −12νk·∂G/∂ui. A wall row is a replaced row, with D=1 and residual ui−uw, so one sweep puts uw there; the code writes uw in directly, so that ui=uw holds to the bit. So a sweep is one residual and one diagonal: no matrix is formed and no linear system is solved. The initial guess is u≡1 (uw on the wall nodes), and the criterion is the rms of the residual, ‖Rbc‖<10−13 ().

为什么用 Jacobi，而不用 LU。本页只有 24 个未知量，理论上最快的办法是直接分解：把精确的 Abc 整个造出来，用 Newton 法，每一步做一次稠密 LU 分解，k=4 时残差就已低于 10−13。可那样就说明不了 matrix-free。Jacobi 是最简单的迭代法：对矩阵它只要对角元，其余的一切都经由残差作用进来（切线方程里是 residual_d，伴随方程里是 residual_b），这正是 matrix-free 的意思。代价是线性收敛：在解附近，误差每一轮乘以迭代矩阵 I−D−1Abc，速率由它的谱半径决定，本算例是 0.787，判据在 k=121 时满足。切线与伴随的迭代矩阵与它同谱，所以进入渐近阶段以后，三条收敛曲线以同一个速率下降（第 10 节）。

Why Jacobi, and not LU. With only 24 unknowns the fastest method in principle is a direct factorisation: form the whole exact Abc, run Newton, and factorise with one dense LU per step; the residual is below 10−13 by k=4. But that would show nothing about matrix-free. Jacobi is the simplest iterative method there is: of the matrix it needs only the diagonal, and everything else comes in through the residual (through residual_d in the tangent equation and residual_b in the adjoint equation), which is exactly what matrix-free means. The price is linear convergence: near the solution the error is multiplied every sweep by the iteration matrix I−D−1Abc, at a rate set by its spectral radius, 0.787 here, and the criterion is met at k=121. The tangent and adjoint iteration matrices have the same spectrum, so once in the asymptotic phase all three convergence curves fall at the same rate (Section 10).

The gradient path in the diagonal: optional here, essential on the cell-centred page. Take the diagonal of the first-order part Ãbc instead and the iteration still converges to the same u* (the residual is exact), only at another rate. Here that hardly shows: the gradient path changes a diagonal entry by at most 3.9%, and the spectral radius is 0.786 instead of 0.787. On the cell-centred page it changes one by up to 15.3%, and the spectral radius rises from 0.915 to 0.990, about nine times as many sweeps.

为什么需要精确的导数

Why the exact derivative is needed

The design variables are the mesh node coordinates X. The state is fixed by Rbc(u,X)=0, and the total derivative of the objective J(u,X) with respect to X is

dJdX=∂J∂X+ψT∂Rbc∂X,AbcTψ=−g

推导与对流页面第 7 节相同。这里要强调的是：伴随方程里的矩阵是精确的 Abc，不是它的一阶部分 Ãbc（第 5 节；很多隐式求解器留用的就是它）。拿一阶部分的转置 ÃbcT 解出来的 ψ 满足的是另一个方程：本算例里它与精确的 ψ 最多差 0.033，给出的形状梯度最多差 2.6e−3（最大分量的 0.8%），而精确伴随与有限差分的差在 10−9 量级（第 12 节）。精确矩阵每行伸到邻居的邻居，形成它既麻烦又浪费，所以和对流页面一样，只需要能算乘积 Abcv 与 AbcTw 的两个函数：residual_d 与 residual_b。这一节是前者。

The derivation is that of Section 7 of the advection page. The point to stress here is that the matrix in the adjoint equation is the exact Abc, not its first-order part Ãbc (Section 5), which many implicit solvers keep. A ψ solved with the transpose of the first-order part, ÃbcT, satisfies a different equation: here it misses the exact ψ by up to 0.033, and its shape gradient misses by up to 2.6e−3 (0.8% of the largest component), whereas the exact adjoint agrees with finite differences to about 10−9 (Section 12). The exact matrix reaches the neighbours of neighbours in every row, awkward and wasteful to form, so, as on the advection page, all that is needed are two functions that compute the products Abcv and AbcTw: residual_d and residual_b. This section is the first of them.

对两遍循环逐遍求导

Differentiating the two loops, pass by pass

沿残差的执行顺序，逐遍线性化。第一遍的梯度对 u 是线性的，所以它的导数就是把同一个循环、同一组权重作用在扰动 v 上。第二遍的通量对两侧状态和平均梯度求导，对流部分的系数 cL、cR 与对流页面相同。最后，替换行 ui−uw 的导数就是 vi：

Linearise the residual pass by pass, in the order it runs. The gradients of pass 1 are linear in u, so their derivative is the same loop with the same weights, applied to the perturbation v. The flux of pass 2 is differentiated with respect to the two states and the averaged gradient, with the convective coefficients cL, cR of the advection page. Finally, the derivative of a replaced row ui−uw is simply vi:

Gd,i=Σswis(vs−vi),hd=cLvL+cRvR−ν[p(vR−vL)+ḡd·k],ḡd=12(Gd,i+Gd,j)

Every path from u to r is differentiated, the one through the gradients included, so this is the exact Abcv. Drop the ḡd·k term and what is left is the first-order part Ãbcv. The difference between the two is exactly what the first-order part drops: with v=ê9 it reaches 0.115 (against 4.03 for Abcê9 itself), on 9 nodes within two rings of n9.

为什么这就是 Abcv

Why this is exactly Abcv

由链式法则，residual_d(u,v) 就是 ddtRbc(u+tv) 在 t=0 处的值，也就是 Abcv。它与显式乘积的差在舍入量级（播放到最后，矩阵视图下方给出两列的最大差），而全程没有形成矩阵。

By the chain rule, residual_d(u,v) is ddtRbc(u+tv) at t=0, that is Abcv. It differs from the explicit product only by round-off (play to the end: the matrix view reports the largest difference between the two columns), and no matrix was ever formed.

把前向写成线性映射的乘积

The forward mode as a product of linear maps

严格推导伴随最稳妥的办法，是先把前向写成一串线性映射的乘积，再逐个转置。第一遍是一个 2N×N 的线性算子 Γ（N=24 是未知量个数）：Gd=Γv，它的第 i 个块行是 Σswis(ês−êi)T。第二遍把状态和梯度一起映到输出：rd=Buv+BgGd。Bu 收集每个面上直接作用在两侧状态上的系数：第 i 行在第 i、j 列分别加 cL+νp、cR−νp，第 j 行加它们的相反数。Bg 收集作用在梯度上的系数：每个面在第 i 行写 −12νkT、在第 j 行写 +12νkT，两行都写进 Gi 和 Gj 两个块。最后是替换：P 把壁面行置零，E 只在壁面行的对角线上为 1，P+E=I。于是

The safest way to derive the adjoint rigorously is to write the forward mode as a product of linear maps and transpose them one by one. Pass 1 is a 2N×N linear operator Γ (N=24 unknowns): Gd=Γv, whose i-th block row is Σswis(ês−êi)T. Pass 2 maps the states and the gradients together to the output: rd=Buv+BgGd. Bu collects the coefficients acting on the states directly: on every face, row i gets cL+νp in column i and cR−νp in column j, and row j gets their negatives. Bg collects those acting on the gradients: each face writes −12νkT into row i and +12νkT into row j, both into the two blocks Gi and Gj. Last comes the replacement: P zeroes the wall rows and E is 1 on the wall rows' diagonal only, so P+E=I. Hence

A=Bu+BgΓ,Abc=PA+E

前向求解：切线方程

The forward solve: the tangent equation

第 7 节只算了乘积 Abcv。前向模式真正要解的是切线方程：选一个输入 α，对 Rbc(u(α),α)=0 求导，u 随 α 的变化率 v=du/dα 满足

Section 7 only computed the product Abcv. What forward mode actually has to solve is the tangent equation: pick one input α and differentiate Rbc(u(α),α)=0 with respect to it; the rate v=du/dα at which u follows α satisfies

Abcv=−f,f=∂Rbc∂α

这里取远场来流值 α=u∞。只有远场入流半面见到它（作为对流通量的右状态；远场没有扩散通量），所以 f=∂Rbc/∂u∞ 就是这些半面上通量对右槽位的导数，写进各自的节点，共 5 个非零分量。壁面行 ui−uw 与 u∞ 无关，那里 fi=0。

Here take the far-field free-stream value, α=u∞. Only the far-field inflow half-faces see it, as the right state of their convective flux (the far field carries no diffusive flux), so f=∂Rbc/∂u∞ is the derivative of those fluxes with respect to their right slot, written into their nodes: 5 non-zero entries. The wall rows ui−uw do not involve u∞, so fi=0 there.

Each sweep calls just one matrix-related function: residual_d of Section 7, which computes Abcv. D is what diagonal of Section 5 returns at u*. 复数步长

The complex step

第 12 节的差分受步长两头夹击：截断误差要 h 小，舍入误差要 h 大。复数步长把这个两难拿掉：把一个坐标分量挪一个虚数步长 ih，整个 primal 求解用复数算，J 的虚部除以 h 就是导数：

The differences of Section 12 are squeezed from both sides: truncation wants a small h, round-off a large one. dJdXm=ImJ(X+ihêm)h+O(h2)

The reason is the Taylor expansion

J(X+ihêm)=J(X)+ihdJdXm−h22d2JdXm2+O(h3)

实部与虚部各自分开：虚部的第一项就是 hdJ/dXm，下一项已是 h3。这里没有两个相近的数相减，也就没有相消，h 可以取 10−30：O(h2) 的截断误差远在舍入误差之下。

Real and imaginary parts separate: the imaginary part starts with hdJ/dXm, and its next term is already h3. No two nearly equal numbers are subtracted, so nothing cancels, and h can be 10−30: the O(h2) truncation error lies far below round-off.

Order of accuracy: a manufactured solution

伴随给出的是离散格式的精确导数，与格式本身是几阶精度无关；格式的精度阶决定的是离散解，以及由它算出的 J 与 dJ/dX，离连续问题有多远。这一节用构造解把它测出来：先选一个光滑函数当精确解，由它反推出源项与边界数据，再在一族逐次一致加密的网格上求解，看误差随网格尺寸按几次方减小。

The adjoint gives the exact derivative of the discrete scheme, whatever that scheme's order of accuracy; the order decides how far the discrete solution, and the J and dJ/dX computed from it, are from the continuous problem. What this means for the adjoint

第 10–13 节验证的是：伴随与几何梯度对离散的 J 精确到舍入误差，这与格式几阶无关。离散的 J 本身则随网格加密以格式的阶逼近连续值：第 6 节在同一族网格上给出的壁面通量 2.749、2.454、2.291、2.201 就在这样收敛。优化器拿到的梯度与它所优化的离散目标函数完全一致，问题是自洽的；网格加密决定的是这个离散问题离连续问题有多近。要让它更近，得提高格式的阶（例如对流通量用重构），而不是改伴随。

What Sections 10 to 13 verified is that the adjoint and the geometric gradient are exact, to round-off, for the discrete J, whatever the scheme's order. The discrete J itself approaches the continuous value at the scheme's order as the mesh is refined: the wall fluxes 2.749, 2.454, 2.291 and 2.201 that Section 6 reports on this same family are converging that way. The gradient the optimiser gets agrees exactly with the discrete objective it optimises, so the problem is self-consistent; refinement decides how close that discrete problem is to the continuous one. To bring it closer one has to raise the scheme's order (a reconstruction in the convective flux, say), not change the adjoint.

Why a discrete multiplier is not automatically a continuous field

正文使用普通数组点积定义 A_hᵀ。若另选带权内积，算子的伴随就会改变：状态权矩阵为 W_U、残差权矩阵为 W_R 时，带权伴随是 W_U⁻¹A_hᵀW_R。代码的普通转置不能在没有换元说明的情况下被写成该带权算子。

The main derivation defines A_hᵀ using ordinary array dot products. A weighted inner product changes the adjoint: with state and residual weights W_U and W_R it is W_U⁻¹A_hᵀW_R. The coded ordinary transpose cannot be relabelled as that weighted operator without a change of variables.

⟨Ahv,w⟩WR=vTAhTWRw=⟨v,Ah†w⟩WU,Ah†=WU−1AhTWR.\langle A_hv,w\rangle_{W_R}=v^TA_h^TW_Rw=\langle v,A_h^\dagger w\rangle_{W_U},\qquad A_h^\dagger=W_U^{-1}A_h^TW_R.

残差的归一化也会改变乘子的数值。若改为 R̃_h=D⁻¹R_h，D 为可逆行尺度矩阵，则在收敛解上 Ã_h=D⁻¹A_h，ψ̃_h=Dᵀψ_h；目标总梯度保持不变。即使 D 随设计改变，由 δD 产生的项也乘以 R_h，在精确收敛时为零。把控制体积分残差改成体积平均残差后，不能仍直接比较原来的乘子曲线。

Residual normalization also changes multiplier values. For R̃_h=D⁻¹R_h with an invertible row-scaling matrix D, at convergence Ã_h=D⁻¹A_h and ψ̃_h=Dᵀψ_h, while the total objective gradient is unchanged. Even when D depends on design, terms from δD multiply R_h and vanish at exact convergence. Multipliers of integrated and volume-normalized residuals cannot be compared without rescaling.

连续附录从微分方程、积分目标和分部积分出发；正文从实际离散残差及其有限维链式法则出发。两者可以比较同一参数的目标总导数与加密极限，但还须匹配符号、变量、积分权重和边界约束。边界替换行的乘子尤其不能直接解释为连续场采样值。

与对流页面相比多了什么

What this page adds to the advection page

  • 两遍循环。扩散通量要用节点梯度，最小二乘梯度又要用邻居的值，所以残差先遍历 48 条边算梯度，再走一遍边循环，在 80 个面上算通量。前向按同样的顺序走，伴随把顺序倒过来。
  • Two loops. The diffusive flux needs nodal gradients, and a least-squares gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the residual first visits the 48 edges for the gradients and then runs the edge loop over the 80 faces for the fluxes. The forward code runs them in the same order; the adjoint reverses it.
  • 一个 Jacobi 迭代，三个方程。精确 Jacobian 每行伸到邻居的邻居（264 个非零元），从不形成：primal、切线、伴随都用同一个 Jacobi 迭代，只用到它的对角元（梯度路径在内）与 residual_d、residual_b 这两个乘积；谱半径 0.787，判据在 k=121 时满足。
  • One Jacobi iteration, three equations. The exact Jacobian reaches the neighbours of neighbours in every row (264 non-zeros) and is never formed: the primal, the tangent and the adjoint are all solved by the same Jacobi iteration, which needs only its diagonal (gradient path included) and the two products residual_d and residual_b; the spectral radius is 0.787, and the criterion is met at k=121.
  • 强施加的壁面。壁面行在循环之后整行替换。由 Abc=PA+E 可知：前向在循环之后覆盖，伴随必须循环之前清零、循环之后加回；照搬前向的做法，点积测试就会失败。
  • A strongly imposed wall. The wall rows are replaced as a whole after the loop. From Abc=PA+E: the forward code overwrites after the loop, while the adjoint must zero before the loop and add back after it; copy the forward recipe and the dot-product test fails.
  • 反力形式的目标函数。流进物面的通量从被替换之前的壁面行里取回：J=−Σi∈WRi(u)。它与远场通量精确平衡；单侧差分公式在本网格上只有它的 33%。
  • An objective in reaction form. The flux into the body is recovered from the wall rows before their replacement: J=−Σi∈WRi(u). It balances the far-field flux exactly; the one-sided formula gives only 33% of it on this mesh.
  • 三个迭代同一个速率。三个迭代矩阵同谱，进入渐近阶段后三条收敛曲线平行（每轮 0.7874 倍）；切线与伴随给出同一个 dJ/du∞，相对差 1.2×10−16。
  • One rate for all three. The three iteration matrices share their spectrum, so once asymptotic the three convergence curves run parallel (a factor 0.7874 per sweep); the tangent and the adjoint give the same dJ/du∞, to 1.2×10−16 relative.
  • φ 与壁面上的 ψ。φ（壁面上取 −1）满足替换之前的齐次伴随方程，−φi 是节点 i 处单位源流进物面的比例；壁面上的 ψi 等于 −dJ/duw,i。几何梯度是以 φ 为权重的一次反向扫描，经过 n、d、p、k 与 Mi−1。
  • φ and ψ on the wall. φ (−1 on the wall) solves the homogeneous adjoint equation before the replacement, and −φi is the fraction of a unit source at node i that ends up in the body; on the wall ψi=−dJ/duw,i. The geometric gradient is one reverse sweep weighted by φ, through n, d, p, k and Mi−1.
  • 验证。点积测试检查转置，差分与复数步长检查梯度。复数步长从收敛解出发，每一轮都与前向迭代一致，与伴随梯度相符到舍入误差。
  • Verification. The dot-product test checks the transpose; finite differences and the complex step check the gradient. Started from the converged solution, the complex step matches the forward iteration at every sweep, and the adjoint gradient to round-off.
  • 精度阶。只有扩散时是二阶（最细一层 L2 范数 2.08、最大范数 1.97）；完整方程受耗散项的数值扩散 ε|n||d|2/(d·n)=O(h) 所限，只有一阶，观测阶从下面靠近 1（第 14 节）。
  • Order of accuracy. Second order with diffusion alone (2.08 in L2 and 1.97 in the max norm on the finest level); the full equation is first order only, limited by the dissipation's numerical diffusivity ε|n||d|2/(d·n)=O(h), its observed order approaching 1 from below (Section 14).

A.1 完整推导与适定性

A.1 Full derivation and well-posedness

1. 符号、积分测度与三类不同的检查

将定常 PDE 写为 R(u,p)=0，目标泛函记为 J，采用拉格朗日泛函

L=J−∫ΩλTR dΩ.\mathcal L=J-\int_\Omega\lambda^T R\,d\Omega.

页面求解的是 A_h^T ψ_h = −g_h。这里的连续乘子采用相反符号：在守恒方程上有 λ_h=−ψ_h。这些残差已经是控制体积分后的守恒平衡，不需要再除以控制体的体积或面积。被代数边界约束替换的行对应约束乘子，其数值不是连续场的点值。

三类检查分别回答不同的问题：

  1. 转置／点积测试检查 A_h^T 的实现。
  2. 对同一离散问题做有限差分，检查 dJ_h/dp。
  3. 将独立求得的连续伴随与一系列加密网格上的离散伴随比较，检查指定范数下的一致性。比较时必须匹配 PDE、边界条件、目标泛函、计算区域与目标数据。

有限组网格上误差下降是数值证据，不是收敛定理。即使积分范数下的误差下降，边界层或特征跳跃仍可能阻止最大范数下的收敛。

2. 适定性与伴随一致性不是同一件事

出现差异提供了诊断机会，但差异本身不能证明问题不适定。应先明确状态空间与边界迹空间、允许的数据、目标泛函以及范数。Hadamard 意义下的适定性要求解存在、唯一且连续依赖于数据。正确推导的连续伴随可以是适定的，而离散边界闭合却不一致；一个边界数据与目标泛函不匹配的适定方程，仍然是错误的伴随。约束乘子、有限正则性和参考解误差也会造成表观差异。

线性化、零空间与稳定性

把原始边界条件的齐次线性化约束纳入算子 D:X→Y 的定义域 X。如果该算子是适当空间之间的有界同构，则其 Banach 伴随也是相应对偶空间之间的有界同构。这给出一种充分的局部分析框架，并不是写出形式微分表达式就能得到的结论。对于无界 PDE 算子，必须先明确其定义域及图范数，或者采用适当的弱形式。对于非线性原始问题，仅有存在唯一性还不能保证解映射可微；需要在所选解支上检查局部光滑性及线性化可逆性。

对于 D*λ=g，原始算子的零模态给出必要的兼容性条件：

Dv=0⟹g[v]=⟨λ,Dv⟩=0.Dv=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g[v]=\langle\lambda,Dv\rangle=0.

若 ker D* 含有非零元素，则伴随一旦有解就不唯一。仅有与原始零空间正交的条件，并不能构成一般的无穷维存在性定理：还需要考虑值域闭性和稳定性。对齐次伴随边界数据，所需估计具有以下形式：

∥λ∥Y∗≤C∥g∥X∗\|\lambda\|_{Y^*}\le C\|g\|_{X^*}

如果边界数据非齐次，还应加上相应的边界迹范数。每个网格尺度下逆算子有界也不够：在正确加权的离散范数下，稳定界必须与网格尺度无关。不考虑积分残差和控制体权重的原始欧氏条件数，本身不能作为连续问题的诊断结论。

一个可显式求解的对流例子

取 c>0，固定原始入口值，目标变分为 ∫₀¹g v dx+b v(1)。对原始线性化 c v' 使用 Green 恒等式，得到

−cλ′=g,cλ(1)=b,λ(x)=bc+1c∫x1g(s) ds.-c\lambda'=g,\qquad c\lambda(1)=b,\qquad \lambda(x)=\frac{b}{c}+\frac1c\int_x^1g(s)\,ds.
  • 缺少出口数据： 解可以加上任意常数，因此不唯一。
  • 额外照搬原始入口条件 λ(0)=0： 存在性要求 b+∫g=0。当 g=1,b=0 时，正确解是 (1−x)/c；额外入口条件使问题无解。
  • 失去一致稳定性： 有 ‖λ‖∞≤(|b|+‖g‖₁)/c。取 g=1,b=0 就达到 1/c 的放大倍数。每个固定的 c>0 都可以对应适定问题，但对于这类数据，不能得到在 c→0 时仍统一有效的界。速度为零时，原始算子退化。

在原二维物体算例中，法向指向流体计算域的外部，所以迎风物面是流体域的原始出流边界。一条已经携带 u=1 的特征线，不可能在这里同时满足零物理通量。首先失败的是所假设的光滑正值原始问题，而不只是某个已经建立的连续伴随的离散逼近。在假定的壁面状态 u=0 处，

δ(12u2β⋅n)=u(β⋅n)v=0\delta(\tfrac12u^2\beta\cdot n)=u(\beta\cdot n)v=0

对任意变分 v 都成立：通量约束丢失了一阶约束信息，特征速度也同时退化。把壁面条件照抄到形式伴随上，不能恢复有效的光滑解支。弱解／熵解表述需要另行分析边界迹与可微性。

对于特征边界对照，每条内部射线都会在有限路程内到达出流边界。边界数据几乎处处确定内部场；切线或角点引出的跳跃可以在允许非光滑解的空间中处理，本身不意味着不适定。特征边界附近的比值 b_u/(a·n) 需要兼容性分析：在这里的物面目标中，分子与分母具有可相消的法向因子，不能只看分母就断言伴随发散。

对流扩散。 正扩散系数提供椭圆主部，但这不是任意低阶项与 Robin 边界系数下的普遍可逆性证明。仍需检查齐次零空间，以及所用能量估计或最大值原理的假设。独立计算支持当前参数组，没有证明对所有参数都成立的定理。把目标所要求的壁面伴随值 1 改成 0，可能得到另一个适定问题，但会给出错误的目标灵敏度。在远场同时规定状态值和法向导数，一般会过约束。把被替换方程行的乘子当成壁面场值，则是另一类代数含义错误。

拟一维 Euler。 对所述正向亚声速分支，特征速度 u−c,u,u+c 解释了原始入口两个条件、出口一个条件，以及伴随的相反条件数。条件数目正确是必要条件，不是充分条件：特征传播与边界约束共同组成的边值算子还必须可逆。声速点处 B 奇异，当前使用 (Bᵀ)⁻¹ 的表示失效，需要喉部兼容性／正则性分析。这不意味着所有跨声速伴随都不存在。激波还需要考虑激波位置变分及相应内部条件。这些现象超出当前数值证据的范围，可参考 Giles 与 Pierce 的连续喷管伴随分析。强、弱边界处理对数值伴随的不同影响，见 Duivesteijn 等人的研究。

因此，教学顺序应当是：先明确连续原始问题及所选解支；推导伴随，包括其边界定义域；分析存在性、唯一性和稳定性；随后才检查对该问题的一致、稳定逼近。反例应明确保留其失败的前提，不能悄悄替换模型后宣布验证成功。

原始的 −ψ_W 是边界约束乘子。把它画成连续壁面迹，会人为制造表观不一致。

固定边界分类下的形状导数

为完整起见，令 x_t=x+tV(x) 表示光滑区域变形，并保持物理参数 β,ν,ε,u∞,u_w=0 为常数。入流／出流划分不变，没有面跨越 b=0。记 DV_ij=∂j V_i。在驻值状态，拉格朗日泛函化为

L=∫Ω[∇λ⋅f(u)−ν∇λ⋅∇u] dx−∫Γ∞λH ds.\mathcal L=\int_\Omega[\nabla\lambda\cdot f(u)-\nu\nabla\lambda\cdot\nabla u]\,dx -\int_{\Gamma_\infty}\lambda H\,ds.

由于 λ=1，壁面目标与残差的壁面边界项相消。保持拉回参考域后的状态与乘子不变，对该表达式求导，得到

δJ=∫Ω{[∇λ⋅f−ν∇λ⋅∇u]∇⋅V−(DVT∇λ)⋅f+ν(DVT∇λ)⋅∇u+ν∇λ⋅(DVT∇u)} dx−∫Γ∞λ{Cβ⋅[(∇⋅V)I−DVT]n+ϵ(u−u∞) 1b<0∇Γ⋅V} ds,\begin{aligned} \delta J={}&\int_\Omega\{[\nabla\lambda\cdot f-\nu\nabla\lambda\cdot\nabla u]\nabla\cdot V -(DV^T\nabla\lambda)\cdot f\\ &\hspace{12mm}+\nu(DV^T\nabla\lambda)\cdot\nabla u +\nu\nabla\lambda\cdot(DV^T\nabla u)\}\,dx\\ &-\int_{\Gamma_\infty}\lambda\{C\beta\cdot[(\nabla\cdot V)I-DV^T]n +\epsilon(u-u_\infty)\,1_{b<0}\nabla_\Gamma\cdot V\}\,ds, \end{aligned}

其中入流处 C=(u²+u∞²)/4，出流处 C=u²/2。如果随变形输运的边界数据不是常数，还需要加入其物质导数。这里给出了该公式的推导，但新增实验实际验证的是 dJ/du∞，不是完整形状导数；原页面的坐标导数检查仍然是离散检查。

1. Signs, measures and three distinct checks

Write the steady PDE as R(u,p)=0, the functional as J, and use

L=J−∫ΩλTR dΩ.\mathcal L=J-\int_\Omega\lambda^T R\,d\Omega.

The pages solve A_h^T ψ_h = −g_h. Our continuous multiplier has the opposite sign: λ_h=−ψ_h on conservation equations. Those residuals are integrated balances, so there is no additional division by control-volume measure. Rows replaced by algebraic boundary constraints carry constraint multipliers; their values are not point samples of the continuous field.

Three tests answer different questions:

  1. A transpose/dot test checks the implementation of A_h^T.
  2. Finite differences of the same discrete problem check dJ_h/dp.
  3. Comparing an independently solved continuous dual with a sequence of refined discrete duals tests consistency in a stated norm. It requires matching PDE, boundary conditions, functional, domain, and target data.

A decreasing error on finitely many meshes is numerical evidence, not a theorem of convergence. Boundary layers or characteristic jumps can prevent maximum-norm convergence even when an integral norm decreases.

2. Well-posedness is not the same as adjoint consistency

A discrepancy is a diagnostic opportunity, not by itself proof of ill-posedness. Specify the state and trace spaces, admissible data, functional, and norms first. Hadamard well-posedness requires existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence. A correctly derived continuous dual can be well posed while a discrete boundary closure is inconsistent; a well-posed equation with the wrong objective boundary data is simply the wrong dual. Constraint multipliers, limited regularity and finite reference error are other sources of apparent disagreement.

Linearization, kernels and stability

Include the homogeneous linearized primal boundary constraints in the domain X of the operator D:X→Y. If this is a bounded isomorphism between suitable spaces, its Banach adjoint is also a bounded isomorphism between the dual spaces. This is a sufficient local framework, not a conclusion obtained merely by writing a formal differential expression. An unbounded PDE operator must first be given a domain/graph norm or a suitable weak realization. For a nonlinear primal, existence and uniqueness alone do not guarantee a differentiable solution map; the local smoothness and invertibility hypotheses must be checked on the chosen branch.

For D*λ=g, a primal null mode implies the necessary compatibility condition

Dv=0⟹g[v]=⟨λ,Dv⟩=0.Dv=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g[v]=\langle\lambda,Dv\rangle=0.

A nonzero element of ker D* makes a solvable dual nonunique. Orthogonality to the primal kernel alone is not a general infinite-dimensional existence theorem: closed range and stability still matter. The required estimate has the form

∥λ∥Y∗≤C∥g∥X∗\|\lambda\|_{Y^*}\le C\|g\|_{X^*}

for homogeneous dual boundary data; inhomogeneous boundary data contribute their appropriate trace norm. A bounded inverse at every mesh size is not enough either: the stability bound must be uniform in the properly weighted discrete norms. Raw Euclidean condition numbers without accounting for integrated residuals and control-volume weights are not themselves a continuum diagnosis.

An explicit transport example

For c>0, fixed primal inflow and objective variation ∫₀¹g v dx+b v(1), the Green identity for the primal linearization c v' gives

−cλ′=g,cλ(1)=b,λ(x)=bc+1c∫x1g(s) ds.-c\lambda'=g,\qquad c\lambda(1)=b,\qquad \lambda(x)=\frac{b}{c}+\frac1c\int_x^1g(s)\,ds.
  • Missing outlet data: an arbitrary additive constant remains, so uniqueness fails.
  • An extra copied primal inlet datum λ(0)=0: existence requires b+∫g=0. With g=1,b=0, the correct solution is (1−x)/c; the extra inlet condition makes the problem unsolvable.
  • Loss of uniform stability: ‖λ‖∞≤(|b|+‖g‖₁)/c. Taking g=1,b=0 attains the 1/c amplification. Each fixed c>0 is well posed, but there is no bound uniform as c→0 for this data class. At zero speed the original operator degenerates.

In the original 2-D body example, normals point out of the fluid domain: the upwind body is therefore primal outflow. A characteristic already carrying u=1 cannot satisfy zero physical flux there. This is first a failure of the assumed smooth positive primal problem, not merely a poor discrete approximation to an otherwise established dual. At a putative wall state u=0,

δ(12u2β⋅n)=u(β⋅n)v=0\delta(\tfrac12u^2\beta\cdot n)=u(\beta\cdot n)v=0

for every variation v: the flux constraint loses its first-order information while the characteristic speed degenerates. Copying the wall condition onto a formal dual cannot restore a valid smooth solution branch. Weak/entropy formulations would need separate trace and differentiability analysis.

For the characteristic-boundary contrast, every interior ray reaches outflow in finite distance. Boundary data determine the field almost everywhere; tangency/corner jumps are compatible with a nonsmooth solution space and do not by themselves imply ill-posedness. The ratio b_u/(a·n) requires compatibility at characteristic boundaries: in this particular body objective its numerator and denominator contain a cancelling normal factor. One must not infer blow-up from the denominator alone.

What the other cases demonstrate

Advection–diffusion. Positive diffusion provides an elliptic principal part, not a universal invertibility proof for arbitrary lower-order and Robin coefficients. Homogeneous kernels and the assumptions of applicable energy or maximum-principle estimates still need attention. The independent solves support this parameter set; they do not prove a theorem for all parameters. Setting the wall dual to zero instead of the objective-required one can define a different well-posed problem, but yields the wrong functional sensitivity. Imposing both state and normal derivative at the far field is generally an overconstraint. Treating a replaced-row multiplier as a wall field sample is yet another, algebraic interpretation error.

Quasi-1-D Euler. The speeds u−c,u,u+c explain the two inlet/one outlet primal conditions and reversed dual counts for the stated positive subsonic branch. Counts are necessary, not sufficient: propagation and the boundary constraints together must yield an invertible boundary-value operator. At a sonic point B is singular, so the current representation using (Bᵀ)⁻¹ fails; throat compatibility/regularity analysis is required. This does not imply that all transonic adjoints fail to exist. Shocks require shock-position variations and internal conditions. These phenomena are outside the current numerical evidence; see the continuous nozzle analysis of Giles and Pierce. The distinct effects of strong/weak boundary treatment on numerical duals are discussed by Duivesteijn et al..

The teaching sequence is therefore: identify the continuous primal and chosen branch; derive the dual including its boundary domain; examine existence, uniqueness and stability; only then test consistent, stable approximation of that problem. A counterexample should retain its failed premise visibly, rather than silently substituting another model and declaring success.

4. Advection–diffusion: keep the total boundary flux

Here

R(u)=∇⋅q,q=12u2β−ν∇u,ν=0.5,a=uβ.R(u)=\nabla\cdot q,\qquad q=\tfrac12u^2\beta-\nu\nabla u, \quad \nu=0.5,\quad a=u\beta.

The complete Green identity is

∫ΩλRuv=∫Ω(−a⋅∇λ−νΔλ)v+∫∂Ω{λ[(a⋅n)v−ν∂nv]+ν(∂nλ)v} ds.\int_\Omega\lambda R_uv =\int_\Omega(-a\cdot\nabla\lambda-\nu\Delta\lambda)v +\int_{\partial\Omega}\{\lambda[(a\cdot n)v-\nu\partial_n v] +\nu(\partial_n\lambda)v\}\,ds.

There is no −(∇·a)λ in the volume dual: the primal linearization is in conservative form.

Wall functional and wall dual value

The wall has fixed Dirichlet data u=u_w=0. Its objective is the total outward fluid flux into the body,

J=∫Γwq⋅n ds.J=\int_{\Gamma_w}q\cdot n\,ds.

At fixed geometry, v=0 on the wall but ∂n v is free. Hence

δJ=−ν∫Γw∂nv ds.\delta J=-\nu\int_{\Gamma_w}\partial_n v\,ds.

Combining this with the boundary term of −∫λR gives ν(λ−1)∂n v. Therefore

−uβ⋅∇λ−νΔλ=0,λ∣Γw=1.-u\beta\cdot\nabla\lambda-\nu\Delta\lambda=0, \qquad \lambda|_{\Gamma_w}=1.

Zero wall adjoint data would be wrong for this flux functional.

What the implemented far field means in the continuous limit

Let b=β·n for the unit outward normal. Dividing the integrated numerical face flux by face length, the implemented far-field total flux is

H(u,u∞,n)={14b(u2+u∞2)+ϵ(u−u∞),b<0,12bu2,b≥0.H(u,u_\infty,n)= \begin{cases} \tfrac14b(u^2+u_\infty^2)+\epsilon(u-u_\infty),&b<0,\\ \tfrac12bu^2,&b\ge0. \end{cases}

The consistent continuum interpretation is the single condition q·n=H. In particular, on inflow this is a nonlinear Robin condition,

−ν∂nu=14b(u∞2−u2)+ϵ(u−u∞).-\nu\partial_nu=\tfrac14b(u_\infty^2-u^2)+\epsilon(u-u_\infty).

The code's “zero diffusive face flux” and external convective state must not be read as simultaneously imposing continuum u=u∞ and ∂n u=0. On outflow, q·n=H reduces to ∂n u=0.

Linearization gives δ(q·n)=H_u v, so the free far-field variation yields

ν∂nλ+Huλ=0,Hu={12bu+ϵ,b<0,bu,b≥0.\nu\partial_n\lambda+H_u\lambda=0,\qquad H_u=\begin{cases}\tfrac12bu+\epsilon,&b<0,\\bu,&b\ge0.\end{cases}

Notice that ε remains in this boundary-value problem. It vanishes with refinement as an interior artificial diffusion, but the boundary flux per unit length retains it.

For the incoming datum, stationarity gives an independently checkable scalar sensitivity:

dJdu∞=−∫Γinλ(12bu∞−ϵ) ds.\frac{dJ}{du_\infty} =-\int_{\Gamma_{\rm in}}\lambda(\tfrac12b u_\infty-\epsilon)\,ds.

Node-centred reaction objective: reconstruct the field

The node page has J_h=−1_W^T R_raw and replaced rows u_i−u_w=0. Partition the raw Jacobian into free and wall blocks. The free part of the adjoint satisfies

AffTψf=AwfT1W.A_{ff}^T\psi_f=A_{wf}^T1_W.

Thus the continuous field to compare is λ_f=−ψ_f and λ_W=1, since

AffTλf+AwfT1W=0.A_{ff}^T\lambda_f+A_{wf}^T1_W=0.

The raw −ψ_W is a boundary-constraint multiplier. Plotting it as the wall trace would manufacture an apparent inconsistency.

Shape derivative, with fixed boundary classification

For completeness, let x_t=x+tV(x) be a smooth domain deformation, with constant physical β,ν,ε,u∞,u_w=0. The inflow/outflow partition is held fixed and no face crosses b=0. Write DV_ij=∂j V_i. At a stationary state the Lagrangian reduces to

L=∫Ω[∇λ⋅f(u)−ν∇λ⋅∇u] dx−∫Γ∞λH ds.\mathcal L=\int_\Omega[\nabla\lambda\cdot f(u)-\nu\nabla\lambda\cdot\nabla u]\,dx -\int_{\Gamma_\infty}\lambda H\,ds.

The wall objective cancels the wall residual boundary term because λ=1. Differentiating this expression while holding the pulled-back state and multiplier fixed gives

δJ=∫Ω{[∇λ⋅f−ν∇λ⋅∇u]∇⋅V−(DVT∇λ)⋅f+ν(DVT∇λ)⋅∇u+ν∇λ⋅(DVT∇u)} dx−∫Γ∞λ{Cβ⋅[(∇⋅V)I−DVT]n+ϵ(u−u∞) 1b<0∇Γ⋅V} ds,\begin{aligned} \delta J={}&\int_\Omega\{[\nabla\lambda\cdot f-\nu\nabla\lambda\cdot\nabla u]\nabla\cdot V -(DV^T\nabla\lambda)\cdot f\\ &\hspace{12mm}+\nu(DV^T\nabla\lambda)\cdot\nabla u +\nu\nabla\lambda\cdot(DV^T\nabla u)\}\,dx\\ &-\int_{\Gamma_\infty}\lambda\{C\beta\cdot[(\nabla\cdot V)I-DV^T]n +\epsilon(u-u_\infty)\,1_{b<0}\nabla_\Gamma\cdot V\}\,ds, \end{aligned}

where C=(u²+u∞²)/4 on inflow and C=u²/2 on outflow. Nonconstant transported boundary data would add their material derivatives. This formula is derived here but the added experiment validates dJ/du∞, not this full shape derivative; the earlier pages' coordinate checks remain discrete checks.

Independent numerical reference

A P1 finite-element reference solves the nonlinear primal weak form on a triangulation of the same polygon, then directly assembles the derived continuous dual weak form

∫Ω[ν∇λ⋅∇v−u(β⋅∇λ)v]+∫Γ∞Huλv=0,\int_\Omega[\nu\nabla\lambda\cdot\nabla v-u(\beta\cdot\nabla\lambda)v] +\int_{\Gamma_\infty}H_u\lambda v=0,

with λ=1 on the wall. It does not call the finite-volume transpose. Triangle and edge quadrature integrate the polynomial integrands. References on 1,088, 4,224 and 16,640 vertices check reference resolution. The final reference is still a numerical approximation. FV state and dual equations use the original page cores, solved to tight algebraic residuals with sparse direct Newton/linear solves for this offline study. This changes the verification solver, not the finite-volume equations or the page's Jacobi demonstrations.

连续伴随与伴随一致性

Continuous adjoints and adjoint consistency

点积测试查转置，差分与复数步长查同一离散目标的梯度；它们都不能证明离散伴随逼近了连续伴随。本节先推导连续问题，再用独立参考解与网格加密比较。

A dot test checks the transpose; finite differences and the complex step check the gradient of the same discrete objective. None proves approximation of the continuous dual. Here we derive the continuous problem first, then compare with an independent reference under refinement.

Laug=J−∫ΩλTRdΩ,λh=−ψh

本页用 AhTψh=−gh（拟一维记 Jacobian 为 Jh），因此连续源项敏感度的符号是 λh=−ψh。守恒残差已做控制体积分，不再除以体积。强加边界行的乘子要单独处理。

The pages use AhTψh=−gh (the quasi-1-D Jacobian is Jh), hence the source-sensitivity sign λh=−ψh. Conservation residuals are already integrated: do not divide by volume. Multipliers of replaced boundary rows require separate treatment.

There is no −(∇·a)λ in the volume operator: the primal linearization is conservative. The objective is a boundary flux only, so the volume dual has zero right-hand side.

壁面目标决定 λ=1，而不是零

固定壁面值使 v=0，但法向导数 ∂nv 自由。目标变分与残差边界项合成 ν(λ−1)∂nv，所以必须令 λ=1。

The fixed wall value sets v=0, but ∂nv is free. Objective and residual boundary variations combine to ν(λ−1)∂nv, requiring λ=1.

远场必须按代码的总通量解释

Interpret the far field through its implemented total flux

令 b=β·n，这里 n 为单位外法向。入流数值通量除以面长后是 H；对应连续条件只有一个：q·n=H。不能同时把外侧状态解释成连续 Dirichlet 条件、又把“零扩散面通量”解释成另一个 Neumann 条件。

Let b=β·n with unit outward normal. Divide the incoming numerical flux by face length to obtain H. Its continuous interpretation is the single condition q·n=H, not both continuum Dirichlet data and a second Neumann condition from the “zero diffusive face flux”.

Inflow is a nonlinear Robin condition; only outflow reduces to ∂nu=0. Linearizing the boundary gives δ(q·n)=Huv, hence the far-field dual condition and derivatives:

这里 ϵ=0.8 保留在连续边界条件里；不能因为内部人工扩散随网格变小就把它从边界也删掉。

不一致时先检查什么：存在、唯一与稳定

What to check when results disagree: existence, uniqueness and stability

伴随不一致与伴随问题不适定不是同一件事。适定性要求在明确的函数空间、边界迹与数据范数下：解存在、唯一，并连续依赖于数据。比较之前先固定同一个原始问题、目标泛函、边界条件和乘子含义；边界层、有限参考误差、约束行乘子或错误的目标边界项，都可能造成差异，不能仅凭一张误差图判定不适定。

Adjoint inconsistency and an ill-posed adjoint problem are different diagnoses. Well-posedness requires existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence on data in specified function spaces, trace spaces and norms. First match the primal problem, functional, boundary conditions and multiplier interpretation. Boundary layers, finite reference error, constraint-row multipliers or an incorrect objective boundary term can all cause discrepancies; an error plot alone cannot diagnose ill-posedness.

在线性化状态及边界约束都已确定后，记原始线性算子为 D，伴随方程为 D*λ=g。若 D 是适当空间之间的有界同构，正确配对的对偶问题也有有界逆。若存在非零零模态 v，则可解性至少要求 g[v]=0；伴随自身有非零齐次解时，解就不唯一。无穷维情形还必须检查值域闭性／稳定估计，不能只数边界条件。

After fixing the linearized state and boundary constraints, denote the primal operator by D and the dual equation by D*λ=g. If D is a bounded isomorphism between suitable spaces, its correctly paired dual also has a bounded inverse. A nonzero primal null mode v requires at least g[v]=0 for solvability; a nonzero homogeneous dual mode makes the dual nonunique. In infinite dimensions one must also check closed range/stability estimates, not merely count boundary conditions.

Dv=0⇒g[v]=⟨λ,Dv⟩=0

非线性原始解存在唯一，也不自动保证其导数存在：还需要所选解支的局部可微性和线性化可逆性。下面讨论的都是已说明的光滑解支；不把有限网格上能解出的转置系统当作连续理论的替代。

Even existence and uniqueness of a nonlinear primal do not automatically ensure differentiability: local smoothness of the chosen solution branch and invertibility of its linearization are additional requirements. The discussion concerns the stated smooth branch; a solvable finite-grid transpose system is not a substitute for continuous analysis.

扩散改善了什么，又没有证明什么

What diffusion improves, and what it does not prove

ν>0 使域内算子具有椭圆主部；壁面 Dirichlet 与远场 Robin 条件共同定义连续边值问题。但“有扩散”本身不是任意系数下存在唯一性的证明，还要核对齐次核、边界系数及适用的能量／最大值原理条件。这里独立求解及参考加密提供了当前参数和解支的数值证据，没有证明所有参数都适定。

ν>0 gives the volume operator an elliptic principal part; wall Dirichlet and far-field Robin conditions define its boundary-value problem together. Diffusion alone does not prove existence and uniqueness for arbitrary coefficients: the homogeneous kernel, boundary coefficients and applicable energy/maximum-principle assumptions still matter. Independent solves and reference refinement provide numerical evidence for this parameter set and branch, not a theorem for all parameters.

把目标所要求的壁面伴随值 1 改成 0，可能仍能解出另一个适定问题，但它不再是本目标的伴随；这是推导／目标不匹配。远场同时施加状态值与法向导数则一般过约束。格点壁面约束乘子与重建场值不同又属于变量含义问题。这三类错误必须分别说明。

Changing the objective-required wall dual value from one to zero may still yield another well-posed problem, but not the adjoint of this objective: that is a derivation/functional mismatch. Prescribing both state and normal derivative at the far field is generally overdetermined. Comparing a node constraint multiplier with the reconstructed wall field is instead a variable-interpretation error. These are distinct diagnoses.

矩阵能求逆为什么还不够

Why an invertible matrix is not enough

即使每个有限网格上的 AhT 都可逆，也不保证在网格相关的正确加权范数中，其逆有与 h 无关的界。人工耗散或边界闭合可能让每个有限系统可解，却不能给出目标连续问题的稳定极限。要判断一致性，应先建立连续问题，再检查残差与目标的逼近、边界乘子的对应关系以及离散稳定性；若这些前提不成立，就把它保留为反例并准确指出失败环节。

Even if every finite-grid AhT is invertible, its inverse need not be bounded independently of h in the appropriate mesh-weighted norms. Artificial dissipation or boundary closure may make every finite system solvable without providing a stable limit for the intended continuous problem. Establish the continuous problem first, then check residual/functional approximation, boundary-multiplier interpretation and discrete stability. If a premise fails, retain the example and identify the failed premise explicitly.
