非结构网格格点格式的二维标量对流扩散方程离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of a two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation, on an unstructured node-centred scheme

算例与对流方程的格点页面是同一张环形（O 型）混合网格，方程多了一项常系数扩散 −ν∇2u（ν=0.5）。有了扩散，内圈物面才能施加 Dirichlet 壁面条件 u=uw=0；本页把它强施加：壁面节点的方程在边循环之后被整行换成 ui−uw。扩散通量要用节点梯度，这里用最小二乘来算，所以残差成了两遍循环：先算梯度，再算通量。目标函数是流进物面的总通量。对照阅读：同一方程的格心页面。The test case is the same annular (O-type) hybrid mesh as the node-centred advection page, but the equation gains a constant-coefficient diffusion term −ν∇2u (ν=0.5). With diffusion the inner body surface can carry a Dirichlet wall condition, u=uw=0, and this page imposes it strongly: after the edge loop, each wall node's equation is replaced as a whole by ui−uw. The diffusive flux needs nodal gradients, taken here by least squares, so the residual becomes two loops: gradients first, then fluxes. The objective is the total flux into the body. 网格：同一张混合网格，多了梯度模板

Mesh: the same hybrid grid, now with gradient stencils

算例网格

The test mesh

网格与对流页面第 1 节完全相同：3 圈节点、每圈 8 个，共 24 个节点；贴壁一层是 8 个四边形，外层是 16 个三角形。未知量放在节点上，控制体是中位对偶体。它的构造方法（在每个单元内把边中点与单元中心相连）在对流页面里有逐步演示，这里不再重复。网格有 48 条边，对应 48 个内部对偶面；另有 32 个边界半面（16 个在壁面上，16 个在远场上），所以边循环一共要走 80 个面。

The mesh is exactly the one of Section 1 of the advection page: 3 rings of 8 nodes, 24 nodes in all, with a layer of 8 quadrilaterals against the wall and 16 triangles outside it. The unknowns sit on the nodes and the control volumes are the median duals. How those are built (inside every cell, join the edge midpoints to the cell centre) is shown step by step on the advection page and is not repeated here. The 48 edges give 48 interior dual faces, and there are 32 boundary half-faces besides (16 on the wall, 16 on the far field), so the edge loop visits 80 faces.

变的是节点的角色（）。内圈 8 个节点落在物面上，而物面现在是 Dirichlet 壁面，这些节点的值是给定的 uw。它们仍然算未知量，在 24 个里占 8 个位置，只是它们的方程要被换掉（第 3 节）。外圈 8 个节点在远场上，其余 8 个是内部节点。

What changes is the role of the nodes (). The 8 inner-ring nodes lie on the body, which is now a Dirichlet wall, so their values are prescribed: uw. They still count as unknowns and take 8 of the 24 slots; only their equations will be swapped (Section 3). The 8 outer-ring nodes lie on the far field, and the remaining 8 are interior nodes.

新的几何量：边向量与梯度模板

New geometry: the edge vector and the gradient stencil

对流页面的边循环在每个面上只需要两样几何量：法向 n 与它的长度 |n|。扩散项还要两样。第一样是边向量 d=xj−xi，它连接对偶面两侧的两个未知量，两点差 uj−ui 就是沿它量出来的。第二样是每个节点的梯度模板，即最小二乘梯度要用哪些邻居的值。本页取最简单的选择：边邻居，也就是和该节点共用一条边的节点。壁面节点有 3 个边邻居，中圈节点有 5 个，外圈节点有 4 个。

The edge loop of the advection page needed two geometric quantities per face: the normal n and its length |n|. Diffusion needs two more. The first is the edge vector d=xj−xi, which joins the two unknowns on either side of the dual face; the two-point difference uj−ui is measured along it. The second is each node's gradient stencil, the neighbours whose values the least-squares gradient uses. This page takes the simplest choice, the edge neighbours: the nodes that share an edge with the node. A wall node has 3 of them, a middle-ring node 5, an outer-ring node 4.

要离散的方程与对偶面上的通量

The equation to discretise, and the flux on a dual face

要离散的方程

The equation to discretise

求解域 Ω 是物体与远场圆之间的环形区域。未知量 u(x,y) 满足定常的对流扩散方程：

The domain Ω is the annulus between the body and the far-field circle. The unknown u(x,y) obeys the steady advection–diffusion equation

∇·(12u2β)−∇·(ν∇u)=0,β=(1,0.5),ν=0.5

第一项就是对流页面的非线性守恒律，对流通量为 12u2β；第二项是常系数扩散，扩散通量为 −ν∇u。扩散把方程从一阶变成了二阶，于是边界上每一处都需要一个条件，物面上可以直接给定 u 的值（第 3 节）。

The first term is the nonlinear conservation law of the advection page, with convective flux 12u2β; the second is constant-coefficient diffusion, with diffusive flux −ν∇u. Diffusion turns the equation from first order into second order, so every part of the boundary now takes one condition, and on the body the value of u itself can be prescribed (Section 3).

有限体积离散

Finite-volume discretisation

在节点 i 的控制体 Vi 上积分，再用散度定理把体积分化成边界积分：

Integrate over the control volume Vi of node i and turn the volume integral into a boundary integral with the divergence theorem:

∮∂Vi(12u2β·n−ν∇u·n)ds=0⇒Ri(u)=Σe∋i±(hc,e+hv,e)=0

求和取遍节点 i 的所有对偶面 e。每个面上的通量只算一次，然后以相反符号分给两侧：i 一侧取 +，j 一侧取 −，所以离散格式是守恒的。hc 是对流通量，hv 是扩散通量。

The sum runs over the dual faces e of node i. Each face's flux is computed once and handed to its two sides with opposite signs, + for side i and − for side j, so the scheme is conservative. hc is the convective flux and hv the diffusive one.

对流通量：与对流页面相同

The convective flux: as on the advection page

hc=14vn(uL2+uR2)−ε|n|(uR−uL),vn=β·n,ε=0.8

中心平均加常数耗散，外加远场上由 vn 的符号冻结的入流、出流开关。它的推导在对流页面第 2、3 节，这里原样沿用。

A central average plus constant dissipation, with the inflow/outflow switch on the far field frozen by the sign of vn. It is derived in Sections 2 and 3 of the advection page and is used here unchanged.

扩散通量：面上的法向导数

The diffusive flux: the normal derivative on the face

扩散通量 hv=−ν∇u·n 要的是面上的法向导数。面两侧只有两个节点值 ui、uj，而二维梯度有两个分量，只靠这两个值不够；除非对偶面恰好垂直于边（n 与 d 平行），那时两点差就是法向导数。一般网格上 n 与 d 并不平行（），做法是把 n 分成沿 d 的部分和垂直于 d 的部分：

The diffusive flux hv=−ν∇u·n needs the normal derivative on the face. Only two nodal values sit either side of it, ui and uj, while a two-dimensional gradient has two components, so those two values are not enough, unless the dual face happens to be perpendicular to the edge (n parallel to d), in which case the two-point difference is the normal derivative. On a general mesh n and d are not parallel (), and the way out is to split n into a part along d and a part perpendicular to it:

n=pd+k,p=d·n|d|2,k=n−pd(k·d=0)

沿 d 的部分用两点差 ∇u·d≈uj−ui；垂直的部分 k 用两端节点梯度的平均 ḡ=12(Gi+Gj)：

The part along d uses the two-point difference ∇u·d≈uj−ui; the perpendicular part k uses the average of the two nodal gradients, ḡ=12(Gi+Gj):

∇u·n≈p(uj−ui)+ḡ·k,hv=−ν[p(uR−uL)+ḡ·k]

它也常写成先修正平均梯度、再投影到 n 上：ḡ·n−(ḡ·d−Δu)p，展开就是上式。两点部分让 i 与 j 直接耦合，是格式稳定的来源；梯度部分补上 n 偏离 d 的那一块。两部分对线性场都精确，所以整个 hv 对线性场精确。

It is often written as a correction of the averaged gradient before projecting it on n, ḡ·n−(ḡ·d−Δu)p; expanded, that is the line above. The two-point part couples i and j directly and is where the scheme's stability comes from; the gradient part makes up for n leaning away from d. Both parts are exact on linear fields, so the whole hv is.

节点梯度：最小二乘

Nodal gradients: least squares

节点 i 的梯度 Gi 取为让线性模型 ui+G·ds 最贴近模板中各邻居值 us 的那个 G（ds=xs−xi）：

The gradient Gi of node i is the G for which the linear model ui+G·ds comes closest to the neighbour values us of the stencil (ds=xs−xi):

Gi=argminGΣs(us−ui−G·ds)2⇒Gi=Σswis(us−ui),wis=Mi−1ds,Mi=ΣsdsdsT

权重 wis 只依赖几何，每张网格算一次。因为 ΣswisdsT=Mi−1Mi=I，线性场的梯度被精确复现。本页不加距离权（非加权最小二乘）。梯度对 u 是线性的，这一点在第 7 节会用到。在 里可以选一个节点，看它的模板、矩阵 Mi、各个权重，以及对三种场取出的梯度。

The weights wis depend on the geometry only and are computed once per mesh. Because ΣswisdsT=Mi−1Mi=I, the gradient of a linear field comes out exactly. This page uses no distance weighting (unweighted least squares). The gradient is linear in u, which Section 7 will use. ν 为什么取 0.5。耗散项 −ε|n|(uR−uL) 与扩散的两点部分 −νp(uR−uL) 形式相同，它就像一个数值扩散系数 ε|n|/p。在这张只有 24 个节点的粗网格上，这个系数在各内部面上介于 0.24 与 1.58 之间，每加密一次减半。在收敛解上，ν=0.5 时各内部面耗散通量的绝对值之和是扩散通量的 1.16 倍，两者相当；若取 ν=0.1，这个比值是 5.6，物理扩散只是数值耗散上的一点修正。第 12 节的精度阶研究会再遇到它。

Why ν=0.5. The dissipation −ε|n|(uR−uL) has the same form as the two-point part of diffusion, −νp(uR−uL), so it acts like a numerical diffusivity ε|n|/p. On this 24-node coarse mesh that diffusivity lies between 0.24 and 1.58 over the interior faces, and it halves with every refinement. At the converged solution with ν=0.5, the absolute dissipative fluxes over the interior faces sum to 1.16 times the diffusive ones: the two are comparable. With ν=0.1 the ratio would be 5.6, and the physical diffusion only a small correction to the numerical one. The order study of Section 12 meets it again.

Section 3

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈：强施加的 Dirichlet 壁面

Inner ring: a strongly imposed Dirichlet wall

物面上 u=uw=0。先看壁面节点 n1 的控制体（）：它有 3 个内部对偶面，分别通往边邻居 n2、n8、n9，另外还有两个壁面半面。如果照常写守恒方程，就是“所有这些面的通量之和为零”。壁面半面上的通量是物面吸收（或放出）的量，事先并不知道：它就是让 u1 保持为 uw 所需要的那个量。由于 uw=0，对流通量 12u2β·n 在物面上为零，穿过物面的只有扩散通量。

On the body u=uw=0. Look first at the control volume of wall node n1 (): it has 3 interior dual faces, towards its edge neighbours n2, n8 and n9, and two wall half-faces. Written as usual, its conservation equation says that the fluxes through all these faces sum to zero. The flux through the wall half-faces is what the body absorbs (or gives off), and it is not known in advance: it is whatever it takes to hold u1 at uw. Because uw=0, the convective flux 12u2β·n vanishes on the body, and only diffusion crosses it.

Dirichlet 条件有两种施加方式。弱施加：把 uw 写进壁面半面上的通量公式，方程照常组装；格心页面只能这样做，因为那里物面上没有未知量。强施加：不写壁面节点的守恒方程，而是把这一行整个换成

A Dirichlet condition can be imposed in two ways. Weakly: write uw into the flux formula of the wall half-faces and assemble the equations as usual; the cell-centred page can only do this, since it has no unknown on the body. Strongly: do not write the wall nodes' conservation equations at all, and replace each of those rows as a whole by

Rbc,i(u)=ui−uw=0,i∈W={1,…,8}

本页用强施加。它的好处是 ui=uw 在收敛解上严格成立，而不只是在网格加密的极限下成立，而且那个未知的壁面通量根本用不到。代价有两个：壁面通量要事后找回来（第 6 节）；前向和伴随都要为这几行做专门的处理（第 7、8 节）。

This page imposes the wall strongly. The gain is that ui=uw holds exactly at the converged solution, not merely in the limit of refinement, and the unknown wall flux is never needed. There are two prices: the wall flux has to be recovered afterwards (Section 6), and the forward and the adjoint code both need special treatment of these rows (Sections 7 and 8).

在代码里：循环之后替换

In the code: replace after the loop

实现很简单。边循环照常把所有内部对偶面的通量累加进 r，壁面半面直接跳过：它们的通量只会进入壁面节点的那一行，而那一行马上就要被丢掉。循环结束后，对每个壁面节点令 ri←ui−uw。在 Jacobian 里，这一行变成单位行 eiT：对角元为 1，其余为 0。替换后的残差与 Jacobian 记为 Rbc 和 Abc；替换之前的记为 R 和 A，第 6 节还要用到它们。

The implementation is short. The edge loop accumulates the fluxes of all interior dual faces into r as usual and simply skips the wall half-faces: their fluxes would only go into the wall nodes' rows, which are about to be thrown away. After the loop, set ri←ui−uw for every wall node. In the Jacobian this row becomes the unit row eiT: 1 on the diagonal, 0 elsewhere. The replaced residual and Jacobian are written Rbc and Abc; the ones before the replacement are R and A, and Section 6 still needs them.

外圈：远场

Outer ring: the far field

对流部分与对流页面相同：远场半面按 vn=β·n 的符号分成入流和出流（在初始几何上冻结），入流取 uR=u∞=1，出流取 uR=uL。扩散部分取零通量 ν∇u·n=0，也就是齐次 Neumann 条件，所以远场半面只带对流通量。16 个远场半面中，8 个入流，8 个出流。

The convective part is as on the advection page: the far-field half-faces are split into inflow and outflow by the sign of vn=β·n (frozen at the initial geometry), with uR=u∞=1 on inflow and uR=uL on outflow. The diffusive part takes zero flux, ν∇u·n=0, a homogeneous Neumann condition, so a far-field half-face carries the convective flux only. Of the 16 far-field half-faces, 8 are inflow and 8 outflow.

为什么对流页面没有这种壁面。纯对流方程是一阶的，边界上只能在特征进入区域的地方给数据；物面是闭合曲线，特征速度沿物面必然有正有负，在特征离开区域的那部分物面上再规定 u，就多给了一个条件（见对流页面第 3 节）。扩散项是二阶的，它让整条物面上都可以、也必须给一个条件，Dirichlet 壁面由此成为适定的。

Why the advection page has no such wall. A purely convective equation is first order: boundary data can be given only where characteristics enter the domain. The body is a closed curve, the characteristic speed takes both signs along it, and prescribing u where characteristics leave the domain is one condition too many (see Section 3 of the advection page). The diffusion term is second order: it lets, and requires, the whole body take one condition, and that is what makes the Dirichlet wall well posed.

残差 = 两遍循环：先梯度，后通量

The residual = two loops: gradients first, then fluxes

对流页面的残差是一个边循环：每个面收集两侧状态、算一维通量、分发到两侧。扩散通量要用面两端的节点梯度，而节点梯度又要用邻居的值，所以不能在同一遍循环里边算边用：必须先把所有节点的梯度算完，才能开始算通量。残差因此成了两遍循环，再加最后的替换：

The residual of the advection page is one edge loop: each face gathers the two states, computes the one-dimensional flux and scatters it to both sides. The diffusive flux needs the nodal gradients at both ends of the face, and a nodal gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the gradients cannot be computed and used in the same sweep: the gradients of all nodes must be finished before the first flux is computed. The residual therefore becomes two loops, plus the replacement at the end:

  • 第一遍（梯度循环）：遍历 48 条边。每条边收集差值 Δ=uj−ui，分发到两端节点的梯度里：Gi←Gi+wijΔ，Gj←Gj+wji(−Δ)。循环结束时，每个 Gi=Σswis(us−ui) 恰好完成。这也是一个“收集、计算、分发”的边循环，只是分发的目标是一个二维向量数组。
  • Pass 1 (the gradient loop): visit the 48 edges. Each edge gathers the difference Δ=uj−ui and scatters it into the gradients at its two ends: Gi←Gi+wijΔ and Gj←Gj+wji(−Δ). When the loop ends, every Gi=Σswis(us−ui) is complete. It is again a gather–compute–scatter edge loop; only its target is an array of two-dimensional vectors.
  • 第二遍（通量循环）：遍历 80 个面。每个面收集两侧的状态与梯度，算 h=hc+hv，分发到 ri 与 rj。壁面半面跳过。
  • Pass 2 (the flux loop): visit the 80 faces. Each face gathers the states and the gradients on both sides, computes h=hc+hv and scatters it into ri and rj. The wall half-faces are skipped.
  • 替换：对 8 个壁面节点令 ri←ui−uw。
  • Replacement: set ri←ui−uw for the 8 wall nodes.

The safest way to derive the adjoint rigorously is to write the forward mode as a product of linear maps and transpose them one by one. Pass 1 is a 2N×N linear operator L: Gd=Lv, whose i-th block row is Σswis(es−ei)T. Pass 2 maps the states and the gradients together to the output: rd=Buv+BgGd. Bu collects the coefficients acting on the states directly: on every face, row i gets cL+νp in column i and cR−νp in column j, and row j gets their negatives. Bg collects those acting on the gradients: each face writes −12νkT into row i and +12νkT into row j, both into the two blocks Gi and Gj. Last comes the replacement: D zeroes the wall rows and E is 1 on the wall rows' diagonal only, so D+E=I. Hence

  • +Ew：循环结束之后，把原来的壁面分量加回去：yi+=wi。
  • +Ew: after the loops, add the original wall components back: yi+=wi.

把三个函数逐项并排。顺序是整件事的关键。A=Bu+BgL 先做 L，所以 AT 最后做 LT；Abc=DA+E 在 A 之后才用 D，所以转置后 D 要在 AT 之前用。前向是循环之后再覆盖：D(Av)+Ev；伴随必须循环之前清零、循环之后加回。照搬前向的做法，在循环之后令 yi←wi，算出来的是 D(ATw)+Ew，它不是转置：下面的点积测试会失败，第一组随机向量的相对误差是 0.18。

sets the three functions side by side. The order is the whole point. A=Bu+BgL applies L first, so AT applies LT last; Abc=DA+E applies D after A, so after transposing D comes before AT. The forward code overwrites after the loop, D(Av)+Ev; the adjoint must zero before the loop and add back after it. Copying the forward recipe, setting yi←wi after the loop, computes D(ATw)+Ew, which is not the transpose: the dot-product test below fails, with a relative error of 0.18 on the first random pair.

逐句执行 residual_b。三行数组依次是输出 y、梯度权重 Gb 和输入 w̃。第二遍倒着走时，每个面只把状态槽位直接写进 y，梯度槽位先存进 Gb；等所有面走完，第一遍倒着走，才把 Gb 经由权重 wis 送回 y。选壁面节点的 e1 作种子，可以看到它在循环前被清零、整个循环什么也不写，最后只在 y1 上加回 1：AbcT 的第 1 列只有对角线上的 1。

executes residual_b statement by statement. The three arrays are the output y, the gradient weights Gb and the input w̃. While pass 2 runs backwards, each face writes only its state slots into y directly and parks its gradient slots in Gb; only when every face is done does pass 1 run backwards and send Gb into y through the weights wis. Seed with the wall node's e1 to see it zeroed before the loop, the whole loop write nothing, and a single 1 added back into y1 at the end: column 1 of AbcT holds only the 1 on its diagonal.

What φ means: how much of a unit source ends up in the body

它的物理意义很直接。在节点 i 的控制体里加一个源项 si，则 dJ/dsi=−φi：−φi 就是这个单位源最终流进物面的比例，其余的从远场流走。壁面节点上是 1（全部）；中圈节点在 0.71 到 0.79 之间；外圈节点在 0.49 到 0.68 之间。对流页面上，伴随场主要集中在物体上游：不在壁面上的下游节点只有 0.01 到 0.07 左右，那是耗散项带过去的；这里有了物理扩散，φ 在所有非壁面节点上都在 −0.79 与 −0.49 之间，下游也不例外。

Its physical meaning is direct. Add a source si in the control volume of node i; then dJ/dsi=−φi, so −φi is the fraction of that unit source that ends up in the body, the rest leaving through the far field. It is 1 on the wall nodes (all of it), between 0.71 and 0.79 on the middle ring, and between 0.49 and 0.68 on the outer ring. 验证：dJ/dX 对有限差分

Verification: dJ/dX against finite differences

上一节把 dJ/dX 推了出来：伴随只解一次线性方程，就拿到全部 48 个分量。这一节只做一件事：验证它是对的。判据是最朴素的那个，逐个坐标分量做中心差分：

The previous section derived dJ/dX: the adjoint solves one linear equation and gets all 48 components. This section does one thing only: check that it is right. The criterion is the plainest one there is, a central difference taken coordinate by coordinate:

dJdXk≈J(Xk+h)−J(Xk−h)2h

关键在于 J(Xk±h) 是完整的目标函数：每次都要改动网格，重建全部几何量（对偶面法向、边向量、p、k、最小二乘权重），重新解一次非线性的原始方程，再算反力通量。所以这里验证的是全导数，状态对几何的响应也算在内。代价也正因如此：中心差分每个分量要两次非线性求解，一共 96 次，而伴随只用了一次线性求解。只往一侧扰动的前向差分把基准值 J(X) 共用，一共 49 次，代价是截断误差从 O(h2) 变成 O(h)。

What matters is that J(Xk±h) is the complete objective: every evaluation moves the mesh, rebuilds all the geometry (dual-face normals, edge vectors, p, k, least-squares weights), solves the nonlinear primal again, and only then computes the reaction flux. So what is verified is the total derivative, the response of the state to the geometry included. That is also what it costs: two nonlinear solves per component, 96 in all, against the single linear solve of the adjoint. A forward difference, perturbing to one side only, shares the baseline J(X) and needs 49, at the price of an O(h) truncation error instead of O(h2).

Summary

与对流页面相比多了什么

What this page adds to the advection page

  • 两遍循环。扩散通量要用节点梯度，最小二乘梯度又要用邻居的值，所以残差先走一遍边循环算梯度，再走一遍面循环算通量。前向按同样的顺序走，伴随把顺序倒过来。
  • Two loops. The diffusive flux needs nodal gradients, and a least-squares gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the residual runs one edge loop for the gradients and then one face loop for the fluxes. The forward code runs them in the same order; the adjoint reverses it.
  • 两个 Jacobian。精确 Jacobian 每行伸到邻居的邻居（264 个非零元），从不形成，只由 residual_d、residual_b 作用；求解器用只含相邻节点的一阶近似 Ãbc（96 个非零元），Newton 法由此变成线性收敛的亏量修正，谱半径 0.0485。
  • Two Jacobians. The exact Jacobian reaches the neighbours of neighbours in every row (264 non-zeros); it is never formed, only applied by residual_d and residual_b. The solver uses the first-order approximation Ãbc, adjacent nodes only (96 non-zeros), which turns Newton into a linearly converging defect correction, with spectral radius 0.0485.
  • 强施加的壁面。壁面行在循环之后整行替换。由 Abc=DA+E 可知：前向在循环之后覆盖，伴随必须循环之前清零、循环之后加回；照搬前向的做法，点积测试就会失败。
  • A strongly imposed wall. The wall rows are replaced as a whole after the loop. From Abc=DA+E: the forward code overwrites after the loop, while the adjoint must zero before the loop and add back after it; copy the forward recipe and the dot-product test fails.
  • 反力形式的目标函数。流进物面的通量从被替换之前的壁面行里取回：J=−Σi∈WRi(u)。它与远场通量精确平衡；单侧差分公式在本网格上只有它的 33%。
  • An objective in reaction form. The flux into the body is recovered from the wall rows before their replacement: J=−Σi∈WRi(u). It balances the far-field flux exactly; the one-sided formula gives only 33% of it on this mesh.
  • 同速的伴随求解。伴随用精确的 residual_b 算残差、用 ÃbcT 作近似逆，两个迭代矩阵的谱相同，10 轮到 10−13。

精度阶

Order of accuracy

伴随给出的是离散格式的精确导数，与格式本身精度几阶无关；但格式的精度决定了这个导数离连续问题有多远。用构造解来测：取 ue=12+14sin(0.9x+0.3)cos(0.7y−0.2)，把它代入方程得到源项，壁面与远场都用它的值，在由基础网格一致细分（每个单元一分为四）得到的四层网格上求解（）。只有扩散时（β=0，ε=0），两种格式在 L2 范数下都是二阶：本页 1.99、2.09、2.08，格心页面 1.94、2.02、2.01。完整方程只有一阶：耗散项 ε|n|(uR−uL) 相当于数值扩散系数 ε|n|/p，它随网格尺寸线性减小，是一个 O(h) 的误差，所以逐层的阶从 0.53、0.71 升到 0.82，向 1 靠拢。

The adjoint gives the exact derivative of the discrete scheme, whatever its order of accuracy; but that order decides how far the derivative is from the continuous problem's. It is measured with a manufactured solution: take ue=12+14sin(0.9x+0.3)cos(0.7y−0.2), substitute it into the equation to get a source term, use its values on the wall and the far field, and solve on four meshes obtained from the base mesh by uniform subdivision, every cell into four (). With diffusion alone (β=0, ε=0) both schemes are second order in the L2 norm: 1.99, 2.09 and 2.08 here, 1.94, 2.02 and 2.01 on the cell-centred page. The full equation is first order only: the dissipation ε|n|(uR−uL) acts as a numerical diffusivity ε|n|/p that shrinks linearly with the mesh size, an O(h) error, so the order climbs level by level from 0.53 through 0.71 to 0.82, towards 1.
