非结构网格格心格式的二维标量对流扩散方程离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of a two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation, on an unstructured cell-centred scheme

算例与对流方程的格心页面是同一张环形（O 型）混合网格，方程多了一项常系数扩散 −ν∇2u（ν=0.5）。有了扩散，内圈物面才能施加 Dirichlet 壁面条件 u=uw=0。格心格式的未知量在单元里，物面上没有未知量，所以壁面值只能弱施加：它进入壁面面上的通量，也作为一个点进入壁面单元的最小二乘梯度。扩散通量要用单元梯度，残差因此成了两遍循环：先算梯度，再算通量。目标函数是流进物面的总通量。对照阅读：同一方程的格点页面，那里的壁面是强施加的。The test case is the same annular (O-type) hybrid mesh as the cell-centred advection page, but the equation gains a constant-coefficient diffusion term −ν∇2u (ν=0.5). With diffusion the inner body surface can carry a Dirichlet wall condition, u=uw=0. A cell-centred scheme keeps its unknowns in the cells and has none on the body, so the wall value can only be imposed weakly: it enters the flux on the wall faces, and it is one more point in the wall cells' least-squares gradients. The diffusive flux needs cell gradients, so the residual becomes two loops: gradients first, then fluxes. The objective is the total flux into the body. Read alongside: the node-centred page for the same equation, where the wall is imposed strongly.

第 1 节
Section 1

网格：同一张混合网格，多了梯度模板

Mesh: the same hybrid grid, now with gradient stencils

算例网格

The test mesh

网格与对流页面第 1 节完全相同：24 个节点，贴壁一层是 8 个四边形，外层是 16 个三角形，一共 24 个单元。未知量放在单元里，控制体就是单元本身。每条网格边就是一个面：32 个内部面，8 个壁面面，8 个远场面，一共 48 个面。

The mesh is exactly the one of Section 1 of the advection page: 24 nodes, with a layer of 8 quadrilaterals against the wall and 16 triangles outside it, 24 cells in all. The unknowns sit in the cells, and each control volume is the cell itself. Every mesh edge is a face: 32 interior faces, 8 wall faces and 8 far-field faces, 48 faces in all.

物面上没有未知量：它只是 8 个壁面面，各自属于一个贴壁的四边形单元（）。壁面值 uw 因此不能像格点格式那样直接写进某个未知量，只能通过这 8 个面进入方程（第 3 节）。

There are no unknowns on the body: it is just the 8 wall faces, each belonging to one quadrilateral against the wall (). So the wall value uw cannot be written into an unknown, as the node-centred scheme does; it can only enter the equations through those 8 faces (Section 3).

新的几何量：形心间向量与梯度模板

New geometry: the centroid-to-centroid vector and the gradient stencil

扩散项要两样新的几何量。第一样是面两侧未知量之间的向量 d：内部面上从左单元形心指向右单元形心，壁面面上从壁面单元形心指向面中点（壁面值 uw 就放在那里）。第二样是每个单元的梯度模板：它的面邻居的形心，再加上它自己壁面面的中点（如果有的话）。远场面不带数据（扩散通量为零），不进入模板。于是壁面单元有 4 个模板点（3 个面邻居加 1 个壁面中点），内层三角形有 3 个，外层三角形只有 2 个：两个点在二维里正好确定一个梯度。

Diffusion needs two new geometric quantities. The first is the vector d between the unknowns on either side of a face: on an interior face from the left cell's centroid to the right cell's, on a wall face from the wall cell's centroid to the face midpoint (where the wall value uw sits). The second is each cell's gradient stencil: the centroids of its face neighbours, plus the midpoint of its own wall face if it has one. Far-field faces carry no data (their diffusive flux is zero) and stay out of the stencils. So a wall cell has 4 stencil points (3 face neighbours and 1 wall midpoint), an inner triangle 3, and an outer triangle only 2: in two dimensions two points fix a gradient exactly.

要离散的方程与面上的通量

The equation to discretise, and the flux on a face

要离散的方程

The equation to discretise

求解域 Ω 是物体与远场圆之间的环形区域。未知量 u(x,y) 满足定常的对流扩散方程：

The domain Ω is the annulus between the body and the far-field circle. The unknown u(x,y) obeys the steady advection–diffusion equation

∇·(12u2β)−∇·(ν∇u)=0,β=(1,0.5),ν=0.5

第一项是对流页面的非线性守恒律，第二项是常系数扩散。扩散把方程变成二阶，于是整条物面都可以给定 u 的值（第 3 节）。

The first term is the nonlinear conservation law of the advection page, the second constant-coefficient diffusion. Diffusion makes the equation second order, so the whole body can take a prescribed value of u (Section 3).

有限体积离散

Finite-volume discretisation

在单元 c 上积分，用散度定理化成边界积分：

Integrate over cell c and turn the volume integral into a boundary integral with the divergence theorem:

∮∂c(12u2β·n−ν∇u·n)ds=0⇒Rc(u)=Σf∋c±(hc,f+hv,f)=0

求和取遍单元 c 的所有面，包括它的壁面面与远场面。每个面上的通量只算一次，以相反符号分给左右两个单元（左取 +、右取 −），格式是守恒的。

The sum runs over all faces of cell c, its wall and far-field faces included. Each face's flux is computed once and handed to its left and right cells with opposite signs (+ left, − right), so the scheme is conservative.

对流通量：与对流页面相同

The convective flux: as on the advection page

hc=14vn(uL2+uR2)−ε|n|(uR−uL),vn=β·n,ε=0.8

中心平均加常数耗散，远场上按冻结的入流、出流取右状态，推导在对流页面第 2、3 节。壁面面是例外，那里状态已知，见第 3 节。

A central average plus constant dissipation, with the right state on the far field taken by the frozen inflow/outflow switch, derived in Sections 2 and 3 of the advection page. The wall faces are the exception, since the state there is known; see Section 3.

扩散通量：面上的法向导数

The diffusive flux: the normal derivative on the face

扩散通量 hv=−ν∇u·n 要面上的法向导数，但面两侧只有两个单元值。只有当 n 与形心连线 d 平行时，两点差才就是法向导数；一般网格上并不平行（）。于是把 n 分成沿 d 的部分和垂直于 d 的部分：

The diffusive flux hv=−ν∇u·n needs the normal derivative on the face, but only two cell values sit either side of it. The two-point difference is the normal derivative only when n is parallel to the centroid-to-centroid vector d, which on a general mesh it is not (). So n is split into a part along d and a part perpendicular to it:

n=pd+k,p=d·n|d|2,k=n−pd(k·d=0)
∇u·n≈p(uR−uL)+ḡ·k,hv=−ν[p(uR−uL)+ḡ·k],ḡ=12(GL+GR)

沿 d 的部分用两点差，垂直的部分用两侧单元梯度的平均。两点部分让左右两个单元直接耦合，梯度部分补上 n 偏离 d 的那一块；两部分对线性场都精确。

The part along d takes the two-point difference, the perpendicular part the average of the two cell gradients. The two-point part couples the left and right cells directly, the gradient part makes up for n leaning away from d; both are exact on linear fields.

单元梯度：最小二乘

Cell gradients: least squares

单元 c 的梯度 Gc 取为让线性模型 uc+G·ds 最贴近各模板点上的值 us 的那个 G；模板点是面邻居的形心，或壁面面的中点（那里的值是 uw）：

The gradient Gc of cell c is the G for which the linear model uc+G·ds comes closest to the values us at the stencil points, which are the centroids of the face neighbours, or the midpoint of a wall face (where the value is uw):

Gc=Σswcs(us−uc),wcs=Mc−1ds,Mc=ΣsdsdsT

权重只依赖几何，线性场被精确复现（ΣswcsdsT=I），本页不加距离权。壁面中点让壁面值进入了壁面单元的梯度，这是弱施加的第二个入口。梯度对 u 是线性的；对 uw 也是，但 uw 是常数，求导时没有它的份（第 7 节）。 可以逐个单元查看。

The weights depend on the geometry only, linear fields come out exactly (ΣswcsdsT=I), and there is no distance weighting. The wall midpoints bring the wall value into the wall cells' gradients: the second door through which the wall is imposed weakly. The gradient is linear in u; in uw too, but uw is a constant and has no share in any derivative (Section 7). shows it cell by cell.

ν 为什么取 0.5。耗散项 −ε|n|(uR−uL) 与扩散的两点部分形式相同，相当于数值扩散系数 ε|n|/p；在这张粗网格的内部面与壁面面上，它介于 0.185 与 0.648 之间，每加密一次减半。在收敛解上，ν=0.5 时各内部面耗散通量的绝对值之和是扩散通量的 0.87 倍；若取 ν=0.1，这个比值是 4.6，物理扩散会被数值耗散淹没。

Why ν=0.5. The dissipation −ε|n|(uR−uL) has the form of the two-point part of diffusion, a numerical diffusivity ε|n|/p; on the interior and wall faces of this coarse mesh it lies between 0.185 and 0.648, and it halves with every refinement. At the converged solution with ν=0.5, the absolute dissipative fluxes over the interior faces sum to 0.87 times the diffusive ones; with ν=0.1 the ratio would be 4.6, and the physical diffusion would be swamped by the numerical one.

第 3 节
Section 3

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈：弱施加的 Dirichlet 壁面

Inner ring: a weakly imposed Dirichlet wall

物面上 u=uw=0。格心格式在物面上没有未知量，壁面值只能从壁面面进入壁面单元的守恒方程（）。壁面面上的状态已知，右状态就是 uw，通量是

On the body u=uw=0. A cell-centred scheme has no unknown on the body, so the wall value can only enter the wall cell's conservation equation through the wall face (). The state on a wall face is known, the right state is uw itself, and the flux is

hw=12uw2vn−ν[p(uw−uL)+GL·k],d=xmid−xL

对流部分直接用已知的壁面状态：12uw2vn，没有平均，也没有耗散；这里 uw=0，所以它为零，穿过物面的只有扩散。扩散部分还是同一个修正公式，只是 d 从壁面单元的形心指向壁面面的中点，而平均梯度就是壁面单元自己的梯度 GL。此外，壁面值还作为一个模板点进入 GL。壁面就从这两处进入方程，都在普通的循环里：没有哪一行是特殊的，24 个方程全是守恒方程。

The convective part uses the known wall state directly, 12uw2vn, with no average and no dissipation; here uw=0 makes it zero, and only diffusion crosses the body. The diffusive part is the same corrected formula, except that d runs from the wall cell's centroid to the wall face's midpoint and the averaged gradient is the wall cell's own GL. Besides, the wall value enters GL as a stencil point. Those are the wall's two doors into the equations, both inside the ordinary loops: no row is special, and all 24 equations are conservation equations.

与格点页面对照：那里壁面节点上 ui=uw 严格成立，但壁面通量要从被替换的行里找回来；这里单元形心上的值不等于 uw，壁面值是经由通量“拉”过去的，只在网格加密的极限下成立。反过来，壁面通量本身就是残差里的一个普通面通量，直接就是目标函数（第 6 节）。

Compared with the node-centred page: there ui=uw holds exactly at the wall nodes, but the wall flux has to be recovered from the replaced rows; here the values at the cell centroids are not uw, the wall value is pulled in through the flux and holds only in the limit of refinement. In return, the wall flux is an ordinary face flux of the residual, and it is directly the objective (Section 6).

外圈：远场

Outer ring: the far field

对流部分与对流页面相同：远场面按 vn 的符号分成入流和出流（在初始几何上冻结），入流取 uR=u∞=1，出流取 uR=uL。扩散部分取零通量，即齐次 Neumann 条件。8 个远场面中，4 个入流，4 个出流。

The convective part is as on the advection page: the far-field faces are split into inflow and outflow by the sign of vn (frozen at the initial geometry), with uR=u∞=1 on inflow and uR=uL on outflow. The diffusive part takes zero flux, a homogeneous Neumann condition. Of the 8 far-field faces, 4 are inflow and 4 outflow.

为什么对流页面没有这种壁面。纯对流方程是一阶的，边界上只能在特征进入区域的地方给数据，而特征速度沿闭合的物面必然有正有负（见对流页面第 3 节）。扩散项是二阶的，它让整条物面都可以、也必须给一个条件。

Why the advection page has no such wall. A purely convective equation is first order: boundary data can be given only where characteristics enter the domain, and along a closed body the characteristic speed takes both signs (see Section 3 of the advection page). The diffusion term is second order: it lets, and requires, the whole body take one condition.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 两遍循环：先梯度，后通量

The residual = two loops: gradients first, then fluxes

扩散通量要用面两侧单元的梯度，而单元梯度又要用邻居的值，所以必须先把所有单元的梯度算完，才能开始算通量。残差因此成了两遍面循环：

The diffusive flux needs the gradients of the cells on either side of a face, and a cell gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the gradients of all cells must be finished before the first flux is computed. The residual therefore becomes two face loops:

  • 第一遍（梯度循环）：遍历 32 个内部面和 8 个壁面面。每个面收集差值 Δ（壁面面上是 uw−uL），分发到两侧单元的梯度里：GL←GL+wLRΔ，GR←GR+wRL(−Δ)；壁面面只有左侧。远场面不参与。
  • Pass 1 (the gradient loop): visit the 32 interior faces and the 8 wall faces. Each face gathers the difference Δ (uw−uL on a wall face) and scatters it into the gradients on both sides: GL←GL+wLRΔ and GR←GR+wRL(−Δ); a wall face has a left side only. Far-field faces take no part.
  • 第二遍（通量循环）：遍历全部 48 个面，收集两侧的状态与梯度，算 h=hc+hv，分发到 rL 与 rR。壁面面也在这里，它的通量照常进入壁面单元的方程。
  • Pass 2 (the flux loop): visit all 48 faces, gather the states and gradients on both sides, compute h=hc+hv and scatter it into rL and rR. The wall faces are here too, their fluxes entering the wall cells' equations as usual.

没有第三步：弱施加不替换任何一行。 把三个函数画成数据流：前向走同样的顺序，伴随把顺序倒过来。

There is no third step: weak imposition replaces no row. draws the three functions as data flows: the forward code runs in the same order, the adjoint reverses it.

逐句执行这两遍循环。单元里的紫色箭头是梯度数组 G：第一遍里一个面一个面地长出来，壁面面上的差值来自面中点的 uw；第二遍里只读不写。下方三行数组依次是状态 u*、梯度 G 和输出 r。

executes both loops statement by statement. The violet arrows in the cells are the gradient array G: in pass 1 it grows face by face, the wall faces taking their difference from the uw at the face midpoint; in pass 2 it is only read. The three arrays below are the state u*, the gradients G and the output r.

播放到第二遍的某个内部面时，留意它收集了两侧的状态和两侧的梯度，而每个梯度又来自整个模板，所以一个面的通量依赖两个模板里的所有单元。播放到壁面面时，右状态是面中点上的 uw，平均梯度只有 GL。循环结束时 r 在收敛解上处处为零，包括 8 个壁面单元：它们的方程和其他单元一样，是一个完整的守恒方程。

When the player reaches an interior face in pass 2, note that it gathers both states and both gradients, and each gradient came from a whole stencil, so one face's flux depends on every cell of the two stencils. On a wall face the right state is the uw at the face midpoint and the averaged gradient is GL alone. When the loop ends, r is zero everywhere at the converged solution, the 8 wall cells included: their equations, like every other cell's, are complete conservation equations.

求解：Jacobian 与 Newton 法

The solve: the Jacobian and Newton

与格点页面一样有两个 Jacobian：残差的精确导数，伴随要用它（第 7、8 节不形成矩阵，直接作用它）；求解器用的一阶近似，只含面邻居。

As on the node-centred page there are two Jacobians: the exact derivative of the residual, which the adjoint needs (Sections 7 and 8 apply it without forming it), and the first-order approximation the solver uses, built from face neighbours only.

精确导数：伸到邻居的邻居

The exact derivative: reaching the neighbours of neighbours

对流部分只依赖面两侧的单元值：∂hc/∂uL=12vnuL+ε|n|=cL，∂hc/∂uR=12vnuR−ε|n|=cR；壁面面上 hw 的对流部分与 uL 无关，cL=0。扩散部分有两条路径：两点差直接依赖 uL、uR，平均梯度经过最小二乘依赖两个模板中的每一个单元：

The convective part depends only on the cell values either side of the face: ∂hc/∂uL=12vnuL+ε|n|=cL and ∂hc/∂uR=12vnuR−ε|n|=cR; on a wall face the convective part of hw does not depend on uL, so cL=0. The diffusive part has two paths: the two-point difference depends directly on uL and uR, the averaged gradient goes through least squares and depends on every cell of the two stencils:

∂hv∂um=−ν[p(δmR−δmL)+12k·(∂GL∂um+∂GR∂um)],∂Gc∂us=wcs,∂Gc∂uc=−Σswcs

（壁面面上没有 R，梯度只有 GL，系数 12 换成 1；壁面中点那个模板点是常数，对它没有导数。）所以精确 Jacobian 的每一行伸到面邻居的面邻居：壁面单元 10 个非零元，三角形单元 7 或 8 个，全矩阵 200 个。

(A wall face has no R, its gradient is GL alone and the 12 becomes 1; the wall-midpoint stencil point is a constant, with no derivative.) So each row of the exact Jacobian reaches the face neighbours of the face neighbours: 10 non-zeros for a wall cell, 7 or 8 for a triangle, 200 in the whole matrix.

求解器用的一阶近似 Jacobian

The first-order Jacobian the solver uses

求解器只取相邻单元之间的耦合：对流导数原样保留，扩散只保留两点部分，梯度路径丢掉：

The solver keeps only the couplings between adjacent cells: the convective derivatives as they are, and of diffusion only the two-point part, the gradient path dropped:

Ã:∂h∂uL≈cL+νp,∂h∂uR≈cR−νp

Ã 的非零结构正好是对流页面的 Jacobian：每行 3 或 4 个，全矩阵 88 个（）。组装是一个面循环：

Ã has exactly the non-zero pattern of the advection page's Jacobian: 3 or 4 per row, 88 in all (). Assembling it is one face loop:

用近似 Jacobian 的 Newton 法

Newton with the approximate Jacobian

sets the three functions side by side. Compared with the node-centred page there is no "zero before the loop, add back after it": weak imposition changes no row, so the transpose needs no special treatment either.

验证：dJ/dX 对有限差分

Verification: dJ/dX against finite differences

上一节把 dJ/dX 推了出来：伴随只解一次线性方程，就拿到全部 48 个分量。这一节只做一件事：验证它是对的。判据是最朴素的那个，逐个坐标分量做中心差分：

The previous section derived dJ/dX: the adjoint solves one linear equation and gets all 48 components. This section does one thing only: check that it is right. The criterion is the plainest one there is, a central difference taken coordinate by coordinate:

dJdXk≈J(Xk+h)−J(Xk−h)2h

关键在于 J(Xk±h) 是完整的目标函数：每次都要改动网格，重建全部几何量（面法向、形心、d、p、k、最小二乘权重），重新解一次非线性的原始方程，再算反力通量。所以这里验证的是全导数，状态对几何的响应也算在内。代价也正因如此：中心差分每个分量要两次非线性求解，一共 96 次，而伴随只用了一次线性求解。只往一侧扰动的前向差分把基准值 J(X) 共用，一共 49 次，代价是截断误差从 O(h2) 变成 O(h)。

What matters is that J(Xk±h) is the complete objective: every evaluation moves the mesh, rebuilds all the geometry (face normals, centroids, d, p, k, least-squares weights), solves the nonlinear primal again, and only then computes the reaction flux. So what is verified is the total derivative, the response of the state to the geometry included. That is also what it costs: two nonlinear solves per component, 96 in all, against the single linear solve of the adjoint. A forward difference, perturbing to one side only, shares the baseline J(X) and needs 49, at the price of an O(h) truncation error instead of O(h2).

Where the optimal step lies. 总结

Summary

与对流页面相比多了什么

What this page adds to the advection page

  • 两遍循环。扩散通量要用单元梯度，最小二乘梯度又要用邻居的值，所以残差先走一遍面循环算梯度（内部面与壁面面），再走一遍面循环算通量。前向按同样的顺序走，伴随把顺序倒过来。
  • Two loops. The diffusive flux needs cell gradients, and a least-squares gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the residual runs one face loop for the gradients (interior and wall faces) and then one for the fluxes. The forward code runs them in the same order; the adjoint reverses it.
  • 两个 Jacobian。精确 Jacobian 每行伸到邻居的邻居（200 个非零元），从不形成；求解器用只含面邻居的一阶近似 Ã（88 个非零元，正是对流页面的结构），谱半径 0.151，17 轮收敛。
  • Two Jacobians. The exact Jacobian reaches the neighbours of neighbours in every row (200 non-zeros) and is never formed; the solver uses the first-order approximation Ã, face neighbours only (88 non-zeros, exactly the advection page's pattern), with spectral radius 0.151 and convergence in 17 sweeps.
  • 弱施加的壁面。壁面值从两处进入：壁面面上的通量，以及壁面单元的最小二乘模板。24 个方程都是守恒方程，前向与伴随里都没有专门处理壁面的行；uw 是常数，求导时没有它的份。
  • A weakly imposed wall. The wall value comes in through two doors: the flux on the wall faces, and the wall cells' least-squares stencils. All 24 equations are conservation equations, and neither the forward nor the adjoint code has rows set aside for the wall; uw is a constant and takes no part in any derivative.
  • 直接的目标函数。J 就是 8 个壁面面通量之和，自动与远场通量平衡，不需要从哪里找回来。
  • A direct objective. J is simply the sum of the 8 wall-face fluxes; it balances the far-field flux automatically, and there is nothing to recover.
  • 同速的伴随求解与 ψ 的含义。伴随用精确的 residual_b 与 ÃT 迭代，16 轮到 10−13；−ψc 是单元 c 里单位源流进物面的比例。几何梯度是一次反向扫描，种子为 ψL−ψR，壁面面再加 1。
  • An adjoint solve at the same pace, and what ψ means. The adjoint iterates with the exact residual_b and ÃT, 16 sweeps to 10−13; −ψc is the fraction of a unit source in cell c that ends up in the body. The geometric gradient is one reverse sweep, seeded with ψL−ψR, plus 1 on the wall faces.

精度阶

Order of accuracy

伴随给出的是离散格式的精确导数，格式的精度决定这个导数离连续问题有多远。用构造解 ue=12+14sin(0.9x+0.3)cos(0.7y−0.2) 来测（源项由它代入方程得到，壁面与远场都用它的值），在由基础网格一致细分得到的四层网格上求解（）。只有扩散时，两种格式在 L2 范数下都是二阶：本页 1.94、2.02、2.01，格点页面 1.99、2.09、2.08。完整方程只有一阶：耗散项相当于一个随网格尺寸线性减小的数值扩散系数 ε|n|/p，本页逐层的阶从 0.59、0.71 升到 0.82，向 1 靠拢。

The adjoint gives the exact derivative of the discrete scheme; the scheme's order decides how far that derivative is from the continuous problem's. It is measured with the manufactured solution ue=12+14sin(0.9x+0.3)cos(0.7y−0.2) (the source term from substituting it, its values on the wall and the far field), solved on four meshes made from the base mesh by uniform subdivision (). With diffusion alone both schemes are second order in the L2 norm: 1.94, 2.02 and 2.01 here, 1.99, 2.09 and 2.08 on the node-centred page. The full equation is first order only: the dissipation acts as a numerical diffusivity ε|n|/p that shrinks linearly with the mesh size, and the order here climbs level by level from 0.59 through 0.71 to 0.82, towards 1.
