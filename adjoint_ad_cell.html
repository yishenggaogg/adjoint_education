非结构网格格心格式的二维标量对流扩散方程离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of a two-dimensional scalar advection–diffusion equation, on an unstructured cell-centred scheme

算例与对流方程的格心页面是同一张环形（O 型）混合网格，方程多了一项常系数扩散 −ν∇2u（ν=0.5）。有了扩散，内圈物面才能施加 Dirichlet 壁面条件 u=uw=0。格心格式的未知量在单元里，物面上没有未知量，所以壁面值只能弱施加：它进入壁面面上的通量，也作为一个点进入壁面单元的最小二乘梯度。扩散通量要用单元梯度，残差因此成了两遍循环：先算梯度，再算通量。目标函数是流进物面的总通量。对照阅读：同一方程的格点页面，那里的壁面是强施加的。The test case is the same annular (O-type) hybrid mesh as the cell-centred advection page, but the equation gains a constant-coefficient diffusion term −ν∇2u (ν=0.5). With diffusion the inner body surface can carry a Dirichlet wall condition, u=uw=0. A cell-centred scheme keeps its unknowns in the cells and has none on the body, so the wall value can only be imposed weakly: it enters the flux on the wall faces, and it is one more point in the wall cells' least-squares gradients. The diffusive flux needs cell gradients, so the residual becomes two loops: gradients first, then fluxes. The objective is the total flux into the body. Read alongside: the node-centred page for the same equation, where the wall is imposed strongly.

网格：同一张混合网格，多了梯度模板

Mesh: the same hybrid grid, now with gradient stencils

算例网格

The test mesh

网格与对流页面第 1 节完全相同：24 个节点，贴壁一层是 8 个四边形，外层是 16 个三角形，一共 24 个单元。未知量放在单元里，控制体就是单元本身。每条网格边就是一个面：32 个内部面，8 个壁面面，8 个远场面，一共 48 个面。

The mesh is exactly the one of Section 1 of the advection page: 24 nodes, with a layer of 8 quadrilaterals against the wall and 16 triangles outside it, 24 cells in all. The unknowns sit in the cells, and each control volume is the cell itself. Every mesh edge is a face: 32 interior faces, 8 wall faces and 8 far-field faces, 48 faces in all.

物面上没有未知量：它只是 8 个壁面面，各自属于一个贴壁的四边形单元（）。壁面值 uw 因此不能像格点格式那样直接写进某个未知量，只能通过这 8 个面进入方程（第 3 节）。

There are no unknowns on the body: it is just the 8 wall faces, each belonging to one quadrilateral against the wall (). So the wall value uw cannot be written into an unknown, as the node-centred scheme does; it can only enter the equations through those 8 faces (Section 3).

新的几何量：形心间向量与梯度模板

New geometry: the centroid-to-centroid vector and the gradient stencil

扩散项要两样新的几何量。第一样是面两侧未知量之间的向量 d：内部面上从左单元形心指向右单元形心，壁面面上从壁面单元形心指向面中点（壁面值 uw 就放在那里）。第二样是每个单元的梯度模板：它的面邻居的形心，再加上它自己壁面面的中点（如果有的话）。远场面不带数据（扩散通量为零），不进入模板。于是壁面单元有 4 个模板点（3 个面邻居加 1 个壁面中点），内层三角形有 3 个，外层三角形只有 2 个：两个点在二维里正好确定一个梯度。

Diffusion needs two new geometric quantities. The first is the vector d between the unknowns on either side of a face: on an interior face from the left cell's centroid to the right cell's, on a wall face from the wall cell's centroid to the face midpoint (where the wall value uw sits). The second is each cell's gradient stencil: the centroids of its face neighbours, plus the midpoint of its own wall face if it has one. Far-field faces carry no data (their diffusive flux is zero) and stay out of the stencils. So a wall cell has 4 stencil points (3 face neighbours and 1 wall midpoint), an inner triangle 3, and an outer triangle only 2: in two dimensions two points fix a gradient exactly.

要离散的方程与面上的通量

The equation to discretise, and the flux on a face

要离散的方程

The equation to discretise

求解域 Ω 是物体与远场圆之间的环形区域。未知量 u(x,y) 满足定常的对流扩散方程：

The domain Ω is the annulus between the body and the far-field circle. The unknown u(x,y) obeys the steady advection–diffusion equation

∇·(12u2β)−∇·(ν∇u)=0,β=(1,0.5),ν=0.5

第一项是对流页面的非线性守恒律，第二项是常系数扩散。扩散把方程变成二阶，于是整条物面都可以给定 u 的值（第 3 节）。

The first term is the nonlinear conservation law of the advection page, the second constant-coefficient diffusion. Diffusion makes the equation second order, so the whole body can take a prescribed value of u (Section 3).

有限体积离散

Finite-volume discretisation

在单元 c 上积分，用散度定理化成边界积分：

Integrate over cell c and turn the volume integral into a boundary integral with the divergence theorem:

∮∂c(12u2β·n−ν∇u·n)ds=0⇒Rc(u)=Σf∋c±(ha,f+hv,f)=0

求和取遍单元 c 的所有面，包括它的壁面面与远场面。每个面上的通量只算一次，以相反符号分给左右两个单元（左取 +、右取 −），格式是守恒的。ha 是对流通量（下标 a 取自 advection），hv 是扩散通量。

The sum runs over all faces of cell c, its wall and far-field faces included. Each face's flux is computed once and handed to its left and right cells with opposite signs (+ left, − right), so the scheme is conservative. ha is the convective flux (the subscript is from advection) and hv the diffusive one.

对流通量：与对流页面相同

The convective flux: as on the advection page

ha=14vn(uL2+uR2)−ε|n|(uR−uL),vn=β·n,ε=0.8

中心平均加常数耗散，远场上按冻结的入流、出流取右状态，推导在对流页面第 2、3 节。壁面面是例外，那里状态已知，见第 3 节。

A central average plus constant dissipation, with the right state on the far field taken by the frozen inflow/outflow switch, derived in Sections 2 and 3 of the advection page. The wall faces are the exception, since the state there is known; see Section 3.

扩散通量：面上的法向导数

The diffusive flux: the normal derivative on the face

扩散通量 hv=−ν∇u·n 要面上的法向导数，但面两侧只有两个单元值。只有当 n 与形心连线 d 平行时，两点差才就是法向导数；一般网格上并不平行（）。于是把 n 分成沿 d 的部分和垂直于 d 的部分：

The diffusive flux hv=−ν∇u·n needs the normal derivative on the face, but only two cell values sit either side of it. The two-point difference is the normal derivative only when n is parallel to the centroid-to-centroid vector d, which on a general mesh it is not (). So n is split into a part along d and a part perpendicular to it:

n=pd+k,p=d·n|d|2,k=n−pd(k·d=0)
∇u·n≈p(uR−uL)+ḡ·k,hv=−ν[p(uR−uL)+ḡ·k],ḡ=12(GL+GR)

沿 d 的部分用两点差，垂直的部分用两侧单元梯度的平均。两点部分让左右两个单元直接耦合，梯度部分补上 n 偏离 d 的那一块；两部分对线性场都精确。

The part along d takes the two-point difference, the perpendicular part the average of the two cell gradients. The two-point part couples the left and right cells directly; the gradient part makes up for n leaning away from d. Both are exact on linear fields.

单元梯度：最小二乘

Cell gradients: least squares

单元 c 的梯度 Gc 取为让线性模型 uc+G·ds 最贴近各模板点上的值 us 的那个 G；模板点是面邻居的形心，或壁面面的中点（那里的值是 uw）：

The gradient Gc of cell c is the G for which the linear model uc+G·ds comes closest to the values us at the stencil points, which are the centroids of the face neighbours, or the midpoint of a wall face (where the value is uw):

Gc=Σswcs(us−uc),wcs=Mc−1ds,Mc=ΣsdsdsT

权重只依赖几何，线性场被精确复现（ΣswcsdsT=I），本页不加距离权。壁面中点让壁面值进入了壁面单元的梯度，这是弱施加的第二个入口。梯度对 u 是线性的；对 uw 也是，但 uw 是常数，求导时没有它的份（第 7 节）。 可以逐个单元查看。

The weights depend on the geometry only, linear fields come out exactly (ΣswcsdsT=I), and there is no distance weighting. The wall midpoints bring the wall value into the wall cells' gradients: the second door through which the wall is imposed weakly. The gradient is linear in u; in uw too, but uw is a constant and has no share in any derivative (Section 7). shows it cell by cell.

ν 为什么取 0.5。耗散项 −ε|n|(uR−uL) 与扩散的两点部分形式相同，相当于数值扩散系数 ε|n|/p；在这张粗网格的内部面上，它介于 0.392 与 0.648 之间（壁面面上没有耗散项），每加密一次减半。在收敛解上，ν=0.5 时各内部面耗散通量的绝对值之和是扩散通量的 0.87 倍；若取 ν=0.1，这个比值是 4.6，物理扩散会被数值耗散淹没。

Why ν=0.5. The dissipation −ε|n|(uR−uL) has the form of the two-point part of diffusion, a numerical diffusivity ε|n|/p; on the interior faces of this coarse mesh it lies between 0.392 and 0.648 (the wall faces carry no dissipation), and it halves with every refinement. At the converged solution with ν=0.5, the absolute dissipative fluxes over the interior faces sum to 0.87 times the diffusive ones; with ν=0.1 the ratio would be 4.6, and the physical diffusion would be swamped by the numerical one.

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈：弱施加的 Dirichlet 壁面

Inner ring: a weakly imposed Dirichlet wall

物面上 u=uw=0。格心格式在物面上没有未知量，壁面值只能从壁面面进入壁面单元的守恒方程（）。壁面面上的状态已知，右状态就是 uw，通量是

On the body u=uw=0. A cell-centred scheme has no unknown on the body, so the wall value can only enter the wall cell's conservation equation through the wall face (). The state on a wall face is known, the right state is uw itself, and the flux is

hw=12uw2vn−ν[p(uw−uL)+GL·k],d=xmid−xL

对流部分直接用已知的壁面状态：12uw2vn，没有平均，也没有耗散；这里 uw=0，所以它为零，穿过物面的只有扩散。扩散部分还是同一个修正公式，只是 d 从壁面单元的形心指向壁面面的中点，而平均梯度就是壁面单元自己的梯度 GL。此外，壁面值还作为一个模板点进入 GL。壁面就从这两处进入方程，都在普通的循环里：没有哪一行是特殊的，24 个方程全是守恒方程。

The convective part uses the known wall state directly, 12uw2vn, with no average and no dissipation; here uw=0 makes it zero, and only diffusion crosses the body. The diffusive part is the same corrected formula, except that d runs from the wall cell's centroid to the wall face's midpoint and the averaged gradient is the wall cell's own GL. Besides, the wall value enters GL as a stencil point. Those are the wall's two doors into the equations, both inside the ordinary loops: no row is special, and all 24 equations are conservation equations.

与格点页面对照：那里壁面节点上 ui=uw 严格成立，但壁面通量要从被替换的行里找回来；这里单元形心上的值不等于 uw，壁面值是经由通量“拉”过去的，只在网格加密的极限下成立。反过来，壁面通量本身就是残差里的一个普通面通量，直接就是目标函数（第 6 节）。

Compared with the node-centred page: there ui=uw holds exactly at the wall nodes, but the wall flux has to be recovered from the replaced rows; here the values at the cell centroids are not uw: the wall value is pulled in through the flux, and u=uw holds only in the limit of refinement. In return, the wall flux is an ordinary face flux of the residual, and it is directly the objective (Section 6).

外圈：远场

Outer ring: the far field

对流部分与对流页面相同：远场面按 vn 的符号分成入流和出流（在初始几何上冻结），入流取 uR=u∞=1，出流取 uR=uL。扩散部分取零通量，即齐次 Neumann 条件。8 个远场面中，4 个入流，4 个出流。

The convective part is as on the advection page: the far-field faces are split into inflow and outflow by the sign of vn (frozen at the initial geometry), with uR=u∞=1 on inflow and uR=uL on outflow. The diffusive part takes zero flux, a homogeneous Neumann condition. Of the 8 far-field faces, 4 are inflow and 4 outflow.

为什么对流页面没有这种壁面。纯对流方程是一阶的，边界上只能在特征进入区域的地方给数据，而特征速度沿闭合的物面必然有正有负（见对流页面第 3 节）。扩散项是二阶的，它让整条物面都可以、也必须给一个条件。

Why the advection page has no such wall. A purely convective equation is first order: boundary data can be given only where characteristics enter the domain, and along a closed body the characteristic speed takes both signs (see Section 3 of the advection page). The diffusion term is second order: it lets, and requires, the whole body take one condition.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 两遍循环：先梯度，后通量

The residual = two loops: gradients first, then fluxes

扩散通量要用面两侧单元的梯度，而单元梯度又要用邻居的值，所以必须先把所有单元的梯度算完，才能开始算通量。残差因此成了两遍面循环：

The diffusive flux needs the gradients of the cells on either side of a face, and a cell gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the gradients of all cells must be finished before the first flux is computed. The residual therefore becomes two face loops:

  • 第一遍（梯度循环）：遍历 32 个内部面和 8 个壁面面。每个面收集差值 Δ（壁面面上是 uw−uL），分发到两侧单元的梯度里：GL←GL+wLRΔ，GR←GR+wRL(−Δ)；壁面面只有左侧。远场面不参与。
  • Pass 1 (the gradient loop): visit the 32 interior faces and the 8 wall faces. Each face gathers the difference Δ (uw−uL on a wall face) and scatters it into the gradients on both sides: GL←GL+wLRΔ and GR←GR+wRL(−Δ); a wall face has a left side only. Far-field faces take no part.
  • 第二遍（通量循环）：遍历全部 48 个面，收集两侧的状态与梯度，算 h=ha+hv，分发到 rL 与 rR。壁面面也在这里，它的通量照常进入壁面单元的方程。
  • Pass 2 (the flux loop): visit all 48 faces, gather the states and gradients on both sides, compute h=ha+hv and scatter it into rL and rR. The wall faces are here too, their fluxes entering the wall cells' equations as usual.

没有第三步：弱施加不替换任何一行。 把三个函数画成数据流：前向走同样的顺序，伴随把顺序倒过来。

There is no third step: weak imposition replaces no row. draws the three functions as data flows: the forward code runs in the same order, the adjoint reverses it.

逐句执行这两遍循环。单元里的紫色箭头是梯度数组 G：第一遍里一个面一个面地长出来，壁面面上的差值来自面中点的 uw；第二遍里只读不写。下方三行数组依次是状态 u*、梯度 G 和输出 r。

executes both loops statement by statement. The violet arrows in the cells are the gradient array G: in pass 1 it grows face by face, the wall faces taking their difference from the uw at the face midpoint; in pass 2 it is only read. The three arrays below are the state u*, the gradients G and the output r.

播放到第二遍的某个内部面时，留意它收集了两侧的状态和两侧的梯度，而每个梯度又来自整个模板，所以一个面的通量依赖两个模板里的所有单元。播放到壁面面时，右状态是面中点上的 uw，平均梯度只有 GL。循环结束时 r 在收敛解上处处为零，包括 8 个壁面单元：它们的方程和其他单元一样，是一个完整的守恒方程。

When the player reaches an interior face in pass 2, note that it gathers both states and both gradients, and each gradient came from a whole stencil, so one face's flux depends on every cell of the two stencils. On a wall face the right state is the uw at the face midpoint and the averaged gradient is GL alone. When the loop ends, r is zero everywhere at the converged solution, the 8 wall cells included: their equations, like every other cell's, are complete conservation equations.

求解：Jacobian 与 Jacobi 迭代

The solve: the Jacobian and Jacobi iteration

这一节先推出残差的精确导数：切线方程与伴随方程的矩阵正是它（第 7、8 节不形成矩阵，直接作用它）；再用 Jacobi 迭代求解 R(u)=0，它只要这个矩阵的对角元。

This section first derives the exact derivative of the residual, which is the matrix of the tangent and the adjoint equation (Sections 7 and 8 apply it without forming it), and then solves R(u)=0 with the Jacobi iteration, which needs only its diagonal.

精确导数：伸到邻居的邻居

The exact derivative: reaching the neighbours of neighbours

对流部分只依赖面两侧的单元值：∂ha/∂uL=12vnuL+ε|n|=cL，∂ha/∂uR=12vnuR−ε|n|=cR；壁面面上 hw 的对流部分与 uL 无关，cL=0。扩散部分有两条路径：两点差直接依赖 uL、uR，平均梯度经过最小二乘依赖两个模板中的每一个单元：

The convective part depends only on the cell values either side of the face: ∂ha/∂uL=12vnuL+ε|n|=cL and ∂ha/∂uR=12vnuR−ε|n|=cR; on a wall face the convective part of hw does not depend on uL, so cL=0. The diffusive part has two paths: the two-point difference depends directly on uL and uR, the averaged gradient goes through least squares and depends on every cell of the two stencils:

∂hv∂um=−ν[p(δmR−δmL)+12k·(∂GL∂um+∂GR∂um)],∂Gc∂us=wcs,∂Gc∂uc=−Σswcs

（壁面面上没有 R，梯度只有 GL，系数 12 换成 1；壁面中点那个模板点是常数，对它没有导数。）所以精确 Jacobian 的每一行伸到面邻居的面邻居：壁面单元 10 个非零元，三角形单元 7 或 8 个，全矩阵 200 个。

(A wall face has no R, its gradient is GL alone and the 12 becomes 1; the wall-midpoint stencil point is a constant, with no derivative.) So each row of the exact Jacobian reaches the face neighbours of the face neighbours: 10 non-zeros for a wall cell, 7 or 8 for a triangle, 200 in the whole matrix.

它的一阶部分

Its first-order part

只保留相邻单元之间的耦合，就得到精确 Jacobian 的一阶部分 Ã：对流导数原样保留，扩散导数只保留两点部分，梯度路径整个丢掉。很多隐式求解器就拿它作迭代矩阵。本页的求解不用它：它在这里只用来看清梯度路径添了什么（），以及第 7 节里，拿它的转置求伴随会错多少。

Keep only the couplings between adjacent cells and what is left is the first-order part Ã of the exact Jacobian: the convective derivatives as they are, and of diffusion only the two-point part, the gradient path dropped. Many implicit solvers take it as their iteration matrix. The solve on this page does not use it: here it only shows what the gradient path adds () and, in Section 7, how wrong an adjoint built on its transpose would be.

Ã:∂h∂uL≈cL+νp,∂h∂uR≈cR−νp

用 Jacobi 迭代求解

Solving with the Jacobi iteration

与格点页面一样，本页的 primal、切线、伴随三个方程（后两个在第 9、10 节）用的是同一个 Jacobi 迭代：每一轮算一次残差，再逐单元除以精确 Jacobian 的对角元：

As on the node-centred page, the primal, the tangent and the adjoint equation (the last two in Sections 9 and 10) are all solved by one and the same Jacobi iteration: every sweep evaluates the residual once and divides it, cell by cell, by the diagonal of the exact Jacobian:

u←u−D−1R(u)
D=diagA(u)

D 不需要把 A 造出来：diagonal(u) 就是残差的那个面循环，每个面只留下落在对角线上的两项，即对流导数、两点部分，以及经由最小二乘梯度的那一项（壁面面上系数 12 换成 1，只经由 GL）。所以一轮只有一次 residual 和一次 diagonal：不形成矩阵，也不解线性方程组。没有替换行：壁面是弱施加的，每个单元都照常更新。初值取 u≡1，判据是残差的均方根 ‖R‖<10−13（）。

D does not need A to be formed: diagonal(u) is the residual's own face loop, keeping of each face only the two entries that land on the diagonal: the convective derivative, the two-point part, and the term through the least-squares gradients (on a wall face with 1 in place of 12, and through GL only). So a sweep is one residual and one diagonal: no matrix is formed and no linear system is solved. There are no replaced rows: the wall is weak, and every cell is updated alike. The initial guess is u≡1, and the criterion is the rms of the residual, ‖R‖<10−13 ().

为什么用 Jacobi，而不用 LU。本页只有 24 个未知量，理论上最快的办法是直接分解：把精确的 A 整个造出来，用 Newton 法，每一步做一次稠密 LU 分解，k=5 时残差就已低于 10−13。可那样就说明不了 matrix-free。Jacobi 是最简单的迭代法：对矩阵它只要对角元，其余的一切都经由残差作用进来（切线方程里是 residual_d，伴随方程里是 residual_b），这正是 matrix-free 的意思。代价是线性收敛：在解附近，误差每一轮乘以迭代矩阵 I−D−1A，速率由它的谱半径决定，本算例是 0.915，判据在 k=315 时满足。切线与伴随的迭代矩阵与它同谱，所以进入渐近阶段以后，三条收敛曲线以同一个速率下降（第 10 节）。

Why Jacobi, and not LU. With only 24 unknowns the fastest method in principle is a direct factorisation: form the whole exact A, run Newton, and factorise with one dense LU per step; the residual is below 10−13 by k=5. But that would show nothing about matrix-free. Jacobi is the simplest iterative method there is: of the matrix it needs only the diagonal, and everything else comes in through the residual (through residual_d in the tangent equation and residual_b in the adjoint equation), which is exactly what matrix-free means. The price is linear convergence: near the solution the error is multiplied every sweep by the iteration matrix I−D−1A, at a rate set by its spectral radius, 0.915 here, and the criterion is met at k=315. The tangent and adjoint iteration matrices have the same spectrum, so once in the asymptotic phase all three convergence curves fall at the same rate (Section 10).

对角元要把梯度路径算进去。只取一阶部分 Ã 的对角元，迭代照样收敛到同一个 u*（残差是精确的），只是速率变了。在这里差别很大：梯度路径能改变一个对角元的 15.3%，谱半径从 0.915 升到 0.990，要多跑九倍左右的轮次。格点页面上它最多只改变 3.9%，谱半径 0.786 与 0.787 几乎相同。

The diagonal must carry the gradient path. Take the diagonal of the first-order part Ã instead and the iteration still converges to the same u* (the residual is exact), only at another rate. Here the difference is large: the gradient path changes a diagonal entry by up to 15.3%, and the spectral radius rises from 0.915 to 0.990, about nine times as many sweeps. On the node-centred page it changes one by at most 3.9%, and the spectral radius, 0.786 against 0.787, hardly moves.

The code runs a fixed 600 sweeps rather than exiting at the criterion: the remaining sweeps take u* down to its round-off floor (from k=386 on), and every measured constant on this page was produced under those sweeps; the u* it returns is within 3.3×10−16 of Newton's. 把残差里的每一个实数换成复数：每个未知量、每个几何量都有实部与虚部两个数。挪动的是节点坐标，所以几何也变成复数：形心、面向量 n 与 |n|、d、p、k、最小二乘权重（其中 2×2 矩阵的逆）都要重算一遍。本页用到复数的加、减、乘、除，以及一次开方：|n|=n·n，取主值平方根。远场面是入流还是出流，沿用实数程序在基准网格上的判断，不随虚部变。两遍循环与 Jacobi 迭代（D 也由同一个复数循环收集）都不变，只是每个数多带一个虚部；判据要连虚部一起看：ImR/h 的均方根也要低于 10−13。从 u* 出发时实部一开始就满足判据，只看它，迭代在第 0 轮就会停下，漏掉状态的响应。伪代码在 的最上面。

伴随给出的是离散格式的精确导数，与格式本身是几阶精度无关；格式的精度阶决定的是离散解，以及由它算出的 J 与 dJ/dX，离连续问题有多远。这一节用构造解把它测出来：先选一个光滑函数当精确解，由它反推出源项与边界数据，再在一族逐次一致加密的网格上求解，看误差随网格尺寸按几次方减小。

The adjoint gives the exact derivative of the discrete scheme, whatever that scheme's order of accuracy; the order decides how far the discrete solution, and the J and dJ/dX computed from it, are from the continuous problem. This section measures it with a manufactured solution: pick a smooth function as the exact solution, derive the source term and the boundary data from it, solve on a family of uniformly refined meshes, and see with what power of the mesh size the error falls.

The full equation: first order

对流通量里的耗散项可以写成扩散两点部分的样子：

The dissipation in the convective flux can be written in the form of the two-point part of diffusion:

−ε|n|(uR−uL)=−νnumd·n|d|2(uR−uL)
νnum=ε|n||d|2d·n

若 d 与 n 平行，d·n/|d|2=|n|/|d|，于是 νnum=ε|d|=O(h)：离散格式实际上是在解扩散系数为 ν+O(h) 的方程，误差是 O(h)，格式只有一阶。本页内部面上 νnum 的中位数逐层是 0.487、0.270、0.140、0.071，每加密一次大致减半；在基础网格上它与 ν=0.5 相当，到最细一层只剩 ν 的 14%。所以观测阶是从下面一步步靠近 1 的：L2 范数 0.59、0.71、0.82，最大范数 0.57、0.62、0.80（格点页面的 L2 阶是 0.53、0.71、0.82）。

If d is parallel to n, d·n/|d|2=|n|/|d| and so νnum=ε|d|=O(h): the discrete scheme is in effect solving the equation with diffusivity ν+O(h), the error is O(h), and the scheme is first order only. On this page the median of νnum over the interior faces is 0.487, 0.270, 0.140 and 0.071 level by level, roughly halving with every refinement; on the base mesh it is about as large as ν=0.5, and on the finest it is 14% of ν. That is why the observed order approaches 1 from below, step by step: 0.59, 0.71 and 0.82 in L2, 0.57, 0.62 and 0.80 in the max norm (the node-centred page's L2 orders are 0.53, 0.71 and 0.82).

What this means for the adjoint

第 10–13 节验证的是：伴随与几何梯度对离散的 J 精确到舍入误差，这与格式几阶无关。离散的 J 本身随网格加密逼近连续值：第 6 节在同一族网格上看到两种格式的壁面通量互相靠拢（格心 2.344、2.331、2.249、2.184，格点最细一层 2.201）。格心这几层相邻两层之差忽大忽小：这几层还在渐近区之前，看不出格式的阶。优化器拿到的梯度与它所优化的离散目标函数完全一致，问题是自洽的；网格加密决定的是这个离散问题离连续问题有多近。要让它更近，得提高格式的阶（例如对流通量用重构），而不是改伴随。

What Sections 10 to 13 verified is that the adjoint and the geometric gradient are exact, to round-off, for the discrete J, whatever the scheme's order. The discrete J itself approaches the continuous value as the mesh is refined: Section 6 shows the two schemes' wall fluxes closing in on each other on this same family (cell-centred 2.344, 2.331, 2.249 and 2.184, node-centred 2.201 on the finest level). The cell-centred steps between levels are still irregular: these levels are pre-asymptotic, and the scheme's order does not show in them yet. The gradient the optimiser gets agrees exactly with the discrete objective it optimises, so the problem is self-consistent; refinement decides how close that discrete problem is to the continuous one. To bring it closer one has to raise the scheme's order (a reconstruction in the convective flux, say), not change the adjoint.

Summary

与对流页面相比多了什么

What this page adds to the advection page

  • 两遍循环。扩散通量要用单元梯度，最小二乘梯度又要用邻居的值，所以残差先走一遍面循环算梯度（内部面与壁面面），再走一遍面循环算通量。前向按同样的顺序走，伴随把顺序倒过来。
  • Two loops. The diffusive flux needs cell gradients, and a least-squares gradient needs the neighbours' values, so the residual runs one face loop for the gradients (interior and wall faces) and then one for the fluxes. The forward code runs them in the same order; the adjoint reverses it.
  • 一个 Jacobi 迭代，三个方程。精确 Jacobian 每行伸到邻居的邻居（200 个非零元），从不形成：primal、切线、伴随都用同一个 Jacobi 迭代，只用到它的对角元（梯度路径在内，少了它谱半径会从 0.915 升到 0.990）与 residual_d、residual_b 这两个乘积；判据在 k=315 时满足。
  • One Jacobi iteration, three equations. The exact Jacobian reaches the neighbours of neighbours in every row (200 non-zeros) and is never formed: the primal, the tangent and the adjoint are all solved by the same Jacobi iteration, which needs only its diagonal (gradient path included: without it the spectral radius would rise from 0.915 to 0.990) and the two products residual_d and residual_b; the criterion is met at k=315.
  • 弱施加的壁面。壁面值从两处进入：壁面面上的通量，以及壁面单元的最小二乘模板。24 个方程都是守恒方程，前向与伴随都没有被替换的行；uw 是常数，求导时没有它的份。
  • A weakly imposed wall. The wall value comes in through two doors: the flux on the wall faces, and the wall cells' least-squares stencils. All 24 equations are conservation equations, and neither the forward nor the adjoint code replaces a row; uw is a constant and takes no part in any derivative.
  • 直接的目标函数。J 就是 8 个壁面面通量之和，自动与远场通量平衡，不需要从哪里找回来。
  • A direct objective. J is simply the sum of the 8 wall-face fluxes; it balances the far-field flux automatically, and there is nothing to recover.
  • 三个迭代同一个速率，ψ 的含义。三个迭代矩阵同谱，进入渐近阶段后三条收敛曲线平行（每轮 0.9149 倍），切线与伴随给出同一个 dJ/du∞；−ψc 是单元 c 里单位源流进物面的比例。几何梯度是一次反向扫描，种子为 ψL−ψR，壁面面再加 1。
  • One rate for all three, and what ψ means. The three iteration matrices share their spectrum, so once asymptotic the three convergence curves run parallel (a factor 0.9149 per sweep), and the tangent and the adjoint give the same dJ/du∞; −ψc is the fraction of a unit source in cell c that ends up in the body. The geometric gradient is one reverse sweep, seeded with ψL−ψR, plus 1 on the wall faces.
  • 验证。点积测试检查转置，差分与复数步长检查梯度。复数步长从收敛解出发，每一轮都与前向迭代一致，与伴随梯度相符到舍入误差。
  • Verification. The dot-product test checks the transpose; finite differences and the complex step check the gradient. Started from the converged solution, the complex step matches the forward iteration at every sweep, and the adjoint gradient to round-off.
  • 精度阶。只有扩散时是二阶（最细一层 L2 范数 2.01、最大范数 1.96）；完整方程受耗散项的数值扩散 ε|n||d|2/(d·n)=O(h) 所限，只有一阶，观测阶从下面靠近 1（第 14 节）。
  • Order of accuracy. Second order with diffusion alone (2.01 in L2 and 1.96 in the max norm on the finest level); the full equation is first order only, limited by the dissipation's numerical diffusivity ε|n||d|2/(d·n)=O(h), its observed order approaching 1 from below (Section 14).

A.1 完整推导与适定性

A.1 Full derivation and well-posedness

1. 符号、积分测度与三类不同的检查

将定常 PDE 写为 R(u,p)=0，目标泛函记为 J，采用拉格朗日泛函

L=J−∫ΩλTR dΩ.\mathcal L=J-\int_\Omega\lambda^T R\,d\Omega.

页面求解的是 A_h^T ψ_h = −g_h。这里的连续乘子采用相反符号：在守恒方程上有 λ_h=−ψ_h。这些残差已经是控制体积分后的守恒平衡，不需要再除以控制体的体积或面积。被代数边界约束替换的行对应约束乘子，其数值不是连续场的点值。

三类检查分别回答不同的问题：

  1. 转置／点积测试检查 A_h^T 的实现。
  2. 对同一离散问题做有限差分，检查 dJ_h/dp。
  3. 将独立求得的连续伴随与一系列加密网格上的离散伴随比较，检查指定范数下的一致性。比较时必须匹配 PDE、边界条件、目标泛函、计算区域与目标数据。

有限组网格上误差下降是数值证据，不是收敛定理。即使积分范数下的误差下降，边界层或特征跳跃仍可能阻止最大范数下的收敛。

2. 适定性与伴随一致性不是同一件事

出现差异提供了诊断机会，但差异本身不能证明问题不适定。应先明确状态空间与边界迹空间、允许的数据、目标泛函以及范数。Hadamard 意义下的适定性要求解存在、唯一且连续依赖于数据。正确推导的连续伴随可以是适定的，而离散边界闭合却不一致；一个边界数据与目标泛函不匹配的适定方程，仍然是错误的伴随。约束乘子、有限正则性和参考解误差也会造成表观差异。

线性化、零空间与稳定性

把原始边界条件的齐次线性化约束纳入算子 D:X→Y 的定义域 X。如果该算子是适当空间之间的有界同构，则其 Banach 伴随也是相应对偶空间之间的有界同构。这给出一种充分的局部分析框架，并不是写出形式微分表达式就能得到的结论。对于无界 PDE 算子，必须先明确其定义域及图范数，或者采用适当的弱形式。对于非线性原始问题，仅有存在唯一性还不能保证解映射可微；需要在所选解支上检查局部光滑性及线性化可逆性。

对于 D*λ=g，原始算子的零模态给出必要的兼容性条件：

Dv=0⟹g[v]=⟨λ,Dv⟩=0.Dv=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g[v]=\langle\lambda,Dv\rangle=0.

若 ker D* 含有非零元素，则伴随一旦有解就不唯一。仅有与原始零空间正交的条件，并不能构成一般的无穷维存在性定理：还需要考虑值域闭性和稳定性。对齐次伴随边界数据，所需估计具有以下形式：

∥λ∥Y∗≤C∥g∥X∗\|\lambda\|_{Y^*}\le C\|g\|_{X^*}

如果边界数据非齐次，还应加上相应的边界迹范数。每个网格尺度下逆算子有界也不够：在正确加权的离散范数下，稳定界必须与网格尺度无关。不考虑积分残差和控制体权重的原始欧氏条件数，本身不能作为连续问题的诊断结论。

取 c>0，固定原始入口值，目标变分为 ∫₀¹g v dx+b v(1)。对原始线性化 c v' 使用 Green 恒等式，得到

−cλ′=g,cλ(1)=b,λ(x)=bc+1c∫x1g(s) ds.-c\lambda'=g,\qquad c\lambda(1)=b,\qquad \lambda(x)=\frac{b}{c}+\frac1c\int_x^1g(s)\,ds.
  • 缺少出口数据： 解可以加上任意常数，因此不唯一。
  • 额外照搬原始入口条件 λ(0)=0： 存在性要求 b+∫g=0。当 g=1,b=0 时，正确解是 (1−x)/c；额外入口条件使问题无解。
  • 失去一致稳定性： 有 ‖λ‖∞≤(|b|+‖g‖₁)/c。取 g=1,b=0 就达到 1/c 的放大倍数。每个固定的 c>0 都可以对应适定问题，但对于这类数据，不能得到在 c→0 时仍统一有效的界。速度为零时，原始算子退化。

在原二维物体算例中，法向指向流体计算域的外部，所以迎风物面是流体域的原始出流边界。一条已经携带 u=1 的特征线，不可能在这里同时满足零物理通量。首先失败的是所假设的光滑正值原始问题，而不只是某个已经建立的连续伴随的离散逼近。在假定的壁面状态 u=0 处，

δ(12u2β⋅n)=u(β⋅n)v=0\delta(\tfrac12u^2\beta\cdot n)=u(\beta\cdot n)v=0

对任意变分 v 都成立：通量约束丢失了一阶约束信息，特征速度也同时退化。把壁面条件照抄到形式伴随上，不能恢复有效的光滑解支。弱解／熵解表述需要另行分析边界迹与可微性。

对于特征边界对照，每条内部射线都会在有限路程内到达出流边界。边界数据几乎处处确定内部场；切线或角点引出的跳跃可以在允许非光滑解的空间中处理，本身不意味着不适定。特征边界附近的比值 b_u/(a·n) 需要兼容性分析：在这里的物面目标中，分子与分母具有可相消的法向因子，不能只看分母就断言伴随发散。

其余算例说明了什么

对流扩散。 正扩散系数提供椭圆主部，但这不是任意低阶项与 Robin 边界系数下的普遍可逆性证明。仍需检查齐次零空间，以及所用能量估计或最大值原理的假设。独立计算支持当前参数组，没有证明对所有参数都成立的定理。把目标所要求的壁面伴随值 1 改成 0，可能得到另一个适定问题，但会给出错误的目标灵敏度。在远场同时规定状态值和法向导数，一般会过约束。把被替换方程行的乘子当成壁面场值，则是另一类代数含义错误。

拟一维 Euler。 对所述正向亚声速分支，特征速度 u−c,u,u+c 解释了原始入口两个条件、出口一个条件，以及伴随的相反条件数。条件数目正确是必要条件，不是充分条件：特征传播与边界约束共同组成的边值算子还必须可逆。声速点处 B 奇异，当前使用 (Bᵀ)⁻¹ 的表示失效，需要喉部兼容性／正则性分析。这不意味着所有跨声速伴随都不存在。激波还需要考虑激波位置变分及相应内部条件。这些现象超出当前数值证据的范围，可参考 Giles 与 Pierce 的连续喷管伴随分析。强、弱边界处理对数值伴随的不同影响，见 Duivesteijn 等人的研究。

因此，教学顺序应当是：先明确连续原始问题及所选解支；推导伴随，包括其边界定义域；分析存在性、唯一性和稳定性；随后才检查对该问题的一致、稳定逼近。反例应明确保留其失败的前提，不能悄悄替换模型后宣布验证成功。

1. Signs, measures and three distinct checks

Write the steady PDE as R(u,p)=0, the functional as J, and use

L=J−∫ΩλTR dΩ.\mathcal L=J-\int_\Omega\lambda^T R\,d\Omega.

The pages solve A_h^T ψ_h = −g_h. Our continuous multiplier has the opposite sign: λ_h=−ψ_h on conservation equations. Those residuals are integrated balances, so there is no additional division by control-volume measure. Rows replaced by algebraic boundary constraints carry constraint multipliers; their values are not point samples of the continuous field.

Three tests answer different questions:

  1. A transpose/dot test checks the implementation of A_h^T.
  2. Finite differences of the same discrete problem check dJ_h/dp.
  3. Comparing an independently solved continuous dual with a sequence of refined discrete duals tests consistency in a stated norm. It requires matching PDE, boundary conditions, functional, domain, and target data.

A decreasing error on finitely many meshes is numerical evidence, not a theorem of convergence. Boundary layers or characteristic jumps can prevent maximum-norm convergence even when an integral norm decreases.

2. Well-posedness is not the same as adjoint consistency

A discrepancy is a diagnostic opportunity, not by itself proof of ill-posedness. Specify the state and trace spaces, admissible data, functional, and norms first. Hadamard well-posedness requires existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence. A correctly derived continuous dual can be well posed while a discrete boundary closure is inconsistent; a well-posed equation with the wrong objective boundary data is simply the wrong dual. Constraint multipliers, limited regularity and finite reference error are other sources of apparent disagreement.

Linearization, kernels and stability

Include the homogeneous linearized primal boundary constraints in the domain X of the operator D:X→Y. If this is a bounded isomorphism between suitable spaces, its Banach adjoint is also a bounded isomorphism between the dual spaces. This is a sufficient local framework, not a conclusion obtained merely by writing a formal differential expression. An unbounded PDE operator must first be given a domain/graph norm or a suitable weak realization. For a nonlinear primal, existence and uniqueness alone do not guarantee a differentiable solution map; the local smoothness and invertibility hypotheses must be checked on the chosen branch.

For D*λ=g, a primal null mode implies the necessary compatibility condition

Dv=0⟹g[v]=⟨λ,Dv⟩=0.Dv=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g[v]=\langle\lambda,Dv\rangle=0.

A nonzero element of ker D* makes a solvable dual nonunique. Orthogonality to the primal kernel alone is not a general infinite-dimensional existence theorem: closed range and stability still matter. The required estimate has the form

∥λ∥Y∗≤C∥g∥X∗\|\lambda\|_{Y^*}\le C\|g\|_{X^*}

for homogeneous dual boundary data; inhomogeneous boundary data contribute their appropriate trace norm. A bounded inverse at every mesh size is not enough either: the stability bound must be uniform in the properly weighted discrete norms. Raw Euclidean condition numbers without accounting for integrated residuals and control-volume weights are not themselves a continuum diagnosis.

An explicit transport example

For c>0, fixed primal inflow and objective variation ∫₀¹g v dx+b v(1), the Green identity for the primal linearization c v' gives

−cλ′=g,cλ(1)=b,λ(x)=bc+1c∫x1g(s) ds.-c\lambda'=g,\qquad c\lambda(1)=b,\qquad \lambda(x)=\frac{b}{c}+\frac1c\int_x^1g(s)\,ds.
  • Missing outlet data: an arbitrary additive constant remains, so uniqueness fails.
  • An extra copied primal inlet datum λ(0)=0: existence requires b+∫g=0. With g=1,b=0, the correct solution is (1−x)/c; the extra inlet condition makes the problem unsolvable.
  • Loss of uniform stability: ‖λ‖∞≤(|b|+‖g‖₁)/c. Taking g=1,b=0 attains the 1/c amplification. Each fixed c>0 is well posed, but there is no bound uniform as c→0 for this data class. At zero speed the original operator degenerates.

In the original 2-D body example, normals point out of the fluid domain: the upwind body is therefore primal outflow. A characteristic already carrying u=1 cannot satisfy zero physical flux there. This is first a failure of the assumed smooth positive primal problem, not merely a poor discrete approximation to an otherwise established dual. At a putative wall state u=0,

δ(12u2β⋅n)=u(β⋅n)v=0\delta(\tfrac12u^2\beta\cdot n)=u(\beta\cdot n)v=0

for every variation v: the flux constraint loses its first-order information while the characteristic speed degenerates. Copying the wall condition onto a formal dual cannot restore a valid smooth solution branch. Weak/entropy formulations would need separate trace and differentiability analysis.

For the characteristic-boundary contrast, every interior ray reaches outflow in finite distance. Boundary data determine the field almost everywhere; tangency/corner jumps are compatible with a nonsmooth solution space and do not by themselves imply ill-posedness. The ratio b_u/(a·n) requires compatibility at characteristic boundaries: in this particular body objective its numerator and denominator contain a cancelling normal factor. One must not infer blow-up from the denominator alone.

What the other cases demonstrate

Advection–diffusion. Positive diffusion provides an elliptic principal part, not a universal invertibility proof for arbitrary lower-order and Robin coefficients. Homogeneous kernels and the assumptions of applicable energy or maximum-principle estimates still need attention. The independent solves support this parameter set; they do not prove a theorem for all parameters. Setting the wall dual to zero instead of the objective-required one can define a different well-posed problem, but yields the wrong functional sensitivity. Imposing both state and normal derivative at the far field is generally an overconstraint. Treating a replaced-row multiplier as a wall field sample is yet another, algebraic interpretation error.

Quasi-1-D Euler. The speeds u−c,u,u+c explain the two inlet/one outlet primal conditions and reversed dual counts for the stated positive subsonic branch. Counts are necessary, not sufficient: propagation and the boundary constraints together must yield an invertible boundary-value operator. At a sonic point B is singular, so the current representation using (Bᵀ)⁻¹ fails; throat compatibility/regularity analysis is required. This does not imply that all transonic adjoints fail to exist. Shocks require shock-position variations and internal conditions. These phenomena are outside the current numerical evidence; see the continuous nozzle analysis of Giles and Pierce. The distinct effects of strong/weak boundary treatment on numerical duals are discussed by Duivesteijn et al..

The teaching sequence is therefore: identify the continuous primal and chosen branch; derive the dual including its boundary domain; examine existence, uniqueness and stability; only then test consistent, stable approximation of that problem. A counterexample should retain its failed premise visibly, rather than silently substituting another model and declaring success.

Wall functional and wall dual value

The wall has fixed Dirichlet data u=u_w=0. Its objective is the total outward fluid flux into the body,

J=∫Γwq⋅n ds.J=\int_{\Gamma_w}q\cdot n\,ds.

At fixed geometry, v=0 on the wall but ∂n v is free. Hence

δJ=−ν∫Γw∂nv ds.\delta J=-\nu\int_{\Gamma_w}\partial_n v\,ds.

Combining this with the boundary term of −∫λR gives ν(λ−1)∂n v. Therefore

−uβ⋅∇λ−νΔλ=0,λ∣Γw=1.-u\beta\cdot\nabla\lambda-\nu\Delta\lambda=0, \qquad \lambda|_{\Gamma_w}=1.

Zero wall adjoint data would be wrong for this flux functional.

What the implemented far field means in the continuous limit

Let b=β·n for the unit outward normal. Dividing the integrated numerical face flux by face length, the implemented far-field total flux is

H(u,u∞,n)={14b(u2+u∞2)+ϵ(u−u∞),b<0,12bu2,b≥0.H(u,u_\infty,n)= \begin{cases} \tfrac14b(u^2+u_\infty^2)+\epsilon(u-u_\infty),&b<0,\\ \tfrac12bu^2,&b\ge0. \end{cases}

−ν∂nu=14b(u∞2−u2)+ϵ(u−u∞).-\nu\partial_nu=\tfrac14b(u_\infty^2-u^2)+\epsilon(u-u_\infty).

AffTψf=AwfT1W.A_{ff}^T\psi_f=A_{wf}^T1_W.

AffTλf+AwfT1W=0.A_{ff}^T\lambda_f+A_{wf}^T1_W=0.

def fem(mesh,uinf=1.):
 """P1 Galerkin primal and directly assembled continuous dual on a fixed polygon.
 Three-point triangle / three-point edge quadrature; no FV transpose used.
 """

   hp=np.where(bn<0,.5*bn*q+eps,bn*q)
   br+=length[:,None]*w*h[:,None]*phi
   be+=length[:,None,None]*w*hp[:,None,None]*np.outer(phi,phi)
  A=coo_matrix((np.r_[local.ravel(),be.ravel()],(np.r_[rows,erows],np.r_[cols,ecols])),shape=(nn,nn)).tocsr()
  R=np.bincount(T.ravel(),weights=r.ravel(),minlength=nn)+np.bincount(E.ravel(),weights=br.ravel(),minlength=nn)

连续伴随与伴随一致性

Continuous adjoints and adjoint consistency

点积测试查转置，差分与复数步长查同一离散目标的梯度；它们都不能证明离散伴随逼近了连续伴随。本节先推导连续问题，再用独立参考解与网格加密比较。

A dot test checks the transpose; finite differences and the complex step check the gradient of the same discrete objective. None proves approximation of the continuous dual. Here we derive the continuous problem first, then compare with an independent reference under refinement.

不一致时先检查什么：存在、唯一与稳定

What to check when results disagree: existence, uniqueness and stability

伴随不一致与伴随问题不适定不是同一件事。适定性要求在明确的函数空间、边界迹与数据范数下：解存在、唯一，并连续依赖于数据。比较之前先固定同一个原始问题、目标泛函、边界条件和乘子含义；边界层、有限参考误差、约束行乘子或错误的目标边界项，都可能造成差异，不能仅凭一张误差图判定不适定。

Adjoint inconsistency and an ill-posed adjoint problem are different diagnoses. Well-posedness requires existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence on data in specified function spaces, trace spaces and norms. First match the primal problem, functional, boundary conditions and multiplier interpretation. Boundary layers, finite reference error, constraint-row multipliers or an incorrect objective boundary term can all cause discrepancies; an error plot alone cannot diagnose ill-posedness.

在线性化状态及边界约束都已确定后，记原始线性算子为 D，伴随方程为 D*λ=g。若 D 是适当空间之间的有界同构，正确配对的对偶问题也有有界逆。若存在非零零模态 v，则可解性至少要求 g[v]=0；伴随自身有非零齐次解时，解就不唯一。无穷维情形还必须检查值域闭性／稳定估计，不能只数边界条件。

After fixing the linearized state and boundary constraints, denote the primal operator by D and the dual equation by D*λ=g. If D is a bounded isomorphism between suitable spaces, its correctly paired dual also has a bounded inverse. A nonzero primal null mode v requires at least g[v]=0 for solvability; a nonzero homogeneous dual mode makes the dual nonunique. In infinite dimensions one must also check closed range/stability estimates, not merely count boundary conditions.

Dv=0⇒g[v]=⟨λ,Dv⟩=0

非线性原始解存在唯一，也不自动保证其导数存在：还需要所选解支的局部可微性和线性化可逆性。下面讨论的都是已说明的光滑解支；不把有限网格上能解出的转置系统当作连续理论的替代。

Even existence and uniqueness of a nonlinear primal do not automatically ensure differentiability: local smoothness of the chosen solution branch and invertibility of its linearization are additional requirements. The discussion concerns the stated smooth branch; a solvable finite-grid transpose system is not a substitute for continuous analysis.

扩散改善了什么，又没有证明什么

What diffusion improves, and what it does not prove

ν>0 使域内算子具有椭圆主部；壁面 Dirichlet 与远场 Robin 条件共同定义连续边值问题。但“有扩散”本身不是任意系数下存在唯一性的证明，还要核对齐次核、边界系数及适用的能量／最大值原理条件。这里独立求解及参考加密提供了当前参数和解支的数值证据，没有证明所有参数都适定。

ν>0 gives the volume operator an elliptic principal part; wall Dirichlet and far-field Robin conditions define its boundary-value problem together. Diffusion alone does not prove existence and uniqueness for arbitrary coefficients: the homogeneous kernel, boundary coefficients and applicable energy/maximum-principle assumptions still matter. Independent solves and reference refinement provide numerical evidence for this parameter set and branch, not a theorem for all parameters.

把目标所要求的壁面伴随值 1 改成 0，可能仍能解出另一个适定问题，但它不再是本目标的伴随；这是推导／目标不匹配。远场同时施加状态值与法向导数则一般过约束。格点壁面约束乘子与重建场值不同又属于变量含义问题。这三类错误必须分别说明。

Changing the objective-required wall dual value from one to zero may still yield another well-posed problem, but not the adjoint of this objective: that is a derivation/functional mismatch. Prescribing both state and normal derivative at the far field is generally overdetermined. Comparing a node constraint multiplier with the reconstructed wall field is instead a variable-interpretation error. These are distinct diagnoses.

矩阵能求逆为什么还不够

Why an invertible matrix is not enough

即使每个有限网格上的 AhT 都可逆，也不保证在网格相关的正确加权范数中，其逆有与 h 无关的界。人工耗散或边界闭合可能让每个有限系统可解，却不能给出目标连续问题的稳定极限。要判断一致性，应先建立连续问题，再检查残差与目标的逼近、边界乘子的对应关系以及离散稳定性；若这些前提不成立，就把它保留为反例并准确指出失败环节。

Even if every finite-grid AhT is invertible, its inverse need not be bounded independently of h in the appropriate mesh-weighted norms. Artificial dissipation or boundary closure may make every finite system solvable without providing a stable limit for the intended continuous problem. Establish the continuous problem first, then check residual/functional approximation, boundary-multiplier interpretation and discrete stability. If a premise fails, retain the example and identify the failed premise explicitly.
