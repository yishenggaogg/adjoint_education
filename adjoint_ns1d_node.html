一维层流 Navier–Stokes 方程的离散伴随：格点格式

Discrete adjoints for 1D laminar Navier–Stokes: node-centred

从 Euler 的守恒通量继续，加入分子黏性、黏性功和热传导。我们在等截面的光滑可压缩流中逐步构造残差、matrix-free tangent 与 adjoint，再求温度和热流目标对热源及物性参数的导数。面向学过微积分、线性代数和数值方法的本科生；全部图和播放器来自本页实际计算。

Continue from Euler fluxes by adding molecular viscosity, viscous work and heat conduction. For smooth compressible flow of constant area, build residuals, matrix-free tangents and adjoints, then differentiate temperature and heat-flux objectives with respect to heating and transport. Calculus, linear algebra and numerical methods are the prerequisites. Plots and players use the actual computations on this page.

第 1 节Section 1

网格与未知量

Mesh and unknowns

从 Euler 到 N–S，未知量仍是密度、动量和总能量。新增困难来自面通量需要相邻状态的梯度，而不是新增一个“黏性未知量”。本页只考虑沿 x 方向变化的可压缩层流，横向速度为零、截面恒定；它不解析管壁边界层，也不包含湍流模型。

The unknowns remain density, momentum and total energy. The new difficulty is that face fluxes depend on state gradients, not a new “viscous unknown”. This is plane one-dimensional compressible laminar flow with zero transverse velocity and constant area. It resolves neither duct-wall boundary layers nor turbulence.

U=(ρ,ρu,ρE)T,E=cvT+12u2,p=ρT,cv=2.5, cp=3.5.U=(\rho,\rho u,\rho E)^T,\quad E=c_vT+\tfrac12u^2,\quad p=\rho T,\quad c_v=2.5,\ c_p=3.5.

本版把 U 放在 N+1 个节点上。内部对偶面是相邻节点中点，两个端点的控制体宽度为 h/2。入口替换三条状态约束；出口保留质量平衡，另外两行施加速度／温度或应力／热流条件。被替换的行不再是守恒平衡，伴随必须先清除其旧种子。

This version places U on N+1 nodes. Interior dual faces lie at midpoints and endpoint volumes have width h/2. Three inlet rows impose state constraints. At the outlet the mass balance remains, while two rows impose velocity/temperature or stress/heat-flux conditions. Replaced rows are no longer balances: their old adjoint seeds must be masked.

第 2 节Section 2

方程、物性与重构

Equations, transport and reconstruction

质量方程没有物理黏性扩散；动量通过轴向正应力传递，总能量通过黏性功和热传导交换。先把物理通量与数值耗散分开：Rusanov 的耗散用于对流离散，不能解释成分子黏度。

There is no physical mass diffusion. Axial normal stress transfers momentum; viscous work and heat conduction transfer energy. Rusanov dissipation belongs to the convective discretization and is not molecular viscosity.

Ut+(Fc−Fv)x=S,Fc=[ρuρu2+pu(ρE+p)],Fv=[0τuτ−q].U_t+(F^c-F^v)_x=S,\quad F^c=\begin{bmatrix}\rho u\\\rho u^2+p\\u(\rho E+p)\end{bmatrix},\quad F^v=\begin{bmatrix}0\\\tau\\u\tau-q\end{bmatrix}.
τ=43μux,q=−κTx,κ=μcp/Pr,μ(T)=μref1.4T3/2T+0.4.\tau=\tfrac43\mu u_x,\quad q=-\kappa T_x,\quad \kappa=\mu c_p/Pr,\quad \mu(T)=\mu_{ref}\frac{1.4T^{3/2}}{T+0.4}.

4/3 来自三维 Newton 应力在平面一维运动下的 Stokes 假设。常物性模式令 μ=μ_ref；变物性模式使用上面的无量纲 Sutherland 形式。尺度取长度 L_ref、密度 ρ_ref、温度 T_ref 和速度 √(R_g T_ref)，因此代码中的 μ_ref 已除以 ρ_ref L_ref √(R_g T_ref)，不再另乘 1/Re。γ=1.4，气体常数在这些单位下为 1。

The factor 4/3 follows from the three-dimensional Newtonian stress with Stokes’ hypothesis restricted to plane motion. Constant transport sets μ=μ_ref; variable transport uses the displayed nondimensional Sutherland law. Scales are L_ref, ρ_ref, T_ref and velocity √(R_g T_ref). The coded μ_ref is already divided by ρ_ref L_ref √(R_g T_ref); no extra 1/Re is applied. Gamma is 1.4 and the scaled gas constant is one.

F^c=12(Fc(U−)+Fc(U+))−α2(U+−U−),α=12(u−+c−+u++c+).\widehat F^c=\tfrac12(F^c(U^-)+F^c(U^+))-\tfrac\alpha2(U^+-U^-),\quad\alpha=\tfrac12(u^-+c^-+u^++c^+).

当前参数限制在正向亚声速，因此这个平均速度在光滑重构下用作 Rusanov 形式的数值耗散；不宣称它是任意跳跃的最大波速界。一阶为常值重构；二阶为无 limiter 的中心线性重构，边界单元用单侧线性模板。两种模式均保留中心黏性梯度及二阶边界梯度，开关改变的是对流重构。

Parameters are restricted to positive subsonic flow. This averaged speed supplies Rusanov-form dissipation for smooth traces; it is not claimed to bound waves for arbitrary jumps. First order uses constant reconstruction; second order uses unlimited centred linear reconstruction with one-sided endpoint stencils. Both retain centred viscous gradients and second-order boundary gradients: the switch changes convective reconstruction.

第 3 节Section 3

边界条件

Boundary conditions

黏性 N–S 不能直接复制 Euler 的入口总量和出口压力条件。热传导需要热边界，应力也参与出口闭合。本页物理加热问题采用入口 ρ=1、u=0.35、T=T_in；出口 p−τ=p_b、q=0。出口不是同时指定 p=p_b 和 τ=0。初始均匀状态与零热源、T_in=p_b=1 相容；小幅热源给出光滑分支。

Viscous N–S cannot simply copy Euler inlet-total/outlet-pressure conditions. Conduction needs a thermal condition and stress enters the outlet closure. The heated case prescribes inlet ρ=1, u=0.35, T=T_in and outlet p−τ=p_b, q=0. It does not prescribe both p=p_b and τ=0. A uniform zero-heating state is compatible with T_in=p_b=1; small heating develops a smooth branch.

制造解采用同一入口条件，出口改为 u=0.35、T=T_out。两种边界配置是不同问题。格心入口使用规定状态的物理通量；黏性梯度由边界值及前两个单元作二次插值。格点用端点二阶单侧梯度，并替换相应边界行。页顶参数改变后，点击计算才更新所有图。

The manufactured case uses the same inlet but prescribes u=0.35 and T=T_out at the outlet. These are different boundary-value problems. Cell-centred inlet convection uses the prescribed physical state; viscous gradients use a quadratic through the boundary and first two cells. Nodes use second-order one-sided endpoint gradients and replace the relevant rows. After changing top controls, use Solve to update every panel.

(ux)0≈−83ub+3u0−13u1h(cell),(ux)0≈−3u0+4u1−u22h(node).(u_x)_0\approx\frac{-\frac83u_b+3u_0-\frac13u_1}{h}\quad\text{(cell)},\qquad (u_x)_0\approx\frac{-3u_0+4u_1-u_2}{2h}\quad\text{(node)}.

对流—扩散系统的可解性不能仅靠数边界条件判定。这里通过独立连续边值解、真实残差、状态正值和加密对照验证所开放的光滑分支。接近声速、负温度或失败的 Newton 更新会报告失败，不能裁剪状态后继续宣称求解了原方程。

Counting boundary conditions alone does not prove well-posedness. An independent continuous BVP, true residuals, positive states and refinement test the exposed smooth branch. Sonic approaches, negative temperatures or failed Newton updates are reported, not silently clipped into a different equation.

第 4 节Section 4

守恒残差

Conservative residual

Ri=Hi+1/2−Hi−1/2−Si,H=F^c−Fv,Si=∫IiS dx.R_i=H_{i+1/2}-H_{i-1/2}-S_i,\quad H=\widehat F^c-F^v,\quad S_i=\int_{I_i}S\,dx.

每个面先重构对流状态，再从相邻单元／节点形成黏性梯度。这里内部面物性在左右守恒状态平均值对应的温度上求值，梯度取原始变量差除以距离。这两个步骤不相同，求导时都要保留。源项使用两点 Gauss 积分。

Reconstruct convective traces, then form viscous gradients from neighbouring states. Interior transport coefficients use the temperature converted from the average conservative state, while gradients use primitive-variable differences divided by distance. These are different operations and both must be differentiated. Sources use two-point Gauss quadrature.

R[:] = 0

The player uses updates from the actual computation. Inspect convective, viscous and net fluxes, then their left/right scatter. Node mode ends with replaced boundary rows; global flux accounting distinguishes raw balances from final constraints.

第 5 节Section 5

Jacobian 与块 GS

Jacobian and block GS

A=R_U 是完整离散残差的 Jacobian：它包含对流重构、状态转换、黏性梯度、温度物性、边界和目标需要的耦合。matrix-free 指用局部链式法则算 Av 或 Aᵀw，不表示这些导数不存在。

A=R_U is the full residual Jacobian, including reconstruction, state conversion, gradients, temperature-dependent transport and boundaries. Matrix-free means evaluating Av or Aᵀw through local chain rules, not omitting those derivatives.

AΔU=−R,P=D+L+Uupper,(D+L)z=r.A\Delta U=-R,\quad P=D+L+U_{upper},\quad (D+L)z=r.

P 用当前状态的一阶对流残差构造，但保留完整黏性与热传导。每个位置是 3×3 块。一次前向块 GS 是 B=(D+L)⁻¹；伴随使用 Bᵀ，即反向扫描并转置每个块。小型教学实现显式存储 P；精确二阶算子仍通过计算图乘积调用。下面额外组装 A 仅用于观察结构。

P uses first-order convection at the current state but retains viscosity and conduction. Each position is a 3×3 block. One forward block sweep is B=(D+L)⁻¹; its adjoint is Bᵀ, with reverse order and transposed blocks. This small teaching implementation stores P explicitly; the exact second-order operator is still applied by graph products. The extra assembled A below is for inspection.

第 6 节Section 6

目标函数

Objectives

JT=12∫01(T−T∗target)2dx,T∗target=1+0.04sin⁡(πx),Jout=T(1),Jq=q(0).J_T=\tfrac12\int_0^1(T-T_*^{target})^2dx,\quad T_*^{target}=1+0.04\sin(\pi x),\quad J_{out}=T(1),\quad J_q=q(0).

温度跟踪是默认目标，固定解析目标在改变设计参数和网格时不变。出口温度是端点目标；入口导热通量含梯度和物性，因此有更长的显式依赖链。制造解的出口温度由边界给定，其对热源的导数应为零，这是有用的退化检查。

Tracking uses a fixed analytic target under parameter changes and mesh refinement. Outlet temperature is an endpoint objective. Inlet heat flux depends on gradients and transport coefficients, giving a longer explicit dependency chain. In the manufactured case outlet temperature is prescribed, so its heat-source derivative should vanish: a useful degenerate check.

第 7 节Section 7

前向 tangent

Forward tangent

给状态一个扰动 v，沿实际计算链传播。先用商法则从守恒量得到 δu、δT，再对梯度、物性和通量应用乘积法则。新增的项不是高阶小量，不能省略。

Perturb the state by v and follow the actual computational chain. Use quotient rules to obtain δu and δT from conservative variables, then differentiate gradients, transport and fluxes by product rules. The new terms are not negligible higher-order terms.

δu=δ(ρu)−uδρρ,δT=δ(ρE)−Eδρρcv−uδucv.\delta u=\frac{\delta(\rho u)-u\delta\rho}{\rho},\quad \delta T=\frac{\delta(\rho E)-E\delta\rho}{\rho c_v}-\frac{u\delta u}{c_v}.
δτ=43(μδux+uxδμ),δq=−κδTx−Txδκ,δ(uτ−q)=τδu+uδτ−δq.\delta\tau=\tfrac43(\mu\delta u_x+u_x\delta\mu),\quad \delta q=-\kappa\delta T_x-T_x\delta\kappa,\quad\delta(u\tau-q)=\tau\delta u+u\delta\tau-\delta q.
δμ=μTδT+μμrefδμref,μT=μ(32T−1T+0.4),δκ=cpPrδμ−κPrδPr.\delta\mu=\mu_T\delta T+\frac{\mu}{\mu_{ref}}\delta\mu_{ref},\quad \mu_T=\mu\left(\frac{3}{2T}-\frac1{T+0.4}\right),\quad\delta\kappa=\frac{c_p}{Pr}\delta\mu-\frac{\kappa}{Pr}\delta Pr.

常物性模式只有 μ_T=0，设计变量 μ_ref 的导数仍需保留。以下播放器使用固定非零状态种子 v_i=sin(i+1)，计算 Av，不把它与求解出的设计 tangent 混淆。

Constant transport sets only μ_T=0; derivatives with respect to μ_ref remain. The player uses the fixed nonzero state seed v_i=sin(i+1) to evaluate Av, distinct from the solved design tangent.

第 8 节Section 8

反向 adjoint

Reverse adjoint

伴随不是重新推导一个相似通量，而是反转同一个计算图。内部面的种子为 w_left−w_right。先反传净通量，再反传黏性功、应力、热流、面物性、梯度，最后回到守恒状态。每条共享路径都使用累加。

The adjoint reverses the same graph rather than inventing a similar flux. An interior face receives w_left−w_right. Reverse the net flux, viscous work, stress, heat flux, transport, gradients and finally conservative states. Every shared dependency accumulates contributions.

gf=(uR−uL)/h:uˉL−=gˉf/h,uˉR+=gˉf/h,wTAv=vTATw.g_f=(u_R-u_L)/h:\quad \bar u_L\mathrel{-}=\bar g_f/h,\quad \bar u_R\mathrel{+}=\bar g_f/h,\qquad w^TAv=v^TA^Tw.

例如 e_v=uτ−q：先累加 ū+=τ ē_v、τ̄+=u ē_v、q̄−=ē_v。再由 q=−κT_x 累加 κ̄−=T_x q̄、T̄_x−=κq̄。这解释了为什么冻结 κ(T) 会得到错误的完整梯度，即使那套冻结算子仍能通过自己的转置测试。

For e_v=uτ−q, accumulate ū+=τ ē_v, τ̄+=u ē_v and q̄−=ē_v. Then q=−κT_x gives κ̄−=T_x q̄ and T̄_x−=κq̄. Freezing κ(T) changes the full derivative even if the frozen operator passes its own transpose test.

第 9 节Section 9

前向方程求解

Tangent solve

R(U(a),a)=0 ⇒ Av=−Raδa,δJ=JUv+Jaδa.R(U(a),a)=0\ \Rightarrow\ Av=-R_a\delta a,\qquad\delta J=J_Uv+J_a\delta a.

先收敛原始流场，再固定该状态建立线性系统。右端来自参数对残差的显式影响，包括热源、入口温度和出口闭合。一个参数方向需要一次 tangent 求解；它给出整条密度、速度和温度响应，不只是一个目标梯度。

Converge the primal before forming the fixed linear system. The right-hand side includes explicit parameter effects in heating, inlet temperature and outlet closure. Each design direction needs one tangent solve and gives a complete field response, not just one objective derivative.

第 10 节Section 10

伴随方程求解

Adjoint solve

L=J+ψTR,ATψ=−JUT,dJ/da=Ja+RaTψ.\mathcal L=J+\psi^TR,\quad A^T\psi=-J_U^T,\quad dJ/da=J_a+R_a^T\psi.

一次伴随求解覆盖全部六个参数。这里的 ψ 对应积分残差；若把残差除以控制体体积，乘子必须相应缩放。格点的边界约束乘子也不能当作同位置的连续场值。图按状态分量显示离散乘子，并显示 Aᵀψ+J_Uᵀ 的真实残差。

One adjoint solve covers all six parameters. These multipliers correspond to integrated residuals; volume-normalized residuals require rescaling. Node boundary-constraint multipliers are not point samples of a continuous field. The plot shows component multipliers and the true residual Aᵀψ+J_Uᵀ.

第 11 节Section 11

设计参数与总导数

Design parameters and total derivatives

参数依次是两个热源幅度 a₀、a₁、μ_ref、Pr、入口温度，以及出口应力 p_b（制造解中改为 T_out）。热源形状为 sin²(πx)、sin²(2πx)。黏度参数会同时改变动量和能量通量；Pr 只改变导热系数。

Parameters are two heat amplitudes a₀,a₁, μ_ref, Pr, inlet temperature and outlet stress p_b (T_out for the manufactured case). Heat shapes are sin²(πx) and sin²(2πx). Viscosity affects both momentum and energy fluxes; Pr changes conduction.

制造源项在建立当前模型时由当前基准 μ_ref、Pr 和物性模式生成，随后在该模型的所有参数差分中固定。改变顶部物性再建立模型会生成新的制造算例。局部设计导数不重新制造源项，也不重新生成目标函数。

Manufactured forcing is generated with the current baseline μ_ref, Pr and transport mode when creating the model, then held fixed for every parameter perturbation of that model. Changing top-level transport creates a new manufactured case. Local design derivatives never regenerate forcing or the objective target.

第 12 节Section 12

有限差分与 Taylor 检验

Finite differences and Taylor tests

每一个差分点都重新求解完整原始问题。选择参数和步长，比较前向、后向、中心差分；同时显示扰动后的真实原始残差。小步长可能暴露求解容差和舍入误差，不能只看一行差分结果。所有差分共用基准的固定目标和固定制造源项。

Every finite-difference point re-solves the full primal. Select a parameter and step to compare forward, backward and centred differences, including perturbed primal residuals. Small steps expose solve tolerances and roundoff. All perturbations share the baseline fixed target and manufactured forcing.

DhcJ=J(a+hek)−J(a−hek)2h,E1(h)=∣J(a+hek)−J(a)∣,E2(h)=∣J(a+hek)−J(a)−hgk∣.D_h^cJ=\frac{J(a+he_k)-J(a-he_k)}{2h},\quad E_1(h)=|J(a+he_k)-J(a)|,\quad E_2(h)=|J(a+he_k)-J(a)-hg_k|.

在可微且误差未被求解容差掩盖的区间，E₁ 通常为 O(h)，E₂ 为 O(h²)。零梯度目标可能有不同的首项，应先检查具体目标再解读斜率。

For a differentiable map above the solve-error floor, E₁ is usually O(h) and E₂ is O(h²). A zero-gradient objective can have a different leading term; inspect the objective before interpreting slopes.

第 13 节Section 13

复数步长

Complex step

gk≈Im⁡J(U(a+ihek),a+ihek)/h,h=10−25.g_k\approx\operatorname{Im}J(U(a+ih e_k),a+ih e_k)/h,\qquad h=10^{-25}.

正温度分支上的 Sutherland 幂函数可以解析延拓。本实现用独立复数算术重新计算全部残差与目标，并迭代复数残差的缩放虚部至收敛；它不是把 tangent 结果换一个标签。正值检查在实基态上进行，复数算术中不对复数执行大小比较。

On the positive-temperature branch the Sutherland power has an analytic continuation. Independent complex arithmetic evaluates the entire residual and objective and iterates the scaled imaginary residual to convergence; it is not a relabelled tangent result. Admissibility checks use the real base state, not ordering comparisons on complex numbers.

第 14 节Section 14

一阶与二阶精度
