变截面流道拟一维 Euler 方程的格点格式离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of the quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations in a variable-area duct, node-centred

算例是一段收缩—扩张流道：截面积 A(x) 从 1 收到喉部的 0.7 再张回 1，左端给总压 p0 与总温 T0，右端给背压 pb，全场亚声速。未知量是 13 个节点上的守恒量 (ρ,ρu,ρE)，一共 39 个。与二维标量那一对页面相比，这里多出三样东西：局部导数从一个数变成 3×3 块、残差里多了一个不经过任何面的源项 pdA/dx、边界条件是对边界节点自身状态的非线性约束。设计变量就是 13 个节点截面积，因此 dL/dA 有直接的物理含义：把流道这里放宽一点，目标会怎么变。The test case is a converging–diverging duct: the area A(x) falls from 1 to 0.7 at the throat and opens back to 1, with total pressure p0 and total temperature T0 prescribed at the left, a back pressure pb at the right, and subsonic flow throughout. The unknowns are the conserved variables (ρ,ρu,ρE) on 13 nodes, 39 in all. Against the two-dimensional scalar pair of pages, three things are new here: the local derivatives become 3×3 blocks, the residual gains a source term pdA/dx that passes through no face at all, and the boundary conditions are nonlinear constraints on the boundary nodes' own states. The design variables are the 13 nodal areas, so dL/dA means something you can point at: widen the duct here, and the objective moves by this much.

第 1 节
Section 1

流道与网格

The duct and the grid

拟一维的意思是：流道沿 x 的截面积 A(x) 在变，但每个截面上的流动当成均匀的。三维的管内流动于是塌缩成一条线上的三个守恒量，而几何完全由 A(x) 承担。本页与格心格式那一页用的是同一段流道、同一张网格：x∈[0,1]，A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx)，两端为 1、喉部 0.7。

Quasi-one-dimensional means the duct's area A(x) varies along x while the flow across each section is treated as uniform. A three-dimensional duct flow collapses onto a line carrying three conserved quantities, with the geometry carried entirely by A(x). This page uses the same duct and the same grid as the cell-centred page: x∈[0,1] with A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx), unity at both ends and 0.7 at the throat.

不同的是未知量放在哪里。格点格式把守恒量放在 13 个节点上，控制体是相邻节点之间的中点围出来的——所以内部节点的控制体长 Δx，而两端的节点只有半个控制体。未知量共 39 个（）。

What differs is where the unknowns live. A node-centred scheme puts the conserved variables on the 13 nodes, with control volumes bounded by the midpoints between neighbouring nodes — so an interior node's volume is Δx long while the two end nodes get half a volume each. There are 39 unknowns ().

这一点差别决定了整页的走向：边界节点就在边界上。格心格式的未知量是单元平均值，物理边界落在两个面上，没有任何未知量住在那里，所以边界条件只能弱加——先用特征关系算出一个外侧状态，再喂给通量。格点格式不同：n1 与 n13 本身就是边界点，它们的状态就是边界状态。于是可以强加——直接把它们那几条方程换掉。第 3 节展开。

That one difference sets the course of this page: a boundary node sits on the boundary. A cell-centred scheme's unknowns are cell averages, the physical boundary falls on two faces, and no unknown lives there — so the conditions can only be imposed weakly, by building an outer state from the characteristic relations and feeding it to the flux. Here n1 and n13 are boundary points and their states are the boundary states. So the conditions can be imposed strongly, by replacing those equations outright. Section 3 does it.

要离散的方程与面上的通量

The equations to discretise, and the flux on a face

守恒形式，面积写进守恒量里：

In conservative form, with the area carried inside the conserved variable:

∂(AU)∂t+∂(AF)∂x=S, U=​ρρuρE, F=​ρuρu2+pρuH, S=​0pdAdx0

配理想气体状态方程 p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2)、H=E+p/ρ，取 γ=1.4、R=1。

closed by the ideal-gas relations p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2) and H=E+p/ρ, with γ=1.4 and R=1.

源项是这一对页面的第一件新东西。S 只进动量方程，而且它是个体积项：它不穿过任何面，是在控制体内部产生的。二维对流那一对页面的残差完全由循环拼成，这里循环之外还要再走一趟。它的转置同样落在节点自己身上、不经过面；漏掉它，点积测试就不再成立——实测相对误差从 10−16 量级升到 10−5 到 10−2，随所取的向量而定。

The source term is the first new thing on these pages. S enters only the momentum equation, and it is a volume term: it crosses no face, it is produced inside the control volume. On the two-dimensional advection pages the residual was assembled entirely by the loop; here one more pass is needed after it. Its transpose likewise lands on the node itself and passes through no face; drop it and the dot-product test fails — measured, the relative error rises from order 10−16 to between 10−5 and 10−2, depending on the vectors drawn.

面上的数值通量

The numerical flux on a face

面上取 Rusanov 通量：中心平均减一个正比于状态跳跃的耗散项，

Each face takes the Rusanov flux: a central average minus a dissipation proportional to the jump in state,

hf=Af[12(FL+FR)−12λf(UR−UL)], λf=12[(|u|+a)L+(|u|+a)R]

为什么 λ 取两侧平均而不是取大者。教科书里的 Rusanov 用 max。max 在两侧相等处不可微：哪一侧更大随状态切换，切换处导数跳变——那一点没有唯一的梯度，差分步长一旦跨过它，有限差分就与线性化对不上。取平均则只要 u≠0 就处处光滑（本算例 u 处处为正），而且在本算例的亚声速流场里耗散量级几乎相同：实测 max 最多只比平均值大 1.4%。真实代码里要用 max 时的做法是把它冻结在 u* 处，与二维页冻结远场入流/出流开关完全一样。

Why λ is the average of the two sides rather than the larger. Textbook Rusanov uses max. But max is not differentiable where the two sides cross: which side is larger switches with the state, and the derivative jumps there — that point has no single gradient, and a finite difference whose step straddles it disagrees with the linearisation. The average is smooth wherever u≠0 (and u>0 throughout this duct) and, in this subsonic flow, dissipates by almost the same amount: measured, the max is at most 1.4% larger than the average. When a real code does use max, the remedy is to freeze it at u*, exactly as the two-dimensional pages freeze the far-field inflow/outflow switch.

本页同样采用一阶空间离散：对偶面上的左右状态直接取两侧节点的当前值，不做重构。代价只在精度上，不在这套代数上。

The discretisation here is likewise first order: the left and right states on a dual face are the current values of the two adjacent nodes, with no reconstruction. The cost is accuracy only, not the algebra this page is about.

在里点流道中的任意一条对偶面，看收敛解在它两侧的状态怎样组成通量：中心项、耗散项各是多少，λf 取平均与取 max 差多少。也可以拖动滑块，自己设定两侧状态。

守恒守的是通量，不是节点值

What is conserved is the flux, not the nodal value

稳态下质量方程没有源项，所以在内部节点上，数值质量通量在各条内部对偶面上必须完全相同——下面实测到机器精度。节点上的 ρuA 则沿程变化约 15%：两者的差是耗散项 −12λ(UR−UL)Af，节点值和通量本来就不是同一个东西。两端却不一样。两个边界节点的质量方程（入口还有动量方程）被边界条件替换掉了，它们的通量平衡并没有被满足：出口节点上的 ρuA 比内部通量高出约 11%，被丢掉的质量平衡是 3.6×10−2。这是强加边界、整行替换的代价，下面的面板把它量了出来。

At steady state the mass equation has no source, so at the interior nodes the numerical mass flux must be identical on every interior dual face — measured below, to machine precision. The nodal value of ρuA, meanwhile, varies by about 15% along the duct: the difference is the dissipation term −12λ(UR−UL)Af, and a nodal value and a flux are simply different objects. The two ends are another matter. The mass equations of the two boundary nodes (and the momentum equation at the inlet) have been replaced by the boundary conditions, so their flux balances are not satisfied: ρuA at the outlet node is about 11% above the interior flux, and the discarded mass balance is 3.6×10−2. That is the price of imposing the boundary conditions strongly, by replacing whole rows, and the panel below measures it.

在里逐条对偶面切换，能看到同一件事的另一面：质量和能量的 h 在全部 12 条对偶面上相同到舍入误差（能量方程同样没有源项），中心项和耗散项却各自在变；动量的 h 逐条对偶面不同，相邻两条对偶面之间的差额正好由源项 pΔA 补上。

Stepping from dual face to dual face in shows the other side of the same fact: the mass and energy components of h agree on all 12 dual faces to round-off (the energy equation has no source either), while the central and dissipation terms each change; the momentum component does change from one dual face to the next, and between two neighbouring dual faces the source pΔA makes up exactly the difference.

边界条件：强加与部分行替换

Boundary conditions: strong imposition and partial row replacement

特征分析和格心页完全一样（）：亚声速时左端有 2 条特征进入区域、右端 1 条，所以左端给 2 个条件（总压 p0、总温 T0）、右端给 1 个（背压 pb）。变的是怎么加。

The characteristic analysis is the same as on the cell-centred page (): subsonic flow lets 2 characteristics into the domain at the left and 1 at the right, so two conditions are prescribed at the left (p0 and T0) and one at the right (pb). What changes is how they are imposed.

边界节点本身就是未知量，所以直接把它的方程换掉：

The boundary node is itself an unknown, so its equations are simply replaced:

R1,mass←p0(u1)−p0given, R1,mom←T0(u1)−T0given, R13,mass←p(u13)−pb

注意：这是部分行替换。入口节点有 3 条方程，只换掉 2 条；出口节点只换掉 1 条。剩下的那些照常由通量平衡给出。换几条不是随便定的——正好等于进入区域的特征条数。二维对流扩散的格点页面里的强加是把壁面节点的整行换成 ui=uw，那是「一个自由度、一个条件」的特例；这里一个节点携带三条方程，约束只落在其中一部分上。

Note that this is a partial row replacement. The inflow node carries three equations and only two are replaced; the outflow node has only one replaced. The rest come from the flux balance as usual. How many get replaced is not a choice — it is exactly the number of characteristics entering the domain. The strong imposition on the two-dimensional advection–diffusion node page replaces a wall node's whole equation by ui=uw, the special case of "one degree of freedom, one condition"; here a node carries three equations and the constraint lands on a subset.

被替换的行是约束的梯度，不是单位行

The replaced rows are constraint gradients, not unit rows

把 R←p0(u)−p0given 对 u 求导，得到的那一行是 ∂p0/∂u——一个一般的行向量，三个分量都不为零（）。二维对流扩散格点页里对应的行是 eiT（只有一个 1）。所以那一页是本页的特例：当约束恰好是「某个自由度等于常数」时，它的梯度才退化成单位行。

Differentiating R←p0(u)−p0given with respect to u gives the row ∂p0/∂u — a general row vector with all three entries non-zero (). The corresponding row on the two-dimensional advection–diffusion node page is eiT, a single 1. So that page is a special case of this one: only when the constraint happens to be "this degree of freedom equals a constant" does its gradient degenerate into a unit row.

这件事在第 8 节会有直接后果：伴随里那句「出循环后把 wi 加回」要推广成「加回 wi 乘上约束梯度」。

That has a direct consequence in Section 8: the adjoint's "after the loop, add wi back" generalises to "add back wi times the constraint gradient".

还有一处简化：格点格式不需要 ghost 状态。格心页为了给边界面一个外侧状态，必须解一个二次方程、再由等熵关系回到 p,ρ，那整套特征重构在本页完全不存在。边界通量就是 F(u1)A1——用节点自己的状态。强加换来的是边界处理的简化，代价是循环之外多了一步。

And a simplification: a node-centred scheme needs no ghost state. To give its boundary face an outer state, the cell-centred page had to solve a quadratic and return through the isentropic relations; that whole characteristic reconstruction does not exist here. The boundary flux is just F(u1)A1, built from the node's own state. Strong imposition buys a simpler boundary treatment, at the price of one step outside the loop.

残差 = 边循环 + 源项 + 行替换

The residual = the edge loop, the source, and the row replacement

残差分三段：边循环走 12 条内部对偶面，两个边界通量直接用端点自己的状态，然后每个节点上走一趟源项。到这里为止和格心页是同一套骨架。

The residual comes in three parts: the edge loop over the 12 interior dual faces, the two boundary fluxes built from the end nodes' own states, and one source pass per node. Up to here the skeleton is the same as the cell-centred page's.

然后是第四段，本页独有：循环结束之后，把 3 条受约束的行替换掉。它必须在循环之后——循环里那些行照常参与累加，替换是把累加的结果扔掉，写上约束残差（逐句播放全部四段）。

Then a fourth part, unique to this page: after the loop has finished, the 3 constrained rows are replaced. It has to come after — those rows take part in the accumulation like any other, and the replacement discards what accumulated and writes the constraint residual instead ( plays all four parts, statement by statement).

边界节点的控制体只有一半。n1 的控制体从 x1 到第一个中点，所以它只被一条对偶面和一个边界通量围住。这在残差里自然出现，不需要特殊处理——真正需要特殊处理的只有那几条被替换的行。

A boundary node's control volume is a half one. The volume of n1 runs from x1 to the first midpoint, so it is bounded by one dual face and one boundary flux. That falls out of the residual by itself and needs no special handling — the only thing that does is the replaced rows.

求解：Jacobian 与块 Jacobi 迭代

The solve: the Jacobian and block Jacobi

第 4 节的 residual(u, A) 就是离散残差 R(u)。它的 Jacobian A=∂R/∂u 是 39×39 的，但真正的结构是 13×13 个 3×3 块：一个节点只经由两侧的对偶面与它的两个邻居节点相连，所以 A 是块三对角的。被替换的 3 行更简单：每行只有本节点那个块里的 3 个元，是约束梯度。下面解 R(u)=0 的块 Jacobi 迭代只用到它的 13 个对角块，但第 7 到 10 节的切线与伴随逐面作用的就是 A 本身，所以它必须精确：下面逐项求导、解析地组装，不用差分近似。

The residual(u, A) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u). Its Jacobian A=∂R/∂u is 39×39, but its real structure is 13×13 blocks of 3×3: a node is connected, through the dual faces on either side, only to its two neighbouring nodes, so A is block tridiagonal. The 3 replaced rows are simpler still: each has just the 3 entries of its own node's block, a constraint gradient. The block-Jacobi iteration that solves R(u)=0 below needs only its 13 diagonal blocks, but the tangent and the adjoint of Sections 7 to 10 apply A itself, face by face, so it has to be exact. Below it is differentiated term by term and assembled analytically, with no finite-difference approximation.

精确 Jacobian：逐项求导

The exact Jacobian, term by term

① 三个原始量的梯度。所有的块都由它们拼出来（E=(ρE)/ρ）：

1. The gradients of three primitive quantities. Every block is built from them (E=(ρE)/ρ):

∂u∂U=(−uρ,1ρ,0),∂p∂U=(γ−1)(12u2,−u,1),∂a∂U=γ(γ−1)2aρ(u2−E,−u,1)

② 物理通量的 Jacobian。经典的一维 Euler 通量 Jacobian（记作 F′，免得与面积 A 混淆）：

2. The Jacobian of the physical flux — the classical one-dimensional Euler flux Jacobian, written F′ so as not to clash with the areas A:

F′=∂F∂U=​010γ−32u2(3−γ)uγ−1(γ−1)u3−γuEγE−32(γ−1)u2γu

③ 耗散系数。λf=12[(u+a)L+(u+a)R]（本算例 u>0，|u|=u），每一侧只依赖本侧的状态：

3. The dissipation coefficient. λf=12[(u+a)L+(u+a)R] (here u>0, so |u|=u), and each side depends on its own state only:

∂λf∂UL=12(∂u∂U+∂a∂U)|UL,∂λf∂UR=12(∂u∂U+∂a∂U)|UR

④ 一个面的两个块。对面通量 hf 的两侧状态分别求导：

4. The two blocks of a face, the derivatives of the face flux hf with respect to each side's state:

cL=∂h∂UL=Af[12F′(UL)+12λfI−12(UR−UL)∂λf∂UL]
cR=∂h∂UR=Af[12F′(UR)−12λfI−12(UR−UL)∂λf∂UR]

第三项是列向量乘行向量，一个秩 1 的 3×3 矩阵，也是最常被忘记的一项：耗散系数 λf 自己也依赖两侧状态，漏掉它 Jacobian 就不精确；第 7、8 节的前向、伴随代码逐面用的正是这两个块，它们求的就成了另一个函数的导数，梯度会错。

The third term, a column times a row — a rank-1 3×3 matrix — is the one most often forgotten: the dissipation coefficient λf depends on both states as well, and without it the Jacobian is not exact, and since the forward and adjoint code of Sections 7 and 8 apply exactly these two blocks face by face, they would differentiate a different function, and the gradient would be wrong.

⑤ 源项。−piΔAi 只在动量方程里，ΔAi=Ai+12−Ai−12 是控制体两侧对偶面的面积差（两端取端点面积）。它对本节点状态的导数是一行 −ΔAi∂p/∂U，只写进对角块的动量行。

5. The source. −piΔAi sits in the momentum equation only, ΔAi=Ai+12−Ai−12 being the difference of the dual-face areas bounding the control volume (the end areas at the two ends). Its derivative with respect to the node's own state is a single row, −ΔAi∂p/∂U, written into the momentum row of the diagonal block.

⑥ 边界通量。边界节点就在边界上，入口、出口的通量直接用节点自己的状态：A1F(u1) 与 A13F(u13)。它们的导数就是 ② 的 F′ 乘面积：节点 1 的对角块减去 A1F′(u1)（通量流入节点 1），节点 13 的加上 A13F′(u13)。没有外侧状态，也就没有 ∂B/∂u。

6. The boundary fluxes. A boundary node sits on the boundary, so the inflow and outflow fluxes use the nodes' own states: A1F(u1) and A13F(u13). Their derivatives are the F′ of step 2 times the area: A1F′(u1) is subtracted from node 1's diagonal block (the flux enters node 1), and A13F′(u13) is added to node 13's. There is no outer state, and so no ∂B/∂u.

为什么用块 Jacobi，而不用 LU。本页只有 39 个未知量，理论上最快的办法是直接分解：把上面精确的 A 整个造出来，跑 Newton，每一步做一次稠密 LU；从同一个初值出发，全步长 4 次更新就降到判据以下。但那样就体现不出 matrix-free。块 Jacobi 是最简单的迭代法：矩阵只用到 13 个 3×3 对角块，其余一律经由残差进来——切线方程里经由 residual_d，伴随方程里经由 residual_b——这正是 matrix-free 的意思。上面的演示把整个 A 组装出来，只是为了看清它的结构；求解本身从不形成它。代价是线性收敛：在解附近误差每一轮乘以迭代矩阵 I−D−1A，速率由它的谱半径决定，这里是 0.977，所以判据要到 k=1043 才满足。切线与伴随的迭代矩阵与它同谱，进入渐近阶段以后，三条收敛曲线以同一个速率下降（第 10 节）。

Why block Jacobi, and not LU. With only 39 unknowns the fastest method in principle is a direct factorisation: form the whole exact A above, run Newton, and factorise with one dense LU per step; from the same guess, full steps meet the criterion after 4 updates. But that would show nothing about matrix-free. Block Jacobi is the simplest iterative method there is: of the matrix it needs only the 13 diagonal 3×3 blocks, and everything else comes in through the residual (through residual_d in the tangent equation and residual_b in the adjoint equation), which is exactly what matrix-free means. The demo above assembles the whole A only to show its structure; the solve itself never forms it. The price is linear convergence: near the solution the error is multiplied every sweep by the iteration matrix I−D−1A, at a rate set by its spectral radius, 0.977 here, so the criterion is not met until k=1043. The tangent and adjoint iteration matrices have the same spectrum, so once in the asymptotic phase all three convergence curves fall at the same rate (Section 10).

残差比一轮大、一轮小。判据附近，相邻两轮的残差比在 0.721 与 1.322 之间来回跳，隔一轮看却很稳定：每两轮乘 0.9772。块三对角矩阵上的 Jacobi 就是这样：迭代矩阵的特征值成对出现，±λ，这里最大的一对是 ±0.977。最慢的误差是这两个模态的叠加，其中一个每轮变号，最大范数轮流看到两者的和与差。所以本页的速率都按整对的轮次来量（k=400 到 600）。

The ratio alternates. Near the criterion the ratio of successive residuals jumps between 0.721 and 1.322, yet over two sweeps it is steady: 0.9772 every two sweeps. That is Jacobi on a block-tridiagonal matrix: the eigenvalues of its iteration matrix come in pairs ±λ, the largest pair here being ±0.977. The slowest error is a mix of the two modes, one of which changes sign every sweep, and the max norm sees their sum and their difference in turn. So every rate on this page is measured over whole pairs of sweeps (from k=400 to 600).

A fixed sweep count, but a checked one. The code runs a fixed 2400 sweeps rather than exiting at the criterion: the remaining sweeps take u* down to its round-off floor (from k=1161 on), and every measured constant on this page was produced under those sweeps; the u* it returns is within 1.3×10−15 of Newton's. It records the sweep at which the criterion was met, and raises an error if 2400 sweeps never meet it, rather than returning an unconverged state as u*.

What differs is the 3 constrained rows. A row replacement transposes into a column replacement, and in code that is two steps whose order cannot be reversed:

  • 进循环之前，在 w 的一份副本上把受约束的行清零。否则那些行会把本不该存在的贡献分发出去；

亚声速带来一件二维对流页看不到的事：信息双向传播。那两个页面上的模型方程是纯对流的，特征只朝一个方向走，伴随因此只向上游铺开。这里三条特征是 u−a<0<u<u+a：一条朝上游、两条朝下游。于是扰动会同时向两边传，伴随也就铺满整个流道——出口的背压能影响到入口附近的解，入口的总条件也能影响到出口。

Subsonic flow brings something the two-dimensional advection pages cannot show: information travels both ways. Their model equation is pure advection, its characteristics all run one way, and the adjoint therefore spreads only upstream. Here the three characteristics are u−a<0<u<u+a: one upstream, two downstream. Disturbances propagate in both directions, and the adjoint fills the whole duct — the back pressure reaches back towards the inlet, and the inlet's total conditions reach forward to the exit.

Why more than a thousand sweeps? On the two-dimensional advection pages each Jacobi sweep shrinks the residual to about 0.79 (0.80 on the node-centred page), and the adjoint reaches the 10−13 criterion at k=121 (k=108 node-centred); here each sweep shrinks it only to about 0.977, and so it does for the primal and the tangent (Sections 5 and 9): it is the matrix that sets the pace, not any one equation. Block Jacobi exchanges information between neighbouring nodes once per sweep. Multiply D−1 into the blocks that tie a node to its upstream and downstream neighbours and look along the characteristic directions (measured, node 7): Rusanov gives all three waves one dissipation, λ=|u|+a, which is exactly the speed of the fastest wave u+a, so that wave is purely upwinded and tied only upstream (strength 0.99, downstream 0.00); the other two are tied both ways: u with 0.64 upstream and 0.35 downstream, u−a with 0.29 upstream and 0.70 downstream. This is the two-way propagation described at the start of this section: an error has to travel back and forth between inlet and outlet many times before it dies out. As for the primal (Section 5), the ratio between successive residuals alternates large and small, for the adjoint between 0.969 and 0.984 (visible in the status line of ). Production codes keep the same two ingredients, residual_b and the block diagonal D, and accelerate them inside a Krylov method such as GMRES.

dLdAk≈L(Ak+h)−L(Ak−h)2h.

关键在于 L(Ak±h) 是全链路的目标函数：每扰动一个 Ak，都重新收敛一次 primal（块 Jacobi，2400 轮），再算目标。所以这里比的是全导数——状态对几何的响应也算在内——而不是冻结状态的偏导数。代价也正因如此：每个分量两次扰动，13 个分量一共 26 次非线性求解，而伴随只用了一次线性求解。这里验证的是第 11 节的整条链路，组装前把 Ψ 在约束行上清零那一步也在内。

What matters is that L(Ak±h) is the full-chain objective: perturb one Ak, re-converge the primal (block Jacobi, 2400 sweeps), recompute the objective. What is compared is then the total derivative, with the state's response to the geometry included, rather than a partial derivative at frozen state. That is also what it costs: two perturbations per component, 26 nonlinear solves for the 13 of them, against the single linear solve of the adjoint. What is verified is the whole chain of Section 11, the zeroing of Ψ on the constrained rows before assembly included.

The right half is dominated by round-off, which rises about tenfold per order for both, so the two curves close together. The two rings mark each curve's own optimum, where the central scheme is usually more accurate than the forward one by two orders of magnitude or more. The dashed violet line is the step the table above used, and its dot that check's error on this component (the largest one when “all” is chosen); for a power of ten the dot sits exactly on the curve of its scheme. The error is relative because dL/dA differs a lot across the 13 components (measured: 2.4e−6 to 2.8e−4 for inverse design, 4.3e−3 to 0.92 for thrust): on an absolute axis a component with a small gradient would look more accurate for reasons that have nothing to do with the step. The curves are swept live in the browser: change the component and the optimal step moves by a decade or two at most, while the best error the central scheme reaches there changes by two to three orders of magnitude — under inverse design, broadly, the smaller the gradient, the worse.

步长为什么要能调。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ϵ/h)（ϵ≈2.2×10−16 为机器精度），随 h 变小反而增大。两者相抵，最佳步长大致在 h∼ϵ1/3≈6×10−6；前向差分的截断误差是 O(h)，平衡点移到 ϵ1/2≈1.5×10−8。实测扫描与此一致：两个目标、13 个节点面积，中心差分的最优步长都在 10−5 到 10−7 之间（反设计多在 10−6，推力多在 10−5）；前向差分都在 10−7 或 10−8，多数在 10−8。离开最优点，校验坏得很快：反设计、中心差分，最差那个分量的相对误差在 h=10−6 时是 8.4e−8，在 h=10−2 时约 3%（截断主导），在 h=10−12 时约 8%（舍入主导）——两头都会让“验证”看起来像是失败了。这正是差分校验最容易骗人的地方，也是点积测试更可靠的原因：它是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。第 11 节的几何点积测试落在 10−16，而这里的全链路差分在 h=10−6 时，与伴随之差最大还有最大分量的 2.0e−9（反设计）与 1.0e−10（推力）。

Why the step has to be adjustable. A central difference has two error contributions: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks with h; and round-off, about O(ϵ/h) (with ϵ≈2.2×10−16 the machine precision), which grows as h shrinks. They balance roughly at h∼ϵ1/3≈6×10−6; a forward difference truncates at O(h), which moves the balance to ϵ1/2≈1.5×10−8. The measured sweeps agree: over both objectives and all 13 nodal areas, the central optimum lies between 10−5 and 10−7 (mostly at 10−6 for inverse design, 10−5 for thrust), and the forward one at 10−7 or 10−8, mostly 10−8. Away from the optimum the check degrades fast: for inverse design with central differences the worst component is off by 8.4e−8 at h=10−6, but by about 3% at h=10−2, where truncation dominates, and by about 8% at h=10−12, where round-off does — at either end the “verification” looks like a failure. This is exactly where a difference check is most deceptive, and why the dot-product test is the more reliable instrument: it is an exact identity, with no step size in it at all. The geometric dot-product test of Section 11 lands at 10−16, while the full-chain differences here, at h=10−6, still differ from the adjoint by up to 2.0e−9 (inverse design) and 1.0e−10 (thrust) of the largest component.

那么哪一侧更糟？对中心差分，两侧的斜率不同：h 每放大一个数量级，截断误差涨约 100 倍（二次）；h 每缩小一个数量级，舍入误差只涨约 10 倍，而且起伏不定（舍入误差本身近乎随机）。实测离最优点两个数量级时，偏大那一侧更糟的，反设计是 13 个节点面积里的 9 个，推力是 11 个。前向差分没有这种不对称——它的截断误差 O(h) 和舍入误差一样，都是每个数量级约 10 倍——哪一侧更糟随分量而变（偏大那一侧更糟的：反设计 9 个，推力 4 个）。所以实用结论只对中心差分成立：宁可把 h 取偏小，也不要取偏大。

So which side is worse? For central differences the two slopes differ: enlarging h by one order multiplies the truncation error by about 100 (quadratic), while shrinking it multiplies the round-off error by only about 10, and raggedly, since round-off is close to random. Measured two orders of magnitude from the optimum, the too-large side is the worse one for 9 of the 13 nodal areas under inverse design and for 11 under thrust. A forward difference has no such asymmetry — its truncation error, O(h), rises about tenfold per order just as the round-off does — and which side is worse changes from area to area (the too-large side for 9 of them under inverse design, 4 under thrust). So the practical rule holds for central differences only: better to take h a little too small than a little too large.

还有一个更强的检验：已知的最优点。第 6 节里目标压力取自一条已知的流道。在那条流道上，L 应当为零，梯度也应当为零——而且不是「很小」，是逐比特为零，因为同一个确定性的块 Jacobi 会复现出同一个状态。下表就是它。一个梯度能在一个独立构造的最优点上归零，比任何差分对照都更有说服力——它根本不需要步长。

And a stronger check still: a known optimum. In Section 6 the target pressures were taken from a known duct. On that duct both L and the gradient must vanish — and not merely be small, but be zero to the last bit, because the same deterministic block Jacobi reproduces the same state. The table below measures it. A gradient that vanishes at an independently constructed optimum is better evidence than any finite-difference comparison — it needs no step at all.

精度阶：构造解检验

Order of accuracy: a manufactured solution

伴随给出的是离散格式的精确导数，与格式本身是几阶精度无关；格式的精度阶决定的是离散解，以及由它算出的 L 与 dL/dA，离连续问题有多远。这一节用构造解把它测出来：先选一组光滑函数当精确解，由它反推出源项与边界数据，再在一族逐次一致加密的网格上求解，看误差随网格尺寸按几次方减小。

The adjoint gives the exact derivative of the discrete scheme, whatever that scheme's order of accuracy; the order decides how far the discrete solution, and the L and dL/dA computed from it, are from the continuous problem. This section measures it with a manufactured solution: pick smooth functions as the exact solution, derive the source term and the boundary data from them, solve on a family of uniformly refined grids, and see with what power of the grid spacing the error falls.

这对伴随意味着什么

What this means for the adjoint

第 10–12 节验证的是：伴随与几何梯度对离散的 L 精确到舍入误差，这与格式几阶无关。离散的 L 本身则随网格加密以格式的阶逼近连续值。优化器拿到的梯度与它所优化的离散目标函数完全一致，问题是自洽的；网格加密决定的是这个离散问题离连续问题有多近。要让它更近，得提高格式的阶（例如 MUSCL 重构），而不是改伴随。

What Sections 10 to 12 verified is that the adjoint and the geometric gradient are exact, to round-off, for the discrete L, whatever the scheme's order. The discrete L itself approaches the continuous value at the scheme's order as the grid is refined. The gradient the optimiser gets agrees exactly with the discrete objective it optimises, so the problem is self-consistent; refinement decides how close that discrete problem is to the continuous one. To bring it closer one has to raise the scheme's order (a MUSCL reconstruction, say), not change the adjoint.

总结

Summary

  • 边循环是核心：残差就是「每条对偶面收集两侧状态、算一次一维通量、按 +,− 分发」，加上两个边界通量、一趟源项。
  • 边界节点就在边界上，所以边界条件可以强加：不需要 ghost 状态，直接换掉方程。
  • 换的是部分行：入口 3 行换 2 行、出口换 1 行，条数等于进入区域的特征条数。
  • 被替换的行是约束的梯度，一般不是单位行；二维对流扩散格点页是它退化成 eiT 的特例。
  • 伴随里对应两步：进循环前在副本上清零，出循环后加回 wi·(∂c/∂u)，顺序不能反。
  • 几何方向还要再清零一次：被替换的行不含面积，忘了清零梯度就错了——反设计 108%，推力 4%。
  • 一个块 Jacobi，三个方程：primal、切线、伴随都用同一个块 Jacobi 迭代，只用到精确 Jacobian 的 13 个 3×3 对角块与 residual_d、residual_b 两个乘积。三个迭代矩阵同谱，进入渐近阶段后收敛曲线平行（谱半径 0.977）；切线与伴随给出同一个 dL/dpb，相对差 1.4×10−16。
  • The edge loop is the whole story: the residual is "gather both states on each dual face, call the flux once, scatter with + and −", plus two boundary fluxes and one source pass.
  • A boundary node sits on the boundary, so the conditions can be imposed strongly: no ghost state, just replace the equations.
  • The replacement is partial: 2 of 3 rows at the inflow, 1 of 3 at the outflow — as many as there are characteristics entering.
  • The replaced rows are constraint gradients, general rows rather than unit rows; the two-dimensional advection–diffusion node page is the case where they degenerate to eiT.
  • The adjoint answers with two steps: zero on a copy before the loop, add back wi·(∂c/∂u) after it, and the order cannot be swapped.
  • The geometric direction needs the zeroing too: the replaced rows contain no areas, and forgetting it makes the gradient wrong — by 108% for inverse design, 4% for thrust.
  • One block Jacobi, three equations: the primal, the tangent and the adjoint are all solved by the same block-Jacobi iteration, which needs only the 13 diagonal 3×3 blocks of the exact Jacobian and the two products residual_d and residual_b. The three iteration matrices share their spectrum, so once asymptotic the convergence curves run parallel (spectral radius 0.977); the tangent and the adjoint give the same dL/dpb, to 1.4×10−16.
