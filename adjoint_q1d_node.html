变截面流道拟一维 Euler 方程的格点格式离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of the quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations in a variable-area duct, node-centred

算例是一段收缩—扩张流道：截面积 A(x) 从 1 收到喉部的 0.7 再张回 1，左端给总压 p0 与总温 T0，右端给背压 pb，全场亚声速。未知量是 13 个节点上的守恒量 (ρ,ρu,ρE)，一共 39 个。与二维标量那一对页面相比，这里多出三样东西：局部导数从一个数变成 3×3 块、残差里多了一个不经过任何面的源项 pdA/dx、边界条件是内点状态的非线性函数。设计变量就是 13 个节点截面积，因此 dL/dA 有直接的物理含义：把流道这里放宽一点，目标会怎么变。The test case is a converging–diverging duct: the area A(x) falls from 1 to 0.7 at the throat and opens back to 1, with total pressure p0 and total temperature T0 prescribed at the left, a back pressure pb at the right, and subsonic flow throughout. The unknowns are the conserved variables (ρ,ρu,ρE) on 13 nodes, 39 in all. Against the two-dimensional scalar pair of pages, three things are new here: the local derivatives become 3×3 blocks, the residual gains a source term pdA/dx that passes through no face at all, and the boundary conditions are nonlinear functions of the interior state. The design variables are the 13 nodal areas, so dL/dA means something you can point at: widen the duct here, and the objective moves by this much.

第 1 节
Section 1

流道与网格

The duct and the grid

拟一维的意思是：流道沿 x 的截面积 A(x) 在变，但每个截面上的流动当成均匀的。三维的管内流动于是塌缩成一条线上的三个守恒量，而几何完全由 A(x) 承担。本页与格心格式那一页用的是同一段流道、同一张网格：x∈[0,1]，A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx)，两端为 1、喉部 0.7。

Quasi-one-dimensional means the duct's area A(x) varies along x while the flow across each section is treated as uniform. A three-dimensional duct flow collapses onto a line carrying three conserved quantities, with the geometry carried entirely by A(x). This page uses the same duct and the same grid as the cell-centred page: x∈[0,1] with A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx), unity at both ends and 0.7 at the throat.

不同的是未知量放在哪里。格点格式把守恒量放在 13 个节点上，控制体是相邻节点之间的中点围出来的——所以内部节点的控制体长 Δx，而两端的节点只有半个控制体。未知量共 39 个。

What differs is where the unknowns live. A node-centred scheme puts the conserved variables on the 13 nodes, with control volumes bounded by the midpoints between neighbouring nodes — so an interior node's volume is Δx long while the two end nodes get half a volume each. There are 39 unknowns.

这一点差别决定了整页的走向：边界节点就在边界上。格心格式的未知量是单元平均值，物理边界落在两个面上，没有任何未知量住在那里，所以边界条件只能弱加——先用特征关系算出一个外侧状态，再喂给通量。格点格式不同：n1 与 n13 本身就是边界点，它们的状态就是边界状态。于是可以强加——直接把它们那几条方程换掉。第 3 节展开。

That one difference sets the course of this page: a boundary node sits on the boundary. A cell-centred scheme's unknowns are cell averages, the physical boundary falls on two faces, and no unknown lives there — so the conditions can only be imposed weakly, by building an outer state from the characteristic relations and feeding it to the flux. Here n1 and n13 are boundary points and their states are the boundary states. So the conditions can be imposed strongly, by replacing those equations outright. Section 3 does it.

第 2 节
Section 2

要离散的方程与面上的通量

The equations to discretise, and the flux on a face

守恒形式，面积写进守恒量里：

In conservative form, with the area carried inside the conserved variable:

∂(AU)∂t+∂(AF)∂x=S, U=(ρρuρE), F=(ρuρu2+pρu H), S=(0pdAdx0)

配理想气体状态方程 p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2)、H=E+p/ρ，取 γ=1.4、R=1。

closed by the ideal-gas relations p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2) and H=E+p/ρ, with γ=1.4 and R=1.

源项是这一对页面的第一件新东西。S 只进动量方程，而且它是个体积项：它不穿过任何面，是在控制体内部产生的。二维标量那一对页面的残差完全由边循环拼成，这里循环之外还要再走一趟。到了第 8 节会看到，它的转置同样落在节点自己身上，不经过面——而漏掉它，点积测试立刻从 10−16 退化到 10−2。

The source term is the first new thing on these pages. S enters only the momentum equation, and it is a volume term: it crosses no face, it is produced inside the control volume. On the two-dimensional scalar pages the residual was assembled entirely by the edge loop; here one more pass is needed after it. Section 8 shows that its transpose likewise lands on the cell itself and passes through no face — and that dropping it collapses the dot-product test from 10−16 to 10−2.

面上的数值通量

The numerical flux on a face

面上取 Rusanov 通量：中心平均减一个正比于状态跳跃的耗散项，

Each face takes the Rusanov flux: a central average minus a dissipation proportional to the jump in state,

hf=Af[12(FL+FR)−12λf(UR−UL)], λf=12[(|u|+a)L+(|u|+a)R]

为什么 λ 取两侧平均而不是取大者。教科书里的 Rusanov 用 max。max 在两侧相等处不可微，而本页的立身之本是点积测试能打到 10−16——一个不可微的地方就会把它毁掉。取平均则处处光滑，而且在本算例的亚声速流场里耗散量级几乎相同。真实代码里要用 max 时的做法是把它冻结在 u* 处，与二维页冻结远场入流/出流开关完全一样。

Why λ is the average of the two sides rather than the larger. Textbook Rusanov uses max. But max is not differentiable where the two sides cross, and these pages live or die by a dot-product test at the 10−16 level — one non-differentiable point would wreck it. The average is smooth everywhere and, in this subsonic duct, dissipates by almost the same amount. When a real code does use max, the remedy is to freeze it at u*, exactly as the two-dimensional pages freeze the far-field inflow/outflow switch.

本页同样采用一阶空间离散：面上的左右状态直接取两侧单元的当前值，不做重构。代价只在精度上，不在这套代数上。

The discretisation here is likewise first order: the left and right states on a face are the current values of the two adjacent cells, with no reconstruction. The cost is accuracy only, not the algebra this page is about.

守恒守的是通量，不是单元值

What is conserved is the flux, not the cell value

稳态下质量方程没有源项，所以数值质量通量在各条对偶面上必须完全相同——下面实测到机器精度。但如果去看单元中心的 ρuA，它沿程能差百分之几。两者的差就是耗散项 −12λ(UR−UL)Af：一阶格式在 13 个节点上就是这个量级。这不是不守恒，这是「节点值」和「通量」不是同一个东西。

At steady state the mass equation has no source, so the numerical mass flux must be identical on every dual face — measured below, to machine precision. Look instead at the cell-centre value of ρuA and it varies by a few percent along the duct. The difference between the two is the dissipation term −12λ(UR−UL)Af, and a first-order scheme on 13 nodes produces exactly this much of it. That is not a loss of conservation; it is the fact that a nodal value and a flux are different objects.

第 3 节
Section 3

边界条件：强加与部分行替换

Boundary conditions: strong imposition and partial row replacement

特征分析和格心页完全一样：亚声速时左端有 2 条特征进入区域、右端 1 条，所以左端给 2 个条件（总压 p0、总温 T0）、右端给 1 个（背压 pb）。变的是怎么加。

The characteristic analysis is the same as on the cell-centred page: subsonic flow lets 2 characteristics into the domain at the left and 1 at the right, so two conditions are prescribed at the left (p0 and T0) and one at the right (pb). What changes is how they are imposed.

边界节点本身就是未知量，所以直接把它的方程换掉：

The boundary node is itself an unknown, so its equations are simply replaced:

R1,mass←p0(u1)−p0given, R1,mom←T0(u1)−T0given, R13,mass←p(u13)−pb

注意：这是部分行替换。入口节点有 3 条方程，只换掉 2 条；出口节点只换掉 1 条。剩下的那些照常由通量平衡给出。换几条不是随便定的——正好等于进入区域的特征条数。二维那一对页面里的强加是把某个节点的整行换成 ui=uw，那是「一个自由度、一个条件」的特例；这里一个节点携带三条方程，约束只落在其中一部分上。

Note that this is a partial row replacement. The inflow node carries three equations and only two are replaced; the outflow node has only one replaced. The rest come from the flux balance as usual. How many get replaced is not a choice — it is exactly the number of characteristics entering the domain. The strong imposition on the two-dimensional pair replaced a node's whole equation by ui=uw, the special case of "one degree of freedom, one condition"; here a node carries three equations and the constraint lands on a subset.

被替换的行是约束的梯度，不是单位行

The replaced rows are constraint gradients, not unit rows

把 R←p0(u)−p0given 对 u 求导，得到的那一行是 ∂p0/∂u——一个一般的行向量，三个分量都不为零。二维页里对应的行是 eiT（只有一个 1）。所以二维页是本页的特例：当约束恰好是「某个自由度等于常数」时，它的梯度才退化成单位行。

Differentiating R←p0(u)−p0given with respect to u gives the row ∂p0/∂u — a general row vector with all three entries non-zero. The corresponding row on the two-dimensional page was eiT, a single 1. So the two-dimensional page is a special case of this one: only when the constraint happens to be "this degree of freedom equals a constant" does its gradient degenerate into a unit row.

这件事在第 8 节会有直接后果：伴随里那句「出循环后把 wi 加回」要推广成「加回 wi 乘上约束梯度」。

That has a direct consequence in Section 8: the adjoint's "after the loop, add wi back" generalises to "add back wi times the constraint gradient".

还有一处简化：格点格式不需要 ghost 状态。格心页为了给边界面一个外侧状态，必须解一个二次方程、再由等熵关系回到 p,ρ，那整套特征重构在本页完全不存在。边界通量就是 F(u1)A1——用节点自己的状态。强加换来的是边界处理的简化，代价是循环之外多了一步。

And a simplification: a node-centred scheme needs no ghost state. To give its boundary face an outer state, the cell-centred page had to solve a quadratic and return through the isentropic relations; that whole characteristic reconstruction does not exist here. The boundary flux is just F(u1)A1, built from the node's own state. Strong imposition buys a simpler boundary treatment, at the price of one step outside the loop.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 边循环 + 源项 + 行替换

The residual = the edge loop, the source, and the row replacement

残差分三段：边循环走 12 条内部对偶面，两个边界通量直接用端点自己的状态，然后每个节点上走一趟源项。到这里为止和格心页是同一套骨架。

The residual comes in three parts: the edge loop over the 12 interior dual faces, the two boundary fluxes built from the end nodes' own states, and one source pass per node. Up to here the skeleton is the same as the cell-centred page's.

然后是第四段，本页独有：循环结束之后，把 3 条受约束的行替换掉。它必须在循环之后——循环里那些行照常参与累加，替换是把累加的结果扔掉，写上约束残差。

Then a fourth part, unique to this page: after the loop has finished, the 3 constrained rows are replaced. It has to come after — those rows take part in the accumulation like any other, and the replacement discards what accumulated and writes the constraint residual instead.

边界节点的控制体只有一半。n1 的控制体从 x1 到第一个中点，所以它只被一条对偶面和一个边界通量围住。这在残差里自然出现，不需要特殊处理——真正需要特殊处理的只有那几条被替换的行。

A boundary node's control volume is a half one. The volume of n1 runs from x1 to the first midpoint, so it is bounded by one dual face and one boundary flux. That falls out of the residual by itself and needs no special handling — the only thing that does is the replaced rows.

求解：Jacobian 与 Newton法

The solve: the Jacobian and Newton

第 4 节的 residual(u, A) 就是离散残差 R(u)。它的 Jacobian A=∂R/∂u 是 36×36 的，但真正的结构是 12×12 个 3×3 块：一个单元只与它的两个面邻居相连，所以 A 是块三对角的。

The residual(u, A) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u). Its Jacobian A=∂R/∂u is 36×36, but its real structure is 12×12 blocks of 3×3: a cell reaches only its two face neighbours, so A is block tridiagonal.

每个面贡献两个块，来自对一维通量求导：

Each face contributes two blocks, obtained by differentiating the one-dimensional flux:

cL=∂h∂UL =Af[12A(UL)+12λfI −12(UR−UL)∂λf∂UL], A=∂F∂U

A 就是经典的 Euler 通量 Jacobian。第三项常被忘记：耗散系数 λf 自己也依赖两侧状态，漏掉它 Jacobian 就不精确，Newton 会退化成拟 Newton。

A is the classical Euler flux Jacobian. The third term is the one that gets forgotten: the dissipation coefficient λf itself depends on both states, and dropping it makes the Jacobian inexact, degrading Newton to a quasi-Newton method.

边界面把 ∂B/∂u 带了进来。f=0 的外侧状态是 B(u0)，所以那个面对 A 的贡献是 cR+cL∂B/∂u——两个槽位合并成一个块，落在贴边单元自己的对角块上。第 3 节量出的秩 1 / 秩 2 结构，就这样进入了矩阵。

The boundary faces drag ∂B/∂u in. The outer state at f=0 is B(u0), so that face contributes cR+cL∂B/∂u — the two slots merge into one block sitting on the boundary cell's own diagonal block. The rank-1 and rank-2 structure measured in Section 3 enters the matrix right here.

怎么把 R(u)=0 解出来

How R(u)=0 is actually solved

Newton 迭代：在当前状态处线性化，解一个 36×36 的线性系统，更新状态。前几轮加一点欠松弛——边界条件是非线性的，从均匀初值直接跨一大步会把某个单元推到负压。

Newton iteration: linearise at the current state, solve one 36×36 linear system, update. The first few sweeps are under-relaxed — the boundary conditions are nonlinear, and a full step from a uniform guess can drive a cell to negative pressure.

这一步显式地把 A 造了出来。本页其余部分讲的是怎样不形成矩阵地算 Av 与 ATw；primal 求解本身用的是最笨也最稳的办法，因为它唯一的任务是造出随后被冻结的 u*。

This step does form A explicitly. The rest of the page is about computing Av and ATw without a matrix; the primal solve itself uses the dullest, most robust method there is, because its only job is to produce the u* that is then frozen.

目标函数

The objective function

本页准备了两个目标函数，可以随时切换。它们对整条链路的「加载方式」完全不同，放在一起看最能说明伴随的结构与目标无关。

This page carries two objectives, switchable at any time. They load the chain in quite different ways, and seeing them side by side is the clearest demonstration that the adjoint machinery does not care which one you pick.

目标Objective 定义Definition 特点Character
反设计Inverse design L=12Σi(pi−pitgt)2 Vi L=12Σi(pi−pitgt)2 Vi g 铺满全场；最优点已知g is non-zero everywhere; the optimum is known
推力（轴向力）Thrust (axial force) L=Σi pi(Ai+1−Ai) L=Σi pi(Ai+1−Ai) 显式几何依赖很强strongly, explicitly geometric

两者都显式依赖面积：反设计通过单元体积 Vi，推力直接通过 Ai+1−Ai。所以 dL/dA 都有一项根本不经过状态——这正是第 10 节要拆开的结构。

Both depend on the areas explicitly: the inverse-design objective through the cell volume Vi, the thrust directly through Ai+1−Ai. So in both cases dL/dA has a term that never passes through the state at all — the structure Section 10 takes apart.

反设计的目标压力取自一个已知的流道。把 A(x) 乘上 1−0.04sin(πx)，重新收敛，把得到的压力当作 ptgt。于是这个优化问题的最优解是已知的：在那条面积分布上 L 与 dL/dA 都应当为零。第 11 节会把这件事量出来——不是「很小」，是逐比特为零。

The target pressures come from a known duct. Multiply A(x) by 1−0.04sin(πx), re-converge, and take the resulting pressures as ptgt. The optimum of this design problem is therefore known in advance: on that area distribution both L and dL/dA must vanish. Section 11 measures exactly that — not "small", but zero to the last bit.

matrix-free 前向：计算 Av

Matrix-free forward mode: computing Av

设计变量 A∈ℝ13 是各节点的截面积。对 R(u(A),A)=0 用链式法则消去 du/dA 有两条路；两条路都要用到同一个乘积，先把它说清楚：给定一个状态扰动 v，怎样算出 Av 而不形成 A。

The design variables A∈ℝ13 are the nodal areas. Applying the chain rule to R(u(A),A)=0 gives two routes to eliminating du/dA, and both need the same product. So first: given a state perturbation v, how to compute Av without forming A.

答案是把同一条边循环逐句求导。每条对偶面上 hd=cLvL+cRvR，两个 3×3 块各乘一个 3 向量，再照原样 +,− 分发；源项那一趟同样线性化。整个函数从头到尾没有出现过矩阵。

The answer is to differentiate the same edge loop statement by statement. On each dual face hd=cLvL+cRvR — two 3×3 blocks, each times a 3-vector — scattered with the same + and −; the source pass is linearised likewise. No matrix appears anywhere in the function.

Play it statement by statement: residual_d
与第 4 节的 residual 逐行对照：结构完全相同，只是每一步都换成了它的导数
Compare it line by line with the residual of Section 4: the structure is identical, every step replaced by its derivative

边界面上两个槽位合并了。外侧扰动不是独立的，它是 (∂B/∂u)v。所以边界面只剩一个有效的 3×3 块 cR+cL∂B/∂u——和第 5 节矩阵里看到的那个块是同一个。

On a boundary face the two slots merge. The outer perturbation is not independent: it is (∂B/∂u)v. One effective 3×3 block is left, cR+cL∂B/∂u — the very block seen in the matrix of Section 5.

第 8 节
Section 8

matrix-free 伴随：计算 AbcTw

Matrix-free adjoint mode: computing AbcTw

伴随仍然是把前向逐句转置：原来写的地方现在读，原来读的地方现在写。面上的一维通量转置成两个 3×3 块的转置，源项转置回单元自己身上。这些和格心页一样。

The adjoint is still the tangent transposed statement by statement: what wrote now reads, what read now writes. The one-dimensional flux transposes into two transposed 3×3 blocks, and the source transposes back onto the cell itself. All of that matches the cell-centred page.

不一样的是那 3 条受约束的行。行替换在转置之后变成列替换，而实现上是两步，顺序不能反：

What differs is the 3 constrained rows. A row replacement transposes into a column replacement, and in code that is two steps whose order cannot be reversed:

  • 进循环之前，在 w 的一份副本上把受约束的行清零。否则那些行会把本不该存在的贡献分发出去；
  • 循环结束之后，把原来的 wi 乘上该约束的梯度，加回该节点。原地清零就无从加回了，所以必须用副本。
  • Before entering the loop, zero the constrained rows on a copy of w. Otherwise those rows scatter contributions that should not exist;
  • After the loop, add back the original wi times that constraint's gradient, onto that node. Zeroing in place would leave nothing to add back, which is why it must be a copy.

这正是二维格点页那条配方的一般形式。那里被替换的行是单位行 eiT，于是「加回 wi 乘约束梯度」退化成「把 wi 加回 yi」。这里约束是 p0、T0、p，梯度是一般的行向量，所以加回的是一个三分量的向量。两页做的是同一件事，只是这里看得见它的一般形状。

This is the general form of the recipe on the two-dimensional node page. There the replaced row was the unit row eiT, so "add back wi times the constraint gradient" degenerates into "add wi back into yi". Here the constraints are p0, T0 and p, their gradients are general rows, and what gets added back is a three-component vector. The two pages do the same thing; this one shows its general shape.

⟨w,Abcv⟩=⟨AbcTw,v⟩ 对受约束的算子同样成立，而且同样是精确恒等式。把清零挪到循环之后，或者忘记加回，这条测试立刻失效。

⟨w,Abcv⟩=⟨AbcTw,v⟩ holds for the constrained operator too, and is just as exact. Move the zeroing to after the loop, or forget the add-back, and the test fails at once.

第 9 节
Section 9

求解伴随方程

Solving the adjoint equation

有了 ATw，伴随方程 ATΨ=−g 就可以解了，其中 g=(∂L/∂u)T 来自第 6 节选定的目标。Ψ 有 39 个分量——每个节点三个，对应质量、动量、能量三条方程。

With ATw in hand the adjoint equation ATΨ=−g can be solved, where g=(∂L/∂u)T comes from whichever objective Section 6 selected. Ψ has 39 components — three per node, one for each of the mass, momentum and energy equations.

−Ψi 的含义是：在第 i 个节点的那条方程里加一个单位源项，目标 L 会变多少。所以伴随场读起来是一张「敏感度地图」。

The meaning of −Ψi is: how much the objective L would move if a unit source were added to that equation in that cell. The adjoint field therefore reads as a map of sensitivity.

亚声速带来一件二维标量页看不到的事：信息双向传播。那两个页面上的模型方程是纯对流的，特征只朝一个方向走，伴随因此只向上游铺开。这里三条特征是 u−a<0<u<u+a：一条朝上游、两条朝下游。于是扰动会同时向两边传，伴随也就铺满整个流道——出口的背压能影响到入口附近的解，入口的总条件也能影响到出口。

Subsonic flow brings something the two-dimensional scalar pages cannot show: information travels both ways. Their model equation is pure advection, its characteristics all run one way, and the adjoint therefore spreads only upstream. Here the three characteristics are u−a<0<u<u+a: one upstream, two downstream. Disturbances propagate in both directions, and the adjoint fills the whole duct — the back pressure reaches back towards the inlet, and the inlet's total conditions reach forward to the exit.

几何导数：dR/dA 与 dL/dA

Geometric derivatives: dR/dA and dL/dA

和格心页一样，

As on the cell-centred page,

dLdA=∂L∂A +ΨT∂R∂A,AbcTΨ=−g

残差对面积的依赖仍然很简单：对偶面的通量对 Ae=12(Ai+Aj) 是线性的，边界通量对端点面积是线性的，源项对两侧面积也是线性的。两个方向都解析，几何点积测试因此也是精确的。

The residual's dependence on the areas is still simple: a dual face's flux is linear in Ae=12(Ai+Aj), a boundary flux is linear in its end area, and the source is linear in the two areas bounding the volume. Both directions are analytic, so the geometric dot-product test is exact here too.

被替换的行里没有面积——所以 Ψ 必须先清零

The replaced rows contain no areas — so Ψ must be zeroed first

这是本页最容易错的一步，而且理由很干脆：被替换的那三行是 p0−p0given、T0−T0given、p−pb，它们根本不含 A。所以 ∂R/∂A 在这三行上恒为零。

This is the step easiest to get wrong on this page, and the reason is blunt: the three replaced rows are p0−p0given, T0−T0given and p−pb, and none of them contains A. Their rows of ∂R/∂A are therefore identically zero.

而 Ψ 在这三行上并不为零（上一节的面板可以直接读到）。如果照常把整个 Ψ 喂进几何循环，那三个分量就会去乘一堆本该为零的行，凭空造出贡献。所以组装几何梯度之前，必须先把 Ψ 在受约束行上清零——和第 8 节伴随循环里那一步是同一件事，只是这次作用在几何方向上。

And Ψ on those rows is not zero — the panel in the previous section reads it out. Feed the whole Ψ into the geometric loop and those three components multiply rows that ought to be zero, manufacturing contributions out of nothing. So before assembling the geometric gradient, Ψ must be zeroed on the constrained rows — the same act as in the adjoint loop of Section 8, applied this time in the geometric direction.

忘记这一步，梯度不是「略有偏差」。下面的面板把正确的梯度和忘记清零的梯度画在一起，并给出相对误差——它是百分之百量级的，不是百分之一。这也是为什么这条链路上每一处都要用点积测试或全链路差分卡一遍：一个漏掉的清零不会让结果看起来可疑，它会让结果看起来完全正常但完全错误。

Forget it and the gradient is not "slightly off". The panel below draws the correct gradient against the one computed without the zeroing, and reports the relative error — it is of order one hundred percent, not one percent. Which is why every link in this chain gets pinned by a dot-product test or a full-chain difference: a missing zeroing does not make the answer look suspicious, it makes it look perfectly normal and completely wrong.

第 11 节
Section 11

验证：dL/dA 对有限差分

Verification: dL/dA against finite differences

最严格的检验是拿 dL/dA 去对全链路中心差分：每扰动一个 Ak，都重新收敛一次 Newton，再算目标。这样比的是全导数——状态对几何的响应也算在内——而不是冻结状态的偏导数。

The strict check is dL/dA against full-chain central differences: perturb one Ak, re-converge Newton, recompute the objective. What is compared is then the total derivative, with the state's response to the geometry included, rather than a partial derivative at frozen state.

还有一个更强的检验：已知的最优点。第 6 节里目标压力取自一条已知的流道。在那条流道上，L 应当为零，梯度也应当为零——而且不是「很小」，是逐比特为零，因为同一个确定性的 Newton 会复现出同一个状态。上表最后两行就是它。一个梯度能在一个独立构造的最优点上归零，比任何差分对照都更有说服力。

And a stronger check still: a known optimum. In Section 6 the target pressures were taken from a known duct. On that duct both L and the gradient must vanish — and not merely be small, but be zero to the last bit, because the same deterministic Newton reproduces the same state. Those are the last two rows above. A gradient that vanishes at an independently constructed optimum is better evidence than any finite-difference comparison.

差分校验本身是会骗人的。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ε/h)，随 h 变小反而增大。h 取得太大或太小，一个正确的梯度都会看起来像是错的。这正是点积测试更可靠的原因——它是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。上面几何方向那条测试就是明证：它落在 10−16，而同一个算子拿差分去比只能到 10−9。

Finite-difference checking is itself capable of lying. A central difference has two error contributions: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks with h; and round-off, about O(ε/h), which grows as h shrinks. Too large or too small a step makes a correct gradient look wrong. That is exactly why the dot-product test is the more reliable instrument — it is an exact identity with no step size in it. The geometric test above proves the point: it lands at 10−16, while comparing the same operator against differences can only reach 10−9.

第 12 节
Section 12

总结

Summary

  • 边循环是核心：残差就是「每条对偶面收集两侧状态、算一次一维通量、按 +,− 分发」，加上两个边界通量、一趟源项。
  • 边界节点就在边界上，所以边界条件可以强加：不需要 ghost 状态，直接换掉方程。
  • 换的是部分行：入口 3 行换 2 行、出口换 1 行，条数等于进入区域的特征条数。
  • 被替换的行是约束的梯度，一般不是单位行；二维格点页是它退化成 eiT 的特例。
  • 伴随里对应两步：进循环前在副本上清零，出循环后加回 wi·(∂c/∂u)，顺序不能反。
  • 几何方向还要再清零一次：被替换的行不含面积，忘了清零梯度会错 100% 量级。
  • The edge loop is the whole story: the residual is "gather both states on each dual face, call the flux once, scatter with + and −", plus two boundary fluxes and one source pass.
  • A boundary node sits on the boundary, so the conditions can be imposed strongly: no ghost state, just replace the equations.
  • The replacement is partial: 2 of 3 rows at the inflow, 1 of 3 at the outflow — as many as there are characteristics entering.
  • The replaced rows are constraint gradients, general rows rather than unit rows; the two-dimensional node page is the case where they degenerate to eiT.
  • The adjoint answers with two steps: zero on a copy before the loop, add back wi·(∂c/∂u) after it, and the order cannot be swapped.
  • The geometric direction needs the zeroing too: the replaced rows contain no areas, and forgetting it puts the gradient out by order 100%.
