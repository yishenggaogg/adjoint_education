变截面流道拟一维 Euler 方程的格点格式离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of the quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations in a variable-area duct, node-centred

算例是一段收缩—扩张流道：截面积 A(x) 从 1 收到喉部的 0.7 再张回 1，左端给总压 p0 与总温 T0，右端给背压 pb，全场亚声速。未知量是 13 个节点上的守恒量 (ρ,ρu,ρE)，一共 39 个。与二维标量那一对页面相比，这里多出三样东西：局部导数从一个数变成 3×3 块、残差里多了一个不经过任何面的源项 pdA/dx、边界条件是对边界节点自身状态的非线性约束。设计变量就是 13 个节点截面积，因此 dL/dA 有直接的物理含义：把流道这里放宽一点，目标会怎么变。The test case is a converging–diverging duct: the area A(x) falls from 1 to 0.7 at the throat and opens back to 1, with total pressure p0 and total temperature T0 prescribed at the left, a back pressure pb at the right, and subsonic flow throughout. The unknowns are the conserved variables (ρ,ρu,ρE) on 13 nodes, 39 in all. Against the two-dimensional scalar pair of pages, three things are new here: the local derivatives become 3×3 blocks, the residual gains a source term pdA/dx that passes through no face at all, and the boundary conditions are nonlinear constraints on the boundary nodes' own states. The design variables are the 13 nodal areas, so dL/dA means something you can point at: widen the duct here, and the objective moves by this much.

第 1 节
Section 1

流道与网格

The duct and the grid

拟一维的意思是：流道沿 x 的截面积 A(x) 在变，但每个截面上的流动当成均匀的。三维的管内流动于是塌缩成一条线上的三个守恒量，而几何完全由 A(x) 承担。本页与格心格式那一页用的是同一段流道、同一张网格：x∈[0,1]，A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx)，两端为 1、喉部 0.7。

Quasi-one-dimensional means the duct's area A(x) varies along x while the flow across each section is treated as uniform. A three-dimensional duct flow collapses onto a line carrying three conserved quantities, with the geometry carried entirely by A(x). This page uses the same duct and the same grid as the cell-centred page: x∈[0,1] with A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx), unity at both ends and 0.7 at the throat.

不同的是未知量放在哪里。格点格式把守恒量放在 13 个节点上，控制体是相邻节点之间的中点围出来的——所以内部节点的控制体长 Δx，而两端的节点只有半个控制体。未知量共 39 个（）。

What differs is where the unknowns live. A node-centred scheme puts the conserved variables on the 13 nodes, with control volumes bounded by the midpoints between neighbouring nodes — so an interior node's volume is Δx long while the two end nodes get half a volume each. There are 39 unknowns ().

这一点差别决定了整页的走向：边界节点就在边界上。格心格式的未知量是单元平均值，物理边界落在两个面上，没有任何未知量住在那里，所以边界条件只能弱加——先用特征关系算出一个外侧状态，再喂给通量。格点格式不同：n1 与 n13 本身就是边界点，它们的状态就是边界状态。于是可以强加——直接把它们那几条方程换掉。第 3 节展开。

That one difference sets the course of this page: a boundary node sits on the boundary. A cell-centred scheme's unknowns are cell averages, the physical boundary falls on two faces, and no unknown lives there — so the conditions can only be imposed weakly, by building an outer state from the characteristic relations and feeding it to the flux. Here n1 and n13 are boundary points and their states are the boundary states. So the conditions can be imposed strongly, by replacing those equations outright. Section 3 does it.

第 2 节
Section 2

要离散的方程与面上的通量

The equations to discretise, and the flux on a face

守恒形式，面积写进守恒量里：

In conservative form, with the area carried inside the conserved variable:

∂(AU)∂t+∂(AF)∂x=S, U=​ρρuρE, F=​ρuρu2+pρuH, S=​0pdAdx0

配理想气体状态方程 p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2)、H=E+p/ρ，取 γ=1.4、R=1。

closed by the ideal-gas relations p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2) and H=E+p/ρ, with γ=1.4 and R=1.

源项是这一对页面的第一件新东西。S 只进动量方程，而且它是个体积项：它不穿过任何面，是在控制体内部产生的。二维标量那一对页面（弱加边界时）的残差完全由循环拼成，这里循环之外还要再走一趟。它的转置同样落在节点自己身上、不经过面；漏掉它，点积测试就不再成立——实测相对误差从 10−16 量级升到 10−5 到 10−2，随所取的向量而定。

The source term is the first new thing on these pages. S enters only the momentum equation, and it is a volume term: it crosses no face, it is produced inside the control volume. On the two-dimensional scalar pages (with weak boundary conditions) the residual was assembled entirely by the loop; here one more pass is needed after it. Its transpose likewise lands on the node itself and passes through no face; drop it and the dot-product test fails — measured, the relative error rises from order 10−16 to between 10−5 and 10−2, depending on the vectors drawn.

面上的数值通量

The numerical flux on a face

面上取 Rusanov 通量：中心平均减一个正比于状态跳跃的耗散项，

Each face takes the Rusanov flux: a central average minus a dissipation proportional to the jump in state,

hf=Af[12(FL+FR)−12λf(UR−UL)], λf=12[(|u|+a)L+(|u|+a)R]

为什么 λ 取两侧平均而不是取大者。教科书里的 Rusanov 用 max。max 在两侧相等处不可微：哪一侧更大随状态切换，切换处导数跳变——那一点没有唯一的梯度，差分步长一旦跨过它，有限差分就与线性化对不上。取平均则只要 u≠0 就处处光滑（本算例 u 处处为正），而且在本算例的亚声速流场里耗散量级几乎相同：实测 max 最多只比平均值大 1.4%。真实代码里要用 max 时的做法是把它冻结在 u* 处，与二维页冻结远场入流/出流开关完全一样。

Why λ is the average of the two sides rather than the larger. Textbook Rusanov uses max. But max is not differentiable where the two sides cross: which side is larger switches with the state, and the derivative jumps there — that point has no single gradient, and a finite difference whose step straddles it disagrees with the linearisation. The average is smooth wherever u≠0 (and u>0 throughout this duct) and, in this subsonic flow, dissipates by almost the same amount: measured, the max is at most 1.4% larger than the average. When a real code does use max, the remedy is to freeze it at u*, exactly as the two-dimensional pages freeze the far-field inflow/outflow switch.

本页同样采用一阶空间离散：对偶面上的左右状态直接取两侧节点的当前值，不做重构。代价只在精度上，不在这套代数上。

The discretisation here is likewise first order: the left and right states on a dual face are the current values of the two adjacent nodes, with no reconstruction. The cost is accuracy only, not the algebra this page is about.

在里点流道中的任意一条对偶面，看收敛解在它两侧的状态怎样组成通量：中心项、耗散项各是多少，λf 取平均与取 max 差多少。也可以拖动滑块，自己设定两侧状态。

守恒守的是通量，不是节点值

What is conserved is the flux, not the nodal value

稳态下质量方程没有源项，所以在内部节点上，数值质量通量在各条内部对偶面上必须完全相同——下面实测到机器精度。节点上的 ρuA 则沿程变化约 15%：两者的差是耗散项 −12λ(UR−UL)Af，节点值和通量本来就不是同一个东西。两端却不一样。两个边界节点的质量方程（入口还有动量方程）被边界条件替换掉了，它们的通量平衡并没有被满足：出口节点上的 ρuA 比内部通量高出约 11%，被丢掉的质量平衡是 3.6×10−2。这是强加边界、整行替换的代价，下面的面板把它量了出来。

At steady state the mass equation has no source, so at the interior nodes the numerical mass flux must be identical on every interior dual face — measured below, to machine precision. The nodal value of ρuA, meanwhile, varies by about 15% along the duct: the difference is the dissipation term −12λ(UR−UL)Af, and a nodal value and a flux are simply different objects. The two ends are another matter. The mass equations of the two boundary nodes (and the momentum equation at the inlet) have been replaced by the boundary conditions, so their flux balances are not satisfied: ρuA at the outlet node is about 11% above the interior flux, and the discarded mass balance is 3.6×10−2. That is the price of imposing the boundary conditions strongly, by replacing whole rows, and the panel below measures it.

在里逐条对偶面切换，能看到同一件事的另一面：质量和能量的 h 在全部 12 条对偶面上相同到舍入误差（能量方程同样没有源项），中心项和耗散项却各自在变；动量的 h 逐条对偶面不同，相邻两条对偶面之间的差额正好由源项 pΔA 补上。

Stepping from dual face to dual face in shows the other side of the same fact: the mass and energy components of h agree on all 12 dual faces to round-off (the energy equation has no source either), while the central and dissipation terms each change; the momentum component does change from one dual face to the next, and between two neighbouring dual faces the source pΔA makes up exactly the difference.

第 3 节
Section 3

边界条件：强加与部分行替换

Boundary conditions: strong imposition and partial row replacement

特征分析和格心页完全一样（）：亚声速时左端有 2 条特征进入区域、右端 1 条，所以左端给 2 个条件（总压 p0、总温 T0）、右端给 1 个（背压 pb）。变的是怎么加。

The characteristic analysis is the same as on the cell-centred page (): subsonic flow lets 2 characteristics into the domain at the left and 1 at the right, so two conditions are prescribed at the left (p0 and T0) and one at the right (pb). What changes is how they are imposed.

边界节点本身就是未知量，所以直接把它的方程换掉：

The boundary node is itself an unknown, so its equations are simply replaced:

R1,mass←p0(u1)−p0given, R1,mom←T0(u1)−T0given, R13,mass←p(u13)−pb

注意：这是部分行替换。入口节点有 3 条方程，只换掉 2 条；出口节点只换掉 1 条。剩下的那些照常由通量平衡给出。换几条不是随便定的——正好等于进入区域的特征条数。二维那一对页面里的强加是把某个节点的整行换成 ui=uw，那是「一个自由度、一个条件」的特例；这里一个节点携带三条方程，约束只落在其中一部分上。

Note that this is a partial row replacement. The inflow node carries three equations and only two are replaced; the outflow node has only one replaced. The rest come from the flux balance as usual. How many get replaced is not a choice — it is exactly the number of characteristics entering the domain. The strong imposition on the two-dimensional pair replaced a node's whole equation by ui=uw, the special case of "one degree of freedom, one condition"; here a node carries three equations and the constraint lands on a subset.

被替换的行是约束的梯度，不是单位行

The replaced rows are constraint gradients, not unit rows

把 R←p0(u)−p0given 对 u 求导，得到的那一行是 ∂p0/∂u——一个一般的行向量，三个分量都不为零（）。二维页里对应的行是 eiT（只有一个 1）。所以二维页是本页的特例：当约束恰好是「某个自由度等于常数」时，它的梯度才退化成单位行。

Differentiating R←p0(u)−p0given with respect to u gives the row ∂p0/∂u — a general row vector with all three entries non-zero (). The corresponding row on the two-dimensional page was eiT, a single 1. So the two-dimensional page is a special case of this one: only when the constraint happens to be "this degree of freedom equals a constant" does its gradient degenerate into a unit row.

这件事在第 8 节会有直接后果：伴随里那句「出循环后把 wi 加回」要推广成「加回 wi 乘上约束梯度」。

That has a direct consequence in Section 8: the adjoint's "after the loop, add wi back" generalises to "add back wi times the constraint gradient".

还有一处简化：格点格式不需要 ghost 状态。格心页为了给边界面一个外侧状态，必须解一个二次方程、再由等熵关系回到 p,ρ，那整套特征重构在本页完全不存在。边界通量就是 F(u1)A1——用节点自己的状态。强加换来的是边界处理的简化，代价是循环之外多了一步。

And a simplification: a node-centred scheme needs no ghost state. To give its boundary face an outer state, the cell-centred page had to solve a quadratic and return through the isentropic relations; that whole characteristic reconstruction does not exist here. The boundary flux is just F(u1)A1, built from the node's own state. Strong imposition buys a simpler boundary treatment, at the price of one step outside the loop.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 边循环 + 源项 + 行替换

The residual = the edge loop, the source, and the row replacement

残差分三段：边循环走 12 条内部对偶面，两个边界通量直接用端点自己的状态，然后每个节点上走一趟源项。到这里为止和格心页是同一套骨架。

The residual comes in three parts: the edge loop over the 12 interior dual faces, the two boundary fluxes built from the end nodes' own states, and one source pass per node. Up to here the skeleton is the same as the cell-centred page's.

然后是第四段，本页独有：循环结束之后，把 3 条受约束的行替换掉。它必须在循环之后——循环里那些行照常参与累加，替换是把累加的结果扔掉，写上约束残差（逐句播放全部四段）。

Then a fourth part, unique to this page: after the loop has finished, the 3 constrained rows are replaced. It has to come after — those rows take part in the accumulation like any other, and the replacement discards what accumulated and writes the constraint residual instead ( plays all four parts, statement by statement).

边界节点的控制体只有一半。n1 的控制体从 x1 到第一个中点，所以它只被一条对偶面和一个边界通量围住。这在残差里自然出现，不需要特殊处理——真正需要特殊处理的只有那几条被替换的行。

A boundary node's control volume is a half one. The volume of n1 runs from x1 to the first midpoint, so it is bounded by one dual face and one boundary flux. That falls out of the residual by itself and needs no special handling — the only thing that does is the replaced rows.

Section 5

求解：Jacobian 与 Newton法

The solve: the Jacobian and Newton

第 4 节的 residual(u, A) 就是离散残差 R(u)。它的 Jacobian A=∂R/∂u 是 39×39 的，但真正的结构是 13×13 个 3×3 块：一个节点只经由两侧的对偶面与它的两个邻居节点相连，所以 A 是块三对角的。被替换的 3 行更简单：每行只有本节点那个块里的 3 个元，是约束梯度。

The residual(u, A) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u). Its Jacobian A=∂R/∂u is 39×39, but its real structure is 13×13 blocks of 3×3: a node reaches only its two neighbouring nodes, through the dual faces on either side, so A is block tridiagonal. The 3 replaced rows are simpler still: each holds just the 3 entries in its own node’s block, the constraint gradient.

每个面贡献两个块，来自对一维通量求导：

Each face contributes two blocks, obtained by differentiating the one-dimensional flux:

cL=∂h∂UL =Af[12F'(UL)+12λfI −12(UR−UL)∂λf∂UL], F'=∂F∂U

F' 就是经典的 Euler 通量 Jacobian（记作 F'，免得与面积向量 A 混淆）。第三项常被忘记：耗散系数 λf 自己也依赖两侧状态，漏掉它 Jacobian 就不精确，Newton 会退化成拟 Newton。

F' is the classical Euler flux Jacobian (written F' so as not to be confused with the vector of areas A). The third term is the one that gets forgotten: the dissipation coefficient λf itself depends on both states, and dropping it makes the Jacobian inexact, degrading Newton to a quasi-Newton method.

这里没有 ghost 状态，也就没有 ∂B/∂u。边界节点本身就在边界上：入口、出口的通量直接用节点自己的状态算，它们的线性化 −A1F'(u1) 与 +A13F'(u13) 落在两个端节点自己的对角块上。然后第 3 节的部分行替换覆盖掉其中 3 行：入口的质量、动量行换成 ∂p0/∂u 与 ∂T0/∂u，出口的质量行换成 ∂p/∂u——都是只落在本节点块里的一般行向量（）。

There is no ghost state here, and so no ∂B/∂u. A boundary node sits on the boundary itself: the inlet and outlet fluxes are computed from the node’s own state, and their linearisations, −A1F'(u1) and +A13F'(u13), land on the two end nodes’ own diagonal blocks. The partial row replacement of Section 3 then overwrites 3 of those rows: the inlet’s mass and momentum rows become ∂p0/∂u and ∂T0/∂u, the outlet’s mass row becomes ∂p/∂u — general row vectors, each confined to its own node’s block ().

怎么把 R(u)=0 解出来

How R(u)=0 is actually solved

Newton 迭代：在当前状态处线性化，解一个 39×39 的线性系统，更新状态。代码在前 6 轮用了欠松弛 ω=0.5。这个算例其实不需要它：实测从同一个均匀初值直接走全步长，4 次更新就收敛，途中最低压力 0.850，离负压很远。保留它有两个原因：下面的演示（）正好能把 Newton 的两种收敛并排看清——欠松弛时是线性的，残差每轮约乘 1−ω；全步长时是二次的；另外，页面上所有实测常数都是沿这条迭代路径得到的，u* 的末位比特随路径而变。

Newton iteration: linearise at the current state, solve one 39×39 linear system, update. The code damps the first 6 sweeps with ω=0.5. This case does not actually need it: measured, full steps from the same uniform guess converge after 4 updates, the lowest pressure along the way being 0.850, nowhere near negative. It is kept for two reasons. The demo below () then shows both kinds of Newton convergence side by side — linear while damped, the residual shrinking by about 1−ω per sweep, and quadratic with full steps. And every constant measured on this page was produced along this iteration path; the last bits of u* depend on the path.

这一步显式地把 A 造了出来。本页其余部分讲的是怎样不形成矩阵地算 Av 与 ATw；primal 求解本身用的是最笨也最稳的办法，因为它唯一的任务是造出随后被冻结的 u*。

This step does form A explicitly. The rest of the page is about computing Av and ATw without a matrix; the primal solve itself uses the dullest, most robust method there is, because its only job is to produce the u* that is then frozen.

第 6 节
Section 6

目标函数

The objective function

matrix-free 前向：计算 Av

Matrix-free forward mode: computing Av

设计变量 A∈ℝ13 是各节点的截面积。对 R(u(A),A)=0 用链式法则消去 du/dA 有两条路；两条路都要用到同一个乘积，先把它说清楚：给定一个状态扰动 v，怎样算出 Av 而不形成 A。

The design variables A∈ℝ13 are the nodal areas. Applying the chain rule to R(u(A),A)=0 gives two routes to eliminating du/dA, and both need the same product. So first: given a state perturbation v, how to compute Av without forming A.

答案是把同一条边循环逐句求导。每条对偶面上 hd=cLvL+cRvR，两个 3×3 块各乘一个 3 向量，再照原样 +,− 分发；源项那一趟同样线性化。整个函数从头到尾没有出现过矩阵（）。

The answer is to differentiate the same edge loop statement by statement. On each dual face hd=cLvL+cRvR — two 3×3 blocks, each times a 3-vector — scattered with the same + and −; the source pass is linearised likewise. No matrix appears anywhere in the function ().

边界上没有「两个槽位合并」。格心页的边界面外侧是 ghost 状态 B(u)，扰动要经过 ∂B/∂u；这里边界通量直接用端节点自己的状态，它的线性化就是一个块 A1F'(u1)（出口是 A13F'(u13)），只作用在该节点上。受约束的行则在循环结束之后整行覆盖。

There are no merged slots at the boundary here. On the cell-centred page a boundary face has a ghost state B(u) outside it, and the perturbation has to pass through ∂B/∂u; here the boundary flux uses the end node’s own state, so its linearisation is a single block, A1F'(u1) (at the outlet A13F'(u13)), acting on that node alone. The constrained rows are overwritten whole, after the loop has finished.

matrix-free 伴随：计算 AbcTw

Matrix-free adjoint mode: computing AbcTw

伴随仍然是把前向逐句转置：原来写的地方现在读，原来读的地方现在写。面上的一维通量转置成两个 3×3 块的转置，源项转置回节点自己身上。这些和格心页一样（逐句播放）。

The adjoint is still the tangent transposed statement by statement: what wrote now reads, what read now writes. The one-dimensional flux transposes into two transposed 3×3 blocks, and the source transposes back onto the node itself. All of that matches the cell-centred page ( plays it statement by statement).

不一样的是那 3 条受约束的行。行替换在转置之后变成列替换，而实现上是两步，顺序不能反：

What differs is the 3 constrained rows. A row replacement transposes into a column replacement, and in code that is two steps whose order cannot be reversed:

  • 进循环之前，在 w 的一份副本上把受约束的行清零。否则那些行会把本不该存在的贡献分发出去；
  • 循环结束之后，把原来的 wi 乘上该约束的梯度，加回该节点。原地清零就无从加回了，所以必须用副本。
  • Before entering the loop, zero the constrained rows on a copy of w. Otherwise those rows scatter contributions that should not exist;
  • After the loop, add back the original wi times that constraint's gradient, onto that node. Zeroing in place would leave nothing to add back, which is why it must be a copy.

这正是二维格点页那条配方的一般形式。那里被替换的行是单位行 eiT，于是「加回 wi 乘约束梯度」退化成「把 wi 加回 yi」。这里约束是 p0、T0、p，梯度是一般的行向量，所以加回的是一个三分量的向量。两页做的是同一件事，只是这里看得见它的一般形状。

This is the general form of the recipe on the two-dimensional node page. There the replaced row was the unit row eiT, so "add back wi times the constraint gradient" degenerates into "add wi back into yi". Here the constraints are p0, T0 and p, their gradients are general rows, and what gets added back is a three-component vector. The two pages do the same thing; this one shows its general shape.

求解伴随方程

Solving the adjoint equation

有了 ATw，伴随方程 ATΨ=−g 就可以解了，其中 g=(∂L/∂u)T 来自第 6 节的目标（本页各面板的目标选择是联动的）。Ψ 有 39 个分量——每个节点三个，对应质量、动量、能量三条方程。

With ATw in hand the adjoint equation ATΨ=−g can be solved, where g=(∂L/∂u)T comes from the objective of Section 6 (the objective selectors on this page are linked). Ψ has 39 components — three per node, one for each of the mass, momentum and energy equations.

−Ψi 的含义是：在第 i 个节点的那条方程里加一个单位源项，目标 L 会变多少。所以伴随场读起来是一张「敏感度地图」（）。

The meaning of −Ψi is: how much the objective L would move if a unit source were added to that equation at that node. The adjoint field therefore reads as a map of sensitivity ().

亚声速带来一件二维标量页看不到的事：信息双向传播。那两个页面上的模型方程是纯对流的，特征只朝一个方向走，伴随因此只向上游铺开。这里三条特征是 u−a<0<u<u+a：一条朝上游、两条朝下游。于是扰动会同时向两边传，伴随也就铺满整个流道——出口的背压能影响到入口附近的解，入口的总条件也能影响到出口。

Subsonic flow brings something the two-dimensional scalar pages cannot show: information travels both ways. Their model equation is pure advection, its characteristics all run one way, and the adjoint therefore spreads only upstream. Here the three characteristics are u−a<0<u<u+a: one upstream, two downstream. Disturbances propagate in both directions, and the adjoint fills the whole duct — the back pressure reaches back towards the inlet, and the inlet's total conditions reach forward to the exit.

块 Jacobi 迭代

The block-Jacobi iteration

最简单的 matrix-free 解法是 Jacobi 迭代。与二维标量页不同，这里每个节点有 3 个未知量，对角元于是成了 3×3 的块：

The simplest matrix-free solver is the Jacobi iteration. Unlike the two-dimensional scalar pages, every node here carries 3 unknowns, so the diagonal entries become 3×3 blocks:

Ψi←Ψi−Di−T(ATΨ+g)i,Di=∂Ri∂ui(3×3)

与矩阵有关的操作仍然只有一次 residual_b 调用（第 8 节的对偶面循环）。D 的 13 个块这里取自第 5 节的 Jacobian，实际代码在同一个面循环里顺手把它们累加出来；入口、出口节点的块里，被替换的行就是约束梯度。每次迭代 = 一次 residual_b + 13 个独立的 3×3 小系统。有两层播放：上面一行按面播放本次迭代内的 residual_b，最后一步是块 Jacobi 更新；下面一行按迭代播放。

The only matrix operation is still one residual_b call (the dual-face loop of Section 8). The 13 blocks of D are taken from the Jacobian of Section 5 here; a production code collects them in the same face loop; in the inlet and outlet nodes' blocks the replaced rows are the constraint gradients. One iteration is one residual_b call plus 13 independent 3×3 solves. has two layers of playback: the upper row plays the face loop of residual_b within the current iteration, ending with the block-Jacobi update; the lower row plays iteration by iteration.

为什么要近一千次？二维标量页的 Jacobi 每次迭代把残差缩到约 0.79，122 次就收敛；这里每次只缩到约 0.977。块 Jacobi 每次迭代只在相邻节点之间交换一次信息。把 D−1 乘上与上下游邻居相连的块，按特征方向看（实测，第 7 个节点）：Rusanov 对三条波只用一个耗散 λ=|u|+a，它恰好等于最快那条 u+a 的波速，所以这条波是纯迎风的，只连上游（强度 0.99，下游 0.00）；另外两条两头都连着：u 上游 0.64、下游 0.35，u−a 上游 0.29、下游 0.70。这正是本节开头说的双向传播：误差要在进出口之间来回走很多趟才消得掉。最慢的误差在相邻节点之间一大一小地交替，所以相邻两次迭代的残差比在 0.969 与 0.984 之间来回跳（的状态栏里能看到）——这是块三对角矩阵上 Jacobi 的典型表现。真实代码保留同样的两样东西，residual_b 与块对角 D，但把它们放进 Krylov 方法（例如 GMRES）里去加速。

Why nearly a thousand iterations? On the two-dimensional scalar pages each Jacobi iteration shrinks the residual to about 0.79 and 122 iterations suffice; here each one shrinks it only to about 0.977. Block Jacobi exchanges information between neighbouring nodes once per iteration. Multiply D−1 into the blocks that tie a node to its upstream and downstream neighbours and look along the characteristic directions (measured, node 7): Rusanov gives all three waves one dissipation, λ=|u|+a, which is exactly the speed of the fastest wave u+a, so that wave is purely upwinded and tied only upstream (strength 0.99, downstream 0.00); the other two are tied both ways: u with 0.64 upstream and 0.35 downstream, u−a with 0.29 upstream and 0.70 downstream. This is the two-way propagation described at the start of this section: an error has to travel back and forth between inlet and outlet many times before it dies out. The slowest error alternates large and small from one node to the next, so the ratio between successive residuals alternates between 0.969 and 0.984 (visible in the status line of ) — typical of Jacobi on a block-tridiagonal matrix. Production codes keep the same two ingredients, residual_b and the block diagonal D, and accelerate them inside a Krylov method such as GMRES.

Geometric derivatives: dR/dA and dL/dA

和格心页一样，

As on the cell-centred page,

dLdA=∂L∂A +ΨT∂R∂A,AbcTΨ=−g

残差对面积的依赖仍然很简单：对偶面的通量对 Ae=12(Ai+Aj) 是线性的，边界通量对端点面积是线性的，源项对两侧面积也是线性的。两个方向都解析，几何点积测试因此也是精确的。

The residual's dependence on the areas is still simple: a dual face's flux is linear in Ae=12(Ai+Aj), a boundary flux is linear in its end area, and the source is linear in the two areas bounding the volume. Both directions are analytic, so the geometric dot-product test is exact here too.

被替换的行里没有面积——所以 Ψ 必须先清零

The replaced rows contain no areas — so Ψ must be zeroed first

这是本页最容易错的一步，而且理由很干脆：被替换的那三行是 p0−p0given、T0−T0given、p−pb，它们根本不含 A。所以 ∂R/∂A 在这三行上恒为零。

This is the step easiest to get wrong on this page, and the reason is blunt: the three replaced rows are p0−p0given, T0−T0given and p−pb, and none of them contains A. Their rows of ∂R/∂A are therefore identically zero.

而 Ψ 在入口 p0 行和出口 pb 行上并不为零（可以直接读到）。如果照常把整个 Ψ 喂进几何循环，这些分量就会去乘一堆本该为零的行，凭空造出贡献。所以组装几何梯度之前，必须先把 Ψ 在受约束行上清零——和第 8 节伴随循环里那一步是同一件事，只是这次作用在几何方向上。T0 行上的 Ψ 恰好为零：p0、pb 给定时，改变 T0 只会把 ρ、u 整体缩放而压力不变（实测把 T0 改成 1.21，压力差 3×10−16），两个目标都只看压力，所以感觉不到 T0。

And Ψ on the inlet p0 row and the outlet pb row is not zero — reads it out. Feed the whole of Ψ into the geometric loop as usual and those components multiply rows that should be zero, conjuring contributions out of nothing. So before the geometric gradient is assembled, Ψ has to be zeroed on the constrained rows — the same step as in the adjoint loop of Section 8, this time in the geometric direction. On the T0 row Ψ is exactly zero: with p0 and pb fixed, changing T0 only rescales ρ and u and leaves the pressure alone (measured: setting T0=1.21 changes the pressure by 3×10−16), and both objectives look only at pressure, so neither can feel T0.

忘记这一步，梯度不是「略有偏差」。下面的面板（）把正确的梯度和忘记清零的梯度画在一起，并给出相对误差：反设计是 108%，推力是 4%——哪一个都不是「略有偏差」。这也是为什么这条链路上每一处都要用点积测试或全链路差分卡一遍：一个漏掉的清零不会让结果看起来可疑，它会让结果看起来完全正常但完全错误。

Forget it and the gradient is not "slightly off". The panel below () draws the correct gradient against the one computed without the zeroing, and reports the relative error: 108% for inverse design, 4% for thrust — neither is “slightly off”. Which is why every link in this chain gets pinned by a dot-product test or a full-chain difference: a missing zeroing does not make the answer look suspicious, it makes it look perfectly normal and completely wrong.

验证：dL/dA 对有限差分

Verification: dL/dA against finite differences

最严格的检验是拿 dL/dA 去对全链路中心差分：每扰动一个 Ak，都重新收敛一次 Newton，再算目标。这样比的是全导数——状态对几何的响应也算在内——而不是冻结状态的偏导数。

The strict check is dL/dA against full-chain central differences: perturb one Ak, re-converge Newton, recompute the objective. What is compared is then the total derivative, with the state's response to the geometry included, rather than a partial derivative at frozen state.

还有一个更强的检验：已知的最优点。第 6 节里目标压力取自一条已知的流道。在那条流道上，L 应当为零，梯度也应当为零——而且不是「很小」，是逐比特为零，因为同一个确定性的 Newton 会复现出同一个状态。上表最后两行就是它。一个梯度能在一个独立构造的最优点上归零，比任何差分对照都更有说服力。

And a stronger check still: a known optimum. In Section 6 the target pressures were taken from a known duct. On that duct both L and the gradient must vanish — and not merely be small, but be zero to the last bit, because the same deterministic Newton reproduces the same state. Those are the last two rows above. A gradient that vanishes at an independently constructed optimum is better evidence than any finite-difference comparison.

差分校验本身是会骗人的。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ϵ/h)，随 h 变小反而增大。h 取得太大或太小，一个正确的梯度都会看起来像是错的。这正是点积测试更可靠的原因——它是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。上面几何方向那条测试就是明证：它落在 10−16，而第 11 节拿差分对照整条链路，只能到 10−9（反设计）与 10−10（推力）。

Finite-difference checking is itself capable of lying. A central difference has two error contributions: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks with h; and round-off, about O(ϵ/h), which grows as h shrinks. Too large or too small a step makes a correct gradient look wrong. That is exactly why the dot-product test is the more reliable instrument — it is an exact identity with no step size in it. The geometric test above proves the point: it lands at 10−16, while the full-chain differences of Section 11 reach only 10−9 (inverse design) and 10−10 (thrust).

第 12 节
Section 12

总结

Summary

  • 边循环是核心：残差就是「每条对偶面收集两侧状态、算一次一维通量、按 +,− 分发」，加上两个边界通量、一趟源项。
  • 边界节点就在边界上，所以边界条件可以强加：不需要 ghost 状态，直接换掉方程。
  • 换的是部分行：入口 3 行换 2 行、出口换 1 行，条数等于进入区域的特征条数。
  • 被替换的行是约束的梯度，一般不是单位行；二维格点页是它退化成 eiT 的特例。
  • 伴随里对应两步：进循环前在副本上清零，出循环后加回 wi·(∂c/∂u)，顺序不能反。
  • 几何方向还要再清零一次：被替换的行不含面积，忘了清零梯度就错了——反设计 108%，推力 4%。
  • The edge loop is the whole story: the residual is "gather both states on each dual face, call the flux once, scatter with + and −", plus two boundary fluxes and one source pass.
  • A boundary node sits on the boundary, so the conditions can be imposed strongly: no ghost state, just replace the equations.
  • The replacement is partial: 2 of 3 rows at the inflow, 1 of 3 at the outflow — as many as there are characteristics entering.
  • The replaced rows are constraint gradients, general rows rather than unit rows; the two-dimensional node page is the case where they degenerate to eiT.
  • The adjoint answers with two steps: zero on a copy before the loop, add back wi·(∂c/∂u) after it, and the order cannot be swapped.
  • The geometric direction needs the zeroing too: the replaced rows contain no areas, and forgetting it makes the gradient wrong — by 108% for inverse design, 4% for thrust.
