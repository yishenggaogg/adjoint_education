第 1 节Section 1

网格：一个位置对应三个守恒量

Mesh: three conserved variables at each location

先从 Burgers 的一个未知量走向 Euler 的三个未知量。把流道长度无量纲化为 1，在 N 个等长区间上给出截面积 A。这里的“拟一维”并非忽略横截面：截面积同时乘在面通量上，并通过壁面压力源项进入动量方程。数组下标从 0 开始，状态排列为 [ρ₀,m₀,e₀,ρ₁,m₁,e₁,…]。

Move from one Burgers unknown to three Euler unknowns. Scale duct length to one and specify area A at the endpoints of N equal intervals. Quasi-one-dimensional flow retains cross-section effects through both area-weighted flux and the wall-pressure momentum source. Arrays are zero-based; the state ordering is [ρ₀,m₀,e₀,ρ₁,m₁,e₁,…].

本页把 U 保存于原始节点 xᵢ=i h。内部控制体为 [xᵢ−h/2,xᵢ+h/2]，端点控制体只有半宽；对偶面截面积由原始节点的 A 线性插值得到。状态有 3(N+1) 个自由度。端点的三条强边界约束在残差装配完成后替换指定方程。

U is stored at primal nodes xᵢ=i h. Interior dual volumes extend half an interval to either side; endpoint volumes have half width. Dual-face areas interpolate primal-node A values. There are 3(N+1) state degrees of freedom. Three strong endpoint constraints replace selected equations after residual assembly.

h=1/N,A(x)=1−0.3sin⁡2(πx),Ui=(ρi,mi,ei)T.h=1/N,\qquad A(x)=1-0.3\sin^2(\pi x),\qquad U_i=(\rho_i,m_i,e_i)^T.

下面选取控制体与显示分量。蓝色点是未知量位置，竖线是计算面，淡紫色区域是当前控制体；流道轮廓表示截面积，不是二维网格。所有后续交互共享顶端选择的空间阶数与线性求解方法。

Select a volume and component below. Blue dots locate unknowns, vertical lines mark computational faces, and violet shading selects a volume. The duct outline represents area, not a two-dimensional mesh. All subsequent interactions share the spatial order and solver selected at the top.

顶部参数改变当前光滑亚声速问题。面积 A(x)=1−d sin²(πx)；参考目标面积固定为 (1−0.3sin²πx)(1−0.04sinπx)，使用本次选定的边界数据生成后冻结。主求解器要求正密度、正压力且所有检查状态 M<0.95；失败时不会显示上一次结果。第 14、15 节加密表仍是固定默认参数的离线基准。

Top controls change the current smooth subsonic problem. Area is A(x)=1−d sin²(πx). The target area remains (1−0.3sin²πx)(1−0.04sinπx); its flow uses the selected boundary data and is then frozen. The primal requires positive density and pressure and M<0.95 at all checked states. Failed runs clear previous results. Sections 14–15 retain offline refinement data for the default parameters.

第 2 节Section 2

方程、热力学与二阶重构

Equations, thermodynamics and second-order reconstruction

本节实验：改变一个未知量，哪些面值会变化？先预测重构邻域，再用图验证。

Experiment: Which traces change when one unknown changes? Predict the stencil, then inspect the diagram.

理想气体取 γ=1.4、气体常数 R=1。m=ρu 是动量密度，e 是总能量密度，不能把 e 当作单位质量能量。先从 U 恢复压力、速度和声速，再计算通量。

Use an ideal gas with γ=1.4 and gas constant R=1. Momentum density is m=ρu; e is total energy per unit volume, not per unit mass. Recover pressure, velocity and sound speed before evaluating flux.

∂t(AU)+∂x(AF(U))=A′p(U)e2,F=[mm2/ρ+p(e+p)m/ρ],e2=(0,1,0)T.\partial_t(AU)+\partial_x(AF(U))=A'p(U)e_2,\quad F=\begin{bmatrix}m\\m^2/\rho+p\\(e+p)m/\rho\end{bmatrix},\quad e_2=(0,1,0)^T.
u=m/ρ,p=(γ−1)(e−m2/(2ρ)),c=γp/ρ,T=p/ρ.u=m/\rho,\quad p=(\gamma-1)(e-m^2/(2\rho)),\quad c=\sqrt{\gamma p/\rho},\quad T=p/\rho.

一阶直接读取 Uᵢ；二阶把守恒量在控制体内重构为直线。内部斜率采用中心差分，两个端点采用单边差分；同一条重构既用于面状态，也用于源项和目标函数的积分点。当前仅研究光滑亚声速流动，不使用限制器。高阶重构的导数包含邻点，不能把斜率冻结后仍称为精确二阶导数。

First order reads Uᵢ directly. Second order reconstructs a linear conservative state inside each volume, using centred interior slopes and one-sided endpoint slopes. The same reconstruction supplies faces, source quadrature and objective quadrature. This smooth subsonic example has no limiter. Reconstruction derivatives include neighbouring states; freezing slopes would not give an exact second-order derivative.

Ui(x)=Ui+(x−xi)si,si=Ui+1−Ui−12h,s0=U1−U0h,sm−1=Um−1−Um−2h.U_i(x)=U_i+(x-x_i)s_i,\quad s_i=\frac{U_{i+1}-U_{i-1}}{2h},\quad s_0=\frac{U_1-U_0}{h},\quad s_{m-1}=\frac{U_{m-1}-U_{m-2}}h.
Hf=Af[F(Uf−)+F(Uf+)2−αf2(Uf+−Uf−)],αf=u−+c−+u++c+2.H_f=A_f\left[\frac{F(U_f^-)+F(U_f^+)}2-\frac{\alpha_f}2(U_f^+-U_f^-)\right],\quad\alpha_f=\frac{u^-+c^-+u^++c^+}{2}.

在本例的正向光滑分支上 u>0；上述平均波速是明确选择的耗散形式，不是 max 波速的近似求导。α 随状态变化，其全部导数保留。每次更新检查密度、压力和重构状态的亚声速条件。图中一阶两侧常值与二阶两侧直线使用同一收敛状态，便于看清重构本身的作用。

On the selected positive-flow branch u>0. The averaged speed defines the chosen dissipation; it is not a differentiated approximation to max. All state derivatives of α are retained. Updates check density, pressure and subsonic reconstructed states. The diagram compares constant and linear traces using the same converged state to isolate reconstruction.

第 3 节Section 3

边界条件：给定哪些量，哪些量由内部决定

Boundary conditions: prescribed and outgoing information

亚声速正向流动有三条特征速度 u−c、u、u+c。左端两条进入计算域、右端一条进入计算域，所以给定入口总压 p₀、总温 T₀ 和出口静压 p_b。把入口全部三个守恒量再加出口压力一起钉死，会过度约束问题。

Positive subsonic flow has characteristic speeds u−c, u and u+c. Two characteristics enter at the left and one at the right. Prescribe inlet total pressure p₀, total temperature T₀ and outlet static pressure p_b. Prescribing all three inlet conservative values as well as exit pressure would overconstrain the problem.

T0=T(1+γ−12M2),p0=p(1+γ−12M2)γ/(γ−1),M=u/c.T_0=T\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right),\quad p_0=p\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right)^{\gamma/(\gamma-1)},\quad M=u/c.

格点页用入口第 0、1 行分别放 p₀(U₀)−p₀,in 和 T₀(U₀)−T₀,in，用出口第 0 行放 p(U_N)−p_b。其余行仍为积分守恒方程。被覆盖的旧行必须在前向和反向计算中一并移除。

At the inlet, rows 0 and 1 become p₀(U₀)−p₀,in and T₀(U₀)−T₀,in. Exit row 0 becomes p(U_N)−p_b. Remaining rows retain integral balances. Both tangent and reverse calculations must remove the overwritten contributions.

δB(U,b)=BUδU+Bbδb,U‾+=BUTB‾,b‾+=BbTB‾.\delta B(U,b)=B_U\delta U+B_b\delta b,\qquad \overline U\mathrel{+}=B_U^T\overline B,\quad\overline b\mathrel{+}=B_b^T\overline B.

边界也是计算图的一部分。固定设计参数不等于固定边界状态：即使 p₀、T₀、p_b 不变，内部扰动仍通过出域特征改变边界状态。

Boundaries belong to the computational graph. Fixed boundary parameters do not imply fixed boundary states: interior perturbations still propagate through outgoing characteristics.

第 4 节Section 4

残差：面循环、体积分与强边界行

Residual: face loops, volume quadrature and boundary rows

Rᵢ 是积分残差，没有除以体积。内部面只算一次，向左控制体加 H、向右控制体减 H；随后从动量分量减去压力壁面源项。面积在各原始区间内为直线；每段采用两点 Gauss 积分。格点对偶体若跨越原始节点，必须先拆成两段，不能跨过 A′ 的跳变只做一次积分。

Rᵢ is an integrated residual, without volume division. Evaluate each internal face once, adding H to the left volume and subtracting it from the right. Subtract the wall-pressure source from momentum afterwards. Area is linear on each primal interval. Apply two-point Gauss quadrature on each piece; split node-centred dual volumes at primal nodes where A′ changes.

Ri=Hi+1/2−Hi−1/2−e2Si,Si=∑q∈Viwqp(Ui(xq))A′(xq).R_i=H_{i+1/2}-H_{i-1/2}-e_2 S_i,\quad S_i=\sum_{q\in V_i}w_qp(U_i(x_q))A'(x_q).
R[:] = 0
for face f:
    UL, UR = reconstruct_and_apply_boundary(U, f)
    H = area[f] * numerical_flux(UL, UR)
    if L exists: R[L] += H
    if R exists: R[R] -= H
for volume i:
    S[i] = sum(wq * pressure(reconstruct(U,i,xq)) * area_derivative(xq))
    R[i, momentum] -= S[i]
if node_centred: replace_three_boundary_rows(R, U, b)

播放器按照真实装配顺序展示累加。最后的强边界行覆盖是一个单独步骤；在格点页，全域所有“最终残差行”相加不再等于边界通量差，因为其中三行已经不是守恒方程。

The player follows the actual assembly order, including a separate final boundary-row replacement. Summing all final rows on the node page no longer yields only boundary flux differences because three rows are constraints rather than conservation equations.

第 5 节Section 5

Jacobian 与 GS：精确算子和低阶近似各做什么

Jacobian and GS: exact operator versus low-order approximation

把所有残差堆成向量，Newton 修正满足 A₂ ΔU=−R₂，A₂=∂R₂/∂U。这里用 A₂ 表示所选离散阶数的精确 Jacobian；一阶选择时它就是 A₁。二阶重构扩展了依赖范围。程序不存储 A₂，而沿标量计算图实施 Av 和 Aᵀw。为解线性系统，单独建立一阶近似 P=A₁；P 的每个对角块是 3×3。

Stack the residuals into a vector. Newton corrections satisfy A₂ ΔU=−R₂, with A₂ denoting the exact Jacobian of the selected order (A₁ when first order is selected). Reconstruction broadens dependencies. The program applies Av and Aᵀw through a scalar computational graph without storing A₂. A separate first-order approximation P=A₁ supplies 3×3 diagonal blocks for linear solves.

P=D+L+V,zi(k+1)=Pii−1(ri−∑j<iPijzj(k+1)−∑j>iPijzj(k)).P=D+L+V,\qquad z_i^{(k+1)}=P_{ii}^{-1}\left(r_i-\sum_{j<i}P_{ij}z_j^{(k+1)}-\sum_{j>i}P_{ij}z_j^{(k)}\right).

一次 GS 扫描使用已更新的左侧块和旧的右侧块。每次预处理从零开始做 12 次扫描，记所得线性映射为 B。外层固定点更新 x←x+0.7 B(b−A₂x)。多次内扫描使二阶缺陷校正在本页网格上稳定；不能把“一次扫描”等同于已解完 Pz=r。伴随使用整个 B 的转置，而不只是把某个局部 3×3 块转置。

A GS sweep uses updated earlier blocks and previous later blocks. Each preconditioner application starts from zero and performs 12 sweeps, defining a linear map B. Outer defect correction updates x←x+0.7 B(b−A₂x). Multiple inner sweeps stabilise this example’s second-order correction; one sweep is not an exact solution of Pz=r. The adjoint uses the transpose of the entire B map, not merely transposed diagonal blocks.

z = 0
repeat 12 times:
    z += solve_lower_blocks(D+L, rhs-P*z)
# Transposed map: reverse block order, transpose blocks and residual operator.
# Never replace the exact residual A2*x by P*x in the outer equation.

符号提示：Euler 的 A(x) 表示截面积；线性系统中的 A 或 A_h 表示残差 Jacobian。P 是低阶近似矩阵，B 是从零开始固定次数 GS 扫描形成的预处理映射，通常不等于精确 P⁻¹。

Notation: Euler A(x) denotes area; A or A_h in a linear system denotes the residual Jacobian. P is the low-order matrix; B is the fixed number of zero-start GS sweeps, generally not the exact inverse of P.

第 6 节Section 6

目标函数：壁面推力与压力反设计

Objectives: wall thrust and pressure inverse design

目标是一个标量，所以其伴随只需求一次，就能得到全部面积和边界参数的梯度。壁面推力取 ∫pA′dx；本例入口出口面积相同，光滑等熵参考解的净推力为零，但粗网格离散值不必为零。反设计目标比较当前压力和固定目标压力，目标面积固定，以便改变当前收缩深度时仍比较同一个目标流道。

A scalar objective needs one adjoint solve for gradients with respect to all area and boundary parameters. Wall thrust is ∫pA′dx. Equal inlet and exit areas give zero thrust for the smooth isentropic reference, although a coarse discrete result need not vanish. Inverse design compares current pressure with a fixed target pressure obtained from a fixed target duct, even when the current contraction depth changes.

JT=∑qwqpqAq′,JI=12∑qwqAq(pq−pq⋆)2,A⋆(x)=(1−0.3sin⁡2πx)(1−0.04sin⁡πx).J_T=\sum_qw_qp_qA'_q,\qquad J_I=\frac12\sum_qw_q A_q(p_q-p_q^\star)^2,\quad A^\star(x)=(1-0.3\sin^2\pi x)(1-0.04\sin\pi x).

目标状态由同一阶数、同一种网格的独立收敛计算生成，并在所有扰动计算中保持不变。重构后再求压力；目标压力也重构到同一积分点。若每次扰动都重新生成目标，验证的就变成了另一个函数。在目标流道及目标状态上，J 与总梯度应归零。

Generate the target with a separate converged solve using the same order and grid, then hold it fixed during perturbations. Reconstruct both states to the same quadrature locations before computing pressure. Regenerating the target after every perturbation would differentiate a different objective. At the target geometry and state, both J and its total gradient should vanish.

第 7 节Section 7

前向传播：把每一步变成乘积法则

Tangent propagation: apply the product rule at every step

本节实验：一次 tangent 计算给出残差对指定方向的变化，不是已经求出了状态对设计的敏感度。比较第 9 节的线性求解。

Experiment: One tangent application gives the residual change along a supplied direction. It is not yet the solved state sensitivity; compare the linear solve in Section 9.

给状态方向 v 和设计方向 δb，前向传播同时给出 R_Uv+R_bδb 与 J_Uv+J_bδb。先对重构加权和求导，再经过压力、声速、边界和通量，最后沿同样的面循环累加。对每个标量操作保存其输入和局部偏导，这就是本页自行实现的前向计算图。

For state direction v and parameter direction δb, the tangent returns R_Uv+R_bδb and J_Uv+J_bδb. Differentiate reconstruction, pressure, sound speed, boundary closure and flux, then scatter in the primal face order. The self-contained scalar graph stores inputs and local derivatives for each arithmetic operation.

第 8 节Section 8

伴随传播：同一条依赖链反向累加

Adjoint propagation: reverse the same dependency graph

本节实验：只给一个输出非零权重，预测反向影响能传到哪些输入；共享输入应累加所有路径。

Experiment: Seed one output and predict which inputs receive reverse contributions. Shared inputs accumulate every path.

反向传播接受残差权重 w 和目标权重 β，返回 R_Uᵀw+βJ_Uᵀ 与 R_bᵀw+βJ_bᵀ。在内部面，H 的种子为 w_L−w_R；源项 S 的种子为 −wᵢ,m。二阶面状态的伴随通过重构权重散回多个节点。共享输入必须累加，不能用赋值覆盖另一条路径。

A reverse sweep accepts residual weights w and objective weight β and returns R_Uᵀw+βJ_Uᵀ and R_bᵀw+βJ_bᵀ. An internal face receives seed w_L−w_R; a source receives −wᵢ,m. Second-order trace adjoints scatter through reconstruction weights to several states. Shared inputs accumulate all paths rather than overwriting them.

H‾f=wL−wR,U‾j+=WfjTU‾f,S‾i=−wi,m,wT(Av)=vT(ATw).\overline H_f=w_L-w_R,\quad \overline U_j\mathrel{+}=W_{fj}^T\overline U_f,\quad \overline S_i=-w_{i,m},\quad w^T(Av)=v^T(A^Tw).

强边界行反传时，先把这三行种子送入边界约束，再把旧守恒行的种子清零，之后才反传源项和面通量。否则会把已经被覆盖的旧行又加回导数。

For strong boundary rows, seed the boundary constraints, zero the overwritten balance-row seeds, and only then reverse sources and faces. Otherwise derivatives of erased rows would be reintroduced.

for operation in reverse_order:
    for input in operation.inputs:
        input.bar += local_partial * output.bar
# z=x*y: x.bar += y*z.bar; y.bar += x*z.bar
# second-order reconstruction: U[j].bar += weight[j]*Uf.bar
第 9 节Section 9

前向方程的求解：一个设计方向一次线性求解

Tangent solve: one linear system per design direction

收敛方程 R(U(b),b)=0 对参数求导，得到 A U_b=−R_b。这里 A 是收敛状态处的精确算子，不是预处理矩阵 P。解出 U_b 后再计算 dJ/db=J_b+J_U U_b。图中状态敏感度是一个解向量，和直接在任意 v 上做一次前向传播是两件不同的事。

Differentiate the converged equation R(U(b),b)=0 to obtain A U_b=−R_b. A is the exact operator at the converged state, not preconditioner P. Then dJ/db=J_b+J_U U_b. The plotted state sensitivity is a solved vector, distinct from a single tangent application to an arbitrary v.

附录 C：小规模伴随方程的直接求解

Appendix C: direct adjoint solves for small systems

本附录的一阶、二阶均不采用 matrix-free 求解：先显式组装并存储完整精确 Jacobian，再对其转置作带主元 LU 分解和三角求解。用精确导数取得矩阵列属于组装步骤；matrix-free 反向算子只在直接解完成后用于独立残差验证。

For both first and second order, this appendix does not use a matrix-free solve: explicitly assemble and store the complete exact Jacobian, then factor its transpose with pivoted LU and solve triangular systems. Derivative products used to obtain columns belong to assembly; the matrix-free reverse operator is used only afterwards for independent residual verification.

C.1 小系统为什么值得直接求解

C.1 Why use a direct method for a small system

默认 12 区间时，格心有 36 个状态未知量，格点有 39 个。这个规模下，可以把精确 Jacobian 全部写出来，采用带部分主元选取的 LU 分解直接解伴随方程。它避免许多次 GS 或 Krylov 算子调用，通常是这种教学小系统中很快的选择；是否最快由下方同条件实测决定，不能只比较迭代次数。

With the default 12 intervals there are 36 cell-centred or 39 node-centred state unknowns. At this size we can assemble the exact Jacobian and solve the adjoint with partial-pivot LU. This avoids many GS or Krylov operator applications and is often a fast choice for a small teaching system. The benchmark below determines which is fastest under the same conditions; iteration counts alone cannot establish that.

C.2 主元、消元、前代与回代

C.2 Pivoting, elimination and triangular solves

在第 k 列，从尚未消元的行中选绝对值最大的主元，把整行交换到第 k 行；右端项也必须按照相同置换重排。用主元消去下面的元素，把乘数保存在严格下三角，更新后的上三角保存 U。L 的对角线默认为 1。完整行交换也交换已经保存的 L 乘数，否则分解关系就会被破坏。

In column k, select the largest-magnitude remaining entry and swap its entire row into position k. Apply the same permutation to the right-hand side. Eliminate entries below the pivot, storing multipliers in the strict lower triangle and the updated U in the upper triangle. L has unit diagonal. Full row swaps also swap previously stored L multipliers, preserving the factorisation.

D.5 How to interpret results

先检查原始真残差与状态有效性，再判断 limiter 分支和单侧导数，再比较同一个线性系统的 GS／GMRES／LU，最后进行网格加密及连续参考比较。两种转置矩阵有相同的二范数条件数；迭代差异要从预处理、右端项、非正规增长及停止标准解释，不能笼统归因于“伴随更不稳定”。

Check primal residual and admissibility first, then limiter branches and one-sided derivatives, then GS/GMRES/LU on the same system, and finally refinement against continuous references. A matrix and its transpose have the same 2-norm condition number; iteration differences require examining preconditioners, right-hand sides, nonnormal growth and stopping criteria, not a blanket claim that adjoints are less stable.
