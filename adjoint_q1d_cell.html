变截面流道拟一维 Euler 方程的格心格式离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of the quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations in a variable-area duct, cell-centred

算例是一段收缩—扩张流道：截面积 A(x) 从 1 收到喉部的 0.7 再张回 1，左端给总压 p0 与总温 T0，右端给背压 pb，全场亚声速。未知量是 12 个单元上的守恒量 (ρ,ρu,ρE)，一共 36 个。与二维对流那一对页面相比，这里多出三样东西：局部导数从一个数变成 3×3 块、残差里多了一个不经过任何面的源项 pdA/dx、边界条件是内点状态的非线性函数。设计变量就是 13 个截面积，因此 dL/dA 有直接的物理含义：把流道这里放宽一点，目标会怎么变。The test case is a converging–diverging duct: the area A(x) falls from 1 to 0.7 at the throat and opens back to 1, with total pressure p0 and total temperature T0 prescribed at the left, a back pressure pb at the right, and subsonic flow throughout. The unknowns are the conserved variables (ρ,ρu,ρE) on 12 cells, 36 in all. Against the two-dimensional advection pair of pages, three things are new here: the local derivatives become 3×3 blocks, the residual gains a source term pdA/dx that passes through no face at all, and the boundary conditions are nonlinear functions of the interior state. The design variables are the 13 areas, so dL/dA means something you can point at: widen the duct here, and the objective moves by this much.

第 1 节
Section 1

流道与网格

The duct and the grid

拟一维的意思是：流道沿 x 的截面积 A(x) 在变，但每个截面上的流动当成均匀的。于是三维的管内流动塌缩成一条线上的三个守恒量——质量、动量、能量——而几何完全由 A(x) 承担。这正是它适合讲伴随的原因：设计变量就是那条面积分布，改动它的含义一目了然。

Quasi-one-dimensional means the duct's cross-sectional area A(x) varies along x, while the flow across each section is treated as uniform. A three-dimensional duct flow then collapses onto a line carrying three conserved quantities — mass, momentum and energy — with the geometry carried entirely by A(x). That is exactly what makes it a good vehicle for the adjoint: the design variables are that area distribution, and changing them means something you can picture.

算例取 x∈[0,1]，面积 A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx)：两端为 1，喉部 0.7。网格分成 12 个单元、13 个面。采用格心格式：每个单元上存一组守恒量的单元平均值 (ρ,ρu,ρE)，所以未知量共 36 个（）。

The test case takes x∈[0,1] with A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx): unity at both ends and 0.7 at the throat. The grid has 12 cells and 13 faces. The scheme is cell-centred: each cell stores one cell average of the conserved variables (ρ,ρu,ρE), so there are 36 unknowns ().

面积住在面上，而且它就是设计变量。13 个面各有一个 Af，单元的体积取两侧面积的平均乘 Δx。第 11 节要求的 dL/dA 就是对这 13 个数的导数——共 13 个设计变量。二维那一对页面把网格节点坐标当设计变量，几何含义要绕一圈才说得清；这里不用绕。

The areas live on the faces, and they are the design variables. Each of the 13 faces carries one Af, and a cell's volume is the average of its two face areas times Δx. The dL/dA of Section 11 is the derivative with respect to those 13 numbers — 13 design variables. The two-dimensional pair used mesh node coordinates, where the geometric meaning takes a paragraph to explain. Here it takes none.

源项是这一对页面的第一件新东西。S 只进动量方程，而且它是个体积项：它不穿过任何面，是在单元内部产生的。二维对流那一对页面的残差完全由循环拼成，这里循环之外还要再走一趟。它的转置同样落在单元自己身上、不经过面；漏掉它，点积测试就不再成立——实测相对误差从 10−16 量级升到 10−4 到 10−1，随所取的向量而定。

The source term is the first new thing on these pages. S enters only the momentum equation, and it is a volume term: it crosses no face; it is produced inside the cell. On the two-dimensional advection pages the residual was assembled entirely by the loop; here one more pass is needed after it. Its transpose likewise lands on the cell itself and passes through no face; drop it and the dot-product test fails — measured, the relative error rises from order 10−16 to between 10−4 and 10−1, depending on the vectors drawn.

面上的数值通量

The numerical flux on a face

面上取 Rusanov 通量：中心平均减一个正比于状态跳跃的耗散项，

Each face takes the Rusanov flux: a central average minus a dissipation proportional to the jump in state,

hf=Af[12(FL+FR)−12λf(UR−UL)], λf=12[(|u|+a)L+(|u|+a)R]

为什么 λ 取两侧平均而不是取大者。教科书里的 Rusanov 用 max。max 在两侧相等处不可微：哪一侧更大随状态切换，切换处导数跳变——那一点没有唯一的梯度，差分步长一旦跨过它，有限差分就与线性化对不上。取平均则只要 u≠0 就处处光滑（本算例 u 处处为正），而且在本算例的亚声速流场里耗散量级几乎相同：实测 max 最多只比平均值大 1.2%。真实代码里要用 max 时的做法是把它冻结在收敛解 u* 处，与二维页冻结远场入流/出流开关完全一样。

Why λ is the average of the two sides rather than the larger. Textbook Rusanov uses max. But max is not differentiable where the two sides cross: which side is larger switches with the state, and the derivative jumps there — that point has no single gradient, and a finite difference whose step straddles it disagrees with the linearisation. The average is smooth wherever u≠0 (and u>0 throughout this duct) and, in this subsonic flow, dissipates by almost the same amount: measured, the max is at most 1.2% larger than the average. When a real code does use max, the remedy is to freeze it at the converged solution u*, exactly as the two-dimensional pages freeze the far-field inflow/outflow switch.

本页同样采用一阶空间离散：面上的左右状态直接取两侧单元的当前值，不做重构。代价只在精度上，不在这套代数上。

The discretisation here is likewise first order: the left and right states on a face are the current values of the two adjacent cells, with no reconstruction. The cost is accuracy only, not the algebra this page is about.

守恒守的是通量，不是单元值

What is conserved is the flux, not the cell value

稳态下质量方程没有源项，所以数值质量通量在 13 个面上必须完全相同——下面实测到机器精度。但如果去看单元中心的 ρuA，它沿程能差百分之几。两者的差就是耗散项 −12λ(UR−UL)Af：一阶格式在 12 个单元上就是这个量级。这不是不守恒，这是「单元值」和「通量」不是同一个东西。

At steady state the mass equation has no source, so the numerical mass flux must be identical on all 13 faces — measured below, to machine precision. Look instead at the cell-centre value of ρuA and it varies by a few percent along the duct. The difference between the two is the dissipation term −12λ(UR−UL)Af, and on 12 cells a first-order scheme makes it about this large. That is not a loss of conservation; it is the fact that a cell value and a flux are different objects.

在里逐个面切换，能看到同一件事的另一面：质量和能量的 h 在全部 13 个面上相同到舍入误差（能量方程同样没有源项），中心项和耗散项却各自在变；动量的 h 逐面不同，相邻两个面之间的差额正好由源项 pΔA 补上。

Stepping from face to face in shows the other side of the same fact: the mass and energy components of h agree on all 13 faces to round-off (the energy equation has no source either), while the central and dissipation terms each change; the momentum component does change from face to face, and between two neighbouring faces the source pΔA makes up exactly the difference.

边界条件

Boundary conditions

一维 Euler 的三条特征速度是 u−a、u、u+a。亚声速时 u<a，所以在左端：u 与 u+a 为正、向右进入区域，u−a 为负、离开区域。该给几个条件，由进入区域的特征条数决定：左端给 2 个，右端给 1 个（）。

The three characteristic speeds of the one-dimensional Euler equations are u−a, u and u+a. Subsonic means u<a, so at the left end u and u+a are positive and enter the domain while u−a is negative and leaves it. How many conditions to impose is decided by how many characteristics enter: two at the left, one at the right ().

边界 Jacobian 的秩 = 离开区域的特征条数

The rank of the boundary Jacobian equals the number of outgoing characteristics

关键在于：边界状态 B 是内点状态的函数，所以 ∂B/∂u 是一个 3×3 块，第 5 节起它会一路进入 Jacobian、前向与伴随。而这个块有一个漂亮的结构——

The point is that the boundary state B is a function of the interior state, so ∂B/∂u is a 3×3 block that propagates into the Jacobian, the tangent and the adjoint from Section 5 on. And that block has a rather elegant structure —

入口的边界状态只通过一个标量 R− 依赖内点，所以 ∂B/∂u 是两个向量的外积，秩为 1。出口通过两个标量（熵与 R+）依赖内点，秩为 2。而 1 和 2 正是各自边界上离开区域的特征条数——内点能送出去的信息，一条特征一份。

The inflow state depends on the interior through the single scalar R−, so ∂B/∂u is an outer product of two vectors and has rank 1. The outflow depends on two scalars (entropy and R+) and has rank 2. Those counts, 1 and 2, are exactly the number of characteristics leaving the domain at each end — one piece of information from inside per outgoing characteristic.

残差 = 面循环 + 源项

The residual = the face loop, plus the source

残差就是一趟面循环加一趟源项（逐句播放这两趟）。面循环和二维那一对页面一模一样——收集两侧状态、算一次一维通量、按 +,− 分发给两个单元——唯一的差别是「一个数」换成了「三个数」。

The residual is one face loop followed by one source pass ( plays both, statement by statement). The face loop is exactly the one from the two-dimensional pair — gather the two states, evaluate one one-dimensional flux, scatter it to the two cells with + and − — the only difference being that "a number" has become "three numbers".

源项那一趟是新的。它不属于任何面：pdA/dx 在单元内部产生，只进动量方程。把它放在面循环之外单独走一遍，是因为它本来就不是面上的东西——这一点在第 8 节做转置时会变得很重要。

The source pass is new. It belongs to no face: pdA/dx is produced inside the cell and enters only the momentum equation. It is a separate pass precisely because it is not a face quantity — which will matter in Section 8, when everything gets transposed.

A boundary face has no "second cell". At f=0 and f=N only one side is a real cell; the other state is computed from the boundary conditions of Section 3. Those two faces therefore scatter into one cell only — and because the outer state is a function of the interior one, their contribution to the Jacobian drags ∂B/∂u along with it, as the next section shows.

求解：Jacobian 与块 Jacobi 迭代

The solve: the Jacobian and block Jacobi

第 4 节的 residual(u, A) 就是离散残差 R(u)。它的 Jacobian J=∂R/∂u（其他页面记作 A，本页的 A 已经是截面积）是 36×36 的，但真正的结构是 12×12 个 3×3 块：一个单元只与它的两个面邻居相连，所以 J 是块三对角的。下面解 R(u)=0 的块 Jacobi 迭代只用到它的 12 个对角块，但第 7 到 10 节的切线与伴随逐面作用的就是 J 本身，所以它必须精确：下面逐项求导、解析地组装，不用差分近似。

The residual(u, A) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u). Its Jacobian J=∂R/∂u (written A on the other pages; here A is already the area) is 36×36, but its real structure is 12×12 blocks of 3×3: a cell is connected only to its two face neighbours, so J is block tridiagonal. The block-Jacobi iteration that solves R(u)=0 below needs only its 12 diagonal blocks, but the tangent and the adjoint of Sections 7 to 10 apply J itself, face by face, so it has to be exact. Below it is differentiated term by term and assembled analytically, with no finite-difference approximation.

为什么用块 Jacobi，而不用 LU。本页只有 36 个未知量，理论上最快的办法是直接分解：把上面精确的 J 整个造出来，跑 Newton，每一步做一次稠密 LU；从同一个初值出发，全步长 4 次更新就降到判据以下。但那样就体现不出 matrix-free。块 Jacobi 是最简单的迭代法：矩阵只用到 12 个 3×3 对角块，其余一律经由残差进来——切线方程里经由 residual_d，伴随方程里经由 residual_b——这正是 matrix-free 的意思。上面的演示把整个 J 组装出来，只是为了看清它的结构；求解本身从不形成它。代价是线性收敛：在解附近误差每一轮乘以迭代矩阵 I−D−1J，速率由它的谱半径决定，这里是 0.977，所以判据要到 k=1086 才满足。切线与伴随的迭代矩阵与它同谱，进入渐近阶段以后，三条收敛曲线以同一个速率下降（第 10 节）。

Why block Jacobi, and not LU. With only 36 unknowns the fastest method in principle is a direct factorisation: form the whole exact J above, run Newton, and factorise with one dense LU per step; from the same guess, full steps meet the criterion after 4 updates. But that would show nothing about matrix-free. Block Jacobi is the simplest iterative method there is: of the matrix it needs only the 12 diagonal 3×3 blocks, and everything else comes in through the residual (through residual_d in the tangent equation and residual_b in the adjoint equation), which is exactly what matrix-free means. The demo above assembles the whole J only to show its structure; the solve itself never forms it. The price is linear convergence: near the solution the error is multiplied every sweep by the iteration matrix I−D−1J, at a rate set by its spectral radius, 0.977 here, so the criterion is not met until k=1086. The tangent and adjoint iteration matrices have the same spectrum, so once in the asymptotic phase all three convergence curves fall at the same rate (Section 10).

残差比一轮大、一轮小。判据附近，相邻两轮的残差比在 0.720 与 1.303 之间来回跳，隔一轮看，平均每两轮乘 0.9772（在 k=400 到 600 之间取平均；判据附近混进了舍入误差，每一对的乘积会差几个百分点）。块三对角矩阵上的 Jacobi 就是这样：迭代矩阵的特征值成对出现，±μ，这里最大的一对是 ±0.977。最慢的误差是这两个模态的叠加，其中一个每轮变号，最大范数轮流看到两者的和与差。所以本页的速率都按整对的轮次来量（k=400 到 600）。

The ratio alternates. Near the criterion the ratio of successive residuals jumps between 0.720 and 1.303, and over two sweeps it averages 0.9772 (the average over k=400 to 600; near the criterion round-off makes each pair's product scatter by a few percent). That is Jacobi on a block-tridiagonal matrix: the eigenvalues of its iteration matrix come in pairs ±μ, the largest pair here being ±0.977. The slowest error is a mix of the two modes, one of which changes sign every sweep, and the max norm sees their sum and their difference in turn. So every rate on this page is measured over whole pairs of sweeps (from k=400 to 600).

固定轮次，但要检查。代码固定跑 2400 轮，而不在判据处退出：多出来的轮次把 u* 一直推到舍入误差平台（从 k=1394 起），页面上所有实测常数都是在这些轮次下得到的；返回的 u* 与 Newton 解相差 2.3×10−15。它记下判据满足的那一轮；若 2400 轮都没有满足判据，就报错，而不是把未收敛的状态当作 u* 返回。

A fixed sweep count, but a checked one. The code runs a fixed 2400 sweeps rather than exiting at the criterion: the remaining sweeps take u* down to its round-off floor (from k=1394 on), and every measured constant on this page was produced under those sweeps; the u* it returns is within 2.3×10−15 of Newton's. It records the sweep at which the criterion was met, and raises an error if 2400 sweeps never meet it, rather than returning an unconverged state as u*.

Matrix-free forward mode: computing Jv

设计变量 A∈ℝ13 是各面的截面积。对 R(u(A),A)=0 用链式法则消去 du/dA 有两条路；两条路都要用到同一个乘积，先把它说清楚：给定一个状态扰动 v，怎样算出 Jv 而不形成 J。

matrix-free 伴随：计算 JTw

Matrix-free adjoint mode: computing JTw

伴随就是把前向逐句转置。规则只有两条：原来写某个数组的地方，现在读它；原来读的地方，现在写。于是数据流整个反过来——输入是残差一侧的权重 w，输出是状态一侧的 y=JTw。

The adjoint is the tangent transposed statement by statement. Two rules suffice: wherever the forward code wrote an array, the reverse code reads it; wherever it read, the reverse writes. The data flow turns around: the input is a weight w on the residual side, the output y=JTw on the state side.

面上的一维通量在前向是「两个输入、一个输出」：(vL,vR)↦hd。它的转置是「一个输入、两个输出」：给定面上的权重 hb，返回 (cLThb,cRThb)。标量页里这是两个数各乘一遍，这里是两个 3×3 块各转置一次（）。

步长为什么要能调。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ϵ/h)（ϵ≈2.2×10−16 为机器精度），随 h 变小反而增大。两者相抵，最佳步长大致在 h∼ϵ1/3≈6×10−6；前向差分的截断误差是 O(h)，平衡点移到 ϵ1/2≈1.5×10−8。实测扫描与此一致：两个目标、13 个面积，中心差分的最优步长都在 10−5 到 10−6 之间，多数就在 10−6；前向差分都在 10−7 到 10−9 之间，多数在 10−8。离开最优点，校验坏得很快：反设计、中心差分，最差那个分量的相对误差在 h=10−6 时是 3.0e−8，在 h=10−2 时约 10%（截断主导），在 h=10−12 时约 7%（舍入主导）——两头都会让“验证”看起来像是失败了。这正是差分校验最容易骗人的地方，也是点积测试更可靠的原因：它是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。第 11 节的几何点积测试落在 10−16，而这里的全链路差分在 h=10−6 时，与伴随之差最大还有最大分量的 3.6e−9（反设计）与 1.1e−10（推力）。

Why the step has to be adjustable. A central difference has two error contributions: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks with h; and round-off, about O(ϵ/h) (with ϵ≈2.2×10−16 the machine precision), which grows as h shrinks. They balance roughly at h∼ϵ1/3≈6×10−6; a forward difference truncates at O(h), which moves the balance to ϵ1/2≈1.5×10−8. The measured sweeps agree: over both objectives and all 13 areas, the central optimum lies between 10−5 and 10−6, mostly at 10−6 itself, and the forward one between 10−7 and 10−9, mostly at 10−8. Away from the optimum the check degrades fast: for inverse design with central differences the worst component is off by 3.0e−8 at h=10−6, but by about 10% at h=10−2, where truncation dominates, and by about 7% at h=10−12, where round-off does — at either end the “verification” looks like a failure. This is exactly where a difference check is most deceptive, and why the dot-product test is the more reliable instrument: it is an exact identity, with no step size in it at all. The geometric dot-product test of Section 11 lands at 10−16, while the full-chain differences here, at h=10−6, still differ from the adjoint by up to 3.6e−9 (inverse design) and 1.1e−10 (thrust) of the largest component.

那么哪一侧更糟？对中心差分，两侧的斜率不同：h 每放大一个数量级，截断误差涨约 100 倍（二次）；h 每缩小一个数量级，舍入误差只涨约 10 倍，而且起伏不定（舍入误差本身近乎随机）。实测离最优点两个数量级时，偏大那一侧更糟的，反设计是 13 个面积里的 13 个，推力是 10 个。前向差分没有这种不对称——它的截断误差 O(h) 和舍入误差一样，都是每个数量级约 10 倍——哪一侧更糟随分量而变（偏大那一侧更糟的：反设计 7 个，推力 4 个）。所以实用结论只对中心差分成立：宁可把 h 取偏小，也不要取偏大。差分的步长两难，在第 13 节的复数步长那里不存在。

So which side is worse? For central differences the two slopes differ: enlarging h by one order multiplies the truncation error by about 100 (quadratic), while shrinking it multiplies the round-off error by only about 10, and raggedly, since round-off is close to random. Measured two orders of magnitude from the optimum, the too-large side is the worse one for 13 of the 13 areas under inverse design and for 10 under thrust. A forward difference has no such asymmetry — its truncation error, O(h), rises about tenfold per order just as the round-off does — and which side is worse changes from area to area (the too-large side for 7 of them under inverse design, 4 under thrust). So the practical rule holds for central differences only: better to take h a little too small than a little too large. The step dilemma of differences does not arise for the complex step of Section 13.

实部与虚部各自分开：虚部的第一项就是 hdL/dAm，下一项已是 h3。这里没有两个相近的数相减，也就没有相消，h 可以取 10−30：O(h2) 的截断误差远在舍入误差之下。

What this means for the adjoint

第 10–13 节验证的是：伴随与几何梯度对离散的 L 精确到舍入误差，这与格式几阶无关。离散的 L 本身则随网格加密以格式的阶逼近连续值。优化器拿到的梯度与它所优化的离散目标函数完全一致，问题是自洽的；网格加密决定的是这个离散问题离连续问题有多近。要让它更近，得提高格式的阶（例如 MUSCL 重构），而不是改伴随。

What Sections 10 to 13 verified is that the adjoint and the geometric gradient are exact, to round-off, for the discrete L, whatever the scheme's order. The discrete L itself approaches the continuous value at the scheme's order as the grid is refined. The gradient the optimiser gets agrees exactly with the discrete objective it optimises, so the problem is self-consistent; refinement decides how close that discrete problem is to the continuous one. To bring it closer one has to raise the scheme's order (a MUSCL reconstruction, say), not change the adjoint.

总结

Summary

整条链路，从头到尾：

The whole chain, end to end:

  • 一维通量是核心：残差就是「对每个面收集两侧状态、调用一次一维通量、按 +,− 分发」，再加一趟不经过任何面的源项。
  • 前向与伴随是同一个循环：前向把每一步换成它的导数（两个 3×3 块），伴随把每一步反过来（同样两个块，转置）。谁都不形成矩阵。
  • 边界条件是内点状态的函数，它的 Jacobian 秩等于离开区域的特征条数：入口 1、出口 2。
  • 点积测试是精确恒等式，不含步长：「漏掉源项」这类错误，它能直接定位到出错的那个算子；全链路差分只能告诉你梯度不对，还得先把步长误差排除掉。
  • 几何导数同样是一趟循环：∂R/∂A 两个方向都解析，所以几何点积测试也能到机器精度。
  • 一个块 Jacobi，三个方程：primal、切线、伴随都用同一个块 Jacobi 迭代，只用到精确 Jacobian 的 12 个 3×3 对角块与 residual_d、residual_b 两个乘积。三个迭代矩阵同谱，进入渐近阶段后收敛曲线平行（谱半径 0.977）；切线与伴随给出同一个 dL/dpb，相对差 8.0×10−16。
  • 验证：点积测试检查转置，差分与复数步长检查梯度。复数步长从收敛解出发，每一轮都与前向迭代一致，与伴随梯度相符到舍入误差。
  • The one-dimensional flux is the whole story: the residual is "gather both states on each face, call the flux once, scatter with + and −", plus one source pass that touches no face.
  • Forward and adjoint are the same loop: the tangent replaces each step by its derivative (two 3×3 blocks), the adjoint runs each step backwards (the same two blocks, transposed). Neither forms a matrix.
  • A boundary condition is a function of the interior state, and the rank of its Jacobian equals the number of characteristics leaving the domain: 1 at the inflow, 2 at the outflow.
  • The dot-product test is an exact identity with no step size: a mistake such as a dropped source term is pinned to the very operator at fault, whereas a full-chain difference can only say the gradient is wrong, after its own step error has been ruled out.
  • The geometric derivative is one more loop: ∂R/∂A is analytic in both directions, so its dot-product test also reaches machine precision.
  • One block Jacobi, three equations: the primal, the tangent and the adjoint are all solved by the same block-Jacobi iteration, which needs only the 12 diagonal 3×3 blocks of the exact Jacobian and the two products residual_d and residual_b. The three iteration matrices share their spectrum, so once asymptotic the convergence curves run parallel (spectral radius 0.977); the tangent and the adjoint give the same dL/dpb, to 8.0×10−16.
  • Verification: the dot-product test checks the transpose, finite differences and the complex step check the gradient. Started from the converged solution, the complex step matches the forward iteration at every sweep, and the adjoint gradient to round-off.
