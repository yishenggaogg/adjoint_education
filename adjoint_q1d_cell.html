变截面流道拟一维 Euler 方程的格心格式离散伴随

The discrete adjoint of the quasi-one-dimensional Euler equations in a variable-area duct, cell-centred

算例是一段收缩—扩张流道：截面积 A(x) 从 1 收到喉部的 0.7 再张回 1，左端给总压 p0 与总温 T0，右端给背压 pb，全场亚声速。未知量是 12 个单元上的守恒量 (ρ,ρu,ρE)，一共 36 个。与二维标量那一对页面相比，这里多出三样东西：局部导数从一个数变成 3×3 块、残差里多了一个不经过任何面的源项 pdA/dx、边界条件是内点状态的非线性函数。设计变量就是 13 个截面积，因此 dL/dA 有直接的物理含义：把流道这里放宽一点，目标会怎么变。The test case is a converging–diverging duct: the area A(x) falls from 1 to 0.7 at the throat and opens back to 1, with total pressure p0 and total temperature T0 prescribed at the left, a back pressure pb at the right, and subsonic flow throughout. The unknowns are the conserved variables (ρ,ρu,ρE) on 12 cells, 36 in all. Against the two-dimensional scalar pair of pages, three things are new here: the local derivatives become 3×3 blocks, the residual gains a source term pdA/dx that passes through no face at all, and the boundary conditions are nonlinear functions of the interior state. The design variables are the 13 areas, so dL/dA means something you can point at: widen the duct here, and the objective moves by this much.

第 1 节
Section 1

流道与网格

The duct and the grid

拟一维的意思是：流道沿 x 的截面积 A(x) 在变，但每个截面上的流动当成均匀的。于是三维的管内流动塌缩成一条线上的三个守恒量——质量、动量、能量——而几何完全由 A(x) 承担。这正是它适合讲伴随的原因：设计变量就是那条面积分布，改动它的含义一目了然。

Quasi-one-dimensional means the duct's cross-sectional area A(x) varies along x, while the flow across each section is treated as uniform. A three-dimensional duct flow then collapses onto a line carrying three conserved quantities — mass, momentum and energy — with the geometry carried entirely by A(x). That is exactly what makes it a good vehicle for the adjoint: the design variables are that area distribution, and changing them means something you can picture.

算例取 x∈[0,1]，面积 A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx)：两端为 1，喉部 0.7。网格分成 12 个单元、13 个面。采用格心格式：每个单元上存一组守恒量的单元平均值 (ρ,ρu,ρE)，所以未知量共 36 个（）。

The test case takes x∈[0,1] with A(x)=1−0.3sin2(πx): unity at both ends and 0.7 at the throat. The grid has 12 cells and 13 faces. The scheme is cell-centred: each cell stores one cell average of the conserved variables (ρ,ρu,ρE), so there are 36 unknowns ().

面积住在面上，而且它就是设计变量。13 个面各有一个 Af，单元的体积取两侧面积的平均乘 Δx。第 10 节要求的 dL/dA 就是对这 13 个数的导数——M=13。二维那一对页面把网格节点坐标当设计变量，几何含义要绕一圈才说得清；这里不用绕。

The areas live on the faces, and they are the design variables. Each of the 13 faces carries one Af, and a cell's volume is the average of its two face areas times Δx. The dL/dA of Section 10 is the derivative with respect to those 13 numbers — M=13. The two-dimensional pair used mesh node coordinates, where the geometric meaning takes a paragraph to explain. Here it takes none.

M<1 everywhere — a regime chosen deliberately, because it keeps every function smooth and lets the adjoint be exact.

Section 2

要离散的方程与面上的通量

The equations to discretise, and the flux on a face

守恒形式，面积写进守恒量里：

In conservative form, with the area carried inside the conserved variable:

∂(AU)∂t+∂(AF)∂x=S, U=​ρρuρE, F=​ρuρu2+pρuH, S=​0pdAdx0

配理想气体状态方程 p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2)、H=E+p/ρ，取 γ=1.4、R=1。

closed by the ideal-gas relations p=(γ−1)ρ(E−12u2) and H=E+p/ρ, with γ=1.4 and R=1.

源项是这一对页面的第一件新东西。S 只进动量方程，而且它是个体积项：它不穿过任何面，是在单元内部产生的。二维标量那一对页面（弱加边界时）的残差完全由循环拼成，这里循环之外还要再走一趟。它的转置同样落在单元自己身上、不经过面；漏掉它，点积测试就不再成立——实测相对误差从 10−16 量级升到 10−4 到 10−1，随所取的向量而定。

The source term is the first new thing on these pages. S enters only the momentum equation, and it is a volume term: it crosses no face, it is produced inside the cell. On the two-dimensional scalar pages (with weak boundary conditions) the residual was assembled entirely by the loop; here one more pass is needed after it. Its transpose likewise lands on the cell itself and passes through no face; drop it and the dot-product test fails — measured, the relative error rises from order 10−16 to between 10−4 and 10−1, depending on the vectors drawn.

面上的数值通量

The numerical flux on a face

面上取 Rusanov 通量：中心平均减一个正比于状态跳跃的耗散项，

Each face takes the Rusanov flux: a central average minus a dissipation proportional to the jump in state,

hf=Af[12(FL+FR)−12λf(UR−UL)], λf=12[(|u|+a)L+(|u|+a)R]

为什么 λ 取两侧平均而不是取大者。教科书里的 Rusanov 用 max。max 在两侧相等处不可微：哪一侧更大随状态切换，切换处导数跳变——那一点没有唯一的梯度，差分步长一旦跨过它，有限差分就与线性化对不上。取平均则只要 u≠0 就处处光滑（本算例 u 处处为正），而且在本算例的亚声速流场里耗散量级几乎相同：实测 max 最多只比平均值大 1.2%。真实代码里要用 max 时的做法是把它冻结在 u* 处，与二维页冻结远场入流/出流开关完全一样。

Why λ is the average of the two sides rather than the larger. Textbook Rusanov uses max. But max is not differentiable where the two sides cross: which side is larger switches with the state, and the derivative jumps there — that point has no single gradient, and a finite difference whose step straddles it disagrees with the linearisation. The average is smooth wherever u≠0 (and u>0 throughout this duct) and, in this subsonic flow, dissipates by almost the same amount: measured, the max is at most 1.2% larger than the average. When a real code does use max, the remedy is to freeze it at u*, exactly as the two-dimensional pages freeze the far-field inflow/outflow switch.

本页同样采用一阶空间离散：面上的左右状态直接取两侧单元的当前值，不做重构。代价只在精度上，不在这套代数上。

The discretisation here is likewise first order: the left and right states on a face are the current values of the two adjacent cells, with no reconstruction. The cost is accuracy only, not the algebra this page is about.

在里点流道中的任意一个面，看收敛解在它两侧的状态怎样组成通量：中心项、耗散项各是多少，λf 取平均与取 max 差多少。也可以拖动滑块，自己设定两侧状态。

In , click any face of the duct to see how the converged states on either side of it make up the flux: how large the central and dissipation terms are, and how far the average λf is from the max. The sliders set the two states to anything you like.

守恒守的是通量，不是单元值

What is conserved is the flux, not the cell value

稳态下质量方程没有源项，所以数值质量通量在 13 个面上必须完全相同——下面实测到机器精度。但如果去看单元中心的 ρuA，它沿程能差百分之几。两者的差就是耗散项 −12λ(UR−UL)Af：一阶格式在 12 个单元上就是这个量级。这不是不守恒，这是「单元值」和「通量」不是同一个东西。

At steady state the mass equation has no source, so the numerical mass flux must be identical on all 13 faces — measured below, to machine precision. Look instead at the cell-centre value of ρuA and it varies by a few percent along the duct. The difference between the two is the dissipation term −12λ(UR−UL)Af, and a first-order scheme on 12 cells produces exactly this much of it. That is not a loss of conservation; it is the fact that a cell value and a flux are different objects.

在里逐个面切换，能看到同一件事的另一面：质量和能量的 h 在全部 13 个面上相同到舍入误差（能量方程同样没有源项），中心项和耗散项却各自在变；动量的 h 逐面不同，相邻两个面之间的差额正好由源项 pΔA 补上。

Stepping from face to face in shows the other side of the same fact: the mass and energy components of h agree on all 13 faces to round-off (the energy equation has no source either), while the central and dissipation terms each change; the momentum component does change from face to face, and between two neighbouring faces the source pΔA makes up exactly the difference.

Section 3

边界条件

Boundary conditions

一维 Euler 的三条特征速度是 u−a、u、u+a。亚声速时 u<a，所以在左端：u 与 u+a 为正、向右进入区域，u−a 为负、离开区域。该给几个条件，由进入区域的特征条数决定：左端给 2 个，右端给 1 个（）。

The three characteristic speeds of the one-dimensional Euler equations are u−a, u and u+a. Subsonic means u<a, so at the left end u and u+a are positive and enter the domain while u−a is negative and leaves it. How many conditions to impose is decided by how many characteristics enter: two at the left, one at the right ().

边界Boundary 进入区域Entering 给定Prescribed 从内部外推Extrapolated
左（入口）Left (inflow) 2 条（u、u+a）2 (u, u+a) 总压 p0、总温 T0total pressure p0, total temperature T0 R−=u−2aγ−1
右（出口）Right (outflow) 1 条（u−a）1 (u−a) 背压 pbback pressure pb 熵 s 与 R+=u+2aγ−1entropy s and R+=u+2aγ−1

入口的解法：由内点状态算出 R−，再与 p0,T0 给出的总焓联立

At the inflow, R− is computed from the interior state and combined with the stagnation enthalpy that p0,T0 imply:

u−2aγ−1=R−,a2γ−1+u22=a02γ−1

这是关于 a 的一个二次方程。它的两个根都是正的：取求根公式里带「+」的那个（较大的根），它给出 u>0 的亚声速解，另一个根给出 u<0 的超声速倒流。由它得 u，再由等熵关系回到 p 与 ρ。出口则相反：p=pb 给定，熵与 R+ 从内部外推，解出 ρ 与 u。

That is a quadratic in a. Both of its roots are positive: take the one with the plus sign in the quadratic formula (the larger), which gives the subsonic solution with u>0 — the other gives supersonic reverse flow, u<0. Recover u from it and return to p and ρ through the isentropic relations. The outflow works the other way: p=pb is given while entropy and R+ come from inside, which fixes ρ and u.

边界 Jacobian 的秩 = 离开区域的特征条数

The rank of the boundary Jacobian equals the number of outgoing characteristics

关键在于：边界状态 B 是内点状态的函数，所以 ∂B/∂u 是一个 3×3 块，第 5 节起它会一路进入 Jacobian、前向与伴随。而这个块有一个漂亮的结构——

The point is that the boundary state B is a function of the interior state, so ∂B/∂u is a 3×3 block that propagates into the Jacobian, the tangent and the adjoint from Section 5 on. And that block has a rather elegant structure —

入口的边界状态只通过一个标量 R− 依赖内点，所以 ∂B/∂u 是两个向量的外积，秩为 1。出口通过两个标量（熵与 R+）依赖内点，秩为 2。而 1 和 2 正是各自边界上离开区域的特征条数——内点能送出去的信息，一条特征一份。

The inflow state depends on the interior through the single scalar R−, so ∂B/∂u is an outer product of two vectors and has rank 1. The outflow depends on two scalars (entropy and R+) and has rank 2. Those counts, 1 and 2, are exactly the number of characteristics leaving the domain at each end — one piece of information from inside per outgoing characteristic.

第 4 节
Section 4

残差 = 面循环 + 源项

The residual = the face loop, plus the source

残差就是一趟面循环加一趟源项（逐句播放这两趟）。面循环和二维那一对页面一模一样——收集两侧状态、算一次一维通量、按 +,− 分发给两个单元——唯一的差别是「一个数」换成了「三个数」。

The residual is one face loop followed by one source pass ( plays both, statement by statement). The face loop is exactly the one from the two-dimensional pair — gather the two states, evaluate one one-dimensional flux, scatter it to the two cells with + and − — the only difference being that "a number" has become "three numbers".

源项那一趟是新的。它不属于任何面：pdA/dx 在单元内部产生，只进动量方程。把它放在面循环之外单独走一遍，是因为它本来就不是面上的东西——这一点在第 8 节做转置时会变得很重要。

The source pass is new. It belongs to no face: pdA/dx is produced inside the cell and enters only the momentum equation. It is a separate pass precisely because it is not a face quantity — which will matter in Section 8, when everything gets transposed.

边界面上并没有「第二个单元」。f=0 与 f=N 各自只有一侧是真实单元，另一侧的状态由第 3 节的边界条件算出来。所以这两个面只往一个单元里分发；而由于外侧状态是内点状态的函数，它们对 Jacobian 的贡献会把 ∂B/∂u 也带进来——下一节就会看到。

A boundary face has no "second cell". At f=0 and f=N only one side is a real cell; the other state is computed from the boundary conditions of Section 3. Those two faces therefore scatter into one cell only — and because the outer state is a function of the interior one, their contribution to the Jacobian drags ∂B/∂u along with it, as the next section shows.

求解：Jacobian 与 Newton法

The solve: the Jacobian and Newton

第 4 节的 residual(u, A) 就是离散残差 R(u)。它的 Jacobian A=∂R/∂u 是 36×36 的，但真正的结构是 12×12 个 3×3 块：一个单元只与它的两个面邻居相连，所以 A 是块三对角的。

The residual(u, A) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u). Its Jacobian A=∂R/∂u is 36×36, but its real structure is 12×12 blocks of 3×3: a cell reaches only its two face neighbours, so A is block tridiagonal.

每个面贡献两个块，来自对一维通量求导：

Each face contributes two blocks, obtained by differentiating the one-dimensional flux:

cL=∂h∂UL =Af[12F'(UL)+12λfI −12(UR−UL)∂λf∂UL], F'=∂F∂U

F' 就是经典的 Euler 通量 Jacobian（记作 F'，免得与面积向量 A 混淆）。第三项常被忘记：耗散系数 λf 自己也依赖两侧状态，漏掉它 Jacobian 就不精确，Newton 会退化成拟 Newton。

F' is the classical Euler flux Jacobian (written F' so as not to be confused with the vector of areas A). The third term is the one that gets forgotten: the dissipation coefficient λf itself depends on both states, and dropping it makes the Jacobian inexact, degrading Newton to a quasi-Newton method.

边界面把 ∂B/∂u 带了进来。f=0 的外侧状态是 B(u0)，所以那个面对 A 的贡献是 cR+cL∂B/∂u——两个槽位合并成一个块，落在贴边单元自己的对角块上。出口面 f=N 同理，只是外侧在右边，块是 cL+cR∂B/∂u。第 3 节量出的秩 1 / 秩 2 结构，就这样进入了矩阵（）。

The boundary faces drag ∂B/∂u in. The outer state at f=0 is B(u0), so that face contributes cR+cL∂B/∂u — the two slots merge into one block sitting on the boundary cell's own diagonal block. The outflow face f=N is the same with the outer state on the right, giving cL+cR∂B/∂u. The rank-1 and rank-2 structure measured in Section 3 enters the matrix right here ().

怎么把 R(u)=0 解出来

How R(u)=0 is actually solved

Newton 迭代：在当前状态处线性化，解一个 36×36 的线性系统，更新状态。代码在前 5 轮用了欠松弛 ω=0.6。这个算例其实不需要它：实测从同一个均匀初值直接走全步长，4 次更新就收敛，途中最低压力 0.833，离负压很远。保留它有两个原因：下面的演示（）正好能把 Newton 的两种收敛并排看清——欠松弛时是线性的，残差每轮约乘 1−ω；全步长时是二次的；另外，页面上所有实测常数都是沿这条迭代路径得到的，u* 的末位比特随路径而变。

Newton iteration: linearise at the current state, solve one 36×36 linear system, update. The code damps the first 5 sweeps with ω=0.6. This case does not actually need it: measured, full steps from the same uniform guess converge after 4 updates, the lowest pressure along the way being 0.833, nowhere near negative. It is kept for two reasons. The demo below () then shows both kinds of Newton convergence side by side — linear while damped, the residual shrinking by about 1−ω per sweep, and quadratic with full steps. And every constant measured on this page was produced along this iteration path; the last bits of u* depend on the path.

这一步显式地把 A 造了出来。本页其余部分讲的是怎样不形成矩阵地算 Av 与 ATw；primal 求解本身用的是最笨也最稳的办法，因为它唯一的任务是造出随后被冻结的 u*。

This step does form A explicitly. The rest of the page is about computing Av and ATw without a matrix; the primal solve itself uses the dullest, most robust method there is, because its only job is to produce the u* that is then frozen.

The objective function

Both depend on the areas explicitly: the inverse-design objective through the cell volume Vi, the thrust directly through Ai+1−Ai. So in both cases dL/dA has a term that never passes through the state at all — the structure Section 10 takes apart.

反设计的目标压力取自一个已知的流道。把 A(x) 乘上 1−0.04sin(πx)，重新收敛，把得到的压力当作 ptgt。于是这个优化问题的最优解是已知的：在那条面积分布上 L 与 dL/dA 都应当为零。第 11 节会把这件事量出来——不是「很小」，是逐比特为零。

The target pressures come from a known duct. Multiply A(x) by 1−0.04sin(πx), re-converge, and take the resulting pressures as ptgt. The optimum of this design problem is therefore known in advance: on that area distribution both L and dL/dA must vanish. Section 11 measures exactly that — not "small", but zero to the last bit.

matrix-free 前向：计算 Av

Matrix-free forward mode: computing Av

设计变量 A∈ℝ13 是各面的截面积。对 R(u(A),A)=0 用链式法则消去 du/dA 有两条路；两条路都要用到同一个乘积，先把它说清楚：给定一个状态扰动 v，怎样算出 Av 而不形成 A。

The design variables A∈ℝ13 are the face areas. Applying the chain rule to R(u(A),A)=0 gives two routes to eliminating du/dA, and both need the same product. So first: given a state perturbation v, how to compute Av without forming A.

答案是把同一个面循环逐句求导。每个面上 hd=cLvL+cRvR，两个 3×3 块各乘一个 3 向量，再照原样 +,− 分发；源项那一趟同样线性化。整个函数从头到尾没有出现过矩阵（）。

The answer is to differentiate the same face loop statement by statement. On each face hd=cLvL+cRvR — two 3×3 blocks, each times a 3-vector — scattered with the same + and −; the source pass is linearised likewise. No matrix appears anywhere in the function ().

边界面上两个槽位合并了。外侧扰动不是独立的，它是 (∂B/∂u)v。所以边界面只剩一个有效的 3×3 块：入口面是 cR+cL∂B/∂u，出口面是 cL+cR∂B/∂u——和第 5 节矩阵里看到的是同一个块。

On a boundary face the two slots merge. The outer perturbation is not independent: it is (∂B/∂u)v. One effective 3×3 block is left: cR+cL∂B/∂u at the inflow face, cL+cR∂B/∂u at the outflow face — the very blocks seen in the matrix of Section 5.

第 8 节
Section 8

matrix-free 伴随：计算 ATw

Matrix-free adjoint mode: computing ATw

伴随就是把前向逐句转置。规则只有两条：原来写某个数组的地方，现在读它；原来读的地方，现在写。于是数据流整个反过来——输入是残差一侧的权重 w，输出是状态一侧的 y=ATw。

The adjoint is the tangent transposed statement by statement. Two rules suffice: wherever the forward code wrote an array, the reverse code reads it; wherever it read, the reverse writes. The data flow turns around: the input is a weight w on the residual side, the output y=ATw on the state side.

面上的一维通量在前向是「两个输入、一个输出」：(vL,vR)↦hd。它的转置是「一个输入、两个输出」：给定面上的权重 hb，返回 (cLThb,cRThb)。标量页里这是两个数各乘一遍，这里是两个 3×3 块各转置一次（）。

In the forward direction the one-dimensional flux takes "two inputs, one output": (vL,vR)↦hd. Its transpose takes "one input, two outputs": given a weight hb on the face, return (cLThb,cRThb). On the scalar pages that was two numbers; here it is two 3×3 blocks, each transposed ().

源项的转置仍然不经过任何面。前向里它是 −ΔA(∂p/∂u)·vi，只写动量那一行；转置之后变成 −ΔA(∂p/∂u)Twimom，从动量那一行散布回三个分量，落在单元自己身上。这是这一对页面独有的一步，而且它极易漏掉——下面的测试（）正是为它准备的。

The transposed source still passes through no face. Forward it is −ΔA(∂p/∂u)·vi, writing the momentum row only; transposed it becomes −ΔA(∂p/∂u)Twimom, spreading from that one row back over all three components of the cell itself. It is the step unique to these pages, and it is easy to forget — which is what the test below () is for.

点积测试

The dot-product test

怎样确认 residual_b 写对了？利用恒等式 ⟨w,Av⟩=⟨ATw,v⟩：任取两个随机向量，分别调用前向与伴随，比较两个内积。这是精确恒等式，不含任何步长，所以它要么成立到机器精度，要么就是代码错了。

How do you confirm that residual_b is right? Use the identity ⟨w,Av⟩=⟨ATw,v⟩: draw two random vectors, call the forward and the adjoint, compare the two inner products. It is an exact identity with no step size in it, so it either holds to machine precision or the code is wrong.

There is no such thing as a transpose that is "roughly right on average": either it is exact everywhere, or the dot-product test finds it out at once.

求解伴随方程

Solving the adjoint equation

有了 ATw，伴随方程 ATΨ=−g 就可以解了，其中 g=(∂L/∂u)T 来自第 6 节选定的目标。Ψ 有 36 个分量——每个单元三个，对应质量、动量、能量三条方程。

With ATw in hand the adjoint equation ATΨ=−g can be solved, where g=(∂L/∂u)T comes from whichever objective Section 6 selected. Ψ has 36 components — three per cell, one for each of the mass, momentum and energy equations.

−Ψi 的含义是：在第 i 个单元的那条方程里加一个单位源项，目标 L 会变多少。所以伴随场读起来是一张「敏感度地图」（）。

The meaning of −Ψi is: how much the objective L would move if a unit source were added to that equation in that cell. The adjoint field therefore reads as a map of sensitivity ().

亚声速带来一件二维标量页看不到的事：信息双向传播。那两个页面上的模型方程是纯对流的，特征只朝一个方向走，伴随因此只向上游铺开。这里三条特征是 u−a<0<u<u+a：一条朝上游、两条朝下游。于是扰动会同时向两边传，伴随也就铺满整个流道——出口的背压能影响到入口附近的解，入口的总条件也能影响到出口。

Subsonic flow brings something the two-dimensional scalar pages cannot show: information travels both ways. Their model equation is pure advection, its characteristics all run one way, and the adjoint therefore spreads only upstream. Here the three characteristics are u−a<0<u<u+a: one upstream, two downstream. Disturbances propagate in both directions, and the adjoint fills the whole duct — the back pressure reaches back towards the inlet, and the inlet's total conditions reach forward to the exit.

为什么要近一千次？二维标量页的 Jacobi 每次迭代把残差缩到约 0.79，122 次就收敛；这里每次只缩到约 0.977。块 Jacobi 每次迭代只在相邻单元之间交换一次信息。把 D−1 乘上与上下游邻居相连的块，按特征方向看（实测，第 7 个单元）：Rusanov 对三条波只用一个耗散 λ=|u|+a，它恰好等于最快那条 u+a 的波速，所以这条波是纯迎风的，只连上游（强度 0.98，下游 0.00）；另外两条两头都连着：u 上游 0.64、下游 0.35，u−a 上游 0.30、下游 0.69。这正是本节开头说的双向传播：误差要在进出口之间来回走很多趟才消得掉。最慢的误差在相邻单元之间一大一小地交替，所以相邻两次迭代的残差比在 0.971 与 0.984 之间来回跳（的状态栏里能看到）——这是块三对角矩阵上 Jacobi 的典型表现。真实代码保留同样的两样东西，residual_b 与块对角 D，但把它们放进 Krylov 方法（例如 GMRES）里去加速。

Why nearly a thousand iterations? On the two-dimensional scalar pages each Jacobi iteration shrinks the residual to about 0.79 and 122 iterations suffice; here each one shrinks it only to about 0.977. Block Jacobi exchanges information between neighbouring cells once per iteration. Multiply D−1 into the blocks that tie a cell to its upstream and downstream neighbours and look along the characteristic directions (measured, cell 7): Rusanov gives all three waves one dissipation, λ=|u|+a, which is exactly the speed of the fastest wave u+a, so that wave is purely upwinded and tied only upstream (strength 0.98, downstream 0.00); the other two are tied both ways: u with 0.64 upstream and 0.35 downstream, u−a with 0.30 upstream and 0.69 downstream. This is the two-way propagation described at the start of this section: an error has to travel back and forth between inlet and outlet many times before it dies out. The slowest error alternates large and small from one cell to the next, so the ratio between successive residuals alternates between 0.971 and 0.984 (visible in the status line of ) — typical of Jacobi on a block-tridiagonal matrix. Production codes keep the same two ingredients, residual_b and the block diagonal D, and accelerate them inside a Krylov method such as GMRES.

几何导数：dR/dA 与 dL/dA

Geometric derivatives: dR/dA and dL/dA

到这里为止算的都是对状态的导数。真正的设计要的是对截面积的导数。把 R(u(A),A)=0 对 A 求导，消去 du/dA，得到

Everything so far has differentiated with respect to the state. Design needs the derivative with respect to the areas. Differentiating R(u(A),A)=0 and eliminating du/dA gives

dLdA=∂L∂A显式项 +ΨT∂R∂A,ATΨ=−g
dLdA=∂L∂Aexplicit +ΨT∂R∂A,ATΨ=−g

残差在两个地方见到面积：每个面的数值通量对 Af 是线性的，源项对两侧面积也是线性的。所以 ∂R/∂A 极其简单，而且两个方向都能解析写出来。

The residual sees the areas in just two places: every numerical flux is linear in its face area, and the source term is linear in the two areas bounding the cell. So ∂R/∂A is simple — and, unusually, analytic in both directions.

所以这里的几何点积测试是精确的。二维那一对页面上，几何方向的点积测试只能做到 10−11 到 10−10 量级（格心页 4.0e−11，格点页 1.0e−10），因为那里没有解析的前向几何算子，前向那一侧是用中心差分逐列装配的——被步长卡住了。这里两个方向都是解析的，于是这条测试和状态空间那条一样，直接落在机器精度上。之前的 10−11、10−10 是差分的锅，不是转置的锅，这一页可以把这件事说死（）。

That is why the geometric dot-product test here is exact. On the two-dimensional pair the geometric test could only reach 10−11 to 10−10 (4.0e−11 on the cell-centred page, 1.0e−10 on the node-centred one), because there was no analytic forward geometric operator: that side was assembled column by column from central differences, and the step size set the floor. Here both directions are analytic, so this test lands at machine precision like the state-space one. The earlier 10−11 and 10−10 were the differencing, not the transpose — and this page can say so with a measurement ().

两条路都能算出同一个 dL/dA：前向对每个设计变量解一次 Awk=−∂R/∂Ak，一共 13 次；伴随只解一次 ATΨ=−g，13 个分量一起出来。设计变量越多，差距越大——这就是伴随方法的全部理由。

Both routes give the same dL/dA: the forward one solves Awk=−∂R/∂Ak once per design variable, 13 times in all; the adjoint solves ATΨ=−g once and gets all 13 components together. The more design variables, the wider the gap — which is the entire reason the adjoint method exists.

第 11 节
Section 11

验证：dL/dA 对有限差分

Verification: dL/dA against finite differences

最严格的检验是拿 dL/dA 去对全链路中心差分：每扰动一个 Ak，都重新收敛一次 Newton，再算目标。这样比的是全导数——状态对几何的响应也算在内——而不是冻结状态的偏导数。

The strict check is dL/dA against full-chain central differences: perturb one Ak, re-converge Newton, recompute the objective. What is compared is then the total derivative, with the state's response to the geometry included, rather than a partial derivative at frozen state.

还有一个更强的检验：已知的最优点。第 6 节里目标压力取自一条已知的流道。在那条流道上，L 应当为零，梯度也应当为零——而且不是「很小」，是逐比特为零，因为同一个确定性的 Newton 会复现出同一个状态。上表最后两行就是它。一个梯度能在一个独立构造的最优点上归零，比任何差分对照都更有说服力。

And a stronger check still: a known optimum. In Section 6 the target pressures were taken from a known duct. On that duct both L and the gradient must vanish — and not merely be small, but be zero to the last bit, because the same deterministic Newton reproduces the same state. Those are the last two rows above. A gradient that vanishes at an independently constructed optimum is better evidence than any finite-difference comparison.

差分校验本身是会骗人的。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ϵ/h)，随 h 变小反而增大。h 取得太大或太小，一个正确的梯度都会看起来像是错的。这正是点积测试更可靠的原因——它是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。上面几何方向那条测试就是明证：它落在 10−16，而第 11 节拿差分对照整条链路，只能到 10−9（反设计）与 10−10（推力）。

Finite-difference checking is itself capable of lying. A central difference has two error contributions: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks with h; and round-off, about O(ϵ/h), which grows as h shrinks. Too large or too small a step makes a correct gradient look wrong. That is exactly why the dot-product test is the more reliable instrument — it is an exact identity with no step size in it. The geometric test above proves the point: it lands at 10−16, while the full-chain differences of Section 11 reach only 10−9 (inverse design) and 10−10 (thrust).

第 12 节
Section 12

总结

Summary

整条链路，从头到尾：

The whole chain, end to end:

  • 一维通量是核心：残差就是「对每个面收集两侧状态、调用一次一维通量、按 +,− 分发」，再加一趟不经过任何面的源项。
  • 前向与伴随是同一个循环：前向把每一步换成它的导数（两个 3×3 块），伴随把每一步反过来（同样两个块，转置）。谁都不形成矩阵。
  • 边界条件是内点状态的函数，它的 Jacobian 秩等于离开区域的特征条数：入口 1、出口 2。
  • 点积测试是精确恒等式，不含步长：「漏掉源项」这类错误，它能直接定位到出错的那个算子；全链路差分只能告诉你梯度不对，还得先把步长误差排除掉。
  • 几何导数同样是一趟循环：∂R/∂A 两个方向都解析，所以几何点积测试也能到机器精度。
  • The one-dimensional flux is the whole story: the residual is "gather both states on each face, call the flux once, scatter with + and −", plus one source pass that touches no face.
  • Forward and adjoint are the same loop: the tangent replaces each step by its derivative (two 3×3 blocks), the adjoint runs each step backwards (the same two blocks, transposed). Neither forms a matrix.
  • A boundary condition is a function of the interior state, and the rank of its Jacobian equals the number of characteristics leaving the domain: 1 at the inflow, 2 at the outflow.
  • The dot-product test is an exact identity with no step size: a mistake such as a dropped source term is pinned to the very operator at fault, whereas a full-chain difference can only say the gradient is wrong, after its own step error has been ruled out.
  • The geometric derivative is one more loop: ∂R/∂A is analytic in both directions, so its dot-product test also reaches machine precision.
