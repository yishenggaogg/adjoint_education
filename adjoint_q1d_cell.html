第 1 节Section 1

网格：一个位置对应三个守恒量

Mesh: three conserved variables at each location

先从 Burgers 的一个未知量走向 Euler 的三个未知量。把流道长度无量纲化为 1，在 N 个等长区间上给出截面积 A。这里的“拟一维”并非忽略横截面：截面积同时乘在面通量上，并通过壁面压力源项进入动量方程。数组下标从 0 开始，状态排列为 [ρ₀,m₀,e₀,ρ₁,m₁,e₁,…]。

Move from one Burgers unknown to three Euler unknowns. Scale duct length to one and specify area A at the endpoints of N equal intervals. Quasi-one-dimensional flow retains cross-section effects through both area-weighted flux and the wall-pressure momentum source. Arrays are zero-based; the state ordering is [ρ₀,m₀,e₀,ρ₁,m₁,e₁,…].

第 3 节Section 3

边界条件：给定哪些量，哪些量由内部决定

Boundary conditions: prescribed and outgoing information

亚声速正向流动有三条特征速度 u−c、u、u+c，即通量 Jacobian ∂F/∂U 的三个特征值（Burgers 只有一个 ∂f/∂u=u；附录 A.1 写出这个 3×3 矩阵）。左端两条进入计算域、右端一条进入计算域，所以给定入口总压 p₀、总温 T₀ 和出口静压 pbp_b。把入口全部三个守恒量再加出口压力一起钉死，会过度约束问题。

Positive subsonic flow has characteristic speeds u−c, u and u+c, the three eigenvalues of the flux Jacobian ∂F/∂U (Burgers has the single one ∂f/∂u=u; Appendix A.1 writes out this 3×3 matrix). Two characteristics enter at the left and one at the right. Prescribe inlet total pressure p₀, total temperature T₀ and outlet static pressure pbp_b. Prescribing all three inlet conservative values as well as exit pressure would overconstrain the problem.

T0=T(1+γ−12M2),p0=p(1+γ−12M2)γ/(γ−1),M=u/c.T_0=T\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right),\quad p_0=p\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right)^{\gamma/(\gamma-1)},\quad M=u/c.

格心页先重构边界内侧状态。入口从内部带出 r₋=u−2c/(γ−1)，用指定总压和总温解出边界状态；出口从内部带出熵 p/ρᵞ 和 r₊=u+2c/(γ−1)，再用 pbp_b 解出密度与速度。边界状态同内侧状态一起进入数值通量。

Reconstruct the cell-side boundary trace first. At the inlet, take outgoing r₋=u−2c/(γ−1) from the interior and solve the prescribed total-pressure/temperature relations. At the outlet, carry interior entropy p/ρᵞ and r₊=u+2c/(γ−1), then recover density and velocity using pbp_b. Boundary and interior states enter the numerical flux together.

At the inlet, T₀=c²/γ+u²/7 with u=r₋+5c gives a quadratic for the sound speed, whose positive root is taken; at the outlet, the interior entropy s and r₊ are kept, and pbp_b gives density, sound speed and velocity. 上式中的点表示前向（tangent）导数，横线表示反向（adjoint）累加权重，记号在第 7 节说明。边界也是计算图的一部分。固定设计参数不等于固定边界状态：即使 p₀、T₀、pbp_b 不变，内部扰动仍通过出域特征改变边界状态。

In the formula above a dot marks a forward (tangent) derivative and a bar a reverse (adjoint) accumulation weight; Section 7 introduces the notation. Boundaries belong to the computational graph. Fixed boundary parameters do not imply fixed boundary states: interior perturbations still propagate through outgoing characteristics.

第 4 节Section 4

残差：面循环与体积分

Residual: face loops and volume quadrature

先固定离散问题与符号

Fix the discrete problem and its notation

本页每个单元存储 3 个未知量。以下用 𝐔\mathbf U 表示把所有位置、所有分量按代码顺序排成的列向量。它是一个有限维向量，不是连续函数。物理方程中的空间导数只用于说明模型；求离散伴随时，求导对象是本页实际装配的有限维函数。

Each cell stores 3 unknowns. Below, 𝐔\mathbf U stacks all locations and components in code order. It is a finite-dimensional vector, not a continuous function. Spatial derivatives in the physical equations explain the model; discrete differentiation acts on the actual finite-dimensional functions assembled on this page.

𝐔∈ℝnu,b∈ℝna,R:ℝnu×ℝna→ℝnu,Q:ℝnu×ℝna→ℝ.\mathbf U\in\mathbb R^{n_u},\quad b\in\mathbb R^{n_a},\quad R:\mathbb R^{n_u}\times\mathbb R^{n_a}\to\mathbb R^{n_u},\quad Q:\mathbb R^{n_u}\times\mathbb R^{n_a}\to\mathbb R.

bb 是本页全部设计变量排成的向量：截面积 AkA_k、背压 pbp_b、入口总压 p0p_0 与总温 T0T_0。本页的 AA 是截面积，所以全局残差 Jacobian 记作 JJ，目标记作 QQ；局部通量 Jacobian ∂F/∂U\partial F/\partial U 又是另一个对象。每个位置的局部状态 UiU_i 是一个小列向量，全局的 𝐔\mathbf U 才是 Jacobian 的输入。

bb stacks all design variables of this page: the areas AkA_k, the back pressure pbp_b, the inlet total pressure p0p_0 and the total temperature T0T_0. On this page AA is the area, so the global residual Jacobian is JJ and the objective is QQ; a local flux Jacobian ∂F/∂U\partial F/\partial U is yet another object. A local state UiU_i is a small column vector; the global 𝐔\mathbf U is the input of the Jacobian.

J=∂R∂𝐔∈ℝnu×nu,Rb=∂R∂b∈ℝnu×na,QUT=(∂Q∂𝐔)T∈ℝnu,QbT=(∂Q∂b)T∈ℝna.J=\frac{\partial R}{\partial \mathbf U}\in\mathbb R^{n_u\times n_u},\quad R_b=\frac{\partial R}{\partial b}\in\mathbb R^{n_u\times n_a},\quad Q_U^T=\Big(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial \mathbf U}\Big)^{T}\in\mathbb R^{n_u},\quad Q_b^T=\Big(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial b}\Big)^{T}\in\mathbb R^{n_a}.

这里 QUTQ_U^T、QbTQ_b^T 都是列向量，它们的转置才是偏导数行。求导采用自动微分（AD）的记号：程序变量 xx 上加点的 x˙\dot x 是前向（tangent）模式沿给定种子方向的导数，加横线的 xˉ\bar x 是反向（adjoint）模式累加到 xx 上的权重，例如 𝐔˙\dot{\mathbf U} 与 𝐔ˉ\bar{\mathbf U}。两者都是对本页有限维代码求导；变分符号 δ\delta 只用于附录 A、B 的连续推导里连续函数的变分。

Both QUTQ_U^T and QbTQ_b^T are columns; their transposes are the derivative rows. Derivatives use the notation of automatic differentiation (AD): a dot x˙\dot x on a program variable xx is its forward (tangent) derivative along the chosen seed direction, and a bar xˉ\bar x is the weight accumulated into xx in reverse (adjoint) mode, for example 𝐔˙\dot{\mathbf U} and 𝐔ˉ\bar{\mathbf U}. Both differentiate the finite-dimensional code of this page; the variation symbol δ\delta is kept for variations of continuous functions in the continuous derivations of Appendices A and B.

Rᵢ 是积分残差，没有除以体积。内部面只算一次，向左控制体加 H、向右控制体减 H；随后从动量分量减去压力壁面源项。面积在各原始区间内为直线；每段采用两点 Gauss 积分。格点对偶体若跨越原始节点，必须先拆成两段，不能跨过 A′ 的跳变只做一次积分。

Rᵢ is an integrated residual, without volume division. Evaluate each internal face once, adding H to the left volume and subtracting it from the right. Subtract the wall-pressure source from momentum afterwards. Area is linear on each primal interval. Apply two-point Gauss quadrature on each piece; split node-centred dual volumes at primal nodes where A′ changes.

    UL, UR = reconstruct_and_apply_boundary(U, f)
    H = area[f] * numerical_flux(UL, UR)
    if L exists: R[L] += H
    if R exists: R[R] -= H

    S[i] = sum(wq * pressure(reconstruct(U,i,xq)) * area_derivative(xq))

第 5 节Section 5

Jacobian 与 GS：精确算子和低阶近似各做什么

Jacobian and GS: exact operator versus low-order approximation

把所有残差堆成向量，Newton 修正满足 J₂ ΔU=−R₂，J₂=∂R₂/∂U。这里用 J₂ 表示所选离散阶数的精确 Jacobian；一阶选择时它就是 J₁。二阶重构扩展了依赖范围。程序不存储 J₂，而沿标量计算图实施 Jv 和 Jᵀw。为解线性系统，单独建立一阶近似 P=J₁；P 的每个对角块是 3×3。

Stack the residuals into a vector. Newton corrections satisfy J₂ ΔU=−R₂, with J₂ denoting the exact Jacobian of the selected order (J₁ when first order is selected). Reconstruction broadens dependencies. The program applies Jv and Jᵀw through a scalar computational graph without storing J₂. A separate first-order approximation P=J₁ supplies 3×3 diagonal blocks for linear solves.

P=D+L+V,zi(k+1)=Pii−1(ri−∑j<iPijzj(k+1)−∑j>iPijzj(k)).P=D+L+V,\qquad z_i^{(k+1)}=P_{ii}^{-1}\left(r_i-\sum_{j<i}P_{ij}z_j^{(k+1)}-\sum_{j>i}P_{ij}z_j^{(k)}\right).

一次 GS 扫描使用已更新的左侧块和旧的右侧块。每次预处理从零开始做 12 次扫描，记所得线性映射为 B。外层固定点更新 x←x+0.7 B(b−J₂x)。多次内扫描使二阶缺陷校正在本页网格上稳定；不能把“一次扫描”等同于已解完 Pz=r。伴随使用整个 B 的转置，而不只是把某个局部 3×3 块转置。

A GS sweep uses updated earlier blocks and previous later blocks. Each preconditioner application starts from zero and performs 12 sweeps, defining a linear map B. Outer defect correction updates x←x+0.7 B(b−J₂x). Multiple inner sweeps stabilise this example’s second-order correction; one sweep is not an exact solution of Pz=r. The adjoint uses the transpose of the entire B map, not merely transposed diagonal blocks.

z = 0
repeat 12 times:
    z += solve_lower_blocks(D+L, rhs-P*z)

The main derivation defines JTJ^T using ordinary array dot products. A weighted inner product changes the adjoint: with state and residual weights WUW_U and WRW_R it is WU−1JTWRW_U^{-1}J^TW_R. Fixed-point iteration and GS-preconditioned GMRES

两种方法解同一个精确系统 Jx=b。传统缺陷校正每步应用 B 后按固定系数更新，收敛要求谱半径 ρ(I−ωBJ)<1；这个条件不是“P 很像 J”就自动满足的。GMRES 则在 Krylov 子空间中选组合系数，减少真残差。这里使用右预处理：解 JB y=b，再取 x=B y。

GMRES chooses combinations in a Krylov subspace to reduce the residual. Right preconditioning solves JB y=b and recovers x=B y.

/* Continuous adjoint of the shock-fitted nozzle of Appendix B.5, with the
   internal shock conditions obtained by finishing the elimination of B.3.

   Convention of Appendix A: L = Q - int lambda^T R dx with (Q the objective,
   J the residual Jacobian on these pages), R = (AF)' - A' p e2, and
   [q] = q+ - q- across the shock at x = xi.
     interior:  -A B^T lambda' - A' lambda_2 p_U^T = j_U^T      (each side)
     shock:     [lambda] = 0,   A'(xi) [p] lambda_2(xi) = j- - j+
     outlet:    A B^T lambda(1) = mu_1 p_U(1)^T,   dQ/dp_b = -mu_1
   Objectives j = w(x) p with w = 1 (pressure integral) or w = A' (thrust).

   The flow is the isentropic / normal-shock solution of B.5 (same area,
   gamma = 1.4, inlet p0 = T0 = 1, R = 1). Downstream of the shock the
   linear adjoint ODE is integrated with RK4 in x from the outlet, as a
   particular solution plus mu_1 times a homogeneous one, and the shock
   condition fixes mu_1. Upstream of the shock, in the supersonic branch,
   continuity alone starts the integration; it runs in s = log(x - 1/2)
   because the pressure-integral adjoint has a logarithmic singularity at
   the sonic throat. Area-bump gradients are integrated alongside:
     dQ = int (j_A + lambda'^T F) dA + (j_A' + lambda_2 p) dA' dx. */
(function (root) {
  'use strict';
  const G = 1.4;
  const area = x => 1 + 2.2 * (x - 0.5) ** 2, areaX = x => 4.4 * (x - 0.5);
  const ratio = M => ((5 / 6) * (1 + 0.2 * M * M)) ** 3 / M;

  function mach(r, sup) {
    if (!(r >= 1 - 1e-14)) throw Error('Area below the sonic area');
    if (Math.abs(r - 1) < 1e-15) return 1;
    let lo = sup ? 1 : 1e-7, hi = sup ? 20 : 1;
    for (let k = 0; k < 60; k++) {
      const m = (lo + hi) / 2;
      if ((ratio(m) < r) === sup) lo = m; else hi = m;
    }
    let M = (lo + hi) / 2;
    for (let k = 0; k < 4; k++) {
      const f = ratio(M) - r, d = ratio(M) * (1.2 * M / (1 + 0.2 * M * M) - 1 / M);
      M -= f / d;
    }
    return M;
  }
  function state(M, p0) {
    const T = 1 / (1 + 0.2 * M * M), p = p0 * T ** 3.5, rho = p / T, u = M * Math.sqrt(G * T);
    return { M, p, rho, u, E: p / (G - 1) / rho + 0.5 * u * u };
  }
  function shock(xi) {
    const M1 = mach(area(xi), true), p1 = state(M1, 1).p;
    const M2 = Math.sqrt((1 + 0.2 * M1 * M1) / (G * M1 * M1 - 0.2));
    const p2 = p1 * (1 + 7 / 6 * (M1 * M1 - 1)), p02 = p2 * (1 + 0.2 * M2 * M2) ** 3.5;
    return { xi, M1, M2, p1, p2, p02 };          // downstream sonic area A*,+ = 1 / p02
  }
  const outletPressure = xi => { const s = shock(xi); return state(mach(area(1) * s.p02, false), s.p02).p; };
  function position(pb) {
    let a = 0.52, b = 0.98;
    const fa = outletPressure(a) - pb, fb = outletPressure(b) - pb;
    if (!(fa * fb < 0)) throw Error('Back pressure is outside the interior-shock bracket');
    let flo = fa;
    for (let k = 0; k < 200 && b - a > 1e-16; k++) {
      const m = (a + b) / 2, fm = outletPressure(m) - pb;
      if (fm * flo > 0) { a = m; flo = fm; } else b = m;
    }
    return (a + b) / 2;
  }
  /* side is explicit: at x = xi the two branches meet, and a rounded x must
     not pick the wrong one */
  const flow = (x, s, side) => side === 'up' ? state(mach(area(x), x > 0.5), 1) : state(mach(area(x) * s.p02, false), s.p02);

  function solve3(A, b) {
    const a = A.map((r, i) => [...r, b[i]]);
    for (let k = 0; k < 3; k++) {
      let p = k;
      for (let i = k + 1; i < 3; i++) if (Math.abs(a[i][k]) > Math.abs(a[p][k])) p = i;
      [a[p], a[k]] = [a[k], a[p]];
      for (let i = k + 1; i < 3; i++) { const f = a[i][k] / a[k][k]; for (let j = k; j < 4; j++) a[i][j] -= f * a[k][j]; }
    }
    const x = [0, 0, 0];
    for (let i = 2; i >= 0; i--) { let t = a[i][3]; for (let j = i + 1; j < 3; j++) t -= a[i][j] * x[j]; x[i] = t / a[i][i]; }
    return x;
  }
  /* A B^T and p_U^T at a state (B = F_U as in Appendix A.1, E = e / rho) */
  function ABt(q, A) {
    const { u, E } = q;
    return [[0, -(3 - G) / 2 * u * u, (G - 1) * u ** 3 - G * u * E],
            [1, (3 - G) * u, G * E - 1.5 * (G - 1) * u * u],
            [0, G - 1, G * u]].map(r => r.map(v => A * v));
  }
  const pU = q => [(G - 1) / 2 * q.u * q.u, -(G - 1) * q.u, G - 1];
  const fluxF = q => [q.rho * q.u, q.rho * q.u * q.u + q.p, (q.rho * q.E + q.p) * q.u];
  const weight = (kind, x) => kind === 'pressure' ? 1 : areaX(x);

  /* lambda' for the particular (with source) or homogeneous part */
  function slope(x, lam, s, side, kind, withSource) {
    const q = flow(x, s, side), P = pU(q), c = (withSource ? weight(kind, x) : 0) + areaX(x) * lam[1];
    return solve3(ABt(q, area(x)), P.map(v => -c * v));
  }
  /* a C3 bump: cos^4 has three continuous derivatives at its edges, so the
     RK4 quadrature of the gradient integrals keeps its order */
  function bump(c, w) {
    return {
      c, w,
      f: x => Math.abs(x - c) < w ? Math.cos(Math.PI * (x - c) / (2 * w)) ** 4 : 0,
      df: x => Math.abs(x - c) < w ? -4 * Math.cos(Math.PI * (x - c) / (2 * w)) ** 3 * Math.sin(Math.PI * (x - c) / (2 * w)) * Math.PI / (2 * w) : 0
    };
  }
  /* integrand of the area-bump gradient for the particular or homogeneous part */
  function bumpDensity(x, lam, s, side, kind, b, withSource) {
    const q = flow(x, s, side), dl = slope(x, lam, s, side, kind, withSource), F = fluxF(q);
    const jAp = withSource && kind === 'thrust' ? q.p : 0;
    return (dl[0] * F[0] + dl[1] * F[1] + dl[2] * F[2]) * b.f(x) + (jAp + lam[1] * q.p) * b.df(x);
  }

  /* RK4 for the pair (z, y) plus the bump integrals, in a variable t with
     x = X(t) and dx/dt = Xt(t) */
  function march(t0, t1, n, X, Xt, z0, y0, s, side, kind, bumps, keep) {
    const h = (t1 - t0) / n, out = [];
    let z = z0.slice(), y = y0.slice(), Iz = bumps.map(() => 0), Iy = bumps.map(() => 0);
    const deriv = (t, z, y) => {
      const x = X(t), j = Xt(t);
      return {
        z: slope(x, z, s, side, kind, true).map(v => v * j),
        y: slope(x, y, s, side, kind, false).map(v => v * j),
        Iz: bumps.map(b => bumpDensity(x, z, s, side, kind, b, true) * j),
        Iy: bumps.map(b => bumpDensity(x, y, s, side, kind, b, false) * j)
      };
    };
    const axpy = (a, v, k) => a.map((x, i) => x + k * v[i]);
    if (keep) out.push([X(t0), z.slice(), y.slice()]);
    for (let k = 0; k < n; k++) {
      const t = t0 + k * h;
      const k1 = deriv(t, z, y);
      const k2 = deriv(t + h / 2, axpy(z, k1.z, h / 2), axpy(y, k1.y, h / 2));
      const k3 = deriv(t + h / 2, axpy(z, k2.z, h / 2), axpy(y, k2.y, h / 2));
      const k4 = deriv(t + h, axpy(z, k3.z, h), axpy(y, k3.y, h));
      const comb = (a, key) => a.map((v, i) => v + h / 6 * (k1[key][i] + 2 * k2[key][i] + 2 * k3[key][i] + k4[key][i]));
      z = comb(z, 'z'); y = comb(y, 'y'); Iz = comb(Iz, 'Iz'); Iy = comb(Iy, 'Iy');
      if (keep) out.push([X(t + h), z.slice(), y.slice()]);
    }
    return { z, y, Iz, Iy, out };
  }

  function field(pb, kind = 'pressure', { steps = 4000, throat = 1e-4, bumps = [] } = {}) {
    if (!['pressure', 'thrust'].includes(kind)) throw Error('Choose pressure or thrust');
    const xi = position(pb), s = shock(xi), bs = bumps.map(([c, w]) => bump(c, w));
    const q1 = flow(1, s, 'down');
    const y1 = solve3(ABt(q1, area(1)), pU(q1));                     // lambda(1) per unit mu_1
    const id = t => t, one = () => 1;
    const down = march(1, xi, steps, id, one, [0, 0, 0], y1, s, 'down', kind, bs, true);

Appendix C: direct adjoint solves for small systems

本附录的一阶、二阶均不采用 matrix-free 求解：先显式组装并存储完整精确 Jacobian，再对其转置作带主元 LU 分解和三角求解。用精确导数取得矩阵列属于组装步骤；matrix-free 反向算子只在直接解完成后用于独立残差验证。

For both first and second order, this appendix does not use a matrix-free solve: explicitly assemble and store the complete exact Jacobian, then factor its transpose with pivoted LU and solve triangular systems. Derivative products used to obtain columns belong to assembly; the matrix-free reverse operator is used only afterwards for independent residual verification.

C.1 小系统为什么值得直接求解

C.1 Why use a direct method for a small system

默认 12 区间时，格心有 36 个状态未知量，格点有 39 个。这个规模下，可以把精确 Jacobian 全部写出来，采用带部分主元选取的 LU 分解直接解伴随方程。它避免许多次 GS 或 Krylov 算子调用，通常是这种教学小系统中很快的选择；是否最快由下方同条件实测决定，不能只比较迭代次数。

With the default 12 intervals there are 36 cell-centred or 39 node-centred state unknowns. At this size we can assemble the exact Jacobian and solve the adjoint with partial-pivot LU. This avoids many GS or Krylov operator applications and is often a fast choice for a small teaching system. The benchmark below determines which is fastest under the same conditions; iteration counts alone cannot establish that.

C.2 主元、消元、前代与回代

C.2 Pivoting, elimination and triangular solves

在第 k 列，从尚未消元的行中选绝对值最大的主元，把整行交换到第 k 行；右端项也必须按照相同置换重排。用主元消去下面的元素，把乘数保存在严格下三角，更新后的上三角保存 U。L 的对角线默认为 1。完整行交换也交换已经保存的 L 乘数，否则分解关系就会被破坏。

In column k, select the largest-magnitude remaining entry and swap its entire row into position k. Apply the same permutation to the right-hand side. Eliminate entries below the pivot, storing multipliers in the strict lower triangle and the updated U in the upper triangle. L has unit diagonal. Full row swaps also swap previously stored L multipliers, preserving the factorisation.

Do not form a matrix inverse. Once LU is factored, another objective needs a new right-hand side and two triangular solves.
