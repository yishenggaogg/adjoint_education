The discrete adjoint of a two-dimensional scalar equation, on an unstructured node-centred scheme

网格：混合网格与对偶控制体的构造

Mesh: a hybrid grid and the construction of dual control volumes

算例网格

The test mesh

算例是一张绕物体的环形 O 型网格，3 圈节点、每圈 8 个，共 24 个节点。贴着物面的一层用四边形（8 个），外面一层用三角形（16 个）——这正是实际 CFD 常用的混合网格：近壁面用规则的四边形层，外场用三角形。物体本身是非对称的：它有弯度，而且相对远场圆心有偏置，所以流场、阻力和形状梯度都不带任何对称性。两圈边界的意义完全不同：

The test case is an annular O-type mesh around a body: 3 rings of nodes, 8 to a ring, 24 nodes in all. The layer against the body uses quadrilaterals (8 of them) and the layer outside it triangles (16) — precisely the hybrid mesh that real CFD uses: regular quadrilateral layers near the wall, triangles in the far field. The body itself is asymmetric: it has camber and is offset from the centre of the far-field circle, so the flow, the drag and the shape gradient carry no symmetry whatever. The two rings of boundary mean quite different things:

  • 内圈（8 条边）：无粘壁面，也就是物面（本页默认；若为无滑移粘性壁面，则改为强加 u=uw，见第 3 节）；
  • Inner ring (8 edges): inviscid wall — the body surface (the default on this page; for a no-slip viscous wall it becomes a strongly imposed u=uw, see Section 3);
  • 外圈（8 条边）：远场特征边界。
  • Outer ring (8 edges): far-field characteristic boundary.

有限体积法需要一组不重叠、铺满全域的控制体。把未知量放在单元里，控制体就是单元本身（格心格式）；也可以把未知量放在节点上，这时控制体必须另外造出来，做法是围绕每个节点“切”出一块，这就是中位对偶控制体（）。本页采用后者，所以 N=24。

A finite-volume method needs a set of control volumes that do not overlap and cover the whole domain. Put the unknowns in the cells and the control volume is the cell itself (the cell-centred scheme); put them on the nodes instead and the control volumes have to be constructed, by "cutting" a piece out around every node — the median-dual control volume (). This page takes the latter, so N=24.

一步一步围出控制体

Building a control volume step by step

构造规则只有一条，而且对三角形和四边形完全一样：在每个单元内，把每条边的中点与单元中心相连。这些线段把单元分成若干块，分别归属它的各个顶点；把所有相邻单元里属于同一个节点的小块拼起来，就是这个节点的控制体。 可以逐步播放这个过程，也可以点击任意节点换一个看（试试壁面节点、远场节点和内部节点的区别）。

There is only one construction rule, and it is exactly the same for triangles and quadrilaterals: inside each cell, join the midpoint of every edge to the cell centre. Those segments cut the cell into pieces, each belonging to one of its vertices; collect the pieces belonging to the same node across all its neighbouring cells and you have that node’s control volume. plays the process step by step, and any node can be clicked to see another one (try the difference between a wall node, a far-field node and an interior node).

对偶面与它的法向

The dual face and its normal

网格的每一条边 (i,j) 对应一个对偶面：它由相邻单元各贡献的一段拼成——内部边有两个相邻单元，所以对偶面是折线 xc1→m→xc2；边界边只有一个单元，只贡献一段。面的法向由各段旋转 90° 后相加得到（）：

Every edge (i,j) of the mesh corresponds to one dual face, assembled from one segment contributed by each adjacent cell — an interior edge has two adjacent cells, so the dual face is the polyline xc1→m→xc2; a boundary edge has only one cell and contributes a single segment. The face normal is obtained by rotating each segment through 90° and adding them ():

nij=Σt∋(i,j)±(dy,−dx),d=xc−m,m=12(xi+xj),

符号取成让 nij 由节点 i 指向节点 j。这样一条边只需要存一个法向向量。|nij| 是这条折线的等效直线长度——等于折线两端之间的距离（内部边就是两个单元中心；边界边的对偶面只有一段，两端是边中点与那一个单元中心），而不是折线自身的长度（算例中折线最多长 13.4%）。通量只用到矢量 nij，所以边循环和格心格式的面循环形式完全一样。

The sign is chosen so that nij points from node i to node j. An edge then needs to store just one normal vector. |nij| is the equivalent straight-face length of that polyline — the distance between its two ends (for an interior edge, the two cell centres; the dual face of a boundary edge has a single segment, running from the edge midpoint to its one cell centre), not the polyline's own length, which runs up to 13.4% longer in this mesh. The flux only ever uses the vector nij, which is why the edge loop has exactly the same form as the face loop of a cell-centred scheme.

四边形的一个重要差别。中点—中心连线把三角形恰好分成面积相等的三块，所以三角形网格上 Vi=13Σ(相邻三角形面积)。但对一般四边形，四块面积并不相等（只有平行四边形才是四分之一）。所以控制体体积必须把各个子块的面积逐个加起来，不能套用固定分数——(b) 给出了算例中真实四边形的四块占比。

One important difference for quadrilaterals. The midpoint-to-centre lines cut a triangle into three pieces of exactly equal area, so on a triangular mesh Vi=13Σ(area of the adjacent triangles). For a general quadrilateral, though, the four pieces are not equal (only a parallelogram gives quarters). So the control-volume area has to be accumulated piece by piece and cannot be taken as a fixed fraction — (b) gives the four shares for a real quadrilateral from the test case.

边界节点：用半条边把控制体封口

Boundary nodes: sealing the control volume with half-edges

内部节点的控制体被相邻单元内的线段完整围住，边界节点却不是：它的控制体在区域边界上是“敞开”的，必须用节点到相邻边界边中点的两条半边补上（）。这两条半边就是边界半面，外法向取该边界边外法向的一半：

The control volume of an interior node is enclosed completely by the segments inside its neighbouring cells; a boundary node’s is not — it is left "open" along the domain boundary and has to be closed with the two half-edges running from the node to the midpoints of its adjacent boundary edges (). Those two half-edges are the boundary half-faces, and their outward normal is half the outward normal of the boundary edge:

nbnd=12(yb−ya,−(xb−xa)),

边界边 (a,b) 的两个端点各得一份。算例中内外圈各有 8 条边界边，共 16 条，所以有 32 个边界半面（16 个壁面 + 16 个远场）；加上 48 条边对应的 48 个内部对偶面，边循环一共要走 80 个面。

Each of the two endpoints of a boundary edge (a,b) receives one share. The test case has 8 boundary edges on the inner ring and 8 on the outer, 16 in all, giving 32 boundary half-faces (16 wall + 16 far-field); together with the 48 interior dual faces of the 48 edges, the edge loop walks 80 faces in total.

要离散的方程与对偶面上的一维通量

The equation to discretise, and the one-dimensional flux on a dual face

要离散的方程

The equation to discretise

为什么用标量方程，而不是 Euler / N–S。本页要讲的是离散伴随的结构：边循环怎么写、怎么对它逐项求导、怎么把它转置。真实的 Euler 方程在每个对偶面上的通量是 4 维（二维）或 5 维（三维）的向量，它的局部导数是 4×4 或 5×5 的块，再加上 Roe 的特征分解、限制器、N–S 的粘性项与湍流模型，∂h/∂uL 就从一个数变成一个矩阵——这些细节会把“收集—计算—分发”这条主线淹没。换成标量守恒律之后，两个局部导数 ∂h/∂uL、∂h/∂uR 各自只是一个数，整条链路才能逐句播放、逐项和差分对照。但骨架完全一样：边循环的写法、边界条件基本上只在一维通量内部起作用（强加的 Dirichlet 条件是唯一的例外，见第 3 节）、前向与伴随的转置关系，在真实求解器里逐字成立——把标量换成状态向量、把局部导数换成块矩阵即可。

Why a scalar equation rather than Euler / N–S. What this page is about is the structure of the discrete adjoint: how the edge loop is written, how it is differentiated term by term, how it is transposed. In the real Euler equations the flux on each dual face is a 4-vector (two-dimensional) or 5-vector (three-dimensional) whose local derivatives are 4×4 or 5×5 blocks, and with Roe’s characteristic decomposition, limiters, the N–S viscous terms and a turbulence model on top, ∂h/∂uL turns from a number into a matrix — details that would drown the "gather — compute — scatter" thread. With a scalar conservation law the two local derivatives ∂h/∂uL and ∂h/∂uR are each just one number, so the whole chain can be played statement by statement and checked term by term against differences. But the skeleton is identical: how the edge loop is written, the fact that boundary conditions act inside the one-dimensional flux — with the one exception of a strongly imposed Dirichlet condition, see Section 3 — and the transpose relation between forward and adjoint all hold verbatim in a real solver — replace the scalar by a state vector and the local derivatives by block matrices.

本页要离散的是二维标量守恒律：

The page discretises a two-dimensional scalar conservation law:

∂u∂t+∇·F(u)=0,F(u)=12u2β,β=(1,0.5).

通量 F 对 u 是非线性的（∂F/∂u=uβ），所以离散之后得到的 R(u) 同样非线性——这正是后面要用 Newton 迭代求解、并在收敛状态 u* 处线性化的原因。

The flux F is nonlinear in u (with ∂F/∂u=uβ), so the discrete R(u) is nonlinear too — which is exactly why it is solved below by Newton iteration and linearised at the converged state u*.

有限体积离散

Finite-volume discretisation

在节点 i 的对偶控制体上积分并用高斯公式，体积分变成对偶控制体边界上的通量之和：

Integrating over the dual control volume of node i and applying the divergence theorem turns the volume integral into a sum of fluxes over the boundary of that dual control volume:

Viduidt+Ri(u)=0,Ri=Σe∋i±He.

到这里，空间离散剩下的唯一问题就是：一个对偶面上的 He 怎么算。

At this point the only question left in the spatial discretisation is how to evaluate He on a single dual face.

为了方便说明，本页采用一阶空间离散。面上的左右状态直接取两侧节点当前的值，不做任何重构——也就是把解看成分片常数，因此空间精度是一阶的。真实求解器会在这一步加上二阶重构（MUSCL 或最小二乘梯度）再配一个限制器，那会带来两件事：一是模板从“两侧节点”扩大到邻居的邻居，面通量的局部导数不再只涉及两个节点；二是限制器通常不可微，实用做法是把它在 u* 处冻结——与第 3 节远场入流/出流开关的处理完全一样。但边循环、前向、伴随的结构一点不变：仍然是收集 → 算一维通量 → 分发，仍然靠转置得到伴随。一阶的代价只体现在精度上，不体现在本页要讲的那套代数上。

For ease of explanation this page uses a first-order spatial discretisation. The left and right states on a face are taken straight from the current values at the two nodes, with no reconstruction at all — the solution is treated as piecewise constant, so the spatial accuracy is first order. A real solver would add second-order reconstruction here (MUSCL or a least-squares gradient) together with a limiter, and that brings two things: the stencil grows from "the two nodes" to the neighbours’ neighbours, so the local derivatives of a face flux involve more than two nodes; and a limiter is usually not differentiable, the practical remedy being to freeze it at u* — exactly as the far-field inflow/outflow switch is treated in Section 3. But the structure of the edge loop, of the forward mode and of the adjoint does not change at all: still gather → evaluate the one-dimensional flux → scatter, still transpose to get the adjoint. First order costs accuracy only, and none of the algebra this page is about.

每个对偶面都是一个一维问题

Every dual face is a one-dimensional problem

通过一个面的通量只和法向分量 F·n 有关。沿法向看，面两侧就是一维问题：左边常数状态 uL，右边常数状态 uR（）。数值通量取左右平均 − 常数耗散系数 ×（右状态 − 左状态）：

The flux through a face depends only on the normal component F·n. Along the normal, the two sides of the face form a one-dimensional problem: a constant state uL on the left and a constant state uR on the right (). The numerical flux is taken as the average of the two sides − a constant dissipation coefficient × (right state − left state):

边界条件

Boundary conditions

内圈和外圈的物理意义完全不同，处理方式也不同。两者都只在上一节那个一维通量内部起作用：边界半面没有“右节点”，远场由边界条件给出右状态 uR，无粘壁面则直接令 h=0。把各个对偶面的通量拼成残差的边循环在第 4 节展开——它本身一行都不用改。

The inner and outer rings mean quite different things physically, and are treated differently. Both act only inside the one-dimensional flux of the previous section: a boundary half-face has no "right node", so at the far field the boundary condition supplies the right state uR, while the inviscid wall sets h=0 outright. The edge loop that assembles the fluxes of all the dual faces into the residual is unfolded in Section 4 — it needs no change at all.

内圈：无粘壁面

Inner ring: the inviscid wall

无粘壁面的物理条件是无穿透：流体不能穿过物面。对本页的标量模型，这意味着通过壁面的对流通量直接为零：

The physical condition at an inviscid wall is no penetration: fluid cannot cross the body surface. For the scalar model on this page that means the convective flux through the wall is simply zero:

hwall=0.

于是壁面半面不改变残差——它只负责把控制体封口。这一点在后面的演示里可以直接看到：播放到壁面半面时，h=0。

So a wall half-face does not change the residual — its only job is to seal the control volume. This is directly visible in the demos below: when the play reaches a wall half-face, h=0.

外圈：远场特征边界

Outer ring: the far-field characteristic boundary

远场要按特征方向决定用哪一侧的信息。标量守恒律 F(u)=12u2β 的波速是 uvn（vn=β·n，n 指向域外）。算例中 u>0，所以 sign(uvn)=sign(vn)，判据是纯几何的：

At the far field the characteristic direction decides which side supplies the information. The wave speed of the scalar law F(u)=12u2β is uvn (with vn=β·n and n pointing out of the domain). In the test case u>0, so sign(uvn)=sign(vn) and the test is purely geometric:

  • 入流（vn<0，特征从域外进来）：右状态取自由来流 uR=u∞=1.0；
  • Inflow (vn<0, the characteristic enters from outside): the right state is the free stream, uR=u∞=1.0;
  • 出流（vn≥0，特征从域内出去）：右状态由内部外推，uR=uL。
  • Outflow (vn≥0, the characteristic leaves the domain): the right state is extrapolated from inside, uR=uL.

算例的 8 条远场边里 4 条入流、4 条出流。开关在冻结状态 u* 处固定（frozen switch），前向与伴随都在这个固定的分支上线性化。

Of the 8 far-field edges in the test case, 4 are inflow and 4 outflow. The switch is fixed at the frozen state u* (a frozen switch), and both the forward and the adjoint mode linearise on that fixed branch.

这是把边界条件收在右状态与一维通量这一层的好处：边循环的骨架——收集、算通量、分发——与边界种类无关（代码里这些分支都在 flux 一侧，下面的清单把它们逐行摊开，只为能逐句播放）。

That is the benefit of keeping the boundary conditions at the level of the right state and the one-dimensional flux: the skeleton of the edge loop — gather, evaluate, scatter — does not depend on how many kinds of boundary there are (in the code these branches all sit on the flux side; the listing below spells them out line by line only so they can be played statement by statement).

强边界条件：物面无滑移

A strong boundary condition: no-slip on the body

上面两种边界都是弱的：条件写在一维通量内部，未知量本身照样参加求解。无滑移壁面不一样——它是直接加在状态上的 Dirichlet 条件（真实 N–S 里是速度矢量在物面为零，本页的标量模型写成 u=uw）。这类条件通常强加：把受约束自由度的方程整行换掉。它不替代上面的无粘壁面，而是“物面改成粘性壁”时的另一套处理。

Both boundaries above are weak: the condition lives inside the one-dimensional flux and the unknown itself still takes part in the solve. No-slip is different — it is a Dirichlet condition imposed directly on the state (in real N–S the velocity vector vanishes on the body; in the scalar model here it is written u=uw). Such conditions are usually imposed strongly: the equation of each constrained degree of freedom is replaced row and all. It does not replace the inviscid wall above; it is the alternative treatment when the body is made a viscous wall.

primal：换行。边循环照常跑完，然后把约束行整行覆盖：

primal: row replacement. The edge loop runs to completion as usual, and then the constrained rows are overwritten entirely:

Ri←ui−uw,i∈W.

这里第一次用到两个记号：W 是受约束的自由度集合（算例中是壁面节点 1–8），Abc 是行被替换之后的 Jacobian——它的第 i 行（i∈W）就是 eiT。后面几节沿用这两个记号。

Two symbols are used here for the first time: W is the set of constrained degrees of freedom (the wall nodes 1–8 in the test case), and Abc is the Jacobian after the rows have been replaced — its row i (i∈W) is exactly eiT. The later sections keep both symbols.

强加需要一个“住在物面上”的自由度。节点中心格式正好有，就是上面的 W。作为对照，格心格式没有这样的自由度：未知量是单元平均值，物面上不存在任何未知量，所以那里的无滑移只能弱加——在通量里给一个常数右状态 uR=uw，结构上和上面的入流分支完全一样（右状态是常数）。

Strong imposition needs a degree of freedom that "lives on the body". A node-centred scheme has one — the set W above. By contrast, a cell-centred scheme has no such degree of freedom: the unknowns are cell averages and there is no unknown on the body at all, so no-slip there can only be imposed weakly — a constant right state uR=uw in the flux, structurally identical to the inflow branch above (the right state is a constant).

残差 = 边循环：收集、计算、分发

The residual = the edge loop: gather, compute, scatter

残差计算对每条边做三件事：从两端节点收集状态，调用一维通量计算 h，再把 h 分发回两个节点——节点 i 加、节点 j 减。同一个 h 以相反符号进入两个控制体，这就是守恒。

Computing the residual does three things on every edge: gather the states from the two nodes, call the one-dimensional flux to compute h, and scatter h back to the two nodes — added at node i, subtracted at node j. The same h enters the two control volumes with opposite signs, and that is conservation.

反过来看一个节点：它的残差就是自己所有对偶面的通量之和，符号由它在每个面上是 i 还是 j 决定（）。内部节点的面数等于它的相邻节点数，随网格而变——这是节点中心格式与格心格式在数据结构上的主要差别。

Seen from a node instead: its residual is the sum of the fluxes of all its own dual faces, each signed by whether the node is i or j on that face (). An interior node has as many faces as it has neighbouring nodes, which varies across the mesh — the main data-structure difference between a node-centred and a cell-centred scheme.

强边界条件在 primal 里的位置。边循环本身不动：约束行照样被分发、照样累加。真正的替换发生在循环结束之后——把 Ri 整行改写成 ui−uw。所以代码结构是“循环 + 一次后处理”，而不是在循环里到处插 if；tangent 也是如此，adjoint 则是“循环前清零 + 循环 + 循环后加回”（第 8 节）。

Where a strong boundary condition sits in the primal. The edge loop itself does not change: constrained rows are scattered into and accumulated just like any other. The replacement happens after the loop finishes — rewriting the whole row Ri as ui−uw. So the code is "a loop plus one post-processing step", not ifs sprinkled through the loop; the tangent is the same, while the adjoint is "zero before the loop, the loop, add back after" (Section 8).

代价要说清楚：约束行不再是守恒陈述。替换之前，第 i 行说的是“该控制体上通量之和为零”；替换之后，它说的是 Dirichlet 条件 ui=uw。于是这几个控制体上不再有守恒——壁面按约束需要吞吐任意通量。物理上这是对的（粘性壁确实与流体交换动量），但要清楚放弃了什么：边循环仍然逐对偶面守恒，被替换掉的是那几行的方程。

The cost has to be stated plainly: a constrained row is no longer a conservation statement. Before the replacement, row i says "the fluxes over this control volume sum to zero"; after it, it states the Dirichlet condition ui=uw. So conservation no longer holds on those control volumes — the wall absorbs or emits whatever flux the constraint requires. Physically that is right (a viscous wall really does exchange momentum with the fluid), but it should be clear what has been given up: the edge loop is still conservative dual face by dual face; what was replaced is the equation on those rows.

演示里可以直接切换两种壁面处理。第 4、7、8 节的三个演示面板底部都有一对按钮：无粘（弱加 h=0） 与 有粘：无滑移（强加 u=uw）。伪代码始终是同一份：标着 if no-slip 的行（primal 与 tangent 是循环之后的一行，伴随是循环前清零、循环后加回两行）只在强加模式下执行，播放步数也随之变化——约束行的处理是循环之外的一步，这一点在逐句播放里看得最清楚。强加模式下矩阵视图会隐藏，因为那张图是按弱加的 A 画出来的。

求解：Jacobian 与 Newton法

The solve: the Jacobian and Newton

从残差到 Jacobian

From residual to Jacobian

The residual(u) of Section 4 is the discrete residual R(u)∈ℝN, with one component per node. Its Jacobian A=∂R/∂u is N×N (24×24 in the test case). This section forms it explicitly — with only 24 unknowns in the test case, Newton can solve with it directly; Sections 7 and 8 then show how to multiply by A without ever forming it.

A 的每一份贡献都来自一个对偶面：对第 2 节那个一维通量求局部导数，

Every contribution to A comes from one dual face: differentiate the one-dimensional flux of Section 2 locally,

cL=∂h∂uL=12vnuL+ε|n|,cR=∂h∂uR=12vnuR−ε|n|.

再把这两个数按和残差完全相同的符号累加到 A 的四个位置：ALL+=cL、ALR+=cR、ARL−=cL、ARR−=cR。边界半面只有 L 一侧，所以只落在 ALL 上。

and accumulate those two numbers into four entries of A with exactly the signs the residual used: ALL+=cL, ALR+=cR, ARL−=cL, ARR−=cR. A boundary half-face has only an L side, so it lands on ALL alone.

物面无滑移时，壁面节点本身就是未知量，按强加处理：整行换成 eiT，这一行不再由通量累加（第 8 节展开）。

For a no-slip body the wall node is itself an unknown, so the condition is imposed strongly: the whole row becomes eiT and is no longer accumulated from fluxes (unfolded in Section 8).

Why the derivatives "merge" on an outflow half-face. At outflow uR=uL, so the same uL enters both slots of the flux; the chain rule adds the two local derivatives and the dissipation terms cancel exactly:

怎么把 R(u)=0 解出来

How R(u)=0 is actually solved

上面只写了 residual(u)：它把状态映成残差，却没有说这个非线性方程组是怎么解开的。这里补上——顺带说清一件容易误会的事：primal 求解这一步并不是 matrix-free 的，而且是故意如此。

So far only residual(u) has been written: it maps a state to a residual, but says nothing about how the nonlinear system is solved. Here it is — along with something easy to misread: the primal solve is not matrix-free, and deliberately so.

A(uk)δu=R(uk),uk+1=uk−δu,A=∂R∂u.

The initial guess is the uniform free stream u0≡1, and the criterion is maxi|δui|<10−13 ().

这一步显式地把 A 造了出来。primal 求解用的是最笨也最稳的办法：逐条对偶面累加出整个 24×24 的 A，再做一次带部分主元的稠密 LU。算例只有 N=24，这样既便宜又不喧宾夺主——它的唯一任务是造出那个随后被冻结的 u*。真实规模下这里会换成 Krylov 加预处理，而那时每一步要的 Av 正是第 7 节的东西。

This step builds A explicitly. The primal solve uses the dullest and most robust method there is: accumulate the whole 24×24 A dual face by dual face, then take one dense LU with partial pivoting. With only N=24 in the test case that is both cheap and unobtrusive, and its sole task is to produce the u* that is then frozen. At realistic sizes this would become Krylov plus a preconditioner, and the Av needed at every step is exactly the subject of Section 7.

固定轮次，但会检查。代码固定跑 60 轮，而不是一收敛就退出：页面上每一个实测常数都是在这些轮次下量出来的，提前退出会让 u* 在末位比特上改变，从而把所有有限差分对照的数字整体挪动。真正要紧的是有没有检查——现在它记录判据在第几轮满足，若 60 轮内从未满足就直接报错，而不会把一个未收敛的状态当成 u* 返回。

A fixed sweep count, but a checked one. The code runs a fixed 60 sweeps rather than exiting on convergence: every measured constant quoted on this page was produced under those sweeps, and exiting early would shift u* in its last bits and move every finite-difference comparison with it. What actually matters is whether it is checked — it now records the sweep at which the criterion was met, and raises an error if 60 sweeps never meet it, rather than returning an unconverged state as u*.

“伴随”就是转置

"Adjoint" means transpose

对任意矩阵 A 和向量 v,w，恒有

For any matrix A and vectors v,w, it always holds that

⟨w,Av⟩=wTAv=(ATw)Tv=⟨ATw,v⟩.

满足这一关系的算子称为伴随算子，对实矩阵就是转置。前向乘积把“状态上的扰动”映射成“残差的变化”；伴随乘积方向相反，把“残差上的权重”映射成“状态上的敏感度”。

An operator satisfying this relation is called the adjoint; for a real matrix it is the transpose. The forward product maps "a perturbation of the state" to "the change in the residual"; the adjoint product runs the other way, mapping "a weight on the residual" to "a sensitivity on the state".

Reversing the one-dimensional flux

前向的一维通量是“两个输入、一个输出”：(vL,vR)↦hd=cLvL+cRvR。它的转置是“一个输入、两个输出”：给面上的权重 hb，返回

The forward one-dimensional flux is "two inputs, one output": (vL,vR)↦hd=cLvL+cRvR. Its transpose is "one input, two outputs": given a weight hb on the face, it returns

(uL,b,uR,b)=(cLhb,cRhb).

整个边循环也按同样的规则倒过来：原来写 r[i] 的地方现在读 w[i]，原来读 u[i] 的地方现在写 y[i]。注意符号：原来是 r[i]+=h、r[j]−=h，转置后就是 hb=w[i]−w[j]；两个分发则都是加。

The whole edge loop reverses by the same rule: where it used to write r[i] it now reads w[i], and where it used to read u[i] it now writes y[i]. Mind the signs: what was r[i]+=h and r[j]−=h becomes hb=w[i]−w[j] after transposing, while both scatters are additions.

在里对比第 7 节：底部数组的箭头方向反过来了——输入权重 w 在下面（残差一侧），结果 y 在上面（状态一侧）。这就是“转置”在数据流上的样子。无粘壁面半面上 cL=0，所以它在伴随里同样什么都不贡献；无滑移强加时则相反：该行的权重要进循环前清零、出循环后加回（行替换 → 列替换）。

Compare with Section 7: the arrows on the bottom arrays have reversed — the input weights w are below (the residual side) and the result y above (the state side). 强边界条件下的 Jacobi 迭代

The Jacobi iteration under a strong boundary condition

把物面换成强加的无滑移壁之后，Jacobi 迭代本身不用改写（约束行的处理已在第 8 节给出：进循环前清零、出循环后加回），但有两处必须看清楚。

Once the body surface becomes a strongly imposed no-slip wall the Jacobi iteration itself needs no rewriting — the constrained rows were dealt with in Section 8 (zero before the loop, add back after) — but two points have to be seen clearly.

一、对角元在约束行上恰好是 1。D=diag(AbcT)，而对角元在转置下不变；约束行已被换成 eiT，所以 Di=1 精确成立。内部行的对角元与同一状态下未约束的 A 相同，依旧逐个不同（算例实测 D∈[1.572,2.601]）。

First, the diagonal entry on a constrained row is exactly 1. D=diag(AbcT), and diagonal entries are unchanged by transposing; the constrained rows have already been replaced by eiT, so Di=1 exactly. The diagonals of the interior rows are the same as those of the unconstrained A at the same state, and still differ from one another (measured here as D∈[1.572,2.601]).

二、约束行的更新退化成直接赋值。AbcT 的第 i 列是 ei，所以 (AbcTψ)i 里已经含有 ψi 本身；再代入 Di=1：

Second, the update on a constrained row degenerates into a direct assignment. The i-th column of AbcT is ei, so (AbcTψ)i already contains ψi itself; substituting Di=1:

ψi←ψi−(ψi+Σj 未约束ajiψj+gi)=−(gi+Σj 未约束ajiψj).
ψi←ψi−(ψi+Σj unconstrainedajiψj+gi)=−(gi+Σj unconstrainedajiψj).

这不是“向解松弛”，而是一步到位的赋值：给定其余各行，约束行在一次扫描内就取到最终值。算例实测这一恒等式的残差为 0（精确成立）。整体收敛（右端用的是第 10 节那个近壁剪切目标 Lv：本页的阻力型 L 一旦强加，右端恒为零，一步就停）：约束 Jacobi 迭代 93 次后残差降到 10−13 量级，与直接解的最大差 3.0e−13。

This is not "relaxation towards the solution" but an assignment that arrives in one step: given the remaining rows, a constrained row reaches its final value within a single sweep. The residual of this identity is measured as 0 (it holds exactly). 一个容易讲错的地方：换一个 D 不会换出另一个解。Jacobi 的不动点由残差 AbcTψ+g=0 决定，任何能收敛的 D 都收敛到同一个 ψ；D 只改变路径，不改变终点。算例里（同样以 Lv 为右端）故意用弱加的 D=diag(A) 去配强加算子，结果与直接解一致，迭代 93 次对 93 次——停在同一轮。所以这里该说的是“D 要取得自洽”，而不是“取错了就会算错”。

A point that is easily stated wrongly: a different D does not give a different solution. The fixed point of Jacobi is fixed by the residual AbcTψ+g=0, so every D that converges converges to the same ψ; D changes the path, not the destination. The test case (again with Lv on the right-hand side) deliberately pairs the weakly imposed D=diag(A) with the strongly imposed operator, and the result matches the direct solve — 93 iterations against 93 — they stop on the same sweep. So what should be said here is that "D has to be chosen self-consistently", not that "getting it wrong gives a wrong answer".

The explicit term of the objective

L=Σ壁面12uL2(n·e) 同样显式依赖坐标，而且只在物面上：

After projection it is a constant vector, simpler even than the flux term. On an inviscid wall h≡0, so the wall contributes nothing to ψT∂R/∂X while it does contribute to ∂L/∂X — the two terms accumulate in the same edge loop, which is why gradient(u, psi) delivers the complete gradient in a single pass.

两项可以分开验证。把种子取成 ψ=0：非壁面分支的 hb 全为零，只剩目标函数那一项，于是 gradient(u, 0) 就是 ∂L/∂X；再用 gradient(u, psi) 减掉它，剩下的正是 ψT∂R/∂X。两半分别与中心差分（u 冻结）对照：8.9e−11 与 8.9e−11。

The two terms can be verified separately. Take the seed ψ=0: every hb outside the wall branch vanishes and only the objective term is left, so gradient(u, 0) is ∂L/∂X; subtract it from gradient(u, psi) and what remains is exactly ψT∂R/∂X. The two halves agree with central differences (with u frozen) to 8.9e−11 and 8.9e−11.

The geometric operator has a forward and an adjoint direction as well, and a dot-product test pins it down just the same:

⟨w,(∂R/∂X)δX⟩=⟨(∂R/∂X)Tw,δX⟩.

Note that w lives in the residual space (24 dimensions) and δX in the coordinate space (48 dimensions) — the two spaces have different dimensions and the transpose relation holds regardless: one more sign that "adjoint" and "transpose" are the same thing.

强加无滑移时的几何项

The geometric term under strongly imposed no-slip

约束行被换成 Ri=ui−uw，而 uw 是常数、与网格无关，于是

The constrained rows are replaced by Ri=ui−uw, and uw is a constant with nothing to do with the mesh, so

∂Ri∂X=∂(ui−uw)∂X=0,i∈W.

约束行对几何梯度毫无贡献。实现上就是进几何循环之前把 ψ 在约束行清零——与第 8 节伴随边循环里那一步完全相同。

The constrained rows contribute nothing whatever to the geometric gradient. In the implementation that is simply zeroing ψ on the constrained rows before entering the geometric loop — exactly the step in the adjoint edge loop of Section 8.

强边界条件下：这个目标函数会退化

Under a strong boundary condition: this objective degenerates

把物面换成强加的无滑移壁之后，壁面上的 u 全部等于常数 uw，于是

Once the body surface becomes a strongly imposed no-slip wall, every u on the wall equals the constant uw, so

L=Σ壁面12uw2(n·e)=12uw2Σ壁面(n·e).
L=Σwall12uw2(n·e)=12uw2Σwall(n·e).

物体封闭，Σ壁面n=0，所以括号里的和恒等于零——不是近似。算例实测 Σ壁面(n·e)=0 精确成立，任意常数 uw 给出的 L 都只是舍入误差（量级随 uw2 增长）。既然 L≡0，dL/dX≡0：强加之后这个阻力型目标函数完全不可用。

The body is closed, so Σwalln=0 and the sum in brackets is identically zero — not approximately so. The test case measures Σwall(n·e)=0 exactly, and any constant uw whatsoever gives L at round-off level only (its size grows like uw2). Since L≡0, also dL/dX≡0: after strong imposition this drag-like objective is entirely unusable.

这不是本页的缺陷，而是真实现象：粘性壁上的阻力从来不用壁面值算，而用近壁梯度（壁面剪切应力）算——近壁梯度依赖内部自由度，才对流场敏感。

This is not a flaw in the page but a real phenomenon: drag on a viscous wall is never computed from wall values but from the near-wall gradient (the wall shear stress) — and the near-wall gradient depends on interior degrees of freedom, which is what makes it sensitive to the flow.

同一件事的第四次出现。primal 替换行、tangent 覆盖行、adjoint 清零列，到几何梯度这里是清零种子。约束自由度既不产生几何敏感性，也不接收几何敏感性——四处动作必须一致，漏掉任何一处，点积测试或差分校验总有一个会立刻失败。

The same thing appearing for a fourth time. primal replaces a row, the tangent overwrites a row, the adjoint zeroes a column, and here in the geometric gradient it zeroes the seed. A constrained degree of freedom neither produces geometric sensitivity nor receives it — the four actions have to agree: miss any one of them and either the dot-product test or the finite-difference check fails at once.

一条必然成立的结构性检查

A structural check that cannot fail

把整个网格刚体平移，所有法向都不变，因此 L 不变。于是梯度必须满足

Translate the whole mesh rigidly and no normal changes, so L does not change either. The gradient must therefore satisfy

ΣkdLdXk=0

（两个坐标分量各自求和为零）。这条与差分无关、与算例无关，纯粹由问题的平移不变性决定，是几何梯度最便宜的自检——不过它只查平移不变性：通量导数的符号即使写错，各分量之和照样为零。算例实测两个分量之和的最大绝对值为 2.4×10−16。

(each of the two coordinate components summing to zero on its own). This owes nothing to finite differences and nothing to this particular test case; it follows purely from the translational invariance of the problem, and it is the cheapest self-check a geometric gradient has, though it only tests translational invariance: even a sign error in the flux derivative still sums to zero. The measured largest absolute value of the two component sums is 2.4×10−16.

A real solver has one more layer. This page treats every node coordinate as a design variable. In real shape optimisation the design variables are surface parameters (FFD control points, spline coefficients, …), and the volume mesh is produced from the surface by a deformation algorithm: Xvol=M(Xsurf). The chain rule then gains one more link, ∂M/∂Xsurf: in the adjoint direction the dL/dXvol obtained here is sent back to the surface through the transpose of the mesh-deformation algorithm. The structure does not change at all, there is simply one more link — and that link has to be verified separately, with a dot-product test of its own.

验证：dL/dX 对有限差分

Verification: dL/dX against finite differences

上一节把 dL/dX 推了出来：伴随只解一次线性系统，就拿到全部分量。这一节只做一件事——验证它是对的。

The previous section derived dL/dX: the adjoint solves one linear system and gets every component. This section does one thing only — check that it is right.

判据是最朴素的那个，逐个坐标分量做中心差分：

The criterion is the plainest one there is, a central difference taken coordinate by coordinate:

dLdXk≈L(Xk+h)−L(Xk−h)2h.

关键在于 L(Xk±h) 是完整的目标函数：每次都要把网格改形、重新解一次非线性 primal、再算目标。所以这里验证的是全导数——状态对几何的响应也算在内——而不是冻结状态的偏导数。代价也正因如此：中心差分每个分量两次扰动，一共 2M 次非线性求解，而伴随只用了一次线性求解。

What matters is that L(Xk±h) is the complete objective: every evaluation deforms the mesh, solves the nonlinear primal again, and only then computes the objective. So what is verified here is the total derivative — the response of the state to the geometry included — and not a partial derivative at a frozen state. That is also what it costs: two perturbations per component, 2M nonlinear solves in all, against the single linear solve of the adjoint.

还有省一半的做法——前向差分，只往一侧扰动：

There is also a way to halve that — a forward difference, perturbing to one side only:

dLdXk≈L(Xk+h)−L(X)h.

基准值 L(X) 全局只算一次，于是总共约 M 次非线性求解而不是 2M 次。代价是精度低一个档次：截断误差是 O(h) 而不是 O(h2)。两者的误差随步长怎么走， 底部可以逐个变量扫描出来。

The baseline L(X) is computed once for all, so the total is about M nonlinear solves rather than 2M. The price is one order of accuracy: the truncation error is O(h) instead of O(h2). The sweep at the foot of plots how the two errors run with the step size, one variable at a time.

这里校验的是无粘（未约束）壁面那一版：目标 L 取物面压力型积分，壁面自由度并不受约束，所以整条链路就是第 10 节推的那条。若把物面换成强加无滑移，链路结构不变，只是目标要换成近壁剪切型 Lv，并且组装几何梯度之前要先把约束行上的 ψ 清零（第 10 节）——同一节还把那一版的四步逐项实测过了。

What is checked here is the inviscid (unconstrained) wall version: L is a pressure-type surface integral and the wall degrees of freedom are not constrained, so the chain is exactly the one derived in Section 10. Replace the body surface by a strongly imposed no-slip wall and the structure of the chain stays the same; the objective has to become the near-wall shear type Lv, and ψ must be zeroed on the constrained rows before the geometric gradient is assembled (Section 10), and the same section measures all four steps of that version.

步长为什么要能调。中心差分的误差有两段：截断误差约 O(h2)，随 h 变小而减小；舍入误差约 O(ε/h)（ε 为机器精度），随 h 变小反而增大。两者相抵，最佳步长大致在 h∼ε1/3，也就是 10−5 到 10−6 量级。把 h 调到 10−3 会看到截断误差主导，调到 10−10 会看到舍入噪声主导——两头都会让“验证”看起来像是失败了。这正是差分校验最容易骗人的地方，也是点积测试（第 8 节）比它更可靠的原因：点积测试是精确恒等式，根本不含步长。

Why the step has to be adjustable. The error of a central difference has two parts: truncation, about O(h2), which shrinks as h shrinks; and round-off, about O(ε/h) (with ε the machine precision), which grows as h shrinks. Between them the best step lies roughly at h∼ε1/3, of order 10−5 to 10−6. Set h to 10−3 and truncation dominates; set it to 10−10 and round-off noise does — at either end the "verification" looks like a failure. This is exactly where a difference check is most deceptive, and why the dot-product test (Section 8) is the more reliable of the two: it is an exact identity, with no step size in it at all.

那么哪一侧更糟？要看离最优点多远，因为两侧的斜率根本不同：h 每放大一个数量级，截断误差涨约 100 倍（二次）；h 每缩小一个数量级，舍入误差只涨约 10 倍（线性）。实测扫描里截断一侧的斜率很干净，舍入一侧则起伏不定（舍入误差本身近乎随机）；中心差分的最小值多数落在 h 约 10−4 到 10−6 之间，与 ε1/3 的量级一致。于是离最优点越远，偏大的那一侧通常越吃亏，但对个别分量两侧可能相差不到一个数量级。实用结论是——宁可把 h 取偏小，也不要取偏大。

So which side is worse? It depends how far from the optimum you are, because the two slopes are quite different: enlarging h by one order multiplies the truncation error by about 100 (quadratic), while shrinking h by one order multiplies the round-off error by only about 10 (linear). In the measured sweep the truncation slope is clean while the round-off side is ragged (round-off is close to random); the central minimum mostly lies between h≈10−4 and 10−6, consistent with the ε1/3 estimate. So the further from the optimum, the more the too-large side usually costs, though for a few components the two sides differ by less than an order of magnitude. The practical conclusion — better to take h a little too small than a little too large.

  • 对偶控制体是造出来的：在每个单元内把边中点与单元中心相连，拼出每个节点的控制体；三角形恰好三等分，四边形不是四等分，所以体积要逐块累加。
  • The dual control volume has to be constructed: inside each cell join the edge midpoints to the cell centre, and assemble the control volume of every node from the pieces; a triangle divides into exactly three equal parts, but a quadrilateral does not divide into four, so the volume has to be accumulated piece by piece.
  • 边界条件只住在 flux 里面：无粘壁面取 h=0，远场按特征方向选入流/出流；边循环一行都不用改。
  • Boundary conditions live inside flux alone: an inviscid wall takes h=0, and the far field picks inflow or outflow by the characteristic direction; not one line of the edge loop changes.
  • 强边界条件是唯一的例外：无滑移强加时，边循环本身不动：primal 在循环之后替换行、tangent 在循环之后覆盖行，adjoint 在循环之前清零对应权重、循环之后加回（行替换变成列替换）；顺序写反了点积测试会立即失败。
  • A strong boundary condition is the one exception: with no-slip strongly imposed, the edge loop itself does not change: primal replaces a row after the loop, the tangent overwrites a row after it, and the adjoint zeroes the matching weights before the loop and adds them back after (row replacement becomes column replacement); get the order backwards and the dot-product test fails at once.
  • 一维通量是核心：控制体造好之后，残差就是“对每个对偶面调用一次一维通量，再收集与分发”。
  • The one-dimensional flux is the core: once the control volumes are built, the residual is simply "call the one-dimensional flux once per dual face, then gather and scatter".
  • matrix-free 前向：对一维通量求局部导数 cL,cR，边循环不变。
  • Matrix-free forward: take the local derivatives cL,cR of the one-dimensional flux; the edge loop is unchanged.
  • matrix-free 伴随：每个面的贡献转置——一维通量从“两个输入、一个输出”变为“一个输入、两个输出”，边循环中收集与分发互换、数据方向反转。
  • Matrix-free adjoint: transpose the contribution of each face — the one-dimensional flux goes from "two inputs, one output" to "one input, two outputs", and in the edge loop gather and scatter swap while the data direction reverses.
  • 验证：点积测试检查转置，差分检查梯度。
  • Verification: the dot-product test checks the transpose, finite differences check the gradient.
